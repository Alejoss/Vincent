> The competition of capital will tend to equalize profits in different branches of industry.

— [[Person – David Ricardo]]

## Interpretation
- Capital seeks the highest returns, leading to shifts in investment between industries.
- As capital moves, it drives profit levels toward equilibrium across different sectors.
- This process illustrates the self-regulating nature of competitive markets.
