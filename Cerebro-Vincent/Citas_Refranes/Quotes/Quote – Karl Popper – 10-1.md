> Theories should be regarded as hypotheses that can be tested and potentially falsified.

— [[Person – Karl Popper]]

## Interpretation
- Theories are not absolute truths but are provisional ideas subject to testing.
- Falsifiability is a key criterion for distinguishing scientific theories from non-scientific ones.
- Testing and potential falsification drive the advancement of scientific knowledge.
