> The law is the collective organization of the individual right to lawful defense.

— [[Person – Frédéric Bastiat]]

## Interpretation
- The law serves to protect individuals' rights to defend themselves.
- It represents a collective agreement among individuals to uphold justice.
- Law is a mechanism for ensuring that personal rights are respected within society.
