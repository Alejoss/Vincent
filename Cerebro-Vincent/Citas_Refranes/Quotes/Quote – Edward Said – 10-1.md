> The act of writing is a political act."

— [[Person – Edward Said]]

## Interpretation
- Writing has the power to influence public opinion and shape political discourse.
- The content and context of writing reflect and challenge existing power structures.
- Authors have a responsibility to recognize the implications of their words in a sociopolitical context.
