> The struggle for freedom is not just a political battle; it is also a moral one.

— [[Person – Isaiah Berlin]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom encompasses both political rights and ethical considerations.
- The fight for freedom involves not only external challenges but also internal moral convictions.
- Moral integrity is essential in the pursuit of true liberty.
