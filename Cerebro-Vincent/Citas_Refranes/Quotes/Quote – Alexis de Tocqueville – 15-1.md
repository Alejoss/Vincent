> The love of wealth is a great hindrance to the love of wisdom.

— [[Person – Alexis de Tocqueville]]

## Interpretation
- The pursuit of wealth can distract individuals from seeking deeper knowledge and understanding.
- Valuing material possessions may lead to superficial thinking rather than philosophical inquiry.
- A focus on accumulating wealth can create obstacles to personal and intellectual growth.
