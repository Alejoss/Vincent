> The state is a social institution which has become the instrument of the ruling classes."

— [[Person – Rudolf Rocker]]

## Interpretation
- The state serves the interests of the elite rather than the general populace.
- It highlights the power dynamics between the ruling class and the governed.
- The quote suggests a critique of the state's role in perpetuating social inequalities.
