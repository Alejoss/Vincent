> If you put the federal government in charge of the Sahara Desert, in five years there’d be a shortage of sand.

— [[Person – Milton Friedman]]

## Interpretation
- Government intervention often leads to inefficiencies and shortages.
- Centralized control can undermine natural resource management.
- Friedman critiques the capability of government to manage complex systems effectively.
