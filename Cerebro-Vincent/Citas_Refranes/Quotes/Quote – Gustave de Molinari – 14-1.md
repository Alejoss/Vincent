> The essence of government is the protection of individual rights."

— [[Person – Gustave de Molinari]]

## Interpretation
- Government's primary role is to safeguard personal freedoms.
- Individual rights are fundamental to a just society.
- The legitimacy of government is derived from its ability to protect these rights.
