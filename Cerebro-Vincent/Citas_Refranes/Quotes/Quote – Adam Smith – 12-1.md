> Commerce is the great civilizer of mankind.

— [[Person – Adam Smith]]

## Interpretation
- Commerce fosters connections between diverse cultures and societies.
- It promotes mutual understanding and cooperation among individuals.
- Economic interactions can lead to social progress and the development of civil society.
