> The role of the economist is to clarify the trade-offs involved in public policy decisions."

— [[Person – James M. Buchanan]]

## Interpretation
- Economists help illuminate the consequences of different policy choices.
- Understanding trade-offs is essential for making informed decisions.
- Clarity in economic implications aids policymakers in evaluating options.
