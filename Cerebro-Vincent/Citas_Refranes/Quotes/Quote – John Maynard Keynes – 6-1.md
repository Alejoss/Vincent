> The boom, not the slump, is the right time for austerity.

— [[Person – John Maynard Keynes]]

## Interpretation
- Austerity measures should be implemented during economic growth periods rather than during recessions.
- This approach can help prevent overheating of the economy and maintain stability.
- Keynes advocates for proactive fiscal policies to manage economic cycles effectively.
