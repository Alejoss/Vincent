> The purpose of life is not to be happy, but to be worthy of happiness.

— [[Person – Ayn Rand]]

## Interpretation
- True fulfillment comes from earning and deserving happiness through one's actions.
- Happiness is a byproduct of living a life aligned with one's values and principles.
- The focus should be on personal integrity and self-improvement rather than the pursuit of happiness itself.
