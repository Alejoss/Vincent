> Life is not a mere sequence of events, but a process of evolution.

— [[Person – Herbert Spencer]]

## Interpretation
- Life involves continuous development rather than isolated occurrences.
- Evolution signifies growth and adaptation over time.
- Understanding life as a process encourages a broader perspective on experiences.
