> Racism is permanent, a structure we need to understand and confront."

— [[Person – Derrick Bell]]

## Interpretation
- Racism is not an isolated incident but an enduring part of societal structures.
- Understanding racism as a permanent fixture calls for a continuous and proactive approach to address it.
- Confronting racism requires both awareness and action to dismantle its ingrained presence.
