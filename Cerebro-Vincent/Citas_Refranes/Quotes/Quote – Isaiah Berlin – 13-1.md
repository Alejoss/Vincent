> The greatest danger to liberty is the belief that it can be taken for granted.

— [[Person – Isaiah Berlin]]

## Interpretation
- Liberty requires active appreciation and vigilance to be maintained.
- Complacency in freedom can lead to its erosion or loss.
- Assuming liberty is permanent undermines the efforts needed to protect it.
