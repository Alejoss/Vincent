> Happiness is a man’s greatest aim in life.

— [[Person – Jean-Jacques Rousseau]]

## Interpretation
- Happiness is a fundamental pursuit that drives human existence.
- It suggests that all actions and choices are ultimately oriented towards achieving happiness.
- Rousseau emphasizes the importance of personal fulfillment and well-being in life.
