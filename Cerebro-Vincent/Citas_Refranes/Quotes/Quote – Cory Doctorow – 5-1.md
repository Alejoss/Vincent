> The Internet is for everyone.

— [[Person – Cory Doctorow]]

## Interpretation
- The Internet is a universal resource that should be accessible to all individuals.
- It emphasizes inclusivity and the democratizing potential of digital technology.
- The statement advocates against restrictions that could limit access based on socioeconomic status or geography.
