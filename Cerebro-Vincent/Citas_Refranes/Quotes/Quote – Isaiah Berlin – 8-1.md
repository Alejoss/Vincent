> Freedom is a process of becoming, not a state of being.

— [[Person – Isaiah Berlin]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom is an ongoing journey of personal and social development.
- It emphasizes the importance of growth and change rather than a fixed condition.
- True freedom involves continual efforts and struggles for self-realization and autonomy.
