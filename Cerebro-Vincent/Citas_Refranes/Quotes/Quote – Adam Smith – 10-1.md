> No society can surely be flourishing and happy, of which the far greater part of the members are poor and miserable.

— [[Person – Adam Smith]]

## Interpretation
- A society's overall well-being is dependent on the welfare of its majority.
- Poverty and misery among the majority undermine societal prosperity.
- True flourishing and happiness require equitable conditions for all members.
