> The unique individual is the highest form of existence.

— [[Person – Max Stirner]]

## Interpretation
- The quote emphasizes the importance of individual identity and autonomy.
- Stirner suggests that true existence is defined by self-realization and personal uniqueness.
- It advocates for the celebration of individuality over collective norms or societal expectations.
