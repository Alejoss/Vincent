> The biggest problem in security today is that we have too much trust in technology.

— [[Person – Bruce Schneier]]

## Interpretation
- We often rely on technological solutions without questioning their limitations.
- Overtrusting technology can lead to vulnerabilities and security breaches.
- A critical approach to technology is necessary to enhance security measures.
