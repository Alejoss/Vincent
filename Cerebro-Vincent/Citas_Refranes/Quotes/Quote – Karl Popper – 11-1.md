> A theory that explains everything explains nothing.

— [[Person – Karl Popper]]

## Interpretation
- Comprehensive theories often lack specificity, making them untestable.
- A theory must be falsifiable to have scientific value.
- Overly broad explanations dilute the meaningfulness of specific predictions.
