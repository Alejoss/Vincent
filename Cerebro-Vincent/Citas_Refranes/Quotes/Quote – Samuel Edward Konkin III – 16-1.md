> The initiation of force is the only crime."

— [[Person – Samuel Edward Konkin III]]

## Interpretation
- The quote emphasizes that any act of coercion or violence against individuals is fundamentally wrong.
- It suggests that all other crimes stem from the initiation of force against others.
- The statement promotes a philosophy of non-aggression and individual liberty.
