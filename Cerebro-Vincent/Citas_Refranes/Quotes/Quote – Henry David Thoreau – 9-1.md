> The price of anything is the amount of life you exchange for it.

— [[Person – Henry David Thoreau]]

## Interpretation
- Value is determined by the time and energy invested in acquiring something.
- Life is finite, and every choice involves a trade-off of our limited resources.
- True worth comes from a deeper understanding of what we sacrifice for material possessions.
