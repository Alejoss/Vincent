> The decline of the industrial society does not mean the end of civilization; rather, it presents new opportunities for human progress."

— [[Person – Daniel Bell]]

## Interpretation
- The transition from industrial society signifies a shift rather than a collapse of civilization.
- This decline opens avenues for innovation and development in human society.
- Progress can be redefined and pursued in new forms beyond traditional industrial frameworks.
