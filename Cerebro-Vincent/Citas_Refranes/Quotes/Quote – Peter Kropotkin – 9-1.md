> The idea of individualism is a creation of the capitalist system, which seeks to isolate individuals.

— [[Person – Peter Kropotkin]]

## Interpretation
- Individualism is a construct that arises from capitalist ideologies.
- Capitalism promotes isolation by prioritizing personal success over community.
- This perspective critiques the notion of individualism as inherently beneficial, suggesting it serves a system that divides rather than unites.
