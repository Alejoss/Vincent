> The essence of the struggle for freedom is the struggle for the right to be human.

— [[Person – Emma Goldman]]

## Interpretation
- The fight for freedom is fundamentally tied to the recognition of human dignity.
- True freedom encompasses the ability to express one's humanity without oppression.
- Struggles for liberation are inherently linked to the affirmation of individual rights and identity.
