> I have a great deal of respect for the human mind, but I have much more for the human heart.

— [[Person – Emma Goldman]]

## Interpretation
- Goldman values emotional intelligence and compassion over purely intellectual reasoning.
- The quote highlights the importance of empathy and the human experience in understanding life.
- It suggests that genuine connection and feeling are more significant than logic alone.
