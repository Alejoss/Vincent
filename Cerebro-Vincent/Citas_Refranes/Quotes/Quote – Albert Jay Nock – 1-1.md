> The individual is not only a part of the world, but also the world is a part of the individual.

— [[Person – Albert Jay Nock]]

## Interpretation
- The individual has a unique identity and role within the broader context of the world.
- The world influences and shapes the individual's perceptions and experiences.
- There exists a mutual relationship between the individual and the world, highlighting interconnectedness.
