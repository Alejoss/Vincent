> Smart contracts are a way to set up the rules of a contract in code, allowing for automated execution without the need for a trusted third party."

— [[Person – Nick Szabo]]

## Interpretation
- Smart contracts automate the execution of agreements through coded rules.
- They eliminate the need for intermediaries, reducing reliance on trust.
- This technology enhances efficiency and transparency in contractual relationships.
