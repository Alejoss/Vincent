> The aim of socialism is not only the liberation of the working class but also the liberation of all oppressed.

— [[Person – Rosa Luxemburg]]

## Interpretation
- Socialism seeks to empower not just workers, but all marginalized groups in society.
- The liberation of the working class is interconnected with the freedom of all oppressed individuals.
- True socialism is inclusive, aiming for a holistic approach to social justice.
