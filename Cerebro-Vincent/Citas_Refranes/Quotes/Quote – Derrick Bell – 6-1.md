> We must be willing to see the world through the eyes of the oppressed."

— [[Person – Derrick Bell]]

## Interpretation
- Empathy towards the oppressed is crucial for understanding systemic injustices.
- Seeing the world through their eyes can challenge our own biases and assumptions.
- This perspective shift is essential for fostering social justice and equity.
