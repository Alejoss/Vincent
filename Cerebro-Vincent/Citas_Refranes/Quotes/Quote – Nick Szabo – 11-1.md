> Blockchains are not just a new technology; they represent a new way of thinking about trust and value."

— [[Person – Nick Szabo]]

## Interpretation
- Blockchains introduce a paradigm shift in how we perceive and establish trust.
- This technology redefines value by decentralizing it and removing intermediaries.
- The implications of blockchains extend beyond technical advancements to fundamental societal changes.
