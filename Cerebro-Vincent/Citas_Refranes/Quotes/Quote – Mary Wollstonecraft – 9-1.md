> I wish to persuade women to be more reasonable and to act from principle.

— [[Person – Mary Wollstonecraft]]

## Interpretation
- Wollstonecraft advocates for women's empowerment through rational thought.
- She emphasizes the importance of making decisions based on moral principles rather than societal expectations.
- The quote reflects a call for women to cultivate their intellect and agency.
