> Liberty is the right to think and act according to one’s own reason."

— [[Person – Lysander Spooner]]

## Interpretation
- Liberty encompasses individual autonomy in thought and action.
- The quote emphasizes the importance of personal reasoning in the exercise of freedom.
- True liberty is rooted in self-determination and personal judgment.
