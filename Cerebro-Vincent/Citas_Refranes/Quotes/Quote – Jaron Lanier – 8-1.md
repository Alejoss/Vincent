> A lot of the time, technology is seen as something that happens to us, but we have to remember that we are the ones who create it.

— [[Person – Jaron Lanier]]

## Interpretation
- Technology is often perceived as a force beyond our control.
- We must acknowledge our active role in the creation and development of technology.
- Understanding our agency in technology can empower us to shape its impact on society.
