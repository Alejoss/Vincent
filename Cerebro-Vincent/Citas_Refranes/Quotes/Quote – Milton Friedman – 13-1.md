> A major source of objection to a free economy is precisely that it does not provide a free hand to the government.

— [[Person – Milton Friedman]]

## Interpretation
- Friedman suggests that criticisms of free markets often stem from a desire for more government control.
- He implies that a truly free economy limits governmental intervention, which some view as a drawback.
- The quote highlights the tension between economic freedom and governmental authority.
