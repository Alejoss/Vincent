> I do not believe in the divine right of kings; I believe in the divine right of people to be free."

— [[Person – Voltairine de Cleyre]]

## Interpretation
- The quote rejects the notion of absolute authority granted by birth or divine sanction.
- It emphasizes the importance of individual freedom and autonomy over hierarchical power structures.
- De Cleyre advocates for the inherent rights of all people to govern their own lives without oppression.
