> The law is not a mere convenience; it is the foundation of society.

— [[Person – William Graham Sumner]]

## Interpretation
- The law serves as a crucial framework for societal order and stability.
- It establishes rules that govern behavior, ensuring coexistence among individuals.
- Without law, society would lack structure, leading to chaos and conflict.
