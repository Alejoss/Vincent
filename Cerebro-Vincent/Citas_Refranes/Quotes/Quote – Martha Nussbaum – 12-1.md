> The challenge of education is to teach students how to think, not what to think."

— [[Person – Martha Nussbaum]]

## Interpretation
- Education should focus on developing critical thinking skills.
- Students should be encouraged to form their own opinions and judgments.
- The goal is to empower individuals to navigate complex ideas independently.
