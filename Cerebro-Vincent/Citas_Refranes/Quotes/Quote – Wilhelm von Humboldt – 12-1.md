> The beauty of the world lies in the diversity of its people.

— [[Person – Wilhelm von Humboldt]]

## Interpretation
- The variety among people enhances the richness of human experience.
- Diversity contributes to the aesthetic and cultural value of the world.
- Embracing differences fosters understanding and appreciation among individuals.
