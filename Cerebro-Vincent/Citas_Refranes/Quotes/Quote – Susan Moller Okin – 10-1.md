> A just society must take into account the contributions of women.

— [[Person – Susan Moller Okin]]

## Interpretation
- A just society recognizes and values the essential roles women play in various aspects of life.
- This acknowledgment is crucial for achieving true equality and fairness.
- Ignoring women's contributions undermines the foundations of justice and societal progress.
