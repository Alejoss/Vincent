> We are all formed in a very different manner, and that can never be changed.

— [[Person – David Hume]]

## Interpretation
- Human nature is inherently diverse and unique.
- These differences are fundamental and immutable.
- Acceptance of our inherent differences is essential to understanding ourselves and others.
