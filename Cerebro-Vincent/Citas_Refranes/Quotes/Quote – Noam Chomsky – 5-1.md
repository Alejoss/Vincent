> Propaganda is to a democracy what the bludgeon is to a totalitarian state.

— [[Person – Noam Chomsky]]

## Interpretation
- In a democracy, propaganda serves to manipulate public opinion rather than using force.
- The comparison highlights the reliance on psychological control in democratic societies versus the physical control in totalitarian regimes.
- Chomsky critiques how democracies can use subtle means of coercion to maintain power.
