> In a democracy, the majority has the power to impose its will on the minority."

— [[Person – James M. Buchanan]]

## Interpretation
- Democracy inherently involves the majority making decisions that can affect the minority.
- This power dynamic raises concerns about the potential oppression of minority viewpoints.
- The quote highlights a fundamental tension between majority rule and minority rights in democratic systems.
