> It is not what you look at that matters, it is what you see.

— [[Person – Henry David Thoreau]]

## Interpretation
- Perception shapes our understanding of the world around us.
- True insight requires deeper observation beyond mere appearance.
- The value of experiences lies in their interpretation, not just their observation.
