> To understand a language is to understand a culture.

— [[Person – Wilhelm von Humboldt]]

## Interpretation
- Language is a reflection of cultural values and beliefs.
- Understanding a language allows for deeper insights into the customs and practices of its speakers.
- Language serves as a bridge that connects individuals to the historical and social contexts of a culture.
