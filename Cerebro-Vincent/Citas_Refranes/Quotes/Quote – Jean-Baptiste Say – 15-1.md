> Wealth is not merely a quantity of goods; it is the capacity to obtain what is desired."

— [[Person – Jean-Baptiste Say]]

## Interpretation
- Wealth transcends physical possessions and reflects the ability to fulfill personal wants.
- The quote emphasizes the importance of access and opportunity over mere accumulation of goods.
- True wealth is defined by one's capability to achieve desires, indicating a more subjective understanding of prosperity.
