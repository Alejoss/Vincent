> The principle of demarcation is central to the philosophy of science.

— [[Person – Karl Popper]]

## Interpretation
- The principle of demarcation distinguishes scientific theories from non-scientific ones.
- It is essential for evaluating the validity and credibility of scientific claims.
- This principle helps to clarify what constitutes genuine science and what does not.
