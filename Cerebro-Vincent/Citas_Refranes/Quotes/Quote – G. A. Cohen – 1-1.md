> A just society is one that is based on equality and the fair distribution of resources."

— [[Person – G. A. Cohen]]

## Interpretation
- A just society prioritizes equality among its members.
- Fair distribution of resources is essential for achieving social justice.
- The quote emphasizes the moral imperative of addressing disparities in wealth and resources.
