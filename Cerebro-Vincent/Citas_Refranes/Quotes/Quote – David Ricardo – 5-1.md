> The distribution of wealth is determined by the relative bargaining power of the different groups.

— [[Person – David Ricardo]]

## Interpretation
- Wealth distribution is influenced by the power dynamics among social groups.
- The ability of different groups to negotiate affects their share of resources.
- Bargaining power is a critical factor in economic inequality.
