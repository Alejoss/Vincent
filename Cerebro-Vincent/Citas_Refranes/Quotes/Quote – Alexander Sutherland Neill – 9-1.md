> The best way to create a better world is to start with the individual.

— [[Person – Alexander Sutherland Neill]]

## Interpretation
- Individual transformation leads to collective change.
- Empowering individuals is essential for societal improvement.
- Change begins at the personal level before it can ripple outwards.
