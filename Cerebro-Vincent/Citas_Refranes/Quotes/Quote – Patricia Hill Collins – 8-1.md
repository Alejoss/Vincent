> The experiences of Black women are often marginalized within feminist discourse."

— [[Person – Patricia Hill Collins]]

## Interpretation
- Black women's experiences are frequently overlooked in mainstream feminist discussions.
- This marginalization reflects broader societal patterns of excluding voices from marginalized groups.
- Recognizing these experiences is essential for a more inclusive and effective feminism.
