> The notion of the 'people' is a construction that must be continually rearticulated."

— [[Person – Chantal Mouffe]]

## Interpretation
- The 'people' is not a fixed entity but a dynamic concept shaped by social and political contexts.
- Rearticulating the 'people' involves ongoing dialogue and negotiation within society.
- This construction reflects the fluid nature of identity and collective belonging.
