> Injustice is tolerable only when it is necessary to avoid an even greater injustice.

— [[Person – John Rawls]]

## Interpretation
- Injustice may be accepted if it prevents a more severe form of injustice.
- This quote emphasizes the moral complexity of ethical decision-making.
- It suggests a pragmatic approach to justice, prioritizing the greater good.
