> We are not trying to create a new currency, we are trying to create a new financial system.

— [[Person – Satoshi Nakamoto]]

## Interpretation
- The focus is on transforming the entire financial infrastructure rather than just introducing a new form of money.
- This indicates a broader vision that encompasses decentralization and financial inclusivity.
- The statement suggests a shift from traditional financial systems to a more innovative and potentially equitable model.
