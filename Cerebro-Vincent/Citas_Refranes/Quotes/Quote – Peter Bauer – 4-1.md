> Economic growth is not a zero-sum game; it benefits everyone."

— [[Person – Peter Bauer]]

## Interpretation
- Economic growth creates opportunities for improvement and prosperity across society.
- The notion of a zero-sum game implies limited resources, but growth expands the overall wealth and resources available.
- Everyone can benefit from economic advancements, rather than just a select few.
