> Surveillance capitalism is a breach of trust that undermines the social contract."

— [[Person – Shoshana Zuboff]]

## Interpretation
- Surveillance capitalism erodes the trust between individuals and institutions.
- It disrupts the implicit agreements that govern societal interactions.
- This breach of trust can lead to significant societal consequences and power imbalances.
