> The wages of labor depend on the supply and demand of that labor.

— [[Person – David Ricardo]]

## Interpretation
- Wages are influenced by the availability of workers and the need for their services.
- A high demand for labor with limited supply will drive wages up.
- Conversely, an oversupply of labor with low demand can lead to lower wages.
