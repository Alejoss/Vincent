> Security is about managing risk.

— [[Person – Bruce Schneier]]

## Interpretation
- Security involves assessing potential threats and vulnerabilities.
- It emphasizes the importance of balancing safety measures with potential costs.
- Managing risk implies that absolute security is unattainable, necessitating informed decision-making.
