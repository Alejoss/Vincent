> We can only be ourselves, and that is enough.

— [[Person – Leo Tolstoy]]

## Interpretation
- Embracing our true selves is essential for authenticity.
- Self-acceptance is a source of contentment and fulfillment.
- The notion of 'enough' suggests that being genuine is valuable in itself.
