> Markets can remain irrational longer than you can remain solvent.

— [[Person – John Maynard Keynes]]

## Interpretation
- Markets may not always behave logically, creating risks for investors.
- Even well-reasoned strategies can fail if market conditions diverge from expectations.
- Financial stability is precarious when relying on market rationality that may not materialize.
