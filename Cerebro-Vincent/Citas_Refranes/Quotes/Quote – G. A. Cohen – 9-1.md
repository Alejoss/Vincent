> The pursuit of justice is not simply a matter of policy, but a matter of moral commitment."

— [[Person – G. A. Cohen]]

## Interpretation
- Justice requires more than just rules; it demands ethical dedication.
- A moral commitment implies that justice is a fundamental principle rather than a negotiable option.
- Policies alone are insufficient; they must be rooted in a deeper sense of right and wrong.
