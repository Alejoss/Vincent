> The rich should contribute to the education of the poor."

— [[Person – Richard Cobden]]

## Interpretation
- Wealthy individuals have a moral obligation to support the education of those less fortunate.
- Investing in education for the poor can lead to societal benefits and reduce inequality.
- The quote emphasizes the role of the privileged in uplifting marginalized communities through access to knowledge.
