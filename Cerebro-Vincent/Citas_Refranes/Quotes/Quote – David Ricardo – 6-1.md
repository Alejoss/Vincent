> Rent is the portion of the produce of the earth which is paid to the landlord for the use of his land.

— [[Person – David Ricardo]]

## Interpretation
- Rent is a payment made to landowners for the use of their property.
- It represents a portion of the agricultural or economic output generated from the land.
- This concept highlights the landlord's role in the economic system and the value of land as a resource.
