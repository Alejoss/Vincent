> The logic of the scientific method is the logic of deduction.

— [[Person – Karl Popper]]

## Interpretation
- Scientific inquiry relies on logical reasoning to formulate hypotheses.
- Deduction involves deriving specific predictions from general principles.
- Popper emphasizes the importance of logical structure in validating scientific theories.
