> Freedom is the essence of all that is human.

— [[Person – Wilhelm von Humboldt]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom is fundamental to human identity and existence.
- The essence of humanity is tied to the capacity for choice and self-determination.
- Without freedom, human potential and individuality are stifled.
