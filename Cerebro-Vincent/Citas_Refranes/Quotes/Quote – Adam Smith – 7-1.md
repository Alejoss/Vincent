> The annual labor of every nation is the fund which originally supplies it with all the necessaries and conveniencies of life.

— [[Person – Adam Smith]]

## Interpretation
- The labor of a nation's workforce is the primary source of its resources.
- All necessities and comforts derive from the productivity of labor.
- Economic prosperity is fundamentally linked to the efforts of individuals within a society.
