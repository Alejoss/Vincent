> The quest for freedom is an ongoing journey, not a destination.

— [[Person – Isaiah Berlin]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom is not a fixed state but a continuous pursuit.
- The journey towards freedom involves constant effort and reflection.
- Achieving freedom requires adaptability to changing circumstances and challenges.
