> The task of politics is to create a space for the articulation of plural identities."

— [[Person – Chantal Mouffe]]

## Interpretation
- Politics should facilitate the expression and recognition of diverse identities.
- Creating a space for plural identities promotes inclusivity and democratic engagement.
- The articulation of these identities is essential for a dynamic and representative political discourse.
