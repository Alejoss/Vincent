> Orientalism is fundamentally a political doctrine willed over the Orient."

— [[Person – Edward Said]]

## Interpretation
- Orientalism is not just a cultural concept but a tool of political power.
- It reflects a Western imposition of ideas and values on the East.
- This doctrine shapes perceptions and justifies domination over the Orient.
