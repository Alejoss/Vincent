> I have always thought the actions of men the best interpreters of their thoughts.

— [[Person – John Locke]]

## Interpretation
- Actions reveal true intentions more than words.
- Behavior provides insight into individual beliefs and values.
- Understanding a person's thoughts requires observing their actions.
