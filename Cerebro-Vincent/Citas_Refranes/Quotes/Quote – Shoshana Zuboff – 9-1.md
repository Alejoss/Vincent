> In the age of surveillance capitalism, personal data is the most valuable currency."

— [[Person – Shoshana Zuboff]]

## Interpretation
- Personal data has become a key asset that drives economic value in modern society.
- Surveillance capitalism utilizes individuals' information to influence behavior and decision-making.
- The commodification of personal data raises ethical concerns about privacy and autonomy.
