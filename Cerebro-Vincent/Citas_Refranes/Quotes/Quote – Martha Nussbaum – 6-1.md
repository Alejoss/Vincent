> One of the main purposes of education is to help people become citizens of the world."

— [[Person – Martha Nussbaum]]

## Interpretation
- Education should foster a sense of global responsibility and awareness.
- Becoming a 'citizen of the world' implies understanding and valuing cultural diversity.
- This perspective encourages individuals to engage with global issues and contribute to a more interconnected society.
