> The great cause of the world’s ills is that men cannot agree upon the nature of the good.

— [[Person – William Graham Sumner]]

## Interpretation
- Disagreement on moral values leads to conflict and suffering in society.
- Different perceptions of what is 'good' hinder cooperation among individuals and nations.
- The quest for a universal understanding of the good is essential for resolving global issues.
