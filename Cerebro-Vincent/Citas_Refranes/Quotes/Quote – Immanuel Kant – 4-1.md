> The only thing that is good without qualification is a good will.

— [[Person – Immanuel Kant]]

## Interpretation
- A good will is intrinsically valuable, independent of outcomes or consequences.
- Moral actions stem from a good will, highlighting the importance of intention in ethical behavior.
- Other qualities or actions may be deemed good or bad based on context, but a good will remains universally and unconditionally good.
