> The wealth of a nation is determined by the productivity of its labor force.

— [[Person – Adam Smith]]

## Interpretation
- A nation's economic prosperity is directly linked to how effectively its workforce can produce goods and services.
- Labor productivity influences not only individual wealth but also overall national wealth.
- Investment in labor skills and efficiency is crucial for enhancing a nation's economic status.
