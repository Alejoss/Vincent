> Coercion is the antithesis of a free society."

— [[Person – Samuel Edward Konkin III]]

## Interpretation
- Coercion undermines individual autonomy and freedom.
- A truly free society relies on voluntary interactions rather than force.
- The presence of coercion indicates a departure from the principles of liberty.
