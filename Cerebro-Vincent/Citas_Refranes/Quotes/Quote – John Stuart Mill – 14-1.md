> The only purpose for which power can be rightfully exercised over any member of a civilized community, against his will, is to prevent harm to others.

— [[Person – John Stuart Mill]]

## Interpretation
- Power should only be used to protect others from harm.
- Individual autonomy is paramount unless it infringes on the rights or safety of others.
- The justification for authority is rooted in the collective well-being of society.
