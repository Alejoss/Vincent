> Freedom is not a gift, but a responsibility.

— [[Person – William Graham Sumner]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom requires active participation and commitment from individuals.
- It emphasizes that true freedom comes with the duty to uphold and protect it.
- Receiving freedom without understanding its responsibilities can lead to misuse or neglect.
