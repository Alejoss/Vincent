> The only legitimate government is one that is based on the consent of the governed."

— [[Person – Lysander Spooner]]

## Interpretation
- Legitimacy in government arises from the explicit agreement of the people it governs.
- Consent implies an active participation of the populace in the political process.
- Governments lacking consent are seen as unjust and potentially tyrannical.
