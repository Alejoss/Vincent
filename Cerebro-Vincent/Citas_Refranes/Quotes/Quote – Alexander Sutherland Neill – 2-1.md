> To be happy, you must be true to yourself.

— [[Person – Alexander Sutherland Neill]]

## Interpretation
- Happiness stems from authenticity and self-acceptance.
- Being true to oneself fosters a sense of inner peace and fulfillment.
- Ignoring personal truths can lead to discontent and unhappiness.
