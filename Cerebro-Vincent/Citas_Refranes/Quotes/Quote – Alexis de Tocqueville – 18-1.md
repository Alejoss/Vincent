> I do not believe that the intellect can be governed by laws.

— [[Person – Alexis de Tocqueville]]

## Interpretation
- The intellect operates independently of external regulations or constraints.
- Attempts to impose laws on intellectual thought may stifle creativity and innovation.
- True understanding and insight cannot be confined within rigid frameworks.
