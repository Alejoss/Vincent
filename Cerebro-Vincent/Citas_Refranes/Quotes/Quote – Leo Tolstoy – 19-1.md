> Without the capacity to love, life is not worth living.

— [[Person – Leo Tolstoy]]

## Interpretation
- Love is essential for a fulfilling and meaningful existence.
- A life devoid of love lacks depth and purpose.
- The ability to love enriches human experience and connection.
