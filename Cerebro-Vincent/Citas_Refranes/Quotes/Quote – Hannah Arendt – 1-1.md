> The sad truth is that most evil is done by people who never make up their minds to be good or evil.

— [[Person – Hannah Arendt]]

## Interpretation
- Many individuals commit harmful acts without a conscious decision to embrace evil.
- Moral ambiguity is often a result of inaction or indifference rather than outright malevolence.
- The quote highlights the dangers of passivity in moral judgment.
