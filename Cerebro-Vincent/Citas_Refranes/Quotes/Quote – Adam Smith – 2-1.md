> It is not from the benevolence of the butcher, the brewer, or the baker that we expect our dinner, but from their regard to their own interest.

— [[Person – Adam Smith]]

## Interpretation
- Economic transactions are driven by self-interest rather than altruism.
- Individuals engage in trade to fulfill their own needs, which inadvertently benefits others.
- The functioning of the market relies on the pursuit of personal gain, not on acts of kindness.
