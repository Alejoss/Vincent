> The question is not whether we will be able to make a revolution, but whether we will be able to live with it."

— [[Person – Cornelius Castoriadis]]

## Interpretation
- The focus is not solely on the act of revolution but on the aftermath and its implications.
- Living with the changes brought about by a revolution poses significant challenges.
- It highlights the importance of adaptability and the ability to navigate transformed social realities.
