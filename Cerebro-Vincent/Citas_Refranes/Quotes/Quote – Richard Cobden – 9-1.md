> Peace and goodwill are the basis of all enduring prosperity."

— [[Person – Richard Cobden]]

## Interpretation
- True prosperity is rooted in harmonious relationships between individuals and nations.
- Without peace and goodwill, economic stability and growth are unlikely to be sustained.
- Collaboration and mutual respect foster an environment conducive to lasting success.
