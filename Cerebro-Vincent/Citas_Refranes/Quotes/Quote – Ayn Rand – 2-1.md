> I swear by my life and my love of it that I will never live for the sake of another man, nor ask another man to live for mine.

— [[Person – Ayn Rand]]

## Interpretation
- The quote emphasizes individualism and the importance of self-fulfillment.
- It rejects the idea of self-sacrifice for others and promotes personal autonomy.
- It advocates for mutual respect in relationships, where neither party is obligated to live for the other.
