> Culture is not just what we create; it is how we participate."

— [[Person – Lawrence Lessig]]

## Interpretation
- Culture is a collective experience shaped by contributions from individuals.
- Participation in culture reflects our engagement and interaction with shared values and practices.
- Creation and participation are intertwined, emphasizing the dynamic nature of cultural expression.
