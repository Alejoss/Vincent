> We can ignore reality, but we cannot ignore the consequences of ignoring reality.

— [[Person – Ayn Rand]]

## Interpretation
- Ignoring reality is a choice, but it leads to unavoidable repercussions.
- The consequences of denial can be severe and inescapable.
- Acknowledge reality to avoid negative outcomes and take responsibility.
