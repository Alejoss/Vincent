> If women are to be excluded from the rights of mankind, they must be excluded from the duties of mankind.

— [[Person – Mary Wollstonecraft]]

## Interpretation
- Wollstonecraft argues that rights and responsibilities are inherently linked.
- If women are denied rights, it is unjust to impose duties upon them.
- The quote advocates for the equality of women in both rights and responsibilities.
