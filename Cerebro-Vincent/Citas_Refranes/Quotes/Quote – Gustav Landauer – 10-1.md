> The liberation of the working class must be the act of the working class itself.

— [[Person – Gustav Landauer]]

## Interpretation
- The working class must take initiative in their own emancipation.
- External forces or leaders cannot achieve true liberation for them.
- Self-empowerment and collective action are essential for meaningful change.
