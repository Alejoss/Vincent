> Science may be described as the art of systematic over-simplification.

— [[Person – Karl Popper]]

## Interpretation
- Science seeks to distill complex phenomena into simpler models for understanding.
- Over-simplification is a necessary process that helps make scientific theories more manageable and testable.
- This approach highlights the balance between complexity in nature and the clarity required for scientific inquiry.
