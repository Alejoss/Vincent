> The great and chief end, therefore, of men's uniting into commonwealths, and putting themselves under government, is the preservation of their property.

— [[Person – John Locke]]

## Interpretation
- Individuals form governments to collectively safeguard their belongings.
- The establishment of a commonwealth is primarily motivated by the desire for property security.
- Government exists to protect the rights and possessions of its citizens.
