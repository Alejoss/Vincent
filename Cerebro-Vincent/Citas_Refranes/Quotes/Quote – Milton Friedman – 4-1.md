> The great danger is not that the government will be too strong, but that it will be too weak.

— [[Person – Milton Friedman]]

## Interpretation
- A weak government may fail to enforce laws and maintain order.
- Insufficient government strength can lead to chaos and a lack of public welfare.
- Friedman suggests that a balanced government is crucial for societal stability.
