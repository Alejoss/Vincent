> Out of the crooked timber of humanity, no straight thing was ever made.

— [[Person – Immanuel Kant]]

## Interpretation
- Human nature is inherently flawed and imperfect.
- Attempts to create perfection from humanity are ultimately futile.
- Society and morality reflect the complexities and contradictions of human behavior.
