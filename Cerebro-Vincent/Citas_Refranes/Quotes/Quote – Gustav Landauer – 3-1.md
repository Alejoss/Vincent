> Freedom is not a gift from the state; it is the product of collective human action.

— [[Person – Gustav Landauer]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom is achieved through the efforts and cooperation of individuals rather than being bestowed by authority.
- The state does not grant true freedom; rather, it is an outcome of communal struggles and actions.
- Collective human action is essential in the pursuit and realization of freedom.
