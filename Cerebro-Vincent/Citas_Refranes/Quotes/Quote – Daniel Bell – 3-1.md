> The post-industrial society is a society that is characterized by knowledge and information rather than the production of goods."

— [[Person – Daniel Bell]]

## Interpretation
- Post-industrial society prioritizes intellectual and informational resources over material production.
- Knowledge and information are the primary drivers of economic and social development in this era.
- This shift reflects a fundamental transformation in the nature of work and value in society.
