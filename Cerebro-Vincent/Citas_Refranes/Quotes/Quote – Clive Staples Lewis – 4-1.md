> Faith is the art of holding on to things your reason has once accepted, in spite of your changing moods.

— [[Person – Clive Staples Lewis]]

## Interpretation
- Faith requires commitment to beliefs even when feelings fluctuate.
- It emphasizes the importance of consistency in one's convictions.
- The quote highlights the struggle between emotional states and rational acceptance.
