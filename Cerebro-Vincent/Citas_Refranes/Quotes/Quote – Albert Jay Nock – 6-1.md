> It is the tendency of the mind to dwell on the negative rather than the positive.

— [[Person – Albert Jay Nock]]

## Interpretation
- The human mind has a natural inclination to focus on problems and challenges.
- This tendency can lead to a pessimistic outlook on life.
- Recognizing this bias can help individuals consciously shift their focus to more positive aspects.
