> The summer soldier and the sunshine patriot will, in this crisis, shrink from the service of their country.

— [[Person – Thomas Paine]]

## Interpretation
- Paine criticizes those who only support their country in favorable times.
- He emphasizes the importance of commitment during difficult situations.
- The quote highlights the difference between true patriotism and superficial loyalty.
