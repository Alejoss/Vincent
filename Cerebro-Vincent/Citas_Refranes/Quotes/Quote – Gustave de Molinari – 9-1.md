> A man is free when he is able to think for himself."

— [[Person – Gustave de Molinari]]

## Interpretation
- True freedom is rooted in the ability to form one's own thoughts and beliefs.
- Autonomy in thinking is essential for personal liberation from societal constraints.
- Being able to think independently empowers individuals to make informed choices.
