> The general population does not know what's happening, and it doesn't even know that it doesn't know.

— [[Person – Noam Chomsky]]

## Interpretation
- The general public is often unaware of critical information affecting them.
- There is a deeper layer of ignorance where people are not even conscious of their lack of knowledge.
- This suggests a significant disconnect between the populace and the realities of their circumstances.
