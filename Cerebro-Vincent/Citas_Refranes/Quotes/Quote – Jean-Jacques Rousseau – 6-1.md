> What wisdom can you find that is greater than kindness?

— [[Person – Jean-Jacques Rousseau]]

## Interpretation
- Kindness is presented as the highest form of wisdom, surpassing intellectual knowledge.
- The quote emphasizes the moral value of compassion in human interactions.
- It suggests that true understanding comes from empathy and care for others.
