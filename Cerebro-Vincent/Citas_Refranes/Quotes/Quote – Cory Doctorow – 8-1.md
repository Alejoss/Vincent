> The more we talk about privacy, the less we have of it.

— [[Person – Cory Doctorow]]

## Interpretation
- Discussing privacy often draws attention to it, making it a target for breaches.
- Increased public discourse can lead to greater surveillance and monitoring.
- The irony of seeking to protect privacy may lead to its erosion through heightened awareness.
