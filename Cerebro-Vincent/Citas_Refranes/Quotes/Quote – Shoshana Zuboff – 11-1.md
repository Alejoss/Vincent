> The future of democracy is at stake when we allow our personal data to be harvested without consent."

— [[Person – Shoshana Zuboff]]

## Interpretation
- The unauthorized collection of personal data undermines individual autonomy.
- Consent is crucial for maintaining democratic principles and protecting personal rights.
- The harvesting of data without consent poses a threat to the integrity of democratic systems.
