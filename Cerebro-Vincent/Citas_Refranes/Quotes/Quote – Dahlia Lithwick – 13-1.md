> Empathy should guide our interpretation of the law."

— [[Person – Dahlia Lithwick]]

## Interpretation
- Empathy encourages a deeper understanding of individuals affected by legal decisions.
- Interpreting the law through an empathetic lens promotes justice and fairness.
- The quote suggests that legal frameworks should not be applied rigidly but should consider human experiences and emotions.
