> What matters is not only the distribution of resources, but also how these resources are acquired.

— [[Person – Robert Nozick]]

## Interpretation
- The process of acquiring resources is as significant as the resources themselves.
- Ethical considerations in resource acquisition can impact perceptions of justice.
- Equity in distribution cannot be divorced from the means by which resources are obtained.
