> Do not fear to be eccentric in opinion, for every opinion now accepted was once eccentric.

— [[Person – Bertrand Russell]]

## Interpretation
- Embrace unique perspectives, as they can lead to significant change.
- Mainstream beliefs often started as unconventional ideas.
- Courage to think differently is essential for progress.
