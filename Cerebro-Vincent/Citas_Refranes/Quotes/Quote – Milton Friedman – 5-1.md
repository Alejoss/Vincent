> The most important single central fact about a free market is that no exchange takes place unless both parties benefit.

— [[Person – Milton Friedman]]

## Interpretation
- A free market relies on voluntary exchanges where each party perceives a benefit.
- Mutual benefit is fundamental to the functioning of market transactions.
- This principle highlights the cooperative nature of economic interactions.
