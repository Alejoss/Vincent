> The law must be based on justice, and justice must be based on the rights of individuals.

— [[Person – Frédéric Bastiat]]

## Interpretation
- The law should serve to uphold and protect individual rights.
- Justice is a fundamental principle that should guide the creation and application of laws.
- Without a foundation in individual rights, laws cannot be considered just.
