> Empowering individuals through technology is one of the key goals of cryptography."

— [[Person – Martin Hellman]]

## Interpretation
- Cryptography serves to give individuals greater control over their personal information.
- The use of technology in cryptography fosters individual autonomy and security.
- Empowerment through cryptography can lead to enhanced privacy and freedom in digital interactions.
