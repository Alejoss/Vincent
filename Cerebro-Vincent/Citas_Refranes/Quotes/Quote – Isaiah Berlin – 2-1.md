> Liberty for wolves is death to the lambs.

— [[Person – Isaiah Berlin]]

## Interpretation
- The quote suggests that absolute freedom can lead to the oppression of the weaker by the stronger.
- In a society where some individuals or groups have unchecked liberty, those who are vulnerable face existential threats.
- It highlights the moral dilemma of balancing freedom with the protection of those who cannot defend themselves.
