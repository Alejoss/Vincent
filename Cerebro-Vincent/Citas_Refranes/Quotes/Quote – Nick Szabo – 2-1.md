> The most important aspect of a financial system is its ability to provide security and trust."

— [[Person – Nick Szabo]]

## Interpretation
- A financial system must ensure that participants feel secure in their transactions.
- Trust is foundational for the stability and efficiency of economic exchanges.
- Without security and trust, the functionality of the financial system is compromised.
