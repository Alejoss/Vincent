> In a free society, the law should be the expression of the will of the people."

— [[Person – Gustave de Molinari]]

## Interpretation
- The law should reflect the collective desires and values of the populace.
- A true democracy relies on the active participation and consent of its citizens in legal matters.
- Legitimacy of legal systems stems from their alignment with the will of the governed.
