> The liberty of man is to be free from any superior power on earth, and not to be under the will or legislative authority of man.

— [[Person – John Locke]]

## Interpretation
- True liberty is defined by the absence of external control or domination.
- Individuals should not be subject to arbitrary authority or the will of others.
- Personal freedom is fundamentally linked to the absence of superior power over one's life.
