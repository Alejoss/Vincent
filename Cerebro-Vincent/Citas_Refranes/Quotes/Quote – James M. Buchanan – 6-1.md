> The most important factor in public policy is the behavior of the decision-makers."

— [[Person – James M. Buchanan]]

## Interpretation
- Public policy is heavily influenced by the choices and actions of those in power.
- The motivations and behaviors of decision-makers can significantly impact policy outcomes.
- Understanding these behaviors is crucial for analyzing the effectiveness of public policies.
