> I cannot be free if I am not free to express myself.

— [[Person – Emma Goldman]]

## Interpretation
- True freedom includes the ability to express one's thoughts and feelings.
- Self-expression is essential for personal autonomy and identity.
- Censorship or repression of expression undermines individual liberty.
