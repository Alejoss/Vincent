> The despotism of custom is everywhere evident.

— [[Person – John Stuart Mill]]

## Interpretation
- Customs and traditions can exert a powerful influence on individual behavior and societal norms.
- This 'despotism' suggests that adherence to custom can limit personal freedom and critical thinking.
- Mill highlights the need to question and challenge established norms to foster progress and individuality.
