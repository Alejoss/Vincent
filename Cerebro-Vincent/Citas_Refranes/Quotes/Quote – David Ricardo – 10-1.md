> The tendency of profits to equalization is one of the most important laws of economic science.

— [[Person – David Ricardo]]

## Interpretation
- Profits across industries tend to level out over time due to competition.
- This process promotes efficiency and resource allocation within the economy.
- Understanding this tendency is crucial for grasping broader economic principles.
