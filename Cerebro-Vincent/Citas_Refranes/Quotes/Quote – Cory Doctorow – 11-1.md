> There’s no such thing as a free lunch, but there is such a thing as a free download.

— [[Person – Cory Doctorow]]

## Interpretation
- The phrase plays on the economic principle that everything has a cost, often hidden or indirect.
- While downloading may seem free, it can come with trade-offs like privacy concerns or loss of support for creators.
- It highlights the paradox of digital goods being easily accessible at no monetary cost, yet still having value.
