> The equality of the sexes is fundamental to a truly just society.

— [[Person – Susan Moller Okin]]

## Interpretation
- Gender equality is essential for achieving social justice.
- A society that values and practices equality between sexes fosters fairness and equity.
- Ignoring gender disparities undermines the overall justness of a community.
