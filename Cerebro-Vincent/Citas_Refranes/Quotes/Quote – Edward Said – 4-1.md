> The task of the intellectual is to speak the truth to power."

— [[Person – Edward Said]]

## Interpretation
- Intellectuals have a moral responsibility to challenge authority and expose injustices.
- Speaking truth to power involves advocating for marginalized voices and critiquing dominant narratives.
- The act of speaking truth is inherently political and can provoke resistance from those in power.
