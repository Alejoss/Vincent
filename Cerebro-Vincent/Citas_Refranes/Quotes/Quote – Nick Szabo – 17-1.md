> Creating a system that incentivizes honest behavior is essential for any decentralized network."

— [[Person – Nick Szabo]]

## Interpretation
- Decentralized networks require mechanisms to promote trust among participants.
- Incentives play a crucial role in guiding user behavior towards honesty.
- Without honest behavior, the integrity of a decentralized system is compromised.
