> It is not reason which is the guide of life, but custom.

— [[Person – David Hume]]

## Interpretation
- Hume emphasizes the primacy of habitual behavior over rational thought in guiding human actions.
- Customs and traditions shape our decisions more than logical reasoning.
- Reason may provide justification, but it is ultimately our learned behaviors that dictate our choices.
