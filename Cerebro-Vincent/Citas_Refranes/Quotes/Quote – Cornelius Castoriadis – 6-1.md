> The imagination is not a luxury; it is a necessity for the transformation of society."

— [[Person – Cornelius Castoriadis]]

## Interpretation
- Imagination is essential for envisioning new possibilities and social change.
- Without imagination, society remains stagnant, unable to innovate or adapt.
- Emphasizing imagination underscores its role in critical thinking and creative problem-solving.
