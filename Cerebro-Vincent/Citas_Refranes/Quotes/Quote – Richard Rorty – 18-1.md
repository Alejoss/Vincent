> The only way to deal with the past is to reinterpret it.

— [[Person – Richard Rorty]]

## Interpretation
- Reinterpreting the past allows for a more flexible understanding of history.
- This perspective emphasizes the subjective nature of historical narratives.
- It suggests that engaging with the past is an active, ongoing process rather than a fixed endeavor.
