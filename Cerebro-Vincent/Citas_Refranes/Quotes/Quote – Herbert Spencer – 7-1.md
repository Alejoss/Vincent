> The law of the survival of the fittest is a law of progress.

— [[Person – Herbert Spencer]]

## Interpretation
- The phrase implies that those best adapted to their environment will thrive and evolve.
- It suggests that competition drives improvement and advancement within species.
- This concept emphasizes an ongoing process of evolution and development in nature.
