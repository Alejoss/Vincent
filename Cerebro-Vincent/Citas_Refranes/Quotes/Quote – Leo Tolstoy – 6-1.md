> Happiness does not depend on external conditions, it depends on the internal condition.

— [[Person – Leo Tolstoy]]

## Interpretation
- True happiness is a product of one's inner state rather than external circumstances.
- External factors can influence happiness, but they do not define it.
- Cultivating a positive internal condition is essential for lasting contentment.
