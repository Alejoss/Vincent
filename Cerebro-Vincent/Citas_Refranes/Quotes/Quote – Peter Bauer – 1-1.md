> The history of economic development is a history of the failure of the intellectuals."

— [[Person – Peter Bauer]]

## Interpretation
- Intellectuals often propose theories that fail to translate into practical solutions for economic development.
- There is a disconnect between academic ideas and real-world applications in economic policy.
- The repeated failures highlight the limitations of theoretical frameworks when addressing complex economic issues.
