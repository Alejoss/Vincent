> The pursuit of wealth is not an end in itself, but a means to improve the human condition.

— [[Person – Adam Smith]]

## Interpretation
- Wealth should be viewed as a tool rather than a goal.
- The ultimate purpose of accumulating wealth is to enhance the quality of life for individuals and society.
- Pursuing wealth should align with broader humanistic values and social improvement.
