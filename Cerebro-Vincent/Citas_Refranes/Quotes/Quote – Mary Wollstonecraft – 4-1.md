> I do not think that the education of women should be neglected; it is the foundation of the education of men.

— [[Person – Mary Wollstonecraft]]

## Interpretation
- Wollstonecraft emphasizes the importance of women's education as essential for societal progress.
- Neglecting women's education undermines the potential development of men and society as a whole.
- The quote suggests a foundational relationship between the education of both genders.
