> Security is not a product, but a process.

— [[Person – Bruce Schneier]]

## Interpretation
- Security requires ongoing effort and adaptation rather than a one-time solution.
- It involves continuous assessment and improvement to address evolving threats.
- Understanding security as a process emphasizes the importance of vigilance and proactive measures.
