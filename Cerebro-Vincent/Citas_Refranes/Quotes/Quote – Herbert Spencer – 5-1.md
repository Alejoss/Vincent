> The survival of the fittest implies that those who succeed are those who are best adapted to their environment.

— [[Person – Herbert Spencer]]

## Interpretation
- Success is determined by the ability to adapt to one's surroundings.
- Adaptation is a crucial factor in the competition for survival.
- The concept emphasizes the dynamic relationship between organisms and their environments.
