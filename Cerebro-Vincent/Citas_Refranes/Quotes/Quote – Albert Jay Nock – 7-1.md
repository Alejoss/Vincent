> In the long run, the best thing you can do for your country is to take care of yourself.

— [[Person – Albert Jay Nock]]

## Interpretation
- Self-care leads to personal empowerment, which can positively influence society.
- A strong individual contributes to a stronger community and nation.
- Prioritizing personal well-being is essential for effective civic responsibility.
