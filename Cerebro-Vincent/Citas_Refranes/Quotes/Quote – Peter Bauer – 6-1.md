> The process of development is complex and cannot be reduced to simple formulas."

— [[Person – Peter Bauer]]

## Interpretation
- Development involves numerous interrelated factors that are not easily simplified.
- Relying on oversimplified models can lead to misunderstandings of the complexities involved.
- A nuanced understanding is essential for effective development strategies.
