> It is the most natural thing in the world for a woman to love her child, but it is also natural for her to love other things and other people.

— [[Person – Emma Goldman]]

## Interpretation
- A mother's love for her child is instinctual and expected.
- Women are multifaceted beings with the capacity to love beyond motherhood.
- Emphasizing the importance of recognizing women's diverse emotional connections.
