> The state exists for the sake of the individual, and not the individual for the state.

— [[Person – Thomas Hill Green]]

## Interpretation
- The primary purpose of government is to serve and enhance individual freedoms and well-being.
- Individuals are not merely subjects of the state, but rather the state should be a mechanism to support their rights and interests.
- This perspective emphasizes the importance of individual autonomy over collective authority.
