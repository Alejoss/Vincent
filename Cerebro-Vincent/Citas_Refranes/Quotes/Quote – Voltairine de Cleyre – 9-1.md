> Love is the only way to freedom."

— [[Person – Voltairine de Cleyre]]

## Interpretation
- Love fosters genuine connections and understanding among individuals.
- True freedom is achieved through compassion and mutual respect rather than coercion.
- Emphasizing love highlights the importance of emotional and social bonds in the pursuit of liberation.
