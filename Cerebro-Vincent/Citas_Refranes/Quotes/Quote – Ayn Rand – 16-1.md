> The highest moral purpose of your life is the achievement of your own happiness.

— [[Person – Ayn Rand]]

## Interpretation
- Happiness is presented as the ultimate goal and measure of a fulfilling life.
- The quote emphasizes individualism and self-interest as moral imperatives.
- It suggests that personal happiness is not only desirable but a moral obligation.
