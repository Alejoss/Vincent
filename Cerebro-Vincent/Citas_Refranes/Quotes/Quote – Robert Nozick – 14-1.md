> A society that respects individual rights must also accept the inequalities that arise from voluntary exchanges.

— [[Person – Robert Nozick]]

## Interpretation
- Individual rights are fundamental and must be prioritized in a society.
- Voluntary exchanges can lead to unequal outcomes, which are acceptable if they arise from free choices.
- Accepting inequalities is part of respecting autonomy and freedom in a society.
