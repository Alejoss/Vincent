> All mankind... being all equal and independent, no one ought to harm another in his life, health, liberty, or possessions.

— [[Person – John Locke]]

## Interpretation
- All individuals possess inherent equality and autonomy.
- Harming others violates the fundamental rights to life, health, liberty, and property.
- Social coexistence is based on mutual respect for one another's rights.
