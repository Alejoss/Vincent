> Social democracy, in its essence, is a class struggle.

— [[Person – Rosa Luxemburg]]

## Interpretation
- Social democracy fundamentally revolves around the conflicts and interests of different social classes.
- It emphasizes the need for collective action and organization to achieve political and economic equality.
- The quote suggests that social democracy is not merely a political ideology but a dynamic process rooted in the struggles of the working class.
