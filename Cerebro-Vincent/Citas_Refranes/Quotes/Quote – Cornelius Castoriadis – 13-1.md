> To think is to act; to act is to think."

— [[Person – Cornelius Castoriadis]]

## Interpretation
- Thinking and acting are inherently connected, suggesting that intellectual engagement leads to real-world actions.
- To act without thought is to risk mindless behavior, while thinking without action is ultimately unproductive.
- This quote highlights the importance of both cognition and behavior in realizing one's ideas and intentions.
