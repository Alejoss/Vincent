> A nation may be said to be great when it possesses a great spirit of liberty.

— [[Person – Alexis de Tocqueville]]

## Interpretation
- A nation's greatness is measured by its commitment to individual freedoms.
- Liberty serves as a foundational element for societal progress and development.
- The spirit of liberty reflects the collective values and aspirations of the people within the nation.
