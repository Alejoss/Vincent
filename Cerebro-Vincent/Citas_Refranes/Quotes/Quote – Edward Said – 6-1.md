> Cultural identity is not a static thing; it is always in formation."

— [[Person – Edward Said]]

## Interpretation
- Cultural identity evolves over time rather than remaining fixed.
- It reflects ongoing processes of change influenced by various social, political, and historical factors.
- Understanding cultural identity requires recognizing its dynamic nature and adaptability.
