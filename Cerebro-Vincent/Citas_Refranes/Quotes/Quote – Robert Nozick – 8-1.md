> We cannot assume that everyone will act in a way that promotes the common good; hence, we need a framework for cooperation.

— [[Person – Robert Nozick]]

## Interpretation
- Human behavior cannot be assumed to be altruistic or beneficial to society.
- A structured system is necessary to facilitate collaboration among individuals.
- Without a cooperative framework, the potential for selfish actions could undermine collective interests.
