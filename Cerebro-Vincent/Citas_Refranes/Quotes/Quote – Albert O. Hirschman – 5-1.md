> Every social phenomenon is the result of countless individual actions, and we must understand these motivations to effect change."

— [[Person – Albert O. Hirschman]]

## Interpretation
- Social change is driven by the collective actions of individuals.
- Understanding individual motivations is crucial for implementing effective change.
- The complexity of social phenomena requires a nuanced analysis of personal intentions.
