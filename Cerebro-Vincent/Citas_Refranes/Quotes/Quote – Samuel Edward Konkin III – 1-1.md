> The state is a criminal organization that uses force to achieve its ends."

— [[Person – Samuel Edward Konkin III]]

## Interpretation
- The quote suggests that the state operates similarly to a criminal gang by employing coercion.
- It implies that the actions of the state are fundamentally unjust and morally questionable.
- This perspective views government authority as inherently violent and exploitative.
