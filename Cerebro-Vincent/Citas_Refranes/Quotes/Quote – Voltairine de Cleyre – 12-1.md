> The aim of life is not to be happy, but to be useful."

— [[Person – Voltairine de Cleyre]]

## Interpretation
- Life's purpose is found in contributing to the greater good rather than solely seeking personal pleasure.
- Fulfillment comes from being of service to others and making a positive impact.
- True value in life is measured by the usefulness of one’s actions and their effects on society.
