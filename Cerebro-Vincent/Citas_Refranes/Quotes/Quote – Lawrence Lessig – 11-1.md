> Intellectual property must be balanced with the public good."

— [[Person – Lawrence Lessig]]

## Interpretation
- Intellectual property rights can sometimes conflict with societal interests.
- A balance is necessary to ensure creativity and innovation benefit the public.
- Overemphasis on intellectual property may hinder access to knowledge and culture.
