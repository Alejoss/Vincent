> The life of man is of no greater importance to the universe than that of an oyster.

— [[Person – David Hume]]

## Interpretation
- Hume emphasizes the insignificance of human life in the grand scale of the universe.
- This suggests a philosophical perspective on existence and the value we assign to life.
- It challenges anthropocentrism by equating human life to that of a simple creature like an oyster.
