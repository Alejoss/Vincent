> I am not a philosopher in the traditional sense; I am more of a storyteller.

— [[Person – Richard Rorty]]

## Interpretation
- Rorty distinguishes himself from traditional philosophers by emphasizing narrative over abstract reasoning.
- He suggests that philosophy can be approached through storytelling, making it more accessible and relatable.
- This statement reflects a shift from objective truth-seeking to subjective interpretation and personal experience.
