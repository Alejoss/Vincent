> The state is a machine for the oppression of the people."

— [[Person – Johann Most]]

## Interpretation
- The state functions primarily to maintain control over its citizens.
- It suggests that governmental structures are inherently oppressive rather than protective.
- Most critiques the idea of state authority as fundamentally harmful to individual freedoms.
