> To think of democracy only in terms of consensus is to deny the reality of social conflict."

— [[Person – Chantal Mouffe]]

## Interpretation
- Democracy involves diverse opinions and interests that often clash rather than align.
- Focusing solely on consensus overlooks the inherent conflicts within society.
- Acknowledging social conflict is essential for a more authentic understanding of democratic processes.
