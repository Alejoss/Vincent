> The United States has been involved in the overthrow of numerous democratically elected governments.

— [[Person – Noam Chomsky]]

## Interpretation
- Chomsky highlights the hypocrisy of U.S. foreign policy that claims to support democracy.
- The quote suggests a pattern of U.S. interventionism that undermines legitimate democratic processes.
- It raises concerns about the consequences of such actions on global stability and sovereignty.
