> Philosophy should not be a retreat from the world, but a way to engage with it more deeply."

— [[Person – Martha Nussbaum]]

## Interpretation
- Philosophy is a tool for understanding real-world issues rather than an abstract pursuit.
- Engagement with the world through philosophical inquiry can lead to deeper insights.
- Philosophy encourages active participation in societal and personal challenges.
