> The aim of philosophy is not to discover the truth, but to create new ways of thinking.

— [[Person – Richard Rorty]]

## Interpretation
- Philosophy is less about uncovering objective truths and more about fostering innovative ideas.
- It emphasizes creativity and the evolution of thought rather than the pursuit of absolutes.
- Rorty challenges traditional notions of truth, suggesting that philosophy can be a tool for expanding perspectives.
