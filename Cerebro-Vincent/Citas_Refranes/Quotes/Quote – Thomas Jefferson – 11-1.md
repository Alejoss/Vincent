> The glow of one warm thought is to me worth more than money.

— [[Person – Thomas Jefferson]]

## Interpretation
- A positive thought can bring more happiness than material wealth.
- Emotional and mental well-being is prioritized over financial gain.
- The value of intangible experiences surpasses that of tangible assets.
