> Development is a process, not a destination."

— [[Person – Peter Bauer]]

## Interpretation
- Development is an ongoing journey that requires continuous effort and adaptation.
- It emphasizes the importance of growth and learning over achieving a final goal.
- This perspective encourages a focus on processes and incremental improvements rather than fixating on end results.
