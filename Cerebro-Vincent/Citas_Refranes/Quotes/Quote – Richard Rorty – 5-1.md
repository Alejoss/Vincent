> The world is not an arena in which we can assert our views, but a conversation in which we must participate.

— [[Person – Richard Rorty]]

## Interpretation
- Engagement in dialogue is more valuable than simply voicing opinions.
- The quote emphasizes collective understanding over individual assertion.
- It suggests that meaningful insights arise from collaboration rather than competition.
