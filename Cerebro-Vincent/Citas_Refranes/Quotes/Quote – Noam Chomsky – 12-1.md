> The more you can increase fear of drugs and crime, welfare mothers, immigrants and aliens, the more you control all the people.

— [[Person – Noam Chomsky]]

## Interpretation
- Fear is used as a tool for social control.
- Targeting specific groups fosters division and compliance.
- Manipulating public perception can enhance authority's power.
