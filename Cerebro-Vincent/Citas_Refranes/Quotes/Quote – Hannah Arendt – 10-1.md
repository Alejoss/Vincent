> The banality of evil is a concept that explains the ordinary nature of those who commit atrocious acts.

— [[Person – Hannah Arendt]]

## Interpretation
- Evil can manifest in ordinary individuals rather than monstrous figures.
- People may commit heinous acts without deep-seated malice or intent, often through conformity.
- The concept challenges the perception of evil as something extraordinary, highlighting its commonplace nature in society.
