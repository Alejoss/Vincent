> The more we use the internet to connect, the more isolated we become.

— [[Person – Jaron Lanier]]

## Interpretation
- Increased online communication can lead to a false sense of connection.
- Virtual interactions may detract from meaningful, in-person relationships.
- The internet can create superficial connections that leave individuals feeling lonelier.
