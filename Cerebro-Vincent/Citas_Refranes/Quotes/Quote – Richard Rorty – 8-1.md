> We cannot take the perspective of the universe; we can only take our own.

— [[Person – Richard Rorty]]

## Interpretation
- We are limited by our personal experiences and viewpoints.
- Understanding is inherently subjective, shaped by individual perspectives.
- There is no objective standpoint from which to view the universe as a whole.
