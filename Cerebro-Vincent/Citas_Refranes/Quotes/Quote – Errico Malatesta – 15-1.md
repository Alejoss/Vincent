> The path to freedom is paved with the truths we are willing to confront.

— [[Person – Errico Malatesta]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom is achieved through self-awareness and honesty.
- Facing uncomfortable truths is essential for personal and societal liberation.
- The willingness to confront reality is a crucial step in the journey towards autonomy.
