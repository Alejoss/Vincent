> The challenge is to balance our own freedoms with the freedoms of others.

— [[Person – Isaiah Berlin]]

## Interpretation
- Individual freedom must be weighed against the rights of others.
- True liberty involves responsibility and consideration for the community.
- Finding harmony between personal and collective freedoms is essential for a just society.
