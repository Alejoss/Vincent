> The law of comparative advantage is the foundation of international trade.

— [[Person – David Ricardo]]

## Interpretation
- Countries benefit from specializing in the production of goods where they have a relative efficiency.
- Trade allows nations to exchange what they produce best, leading to increased overall economic efficiency.
- The principle encourages collaboration and interdependence among nations in the global economy.
