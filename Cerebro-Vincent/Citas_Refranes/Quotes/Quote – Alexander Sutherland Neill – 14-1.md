> Learning should be a joyful experience, not a chore.

— [[Person – Alexander Sutherland Neill]]

## Interpretation
- Education should inspire curiosity and excitement in learners.
- When learning is perceived as a chore, it can lead to disengagement and resentment.
- Joyful learning fosters a deeper understanding and retention of knowledge.
