> Our legal system must reflect the diversity of our society."

— [[Person – Dahlia Lithwick]]

## Interpretation
- The legal system should be inclusive and representative of all societal groups.
- Diversity in the legal system can enhance justice and fairness.
- Reflecting societal diversity is crucial for legitimacy and public trust in the law.
