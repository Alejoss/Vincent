> The fact that an opinion has been widely held is no evidence whatever that it is not utterly absurd.

— [[Person – Bertrand Russell]]

## Interpretation
- Widespread belief does not equate to truth or validity.
- Popularity of an opinion can mask its irrationality.
- Critical thinking is essential to discern the merit of beliefs.
