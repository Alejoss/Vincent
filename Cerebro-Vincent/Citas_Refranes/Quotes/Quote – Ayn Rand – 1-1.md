> The question isn't who is going to let me; it's who is going to stop me.

— [[Person – Ayn Rand]]

## Interpretation
- The quote emphasizes personal agency and self-determination.
- It challenges the notion of external permission and encourages individual empowerment.
- It suggests a defiance against obstacles and limitations imposed by others.
