> The mind is furnished with ideas by experience alone.

— [[Person – John Locke]]

## Interpretation
- Human understanding is shaped by sensory experiences.
- Knowledge is not innate but acquired through interaction with the world.
- Experience serves as the foundation for all ideas and beliefs.
