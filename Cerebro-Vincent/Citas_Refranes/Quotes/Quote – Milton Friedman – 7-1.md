> A society that puts equality before freedom will get neither.

— [[Person – Milton Friedman]]

## Interpretation
- Prioritizing equality over freedom may lead to the loss of both values.
- The pursuit of enforced equality can infringe on individual liberties.
- True freedom allows for individual choices, which may result in unequal outcomes.
