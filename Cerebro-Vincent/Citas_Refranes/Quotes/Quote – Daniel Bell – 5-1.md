> In an information society, the ability to access and use information is as important as the ability to produce it."

— [[Person – Daniel Bell]]

## Interpretation
- Accessing information is crucial for participation in modern society.
- Utilizing information effectively can be as valuable as creating new information.
- The emphasis is on the importance of both skills in a knowledge-driven economy.
