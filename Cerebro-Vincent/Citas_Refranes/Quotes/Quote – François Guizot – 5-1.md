> The greatest happiness of the greatest number is the foundation of morals and legislation."

— [[Person – François Guizot]]

## Interpretation
- The quote emphasizes utilitarianism as a guiding principle for ethics and law.
- It suggests that moral and legislative decisions should prioritize the well-being of the majority.
- This perspective advocates for a collective approach to happiness over individual interests.
