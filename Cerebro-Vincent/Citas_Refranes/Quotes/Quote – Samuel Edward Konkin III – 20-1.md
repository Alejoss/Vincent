> Liberty is not a gift from the government; it is a natural right."

— [[Person – Samuel Edward Konkin III]]

## Interpretation
- Liberty is inherently part of human existence, not something bestowed by authority.
- Governments may attempt to regulate or restrict liberty, but they cannot create it.
- The recognition of liberty as a natural right emphasizes individual autonomy and personal freedom.
