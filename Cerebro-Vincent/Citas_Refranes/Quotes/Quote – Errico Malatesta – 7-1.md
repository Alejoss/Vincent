> Solidarity is the foundation upon which we can build a society without oppression.

— [[Person – Errico Malatesta]]

## Interpretation
- Solidarity among individuals is essential for creating a just society.
- A society free from oppression relies on mutual support and cooperation.
- Collective action is necessary to challenge and dismantle oppressive systems.
