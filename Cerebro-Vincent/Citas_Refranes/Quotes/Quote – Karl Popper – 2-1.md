> All swans are white is not a scientific statement.

— [[Person – Karl Popper]]

## Interpretation
- The statement 'All swans are white' cannot be scientifically verified because it is based on universal generalization.
- A scientific statement must be falsifiable, meaning it should be possible to prove it wrong with evidence.
- Popper emphasizes the importance of skepticism and the need for empirical testing in science.
