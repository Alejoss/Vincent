> We must not confuse the welfare of the state with the welfare of the individual."

— [[Person – James M. Buchanan]]

## Interpretation
- The interests of the state do not always align with the well-being of individuals.
- Policies aimed at benefiting the state may overlook or harm individual rights and needs.
- Recognizing the distinction is crucial for protecting personal freedoms and welfare.
