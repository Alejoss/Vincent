> Cryptography is the foundation of trust in the digital world."

— [[Person – Nick Szabo]]

## Interpretation
- Cryptography secures transactions and communications in the digital realm.
- It establishes a reliable environment where parties can trust each other without needing a central authority.
- The quote highlights the essential role of cryptography in maintaining digital security and integrity.
