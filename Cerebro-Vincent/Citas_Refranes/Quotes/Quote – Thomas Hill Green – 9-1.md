> The ideal of a good life is one where personal happiness is harmonized with the welfare of others.

— [[Person – Thomas Hill Green]]

## Interpretation
- Personal happiness should not be pursued at the expense of others' well-being.
- A truly good life involves a balance between self-interest and altruism.
- The ideal life is one of interconnectedness, where individual fulfillment contributes to the greater good.
