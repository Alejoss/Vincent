> The concept of self-ownership is central to understanding individual rights and freedoms.

— [[Person – Robert Nozick]]

## Interpretation
- Self-ownership implies that individuals have control over their own bodies and lives.
- Understanding self-ownership is crucial for recognizing and justifying personal rights.
- The concept underscores the importance of personal autonomy in the context of freedom.
