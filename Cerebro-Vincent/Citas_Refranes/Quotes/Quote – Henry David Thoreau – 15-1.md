> What lies behind us and what lies ahead of us are tiny matters compared to what lies within us.

— [[Person – Henry David Thoreau]]

## Interpretation
- Our past experiences and future possibilities are less significant than our inner values and character.
- Self-discovery and inner strength are vital for personal growth and fulfillment.
- True essence and potential reside within us, overshadowing external circumstances.
