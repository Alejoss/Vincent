> Freedom is the right to do anything that does not harm others.

— [[Person – Pierre-Joseph Proudhon]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom is defined by the absence of harm to others, establishing a moral boundary.
- This perspective emphasizes individual liberty while also advocating for social responsibility.
- It suggests that true freedom is not absolute but is constrained by the rights and well-being of others.
