> Our life is frittered away by detail. Simplify, simplify.

— [[Person – Henry David Thoreau]]

## Interpretation
- We often get bogged down by trivial details in our daily lives.
- Emphasizing simplicity can lead to a more fulfilling and meaningful existence.
- Stripping away the unnecessary allows us to focus on what truly matters.
