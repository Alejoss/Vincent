> The greatest danger to freedom is the illusion of freedom."

— [[Person – Isabel Paterson]]

## Interpretation
- The perception of being free can lead individuals to ignore real restrictions on their liberties.
- Illusions of freedom can create complacency, preventing people from recognizing and confronting actual oppression.
- True freedom requires awareness and vigilance against false narratives that suggest liberty where it may not exist.
