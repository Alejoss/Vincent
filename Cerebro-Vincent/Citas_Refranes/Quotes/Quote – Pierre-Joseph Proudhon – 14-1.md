> To be free is to be able to do what one wants, without causing harm to others.

— [[Person – Pierre-Joseph Proudhon]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom is defined by the ability to act according to one's desires.
- True freedom is contingent upon the consideration of others' well-being.
- The balance between individual liberty and social responsibility is essential.
