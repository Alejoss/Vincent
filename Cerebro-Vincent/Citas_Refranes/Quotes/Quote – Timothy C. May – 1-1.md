> The future will be a world of abundance, a world of freedom and privacy."

— [[Person – Timothy C. May]]

## Interpretation
- The future is envisioned as a place where resources and opportunities are plentiful.
- Emphasis is placed on individual autonomy and the right to personal space.
- Privacy is highlighted as a fundamental aspect of a free society.
