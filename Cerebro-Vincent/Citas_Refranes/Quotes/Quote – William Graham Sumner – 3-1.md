> A man who has nothing for which he is willing to fight is a miserable creature.

— [[Person – William Graham Sumner]]

## Interpretation
- The quote suggests that a lack of purpose or values leads to a sense of misery.
- Fighting for something implies a commitment to ideals or causes that give life meaning.
- A person without convictions or passions is depicted as lacking fulfillment and vitality.
