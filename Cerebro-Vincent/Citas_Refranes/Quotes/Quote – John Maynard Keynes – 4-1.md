> The avoidance of taxes is the only intellectual pursuit that still carries any reward.

— [[Person – John Maynard Keynes]]

## Interpretation
- Keynes suggests that evading taxes has become a valued skill in society.
- This quote reflects a cynical view of intellectual pursuits, implying that many are unproductive or unrewarding.
- It highlights the idea that financial motivations overshadow other intellectual endeavors.
