> What you do with your money is a testimony to what you think about God.

— [[Person – Clive Staples Lewis]]

## Interpretation
- Your financial choices reflect your values and priorities.
- How you spend money can indicate your understanding of divine principles.
- Money serves as a measure of one's faith and commitment to God.
