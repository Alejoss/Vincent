> The challenge of law is to make a moral distinction between what the law permits and what it requires."

— [[Person – Ronald Dworkin]]

## Interpretation
- Law should not only define allowable actions but also highlight ethical obligations.
- There is a moral responsibility in interpreting the law beyond mere legality.
- Understanding the law involves discerning between permissive actions and moral duties.
