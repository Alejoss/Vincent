> Many people would rather die than think; in fact, most do.

— [[Person – Bertrand Russell]]

## Interpretation
- The quote suggests that many individuals avoid critical thinking and self-reflection.
- It implies a preference for ignorance over the discomfort of engaging with complex ideas.
- Russell highlights a societal tendency to conform rather than challenge one's own beliefs.
