> The progress of society is the most important of all the interests of mankind."

— [[Person – Richard Cobden]]

## Interpretation
- Societal progress is fundamental to human well-being and development.
- The advancement of society transcends individual interests and benefits all of humanity.
- Fostering societal improvement should be a priority for individuals and governments alike.
