> The most important thing is to be able to see the world from other people's points of view.

— [[Person – Isaiah Berlin]]

## Interpretation
- Understanding diverse perspectives fosters empathy and compassion.
- Seeing the world through others' eyes enhances communication and reduces conflict.
- This ability is crucial for personal growth and societal harmony.
