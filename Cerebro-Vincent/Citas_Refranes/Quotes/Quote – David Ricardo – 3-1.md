> The value of a commodity is determined by the quantity of labor required to produce it.

— [[Person – David Ricardo]]

## Interpretation
- The intrinsic value of goods is linked to the labor input needed for their production.
- Labor serves as a measure of value, reflecting the effort and resources invested.
- This perspective emphasizes the social nature of production and its economic implications.
