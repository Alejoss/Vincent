> Freedom is the alone unoriginated birthright of man, and it is in his power to realize it.

— [[Person – Immanuel Kant]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom is an inherent right that belongs to every individual from birth.
- It is the responsibility of each person to recognize and actualize their freedom.
- True freedom is not granted by external forces but is a self-derived power.
