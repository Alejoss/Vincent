> We should aim for a society where individuals can pursue their own interests while respecting the rights of others.

— [[Person – Robert Nozick]]

## Interpretation
- Individuals have the freedom to pursue personal goals.
- Respecting the rights of others is a fundamental requirement.
- A balanced society allows for personal interests without infringing on others' freedoms.
