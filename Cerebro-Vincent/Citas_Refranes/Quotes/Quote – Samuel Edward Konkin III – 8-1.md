> The idea of the state is inherently anti-liberty."

— [[Person – Samuel Edward Konkin III]]

## Interpretation
- The state imposes authority and regulations that restrict individual freedoms.
- Inherently, the state's existence relies on control, which contrasts with the principle of personal liberty.
- Konkin suggests that true liberty cannot coexist with a governing body that dictates actions.
