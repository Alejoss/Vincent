> The personal is political.

— [[Person – Susan Moller Okin]]

## Interpretation
- Personal experiences and struggles are deeply intertwined with broader political systems.
- Issues traditionally considered private, such as family dynamics and gender roles, have significant political implications.
- The quote advocates for recognizing the impact of personal life on societal structures and policies.
