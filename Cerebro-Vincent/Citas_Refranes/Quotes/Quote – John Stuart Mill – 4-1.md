> Originality is the one thing which unoriginal minds cannot feel the use of.

— [[Person – John Stuart Mill]]

## Interpretation
- Original thinkers appreciate the value of creativity and innovation.
- Those lacking originality may fail to recognize its importance in progress and thought.
- The quote highlights a divide between creative and conventional thinking.
