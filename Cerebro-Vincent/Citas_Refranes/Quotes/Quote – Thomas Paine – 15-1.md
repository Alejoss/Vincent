> When we are planning for posterity, we ought to remember that virtue is not hereditary.

— [[Person – Thomas Paine]]

## Interpretation
- Future generations must develop their own values and ethics.
- Inheriting wealth or status does not guarantee moral character.
- We have a responsibility to instill virtue in future citizens rather than assume it will be passed down.
