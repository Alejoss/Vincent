> We need to think of democracy as a practice rather than as an institution."

— [[Person – Chantal Mouffe]]

## Interpretation
- Democracy should be viewed as an active engagement rather than a fixed structure.
- This perspective emphasizes participation and ongoing dialogue among citizens.
- It suggests that the health of democracy relies on continuous practice and not merely on established institutions.
