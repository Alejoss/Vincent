> I prefer liberty with danger than peace with slavery.

— [[Person – Jean-Jacques Rousseau]]

## Interpretation
- Rousseau values freedom above all else, even if it comes with risks.
- He equates peace derived from subjugation with a form of slavery.
- The quote emphasizes the importance of autonomy in human existence.
