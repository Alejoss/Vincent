> I do not wish them [women] to have power over men; but over themselves.

— [[Person – Mary Wollstonecraft]]

## Interpretation
- Wollstonecraft advocates for women's autonomy and self-determination.
- The emphasis is on personal empowerment rather than domination over others.
- True liberation for women lies in self-governance and independence.
