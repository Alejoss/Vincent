> Freedom of men under government is to be under no other legislative power than that established by consent.

— [[Person – John Locke]]

## Interpretation
- Individuals should only be governed by laws that they have agreed to.
- Legitimacy of government arises from the consent of the governed.
- Freedom is maintained by limiting legislative power to that which is consented to by the people.
