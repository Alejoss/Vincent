> The power of the state lies in its ability to manufacture consent.

— [[Person – Noam Chomsky]]

## Interpretation
- The state exercises control by shaping public opinion and perceptions.
- Consent is not merely given but constructed through propaganda and media manipulation.
- Understanding this process is crucial for recognizing the limits of perceived democracy.
