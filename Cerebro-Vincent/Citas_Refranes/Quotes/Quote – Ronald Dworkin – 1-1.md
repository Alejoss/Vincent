> The law is not a set of rules, but a set of principles."

— [[Person – Ronald Dworkin]]

## Interpretation
- Law encompasses broader moral and ethical principles rather than just strict rules.
- Principles provide a framework for understanding justice and fairness in legal contexts.
- Dworkin emphasizes the importance of interpretation and context in applying the law.
