> Information wants to be free, but that doesn’t mean it should be free for the taking.

— [[Person – Jaron Lanier]]

## Interpretation
- Information has an intrinsic value and should be treated with respect.
- The notion of freedom for information does not justify exploitation or theft.
- Access to information must be balanced with ethical considerations and rights.
