> The essence of democracy is the realization of the moral development of every individual.

— [[Person – Thomas Hill Green]]

## Interpretation
- Democracy is fundamentally about fostering the growth and moral progress of individuals.
- The realization of democracy is tied to the collective advancement of moral values in society.
- Individual moral development is essential for a thriving democratic system.
