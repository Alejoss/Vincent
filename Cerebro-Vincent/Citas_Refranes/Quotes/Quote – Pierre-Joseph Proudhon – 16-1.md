> The only true basis of society is the mutual exchange of goods and services.

— [[Person – Pierre-Joseph Proudhon]]

## Interpretation
- Society is fundamentally built on reciprocal relationships between individuals.
- The exchange of goods and services fosters cooperation and interdependence.
- True social cohesion arises from the recognition of mutual benefit in exchanges.
