> Where there is no law, there is no freedom.

— [[Person – John Locke]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom requires a structure that laws provide to prevent chaos.
- Without laws, individuals may act on their impulses, leading to potential harm and loss of true freedom.
- Law serves to protect individual rights, thereby enabling a more meaningful sense of freedom.
