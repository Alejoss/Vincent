> The only thing that we learn from history is that we learn nothing from history."

— [[Person – Albert O. Hirschman]]

## Interpretation
- Human beings often repeat the same mistakes despite historical lessons.
- History tends to be overlooked or misinterpreted by societies and individuals.
- The quote suggests a skepticism about the effectiveness of history as a teacher.
