> There is no nonsense so arrant that it cannot be made the creed of the vast majority by adequate governmental action.

— [[Person – Bertrand Russell]]

## Interpretation
- Government has the power to shape public beliefs, regardless of their validity.
- Mass persuasion can transform irrational ideas into widely accepted doctrines.
- The influence of authority can lead to the acceptance of absurdities by the majority.
