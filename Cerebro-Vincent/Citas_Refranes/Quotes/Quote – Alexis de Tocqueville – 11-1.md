> Democracy extends the sphere of individual freedom.

— [[Person – Alexis de Tocqueville]]

## Interpretation
- Democracy empowers individuals by increasing their personal liberties.
- It fosters an environment where individual choices are valued and respected.
- This expansion of freedom contributes to a more participatory and engaged society.
