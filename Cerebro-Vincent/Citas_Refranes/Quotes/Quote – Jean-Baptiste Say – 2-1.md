> Production is the source of all wealth."

— [[Person – Jean-Baptiste Say]]

## Interpretation
- Wealth is created through the process of producing goods and services.
- The quote emphasizes the importance of production in driving economic growth.
- It suggests that without production, wealth cannot be generated or sustained.
