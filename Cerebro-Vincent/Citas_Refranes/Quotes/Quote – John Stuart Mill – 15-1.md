> A great part of the mischiefs that men do arises from their not being able to see the connection between their acts and the consequences of those acts.

— [[Person – John Stuart Mill]]

## Interpretation
- People often fail to recognize how their actions lead to specific outcomes.
- This lack of awareness can result in negative consequences for both individuals and society.
- Understanding the connection between actions and consequences is crucial for responsible behavior.
