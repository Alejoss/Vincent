> The trouble with the world is that the stupid are cocksure and the intelligent are full of doubt.

— [[Person – Bertrand Russell]]

## Interpretation
- People with limited understanding often express their opinions with confidence.
- Those who are more knowledgeable tend to recognize the complexities of issues, leading to uncertainty.
- This dynamic can create a disparity in decision-making and societal discourse.
