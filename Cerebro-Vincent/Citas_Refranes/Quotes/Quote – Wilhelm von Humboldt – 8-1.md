> The greatness of a nation and its moral progress can be judged by the way its animals are treated.

— [[Person – Wilhelm von Humboldt]]

## Interpretation
- A nation's ethical values are reflected in its treatment of animals.
- Compassion towards animals indicates a society's moral development.
- The welfare of animals serves as a measure of human civilization and empathy.
