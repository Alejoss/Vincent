> Happiness is that state of consciousness which proceeds from the achievement of one’s values.

— [[Person – Ayn Rand]]

## Interpretation
- Happiness is a mental state that arises from fulfilling personal goals and values.
- The achievement of one's values is essential to one’s sense of well-being.
- True happiness is subjective and rooted in individual pursuits rather than external validation.
