> Economic growth is not an end in itself but a means to an end."

— [[Person – Amartya Sen]]

## Interpretation
- Economic growth should serve broader human goals rather than be the ultimate objective.
- It highlights the importance of focusing on quality of life and well-being.
- Economic progress must be aligned with social and ethical considerations.
