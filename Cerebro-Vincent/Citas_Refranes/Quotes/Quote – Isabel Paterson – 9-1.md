> It is the individual who is the unit of social progress."

— [[Person – Isabel Paterson]]

## Interpretation
- Social progress is driven by the actions and choices of individuals.
- Each person's contributions and innovations play a crucial role in societal advancement.
- Collective change starts with individual initiative and responsibility.
