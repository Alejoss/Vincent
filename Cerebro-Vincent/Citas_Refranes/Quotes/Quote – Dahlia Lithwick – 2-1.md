> The law is not a set of rules; it is a set of principles that guide us."

— [[Person – Dahlia Lithwick]]

## Interpretation
- The law serves as a moral compass rather than merely a collection of regulations.
- It emphasizes the underlying values and ethics that inform legal decisions.
- Understanding law requires insight into its guiding principles rather than just its technicalities.
