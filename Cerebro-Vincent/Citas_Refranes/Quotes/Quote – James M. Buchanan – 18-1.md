> The study of economics cannot be divorced from the study of politics."

— [[Person – James M. Buchanan]]

## Interpretation
- Economics and politics are interconnected fields that influence each other.
- Understanding economic policies requires an understanding of political motivations and structures.
- Economic decisions often reflect political power dynamics and interests.
