> If you don't believe it or don't get it, I don't have the time to try to convince you, sorry.

— [[Person – Satoshi Nakamoto]]

## Interpretation
- Satoshi emphasizes the importance of self-discovery and personal understanding in adopting new ideas.
- There is a sense of urgency and prioritization of time, indicating that not everyone is worth convincing.
- The quote reflects a confidence in the validity of the concept, suggesting that belief is subjective and personal.
