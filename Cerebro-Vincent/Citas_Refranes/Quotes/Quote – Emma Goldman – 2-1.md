> The most violent element in society is ignorance.

— [[Person – Emma Goldman]]

## Interpretation
- Ignorance can lead to fear and hostility, resulting in societal violence.
- Lack of knowledge prevents understanding and empathy among individuals.
- Educating society is essential to mitigate the destructive consequences of ignorance.
