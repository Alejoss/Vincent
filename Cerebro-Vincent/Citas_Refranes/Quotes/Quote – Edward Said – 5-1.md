> We are all of us in a sense the product of our histories."

— [[Person – Edward Said]]

## Interpretation
- Our identities are shaped by the historical contexts we come from.
- Understanding our past is crucial to understanding ourselves.
- Collective and personal histories influence our perspectives and actions.
