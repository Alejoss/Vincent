> The ideal of law as integrity is that legal principles should be interpreted in the light of moral principles."

— [[Person – Ronald Dworkin]]

## Interpretation
- Legal principles are not isolated rules but should align with broader moral values.
- Interpretation of law requires a consideration of ethics to ensure justice and fairness.
- Law as integrity emphasizes coherence between legal and moral frameworks.
