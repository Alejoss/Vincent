> The difference principle permits inequalities only if they benefit the least advantaged members of society.

— [[Person – John Rawls]]

## Interpretation
- Inequalities in society are acceptable only when they lead to improvements for the most disadvantaged.
- The principle emphasizes fairness and justice in the distribution of resources.
- It challenges the notion of meritocracy by prioritizing the welfare of the least advantaged.
