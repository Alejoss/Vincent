> Those who expect to reap the blessings of freedom must, like men, undergo the fatigue of supporting it.

— [[Person – Thomas Paine]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom is not free; it requires effort and sacrifice.
- Individuals must actively participate in the protection and maintenance of their liberties.
- Expecting freedom without personal investment is unrealistic.
