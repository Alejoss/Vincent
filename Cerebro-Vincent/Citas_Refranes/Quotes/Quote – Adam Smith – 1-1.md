> The division of labor is limited by the extent of the market.

— [[Person – Adam Smith]]

## Interpretation
- The specialization of tasks in production increases efficiency but is constrained by market demand.
- A larger market allows for greater division of labor, leading to increased productivity.
- Without sufficient market size, the benefits of labor division cannot be fully realized.
