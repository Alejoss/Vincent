> The strength of a nation lies in the moral integrity of its people.

— [[Person – Thomas Hill Green]]

## Interpretation
- A nation's power is derived from the ethical character of its citizens.
- Moral integrity fosters trust and cohesion within society.
- The collective values of the people directly influence national strength and stability.
