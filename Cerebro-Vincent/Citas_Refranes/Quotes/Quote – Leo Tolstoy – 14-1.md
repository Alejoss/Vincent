> Love is life. And if you miss love, you miss life.

— [[Person – Leo Tolstoy]]

## Interpretation
- Love is the essence of existence, giving life its true meaning.
- Without love, one cannot experience the fullness of life.
- Embracing love is essential for a fulfilling and vibrant life.
