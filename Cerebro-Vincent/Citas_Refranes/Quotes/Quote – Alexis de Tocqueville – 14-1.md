> When the taste for luxury is in the heart, it spreads everywhere.

— [[Person – Alexis de Tocqueville]]

## Interpretation
- A desire for luxury influences individual behavior and choices.
- This taste can permeate social classes and communities, affecting collective values.
- Luxury can shift societal norms, leading to increased materialism and consumerism.
