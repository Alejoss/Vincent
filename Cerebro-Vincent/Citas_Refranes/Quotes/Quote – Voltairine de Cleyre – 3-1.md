> Anarchism is not a crime, it is a philosophy of life."

— [[Person – Voltairine de Cleyre]]

## Interpretation
- Anarchism should not be viewed negatively or criminalized but understood as a legitimate worldview.
- It emphasizes a way of living that values personal freedom and autonomy.
- De Cleyre highlights the philosophical foundations of anarchism rather than its misrepresentation as lawlessness.
