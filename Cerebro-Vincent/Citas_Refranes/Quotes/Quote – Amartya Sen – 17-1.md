> The challenge of development is not just economic growth, it is about enhancing lives."

— [[Person – Amartya Sen]]

## Interpretation
- Development should focus on improving the quality of life, not just increasing GDP.
- True progress is measured by the well-being and capabilities of individuals.
- Economic growth alone cannot address social inequalities and human needs.
