> It is not the position of the person that makes the man; it is the position of the man that makes the person."

— [[Person – Voltairine de Cleyre]]

## Interpretation
- A person's character and identity are shaped by their actions and choices, not merely by their social status or title.
- The quote emphasizes personal responsibility and the idea that one's true worth is derived from their behavior and decisions.
- It challenges the notion that external circumstances define an individual's value or capabilities.
