> The existence of rights implies that there are limits to what can be done to individuals, regardless of the consequences.

— [[Person – Robert Nozick]]

## Interpretation
- Rights establish moral boundaries that protect individuals from harm.
- Consequences cannot justify violating an individual's rights.
- The quote emphasizes the intrinsic value of individual autonomy and dignity.
