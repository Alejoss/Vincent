> Truth is what your contemporaries let you get away with.

— [[Person – Richard Rorty]]

## Interpretation
- Truth is subjective and shaped by social consensus.
- What is accepted as true can vary across different contexts and times.
- The idea of truth is fluid and contingent upon societal norms and pressures.
