> The only thing that will redeem mankind is cooperation.

— [[Person – Bertrand Russell]]

## Interpretation
- Cooperation is essential for humanity's progress and survival.
- The quote implies that individualistic approaches are insufficient to solve global challenges.
- A collective effort is necessary to achieve meaningful change in society.
