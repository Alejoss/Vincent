> Property rights are the foundation of a free society."

— [[Person – Samuel Edward Konkin III]]

## Interpretation
- Property rights ensure individual autonomy and control over personal resources.
- The recognition of property rights prevents coercion and protects individuals from infringement by others.
- A society that upholds property rights fosters economic innovation and personal freedom.
