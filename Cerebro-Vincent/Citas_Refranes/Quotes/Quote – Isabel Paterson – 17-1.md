> The world is not a playground, but a battleground."

— [[Person – Isabel Paterson]]

## Interpretation
- Life is filled with challenges and struggles, rather than being a space for carefree enjoyment.
- Individuals must be prepared to confront conflicts and adversities in their journey.
- The quote emphasizes a realistic perspective on the nature of existence and human experience.
