> I am more convinced than ever that it is the power of the imagination which is the true source of all human endeavour.

— [[Person – Augusta Ada King, Countess of Lovelace]]

## Interpretation
- Imagination is a fundamental driving force behind human creativity and progress.
- All human efforts and achievements stem from the capacity to envision possibilities.
- The quote emphasizes the importance of imaginative thinking in shaping our endeavors.
