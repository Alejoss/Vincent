> The feminist movement has always been about more than just women’s rights; it’s about challenging the structures that perpetuate inequality."

— [[Person – Nancy Fraser]]

## Interpretation
- The feminist movement aims to address systemic issues beyond women's rights.
- It seeks to dismantle societal structures that maintain various forms of inequality.
- Feminism encompasses a broader social justice agenda that includes intersectionality.
