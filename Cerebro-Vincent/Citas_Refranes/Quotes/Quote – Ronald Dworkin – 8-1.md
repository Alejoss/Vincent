> Judges must interpret the law with an eye toward its moral implications."

— [[Person – Ronald Dworkin]]

## Interpretation
- Judges are not just legal technicians but must consider the ethical dimensions of the law.
- The interpretation of law should reflect societal values and moral principles.
- This approach emphasizes the connection between law and justice.
