> The idea of the 'Orient' is a construct of the West."

— [[Person – Edward Said]]

## Interpretation
- The 'Orient' is not an inherent identity but a representation shaped by Western perspectives.
- This construct serves to justify colonial and imperial ambitions by creating a binary between the West and the East.
- It highlights the power dynamics involved in cultural representation and knowledge production.
