> In science, there are no shortcuts to truth.

— [[Person – Karl Popper]]

## Interpretation
- Scientific understanding requires rigorous inquiry and experimentation.
- There are no easy paths or quick fixes in the pursuit of knowledge.
- Truth in science is achieved through consistent testing and validation of hypotheses.
