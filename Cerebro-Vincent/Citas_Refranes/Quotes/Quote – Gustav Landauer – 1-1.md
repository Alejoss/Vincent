> The state is a social relationship, not a thing or an institution.

— [[Person – Gustav Landauer]]

## Interpretation
- The state is defined by the interactions and relationships between individuals rather than by its physical structures.
- This perspective emphasizes the importance of community and social bonds over bureaucratic institutions.
- It suggests that change in society requires a transformation of these relationships, not just institutional reform.
