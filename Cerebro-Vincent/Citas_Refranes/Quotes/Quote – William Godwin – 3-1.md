> It is a man's own mind, not his enemy or foe, that lures him to evil ways.

— [[Person – William Godwin]]

## Interpretation
- The greatest temptations and moral failures originate from within oneself.
- External adversaries are not the primary source of one's wrongdoing.
- Self-awareness and introspection are crucial in understanding one's moral choices.
