> Security is not a state; it is a dynamic process that requires constant attention."

— [[Person – Whitfield Diffie]]

## Interpretation
- Security is not a fixed condition but an ongoing effort.
- Maintaining security involves vigilance and adaptability.
- It emphasizes the necessity of proactive measures in safeguarding systems.
