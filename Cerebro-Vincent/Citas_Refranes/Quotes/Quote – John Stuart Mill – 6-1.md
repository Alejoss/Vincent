> All good things which exist are the fruits of originality.

— [[Person – John Stuart Mill]]

## Interpretation
- Originality is essential for the creation of valuable and beneficial ideas.
- Good things in society arise from innovative thinking and creativity.
- The quote emphasizes the importance of individual contribution to progress and improvement.
