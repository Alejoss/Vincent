> The fact that we can’t imagine alternatives is a sign of how successful the dominant ideologies have been."

— [[Person – David Graeber]]

## Interpretation
- The inability to envision alternatives reflects the power of prevailing ideologies.
- Successful ideologies limit our creative and critical thinking about societal structures.
- This suggests a need for greater awareness and exploration of different possibilities.
