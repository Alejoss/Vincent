> The self is the only certainty.

— [[Person – Max Stirner]]

## Interpretation
- The individual self is the foundation of all knowledge and existence.
- Outside of the self, all beliefs and truths are subjective and uncertain.
- Personal experience and self-awareness are paramount in understanding one's reality.
