> The government is a monster that cannot be controlled."

— [[Person – Lysander Spooner]]

## Interpretation
- The government is perceived as a dangerous entity that operates beyond the control of citizens.
- This suggests a view of government as inherently aggressive and unpredictable.
- It implies that attempts to regulate or manage government actions may ultimately fail.
