> We learn from our mistakes; we learn from our failures.

— [[Person – Karl Popper]]

## Interpretation
- Mistakes and failures are essential components of the learning process.
- Experiencing setbacks allows for personal and intellectual growth.
- Acknowledging errors fosters resilience and adaptability in future endeavors.
