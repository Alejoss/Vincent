> Our greatest glory is not in never falling, but in rising every time we fall.

— [[Person – Wilhelm von Humboldt]]

## Interpretation
- True success is measured by resilience and the ability to recover from setbacks.
- Falling is a natural part of life, but what defines us is our response to failure.
- Embracing challenges and learning from mistakes leads to personal growth.
