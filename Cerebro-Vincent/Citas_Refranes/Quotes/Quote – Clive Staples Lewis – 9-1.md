> The great thing to remember is that though our feelings come and go, God’s love for us does not.

— [[Person – Clive Staples Lewis]]

## Interpretation
- Our emotions are transient and can fluctuate, affecting our perception of reality.
- God's love remains constant and unwavering, regardless of our emotional state.
- This highlights the importance of relying on enduring truths rather than changing feelings.
