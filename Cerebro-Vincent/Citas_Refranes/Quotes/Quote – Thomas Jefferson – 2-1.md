> The tree of liberty must be refreshed from time to time with the blood of patriots and tyrants.

— [[Person – Thomas Jefferson]]

## Interpretation
- Liberty requires sacrifice and sometimes violence to maintain.
- The struggle between patriots and tyrants is a necessary part of preserving freedom.
- A vibrant democracy may demand the confrontation of oppressive forces.
