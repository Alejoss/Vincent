> To be free is not merely to cast off one's chains, but to live in a way that respects and enhances the freedom of others."

— [[Person – Lucy Parsons]]

## Interpretation
- True freedom involves not just personal liberation but also the responsibility to uphold the freedom of others.
- Genuine liberation is interconnected; one person's freedom is contingent upon the freedom of the collective.
- Living freely means fostering an environment where everyone can thrive without oppression.
