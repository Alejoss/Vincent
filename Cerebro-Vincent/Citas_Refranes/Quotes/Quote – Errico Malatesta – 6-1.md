> To be free is to be able to enjoy all the fruits of our labor without the interference of others.

— [[Person – Errico Malatesta]]

## Interpretation
- True freedom is defined by the ability to reap the rewards of one's own efforts.
- Interference from others can undermine personal autonomy and enjoyment of labor's outcomes.
- The quote emphasizes the importance of self-determination in achieving fulfillment.
