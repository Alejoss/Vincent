> The law can be a tool for liberation, but it can also be a weapon of oppression."

— [[Person – Derrick Bell]]

## Interpretation
- The law has the potential to empower marginalized communities and promote justice.
- Conversely, it can be manipulated to reinforce existing power structures and perpetuate inequality.
- The dual nature of the law highlights the importance of critical examination and advocacy for reform.
