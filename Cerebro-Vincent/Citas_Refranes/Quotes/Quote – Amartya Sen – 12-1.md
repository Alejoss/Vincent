> Social justice is not about equality alone; it is about ensuring freedom and opportunities."

— [[Person – Amartya Sen]]

## Interpretation
- Social justice encompasses more than just equal distribution of resources.
- It emphasizes the importance of individual freedoms and personal opportunities.
- True social justice aims to empower individuals to achieve their potential.
