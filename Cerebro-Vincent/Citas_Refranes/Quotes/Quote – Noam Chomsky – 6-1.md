> There is no such thing as a free market. There are only markets that are free for the powerful.

— [[Person – Noam Chomsky]]

## Interpretation
- Chomsky suggests that the concept of a free market is a myth, as true freedom is not equally accessible to all.
- The quote highlights the disparity in power dynamics, where the wealthy and influential manipulate market conditions to their advantage.
- It critiques the notion that markets operate on equal footing, emphasizing that freedom is often a privilege of the powerful.
