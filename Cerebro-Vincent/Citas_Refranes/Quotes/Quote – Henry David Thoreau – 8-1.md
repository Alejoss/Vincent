> Things do not change; we change.

— [[Person – Henry David Thoreau]]

## Interpretation
- The essence of the world remains constant, but our perceptions and responses to it evolve.
- Personal growth and change are internal processes that shape our experiences.
- Our ability to adapt and change influences how we interact with unchanging circumstances.
