> There is much pleasure to be gained from useless knowledge.

— [[Person – Bertrand Russell]]

## Interpretation
- The pursuit of knowledge can be enjoyable even if it serves no practical purpose.
- Useless knowledge enriches our minds and can lead to personal satisfaction.
- Engaging with trivia and seemingly irrelevant facts fosters creativity and curiosity.
