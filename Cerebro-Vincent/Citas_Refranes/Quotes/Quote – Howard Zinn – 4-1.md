> Civil disobedience is not our problem. Our problem is civil obedience.

— [[Person – Howard Zinn]]

## Interpretation
- Civil obedience leads to the perpetuation of unjust systems.
- The true issue lies in people's uncritical compliance with authority.
- Challenging the status quo through disobedience is necessary for social change.
