> Trust is a scarce resource, and decentralization can help to alleviate the scarcity."

— [[Person – Nick Szabo]]

## Interpretation
- Trust is limited and not universally available.
- Decentralization offers a solution to distribute and enhance trust.
- By removing central authority, trust can be fostered among individuals.
