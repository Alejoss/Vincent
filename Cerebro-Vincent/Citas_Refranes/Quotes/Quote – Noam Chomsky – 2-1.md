> If we do not believe in freedom of expression for people we despise, we do not believe in it at all.

— [[Person – Noam Chomsky]]

## Interpretation
- True commitment to freedom of expression requires tolerance for all viewpoints, even those we disagree with.
- Limiting freedom of expression based on personal bias undermines the principle of free speech.
- Genuine support for freedom must encompass protecting the rights of those we find objectionable.
