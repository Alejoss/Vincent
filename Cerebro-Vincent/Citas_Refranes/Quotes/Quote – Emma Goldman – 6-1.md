> I want freedom, the right to self-expression, everybody's right to beautiful, radiant things.

— [[Person – Emma Goldman]]

## Interpretation
- Goldman emphasizes the fundamental importance of individual freedom and self-expression.
- She advocates for the universal right to access beauty and joy in life.
- The quote reflects a vision of a society where personal liberties are celebrated and nurtured.
