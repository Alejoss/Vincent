> The unique one is the true man.

— [[Person – Max Stirner]]

## Interpretation
- The concept of individuality is paramount in defining true existence.
- Emphasizing uniqueness suggests that authenticity is central to human identity.
- Rejecting conformity, the quote promotes self-actualization and personal freedom.
