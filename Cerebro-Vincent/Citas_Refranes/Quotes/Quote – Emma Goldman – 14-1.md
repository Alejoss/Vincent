> The revolution is not the end; it is the beginning of a new struggle.

— [[Person – Emma Goldman]]

## Interpretation
- The revolution signifies a significant change in society but is not a final destination.
- It marks the start of ongoing efforts to achieve further progress and justice.
- The struggle for equality and freedom continues even after a successful revolution.
