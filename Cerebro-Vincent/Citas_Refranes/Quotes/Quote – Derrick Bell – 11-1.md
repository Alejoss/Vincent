> True justice requires a fundamental change in the way we think about race and power."

— [[Person – Derrick Bell]]

## Interpretation
- Justice cannot be achieved without altering our perceptions of race and the dynamics of power.
- A superficial approach to justice is inadequate; systemic change is necessary.
- Rethinking race and power dynamics is essential for genuine equity and fairness.
