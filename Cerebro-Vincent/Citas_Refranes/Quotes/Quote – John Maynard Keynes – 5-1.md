> Practical men, who believe themselves to be quite exempt from any intellectual influences, are usually the slaves of some defunct economist.

— [[Person – John Maynard Keynes]]

## Interpretation
- Practical individuals often underestimate the impact of economic theories on their decision-making.
- They may believe they operate independently of economic thought, yet they are influenced by outdated ideas.
- This highlights the irony of how unexamined beliefs can govern actions in economic practices.
