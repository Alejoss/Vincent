> Injustice is not only a matter of poverty; it is a matter of capabilities."

— [[Person – Amartya Sen]]

## Interpretation
- Injustice extends beyond mere economic deprivation to encompass a lack of opportunities and abilities.
- Capabilities reflect what individuals can do and be, highlighting the importance of personal and social development.
- Addressing injustice requires a broader focus on enhancing individual capabilities, not just alleviating poverty.
