> The narratives of marginalized groups are essential in understanding the full scope of social justice."

— [[Person – Patricia Hill Collins]]

## Interpretation
- Marginalized groups offer unique perspectives that enrich our understanding of social issues.
- Ignoring these narratives limits our comprehension of justice and equity.
- Incorporating diverse voices is crucial for a holistic approach to social justice.
