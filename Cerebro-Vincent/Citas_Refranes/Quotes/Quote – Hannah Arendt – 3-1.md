> Power and violence are opposites; where the one rules absolutely, the other is absent.

— [[Person – Hannah Arendt]]

## Interpretation
- Power is based on collective agreement and legitimacy, while violence relies on fear and coercion.
- When power is effectively established, the need for violence diminishes.
- The coexistence of power and violence is inherently unstable, as true power negates the necessity of violence.
