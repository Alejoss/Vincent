> The personal is political, and the political is personal."

— [[Person – Patricia Hill Collins]]

## Interpretation
- Individual experiences are shaped by larger societal structures and power dynamics.
- Personal struggles often reflect systemic inequalities and injustices.
- Political actions and policies can have direct, personal impacts on individuals' lives.
