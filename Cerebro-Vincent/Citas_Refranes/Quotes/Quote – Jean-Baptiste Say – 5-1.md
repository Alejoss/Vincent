> The law of markets is the law of life."

— [[Person – Jean-Baptiste Say]]

## Interpretation
- Economic activity is fundamental to human existence.
- Market dynamics reflect the natural order of life and society.
- Understanding supply and demand is essential to grasping the complexities of life.
