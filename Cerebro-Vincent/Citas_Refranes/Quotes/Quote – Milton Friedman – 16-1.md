> It is a common misconception that the free market is based on selfishness.

— [[Person – Milton Friedman]]

## Interpretation
- The free market encourages voluntary exchanges that can benefit all parties involved.
- Self-interest in a free market can lead to positive societal outcomes, contrary to the notion of inherent selfishness.
- Understanding the dynamics of the free market requires a recognition of cooperation and mutual benefit rather than mere self-serving behavior.
