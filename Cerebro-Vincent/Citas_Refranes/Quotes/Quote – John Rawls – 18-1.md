> The principles of justice are not to be derived from the actual history of a society.

— [[Person – John Rawls]]

## Interpretation
- Justice principles should be based on ideals rather than historical realities.
- Historical injustices or circumstances do not define what is just.
- A fair society requires abstract reasoning about justice, independent of past actions.
