> It is not in the stars to hold our destiny but in ourselves.

— [[Person – Thomas Paine]]

## Interpretation
- Our future is determined by our own actions and choices.
- We have the power to shape our own lives rather than relying on fate or external forces.
- Personal responsibility and agency are essential in determining our paths.
