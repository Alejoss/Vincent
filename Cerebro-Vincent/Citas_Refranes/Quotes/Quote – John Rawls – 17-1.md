> A theory of justice must take into account the moral nature of individuals.

— [[Person – John Rawls]]

## Interpretation
- Justice cannot be purely abstract and must consider human morality.
- Individuals' moral nature influences their understanding and need for justice.
- A comprehensive theory of justice integrates ethical considerations into its framework.
