> The importance of cultural diversity cannot be overstated in a globalized world.

— [[Person – Isaiah Berlin]]

## Interpretation
- Cultural diversity enriches societies by introducing varied perspectives and experiences.
- In a globalized world, recognizing and valuing different cultures promotes coexistence and understanding.
- Neglecting cultural diversity can lead to homogenization and the loss of unique identities.
