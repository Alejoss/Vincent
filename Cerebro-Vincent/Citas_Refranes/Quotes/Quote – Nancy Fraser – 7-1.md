> The intersection of various injustices demands a multifaceted approach to social theory and activism."

— [[Person – Nancy Fraser]]

## Interpretation
- Different forms of injustice are interconnected and cannot be addressed in isolation.
- Social theory and activism must adapt to consider the complexity of these intersections.
- A comprehensive approach is necessary for effective solutions to social issues.
