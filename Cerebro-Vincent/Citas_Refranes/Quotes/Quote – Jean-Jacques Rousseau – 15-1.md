> In the state of nature, the man is a solitary being.

— [[Person – Jean-Jacques Rousseau]]

## Interpretation
- In his natural state, man exists independently and without social ties.
- Rousseau suggests that human beings are inherently solitary before the influence of society.
- This solitude highlights the contrast between natural existence and social constructs.
