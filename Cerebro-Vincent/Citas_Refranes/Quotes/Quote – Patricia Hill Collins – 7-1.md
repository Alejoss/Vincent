> Understanding the complexity of our social identities helps us to see the bigger picture of oppression."

— [[Person – Patricia Hill Collins]]

## Interpretation
- Social identities are multifaceted and interconnected.
- A deeper understanding of these identities reveals the systemic nature of oppression.
- Recognizing complexity fosters empathy and solidarity among diverse groups.
