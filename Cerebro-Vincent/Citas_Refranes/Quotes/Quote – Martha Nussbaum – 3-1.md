> Justice is not only a matter of distributing goods and resources; it is also about the capacity to live a dignified life."

— [[Person – Martha Nussbaum]]

## Interpretation
- Justice involves equitable distribution of resources.
- A dignified life encompasses more than material wealth.
- True justice ensures individuals have the capability to thrive and flourish.
