> I believe that every human mind feels pleasure in doing good to another.

— [[Person – Thomas Jefferson]]

## Interpretation
- Humans have an innate inclination towards altruism and kindness.
- Acts of goodness not only benefit others but also provide personal satisfaction.
- The pleasure derived from helping others reinforces social bonds and community spirit.
