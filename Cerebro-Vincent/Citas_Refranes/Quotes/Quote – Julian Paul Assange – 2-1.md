> If we can’t find a way to keep people informed, we’re going to lose the ability to govern ourselves.

— [[Person – Julian Paul Assange]]

## Interpretation
- Access to information is essential for informed citizenship.
- Without transparency, democratic governance is at risk.
- The ability to govern ourselves relies on the flow of accurate information.
