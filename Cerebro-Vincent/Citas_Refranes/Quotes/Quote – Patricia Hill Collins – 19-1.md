> The intersections of race, class, and gender must be acknowledged in any movement for equality."

— [[Person – Patricia Hill Collins]]

## Interpretation
- Equality movements must consider the complex ways in which race, class, and gender intersect and affect individuals.
- Ignoring these intersections can lead to ineffective or exclusionary approaches to achieving equality.
- A comprehensive understanding of social justice requires an analysis of multiple identities and their impact on experiences of oppression.
