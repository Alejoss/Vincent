> The question of whether the people should be governed by the state can arise only if the state is not a necessary condition for the achievement of their ends.

— [[Person – Robert Nozick]]

## Interpretation
- The legitimacy of state governance is questioned when individuals can achieve their goals without it.
- The state’s role is contingent on its necessity for fulfilling human purposes.
- If alternatives exist that allow people to pursue their ends, the justification for state authority diminishes.
