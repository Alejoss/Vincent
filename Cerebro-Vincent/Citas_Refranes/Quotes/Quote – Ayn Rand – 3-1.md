> The most important thing to remember about your own happiness is that it is not your responsibility to make others happy.

— [[Person – Ayn Rand]]

## Interpretation
- Your own happiness is a personal journey that should not be contingent on the feelings of others.
- It emphasizes the importance of self-fulfillment over the obligation to please others.
- It suggests that seeking personal happiness may require prioritizing oneself over communal expectations.
