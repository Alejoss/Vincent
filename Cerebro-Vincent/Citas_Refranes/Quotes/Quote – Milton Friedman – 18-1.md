> The key to solving the social problems of our time is to restore the family as the basic unit of society.

— [[Person – Milton Friedman]]

## Interpretation
- Friedman emphasizes the importance of the family structure in addressing societal issues.
- He suggests that strengthening families can lead to broader social improvements.
- The quote implies that many contemporary problems may stem from the weakening of familial bonds.
