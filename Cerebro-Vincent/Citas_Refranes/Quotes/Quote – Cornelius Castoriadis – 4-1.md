> We need to understand that the world is not a given, but a project."

— [[Person – Cornelius Castoriadis]]

## Interpretation
- The world is not a fixed reality but something we actively shape and construct.
- This perspective emphasizes the importance of human agency and creativity in societal development.
- It challenges the notion of passivity in our relationship with the world, urging proactive engagement.
