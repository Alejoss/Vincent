> Information wants to be free."

— [[Person – Lawrence Lessig]]

## Interpretation
- Information inherently seeks to be accessible and disseminated.
- The phrase suggests a conflict between the ownership of information and the public's right to access it.
- It reflects a belief in the democratizing power of technology and open communication.
