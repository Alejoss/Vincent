> The idea of freedom is not the individual alone; it is the community as a whole.

— [[Person – Emma Goldman]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom is a collective concept that transcends individual interests.
- True liberty involves the well-being and participation of the entire community.
- Individual freedom cannot be fully realized without the freedom of others in the society.
