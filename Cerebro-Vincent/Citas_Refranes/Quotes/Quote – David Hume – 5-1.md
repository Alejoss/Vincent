> A propensity to hope and joy is real riches; one to fear and sorrow real poverty.

— [[Person – David Hume]]

## Interpretation
- Hope and joy contribute to a fulfilling life, representing true wealth.
- Fear and sorrow detract from happiness, symbolizing a form of poverty.
- The quote emphasizes the importance of emotional and psychological states over material wealth.
