> The great object of the institution of society and government is the improvement of the conditions of the human race.

— [[Person – David Ricardo]]

## Interpretation
- Society and government exist primarily to enhance the well-being of individuals.
- The quote emphasizes the role of institutions in facilitating progress and improvement.
- It suggests a moral obligation for societal structures to prioritize human welfare.
