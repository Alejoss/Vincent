> I believe in the equality of man; and I believe that religious duties consist in doing justice, loving mercy, and endeavoring to make our fellow creatures happy.

— [[Person – Thomas Paine]]

## Interpretation
- Paine emphasizes the inherent equality of all individuals, rejecting hierarchies based on class or status.
- He suggests that true religious expression is found in ethical actions rather than dogma.
- The call to justice, mercy, and happiness highlights the importance of compassion in human relationships.
