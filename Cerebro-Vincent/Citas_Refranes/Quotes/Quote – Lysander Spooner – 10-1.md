> A government that robs Peter to pay Paul can always depend on the support of Paul."

— [[Person – Lysander Spooner]]

## Interpretation
- The quote highlights the reliance of a government on the support of those who benefit from redistribution of wealth.
- It suggests a moral critique of a system that fosters dependency through unequal financial practices.
- It implies that such practices can create a political alliance based on self-interest rather than fairness.
