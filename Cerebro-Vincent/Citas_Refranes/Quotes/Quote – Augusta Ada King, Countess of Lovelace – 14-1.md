> I believe that the future of computing will be in the imaginations of those who create it.

— [[Person – Augusta Ada King, Countess of Lovelace]]

## Interpretation
- The potential of computing is driven by creativity and innovation.
- Imagination is essential for future advancements in technology.
- Creators play a pivotal role in shaping the direction and impact of computing.
