> It is essential to recognize that individuals are motivated by their own interests, even in a collective context."

— [[Person – James M. Buchanan]]

## Interpretation
- Individuals prioritize their personal interests over collective goals.
- Self-interest plays a crucial role in decision-making within groups.
- Understanding this motivation is key to analyzing social and economic interactions.
