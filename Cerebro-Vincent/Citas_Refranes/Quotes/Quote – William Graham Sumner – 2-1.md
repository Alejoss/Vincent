> The people who are the most important in this world are the people who have the most to do with the future.

— [[Person – William Graham Sumner]]

## Interpretation
- Individuals shaping future outcomes hold significant importance in society.
- The value of a person's contributions is often measured by their impact on future generations.
- This perspective emphasizes foresight and responsibility in leadership and decision-making.
