> There is no greatness where there is not simplicity, goodness, and truth.

— [[Person – Leo Tolstoy]]

## Interpretation
- Greatness is rooted in fundamental values rather than complexity or superficial traits.
- Simplicity, goodness, and truth are essential qualities that define true greatness.
- Without these virtues, any perceived greatness is likely to be shallow or insincere.
