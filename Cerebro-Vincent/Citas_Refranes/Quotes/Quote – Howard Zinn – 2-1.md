> We are not in a position to evaluate those who have come before us.

— [[Person – Howard Zinn]]

## Interpretation
- We lack the full context to judge past actions and decisions.
- Historical perspectives are shaped by present circumstances and knowledge.
- Understanding the past requires empathy and recognition of its complexities.
