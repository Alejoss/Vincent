> Those who do not move, do not notice their chains.

— [[Person – Rosa Luxemburg]]

## Interpretation
- Individuals who remain passive or complacent are often unaware of their oppression.
- Movement and action are necessary to recognize and challenge one's limitations or constraints.
- Awareness of one's circumstances often requires active engagement and struggle against the status quo.
