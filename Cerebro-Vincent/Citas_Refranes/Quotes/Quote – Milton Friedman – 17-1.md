> The government can’t do anything in a better way than the private sector.

— [[Person – Milton Friedman]]

## Interpretation
- Friedman argues that the private sector is inherently more efficient than government.
- He suggests that government intervention often leads to inferior outcomes.
- The quote reflects a belief in free-market principles over government control.
