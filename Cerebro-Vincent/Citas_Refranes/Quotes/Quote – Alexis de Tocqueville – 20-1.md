> The opinion of the majority is often the only one that is heard.

— [[Person – Alexis de Tocqueville]]

## Interpretation
- The majority's viewpoint tends to dominate public discourse.
- Minority opinions may be marginalized or ignored in decision-making.
- This dynamic can lead to a lack of diverse perspectives in society.
