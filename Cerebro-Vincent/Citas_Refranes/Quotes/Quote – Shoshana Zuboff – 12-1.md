> Surveillance capitalism is about the capture of personal experience for profit."

— [[Person – Shoshana Zuboff]]

## Interpretation
- Surveillance capitalism commodifies individual data and experiences.
- It prioritizes profit over privacy, often without informed consent.
- Personal experiences are transformed into products that can be sold to third parties.
