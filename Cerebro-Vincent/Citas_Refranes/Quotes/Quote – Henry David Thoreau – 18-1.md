> The language of friendship is not words but meanings.

— [[Person – Henry David Thoreau]]

## Interpretation
- True friendship transcends verbal communication and relies on deeper understanding.
- The essence of friendship is found in shared experiences and emotions rather than spoken language.
- Meaningful connections often exist beyond the limitations of words.
