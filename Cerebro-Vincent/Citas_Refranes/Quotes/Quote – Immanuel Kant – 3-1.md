> Enlightenment is man's emergence from his self-imposed immaturity.

— [[Person – Immanuel Kant]]

## Interpretation
- Enlightenment represents the process of gaining knowledge and understanding.
- Self-imposed immaturity refers to the reluctance to think independently and rely on one's own reason.
- The quote emphasizes the importance of intellectual freedom and personal responsibility in achieving maturity.
