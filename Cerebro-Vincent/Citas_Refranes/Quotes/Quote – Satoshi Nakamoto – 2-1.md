> The main advantages of Bitcoin are that it is a decentralized currency and it allows for peer-to-peer transactions without a central authority.

— [[Person – Satoshi Nakamoto]]

## Interpretation
- Bitcoin operates without a central governing body, promoting autonomy.
- It facilitates direct transactions between users, enhancing efficiency and reducing costs.
- Decentralization increases security and reduces the risk of censorship or control by any single entity.
