> Men are by nature free and equal in rights.

— [[Person – John Locke]]

## Interpretation
- All individuals possess inherent freedoms that should not be infringed upon.
- Equality in rights implies that no one is superior or inferior by nature.
- This concept serves as a foundation for democratic governance and human rights.
