> The key to understanding social behavior is to recognize the importance of incentives."

— [[Person – Charles Murray]]

## Interpretation
- Incentives shape individual choices and actions within society.
- Understanding social behavior requires analyzing the motivations behind people's decisions.
- Different incentives can lead to varying social outcomes and behaviors.
