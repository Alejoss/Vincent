> The law of nature is the will of God, revealed in the moral sense of mankind.

— [[Person – John Locke]]

## Interpretation
- The law of nature represents a universal moral order inherent in human beings.
- It suggests that moral truths are divinely inspired and accessible through human conscience.
- Human beings possess an innate ability to discern right from wrong, reflecting God's will.
