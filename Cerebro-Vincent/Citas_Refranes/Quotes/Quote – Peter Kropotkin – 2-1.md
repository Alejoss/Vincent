> The state is the most powerful of all the machines of oppression, and it is the most powerful because it has the monopoly of the use of force.

— [[Person – Peter Kropotkin]]

## Interpretation
- The state wields significant power by controlling the means of coercion.
- This monopoly on force enables the state to suppress dissent and maintain order.
- Kropotkin suggests that the state's power is rooted in its ability to enforce its will through violence.
