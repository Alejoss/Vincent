> The self is not a substance; it is a narrative we tell ourselves.

— [[Person – Richard Rorty]]

## Interpretation
- The self is constructed through the stories and experiences we share.
- Identity is fluid and shaped by personal narratives rather than fixed traits.
- Understanding ourselves involves reflecting on the meanings we attribute to our experiences.
