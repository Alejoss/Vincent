> The internet is a platform for innovation."

— [[Person – Lawrence Lessig]]

## Interpretation
- The internet serves as a foundational space where new ideas and technologies can emerge.
- It democratizes access to resources and opportunities for creative expression and entrepreneurship.
- The platform nature of the internet allows diverse participants to contribute and innovate without traditional barriers.
