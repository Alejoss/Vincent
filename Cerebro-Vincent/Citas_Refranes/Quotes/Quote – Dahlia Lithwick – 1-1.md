> The Supreme Court is not a political institution, but a legal one."

— [[Person – Dahlia Lithwick]]

## Interpretation
- The quote emphasizes the judicial role of the Supreme Court in interpreting laws rather than engaging in political maneuvering.
- It suggests that the Court's decisions should be based on legal principles rather than partisan agendas.
- This perspective reinforces the idea of judicial independence and the rule of law.
