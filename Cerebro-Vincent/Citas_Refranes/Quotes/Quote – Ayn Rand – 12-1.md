> To say “I love you” one must first be able to say the “I.”

— [[Person – Ayn Rand]]

## Interpretation
- Self-identity is a prerequisite for genuine love.
- One must have a strong sense of self before being able to connect with others.
- True love arises from an authentic understanding of oneself.
