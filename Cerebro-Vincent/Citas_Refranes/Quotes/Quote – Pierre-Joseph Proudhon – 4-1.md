> Justice is the constant and perpetual will to render to each his due.

— [[Person – Pierre-Joseph Proudhon]]

## Interpretation
- Justice is an ongoing commitment rather than a one-time act.
- It emphasizes fairness and the importance of recognizing individual rights.
- The phrase 'render to each his due' highlights the moral obligation to acknowledge and fulfill what is owed to others.
