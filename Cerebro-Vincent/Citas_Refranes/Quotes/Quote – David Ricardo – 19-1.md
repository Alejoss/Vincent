> The value of money is affected by the quantity of money in circulation.

— [[Person – David Ricardo]]

## Interpretation
- The value of money fluctuates based on its supply in the economy.
- An increase in the quantity of money can lead to inflation, reducing money's purchasing power.
- Conversely, a decrease in money supply can increase its value, tightening the economy.
