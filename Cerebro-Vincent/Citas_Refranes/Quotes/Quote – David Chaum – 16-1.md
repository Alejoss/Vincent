> Empowering individuals with control over their identity is essential."

— [[Person – David Chaum]]

## Interpretation
- Control over one's identity fosters personal autonomy.
- Empowerment in identity management can enhance privacy and security.
- Individuals should have the agency to define and manage their own digital presence.
