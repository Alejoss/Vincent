> To think is to be alive and to think is to be free.

— [[Person – Hannah Arendt]]

## Interpretation
- Thinking is an essential aspect of human existence, reflecting our vitality.
- The act of thinking liberates us from dogma and allows for personal autonomy.
- Freedom is intrinsically linked to our capacity for critical thought and reflection.
