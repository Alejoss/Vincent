> We should not let the pursuit of perfection distract us from the pursuit of justice.

— [[Person – John Rawls]]

## Interpretation
- The quest for an ideal society should not overshadow the need for fair and just practices.
- Focusing too much on achieving perfection can lead to neglecting the essential principles of justice.
- Justice should be prioritized as a foundational value over the unattainable goal of perfection.
