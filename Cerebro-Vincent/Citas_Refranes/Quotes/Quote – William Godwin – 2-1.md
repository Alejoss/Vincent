> The greatest difficulty in the world is to be able to understand ourselves.

— [[Person – William Godwin]]

## Interpretation
- Self-understanding is a complex and challenging endeavor.
- Many external factors can cloud our self-perception and awareness.
- True introspection requires honesty and courage to confront one's own thoughts and feelings.
