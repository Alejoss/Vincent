> You don’t have to be a genius to create something great; you just have to be willing to work hard."

— [[Person – Mitchell Kapor]]

## Interpretation
- Greatness is achievable by anyone, not just the exceptionally intelligent.
- Diligence and perseverance are key factors in the creative process.
- Hard work is often more important than innate talent in achieving success.
