> The market does not create a just society; it is society that must shape the market."

— [[Person – C. B. Macpherson]]

## Interpretation
- The market operates on principles that may not inherently promote justice or equality.
- It is the responsibility of society to establish ethical frameworks that guide market activities.
- Social values and norms should influence economic systems to ensure fairness and justice.
