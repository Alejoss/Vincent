> To engage in the legal process is to engage in the process of democracy."

— [[Person – Dahlia Lithwick]]

## Interpretation
- The legal process is a fundamental aspect of democratic governance.
- Participation in legal proceedings empowers citizens to influence societal norms.
- Engagement in law reflects a commitment to upholding democratic values.
