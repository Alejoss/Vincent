> The principle of justice in acquisition states that individuals can acquire holdings as long as it doesn't disadvantage others.

— [[Person – Robert Nozick]]

## Interpretation
- Individuals have the right to acquire resources and property.
- Acquisition must be done in a way that does not harm or disadvantage others.
- The principle promotes fairness and respect for the rights of all individuals.
