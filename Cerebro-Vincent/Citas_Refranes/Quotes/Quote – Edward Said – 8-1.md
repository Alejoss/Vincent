> To speak of 'the West' and 'the East' is to oversimplify the complex realities of cultural interaction."

— [[Person – Edward Said]]

## Interpretation
- The terms 'West' and 'East' create a false dichotomy that ignores cultural nuances.
- Cultural interactions are multifaceted and cannot be reduced to simplistic labels.
- Understanding these complexities is crucial for a more accurate representation of global dynamics.
