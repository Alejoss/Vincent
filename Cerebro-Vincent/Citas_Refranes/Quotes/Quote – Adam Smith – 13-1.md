> To expect, indeed, that the freedom of trade should ever be established in any country, is to expect that the rulers should be wiser than the people.

— [[Person – Adam Smith]]

## Interpretation
- The establishment of free trade relies on the ability of rulers to make decisions that align with the people's interests.
- Smith suggests that rulers often lack the wisdom or insight to prioritize trade freedom, which reflects societal values.
- Expecting free trade without this wisdom implies a disconnect between leadership and the general populace.
