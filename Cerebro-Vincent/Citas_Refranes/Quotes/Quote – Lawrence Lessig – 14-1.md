> The fight for freedom is a fight for the future."

— [[Person – Lawrence Lessig]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom is not just about the present; it shapes what is to come.
- The struggle for liberty directly influences future generations and their opportunities.
- Engaging in the fight for freedom is essential for a progressive and just society.
