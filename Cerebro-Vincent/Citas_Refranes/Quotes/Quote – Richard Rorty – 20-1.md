> It is not enough to be right; we must also be persuasive.

— [[Person – Richard Rorty]]

## Interpretation
- Being correct in one's beliefs or arguments is insufficient for making an impact.
- Effective communication and the ability to persuade others are equally important.
- The interplay between truth and persuasion highlights the social nature of knowledge.
