> It is better to be a human being dissatisfied than a pig satisfied; better to be Socrates dissatisfied than a fool satisfied.

— [[Person – John Stuart Mill]]

## Interpretation
- The quote emphasizes the value of human experience and intellectual fulfillment over simple pleasure.
- It suggests that a deeper understanding of life, even if it leads to dissatisfaction, is preferable to ignorance and superficial happiness.
- The comparison between Socrates and a fool highlights the importance of wisdom and self-awareness in the pursuit of a meaningful life.
