> I have no patience with the idea that the masses are incapable of thinking for themselves.

— [[Person – Rosa Luxemburg]]

## Interpretation
- Luxemburg emphasizes the capacity of the masses to engage in critical thought.
- She challenges elitist views that underestimate the collective intelligence of people.
- The quote advocates for empowerment and self-determination among the working class.
