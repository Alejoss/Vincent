> We need to reclaim the idea of the commons as a space for collective action."

— [[Person – David Graeber]]

## Interpretation
- The concept of the commons emphasizes shared resources that belong to all members of a community.
- Reclaiming the commons suggests a need to revive collective responsibility and cooperation.
- Collective action is essential for addressing societal issues and fostering solidarity among individuals.
