> True freedom is not the absence of constraints, but the ability to choose one's own path.

— [[Person – Thomas Hill Green]]

## Interpretation
- True freedom involves having the autonomy to make choices rather than simply being free from restrictions.
- It emphasizes the importance of personal agency in defining one's life direction.
- Constraints can exist, but they do not negate the essence of freedom if one can still choose their path.
