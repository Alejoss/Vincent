> I would never die for my beliefs because I might be wrong.

— [[Person – Bertrand Russell]]

## Interpretation
- Russell expresses a skepticism about the certainty of beliefs.
- He emphasizes the importance of questioning and doubt in forming convictions.
- The quote suggests a prioritization of life and open-mindedness over dogmatic adherence to beliefs.
