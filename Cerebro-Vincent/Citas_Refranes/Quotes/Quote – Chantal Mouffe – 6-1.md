> Democracy is not a fixed state, but a process that is constantly being redefined."

— [[Person – Chantal Mouffe]]

## Interpretation
- Democracy evolves over time and is not static.
- The definition and practice of democracy are influenced by societal changes and struggles.
- Active participation and engagement are essential for democracy to thrive.
