> The only way to achieve true happiness is through the pursuit of knowledge.

— [[Person – William Godwin]]

## Interpretation
- True happiness is linked to intellectual growth and understanding.
- The pursuit of knowledge is portrayed as a lifelong journey that fulfills human potential.
- This quote suggests that superficial pleasures are insufficient for genuine contentment.
