> A wise man proportions his belief to the evidence.

— [[Person – David Hume]]

## Interpretation
- Beliefs should be based on the strength and quality of evidence available.
- A rational approach involves adjusting one's confidence in a belief according to the amount of supporting evidence.
- This perspective encourages critical thinking and skepticism towards unfounded claims.
