> There are two types of people in the world: those who have been hacked, and those who don’t know they’ve been hacked.

— [[Person – Bruce Schneier]]

## Interpretation
- Cybersecurity is a universal issue affecting everyone, regardless of their awareness.
- Many individuals and organizations may be compromised without their knowledge.
- The quote emphasizes the inevitability of hacking in the digital age.
