> Knowledge of the Orient is a way of dominating, restructuring, and having authority over the Orient."

— [[Person – Edward Said]]

## Interpretation
- The study of the Orient serves to reinforce power dynamics between the West and the East.
- Knowledge is used as a tool for control and manipulation of Eastern societies.
- This perspective highlights the constructed nature of cultural narratives that justify domination.
