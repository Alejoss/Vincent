> The only obligation which I have a right to assume is to do at any time what I think right.

— [[Person – Henry David Thoreau]]

## Interpretation
- Individuals have the autonomy to choose their own moral path.
- One's personal conscience should guide actions over societal expectations.
- Taking responsibility for one's actions is fundamental to personal integrity.
