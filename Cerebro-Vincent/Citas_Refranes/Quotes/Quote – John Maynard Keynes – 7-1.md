> The difficulty lies not so much in developing new ideas as in escaping from old ones.

— [[Person – John Maynard Keynes]]

## Interpretation
- Innovation often requires challenging established beliefs and paradigms.
- The resistance to change can hinder progress and the acceptance of new ideas.
- Overcoming ingrained mental models is a significant barrier to creativity and advancement.
