> The West has often had a distorted view of the East."

— [[Person – Edward Said]]

## Interpretation
- The West perceives the East through a lens of stereotypes and misconceptions.
- This distortion often leads to a lack of understanding and appreciation of Eastern cultures.
- Such views can perpetuate colonial attitudes and influence international relations.
