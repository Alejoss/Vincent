> The law of love could be best understood and learned through little children.

— [[Person – Leo Tolstoy]]

## Interpretation
- Children embody pure love, free from the complexities of adult relationships.
- Their simple and unconditional affection serves as a model for understanding love.
- Learning from children encourages adults to embrace compassion and authenticity in their interactions.
