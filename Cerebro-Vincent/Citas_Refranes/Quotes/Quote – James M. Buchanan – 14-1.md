> The notion of the public good is often used to justify actions that benefit a few at the expense of many."

— [[Person – James M. Buchanan]]

## Interpretation
- The concept of public good can be manipulated to serve the interests of a minority.
- Actions framed as benefiting society may actually prioritize specific groups.
- This highlights a potential conflict between collective welfare and individual interests.
