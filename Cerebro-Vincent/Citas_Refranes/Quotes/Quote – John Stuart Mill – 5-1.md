> A person may cause evil to others not only by his actions but by his inaction, and in either case he is justly accountable to them for the injury.

— [[Person – John Stuart Mill]]

## Interpretation
- Individuals have a moral responsibility for both their actions and their failure to act.
- Inaction can lead to harm, making one culpable for the consequences.
- Accountability extends beyond direct actions to include neglect and indifference.
