> The focus should be on what people are actually able to do and to be."

— [[Person – Amartya Sen]]

## Interpretation
- The quote emphasizes the importance of individual capabilities and freedoms.
- It suggests that true progress should be measured by what people can achieve in their lives.
- The focus shifts from mere economic metrics to human development and well-being.
