> The emancipation of the working class must be the act of the working class itself.

— [[Person – Rosa Luxemburg]]

## Interpretation
- The working class must take initiative in their own liberation rather than relying on external forces.
- True emancipation is an active, self-driven process rather than a passive one.
- The quote emphasizes the importance of self-agency and collective action within the working class.
