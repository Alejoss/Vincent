> The greatest human freedom is the freedom to choose one's own path.

— [[Person – Immanuel Kant]]

## Interpretation
- True freedom is found in the ability to make personal choices.
- Choosing one's own path reflects individual autonomy and self-determination.
- This freedom is essential for personal growth and fulfillment.
