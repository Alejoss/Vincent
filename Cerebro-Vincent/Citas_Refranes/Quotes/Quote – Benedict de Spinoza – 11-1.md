> The power of the mind is such that it can be used to understand nature, which is the ultimate source of our well-being.

— [[Person – Benedict de Spinoza]]

## Interpretation
- The mind has the capacity to comprehend the natural world.
- Understanding nature leads to a deeper sense of well-being.
- Knowledge and reason are crucial for achieving harmony with our environment.
