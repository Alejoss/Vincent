> We must be prepared to see that there are many different ways of being human.

— [[Person – Isaiah Berlin]]

## Interpretation
- Humanity is diverse and complex, encompassing various experiences and perspectives.
- Understanding this diversity is essential for fostering empathy and tolerance.
- Recognizing multiple ways of being human challenges a singular or absolute view of existence.
