> The task of the modern educator is not to cut down jungles, but to irrigate deserts.

— [[Person – Clive Staples Lewis]]

## Interpretation
- Educators should focus on fostering potential rather than merely removing obstacles.
- Irrigating deserts symbolizes nurturing and cultivating knowledge in barren areas.
- The quote emphasizes the importance of inspiring creativity and critical thinking in learners.
