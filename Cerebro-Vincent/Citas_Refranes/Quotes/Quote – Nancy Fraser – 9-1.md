> The politics of recognition cannot stand alone; it must be accompanied by a commitment to redistribution."

— [[Person – Nancy Fraser]]

## Interpretation
- Recognition of marginalized groups is essential but insufficient for true equality.
- Redistribution of resources and power is necessary to address systemic inequalities.
- Both recognition and redistribution must work together to achieve social justice.
