> The ultimate goal of the war on terror is to maintain a state of perpetual war.

— [[Person – Noam Chomsky]]

## Interpretation
- The phrase suggests that the war on terror is not genuinely aimed at achieving peace or security.
- It implies that ongoing conflict serves political or economic interests.
- The concept of 'perpetual war' raises concerns about civil liberties and government overreach.
