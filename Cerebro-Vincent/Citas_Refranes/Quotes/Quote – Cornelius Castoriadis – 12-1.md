> Real freedom involves the ability to reflect on our desires and to choose among them."

— [[Person – Cornelius Castoriadis]]

## Interpretation
- Real freedom is not merely the absence of constraints, but the presence of self-reflective choice.
- To be truly free, individuals must engage in critical thinking about their own desires.
- The ability to choose among desires indicates a deeper level of autonomy and personal agency.
