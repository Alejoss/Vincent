> The goal of security is to make it hard for an attacker to succeed.

— [[Person – Bruce Schneier]]

## Interpretation
- Security measures are designed to increase the difficulty of successful attacks.
- The emphasis is on proactive defense rather than reactive response.
- Success in security is measured by the resilience against potential threats.
