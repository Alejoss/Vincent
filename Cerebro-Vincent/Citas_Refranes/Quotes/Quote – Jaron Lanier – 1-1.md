> You are not a gadget.

— [[Person – Jaron Lanier]]

## Interpretation
- Human identity should not be reduced to mere functionality or utility.
- Individuals possess intrinsic value that transcends technological definitions.
- Recognizing our humanity is essential in an age dominated by technology.
