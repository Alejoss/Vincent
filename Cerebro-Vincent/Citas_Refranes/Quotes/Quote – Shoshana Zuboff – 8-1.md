> The Internet of Things is not just about devices; it's about the invisible architecture of our lives."

— [[Person – Shoshana Zuboff]]

## Interpretation
- The Internet of Things (IoT) extends beyond physical devices to influence how we live our daily lives.
- There is an underlying framework of connectivity and data exchange that shapes our experiences and interactions.
- Understanding IoT requires recognizing its impact on our social and personal environments, not just its technological aspects.
