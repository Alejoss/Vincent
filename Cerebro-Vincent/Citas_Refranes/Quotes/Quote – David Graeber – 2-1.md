> The idea that we should be working all the time is a new one that emerged in the 19th century."

— [[Person – David Graeber]]

## Interpretation
- The notion of constant work is a relatively modern social construct.
- This idea reflects the economic and cultural shifts that occurred during the Industrial Revolution.
- It suggests that societal values around labor are not universal or timeless, but rather historically contingent.
