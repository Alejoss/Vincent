> What is needed is an electronic payment system based on cryptographic proof instead of trust.

— [[Person – Satoshi Nakamoto]]

## Interpretation
- The quote emphasizes the importance of security and verification in digital transactions.
- It suggests a shift from traditional trust-based systems to ones reliant on cryptographic technology.
- The statement highlights the need for a decentralized approach to financial transactions.
