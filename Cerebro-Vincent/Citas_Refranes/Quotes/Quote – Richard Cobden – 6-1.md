> The principle of free trade is a principle of peace."

— [[Person – Richard Cobden]]

## Interpretation
- Free trade fosters mutual dependence among nations, reducing the likelihood of conflict.
- Economic cooperation through trade encourages dialogue and understanding between countries.
- The promotion of free trade can lead to a more stable and peaceful international environment.
