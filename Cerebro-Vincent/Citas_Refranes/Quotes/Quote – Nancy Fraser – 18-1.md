> Our understanding of justice must be inclusive, encompassing diverse experiences and histories."

— [[Person – Nancy Fraser]]

## Interpretation
- Justice should reflect the varied experiences of all individuals in society.
- An inclusive approach to justice acknowledges the impact of different historical contexts.
- Recognizing diversity is essential to achieving a fair and equitable understanding of justice.
