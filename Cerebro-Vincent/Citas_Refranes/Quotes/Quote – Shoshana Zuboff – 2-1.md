> Surveillance capitalism is a new economic order that claims human experience as free raw material for hidden commercial practices."

— [[Person – Shoshana Zuboff]]

## Interpretation
- Surveillance capitalism monetizes personal data without individuals' awareness or consent.
- It transforms human experiences into commodities for profit, often prioritizing corporate interests over individual rights.
- This economic model operates secretly, obscuring the true nature of its practices from those it affects.
