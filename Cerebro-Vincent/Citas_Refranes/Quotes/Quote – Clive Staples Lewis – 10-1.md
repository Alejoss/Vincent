> If you want a thing done well, do it yourself.

— [[Person – Clive Staples Lewis]]

## Interpretation
- Reliance on personal effort often guarantees better quality results.
- Delegating tasks may lead to unsatisfactory outcomes due to varying standards.
- Self-sufficiency emphasizes the importance of individual responsibility.
