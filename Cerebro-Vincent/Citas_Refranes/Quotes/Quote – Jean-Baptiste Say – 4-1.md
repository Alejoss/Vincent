> Supply creates its own demand."

— [[Person – Jean-Baptiste Say]]

## Interpretation
- Producers create goods and services with the expectation that consumers will purchase them.
- As supply increases, it encourages consumption and stimulates economic activity.
- The concept suggests that creating products can lead to an increase in overall demand in the market.
