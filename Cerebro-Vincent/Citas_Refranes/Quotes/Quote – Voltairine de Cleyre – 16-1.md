> Education is not a preparation for life; education is life itself."

— [[Person – Voltairine de Cleyre]]

## Interpretation
- Education is an ongoing process that encompasses all experiences in life.
- It emphasizes the idea that learning happens continuously rather than being confined to formal schooling.
- This perspective challenges traditional views that see education merely as a means to an end.
