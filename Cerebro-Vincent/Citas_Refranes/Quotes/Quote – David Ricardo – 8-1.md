> A country’s wealth is measured by the quantity of labor it can command.

— [[Person – David Ricardo]]

## Interpretation
- Wealth is fundamentally tied to the productive capacity of labor.
- The ability to command labor reflects a country's economic strength and efficiency.
- Labor quantity is a key indicator of overall economic prosperity.
