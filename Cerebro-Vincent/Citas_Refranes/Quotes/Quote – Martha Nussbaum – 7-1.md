> The capability approach is not just an economic theory; it is a way of thinking about human dignity."

— [[Person – Martha Nussbaum]]

## Interpretation
- The capability approach emphasizes the importance of human potential beyond mere economic metrics.
- It advocates for a holistic understanding of dignity that includes individual freedoms and opportunities.
- This perspective shifts the focus from wealth to what individuals can actually achieve in their lives.
