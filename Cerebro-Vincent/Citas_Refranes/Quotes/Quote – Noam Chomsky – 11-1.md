> It is the responsibility of intellectuals to speak the truth and expose lies.

— [[Person – Noam Chomsky]]

## Interpretation
- Intellectuals have a moral obligation to seek and communicate the truth.
- They must challenge and reveal falsehoods that can mislead society.
- This role is crucial for promoting informed discourse and accountability.
