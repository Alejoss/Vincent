> Slavery is not a condition of the body, but a condition of the spirit."

— [[Person – Lysander Spooner]]

## Interpretation
- Slavery transcends physical confinement and instead affects one's inner sense of freedom and autonomy.
- True bondage lies in the mental and emotional constraints imposed by society and oneself.
- A person's spirit can remain enslaved even when their body is free, highlighting the importance of mental liberation.
