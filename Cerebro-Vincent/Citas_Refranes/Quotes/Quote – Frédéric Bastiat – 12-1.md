> The science of economics is the study of human action.

— [[Person – Frédéric Bastiat]]

## Interpretation
- Economics fundamentally examines the choices individuals make in their daily lives.
- Human actions are driven by needs, desires, and the constraints they face.
- Understanding economics requires analyzing the motivations and consequences of these actions.
