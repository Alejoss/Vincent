> Free trade is a great moral revolution."

— [[Person – Richard Cobden]]

## Interpretation
- Free trade promotes fairness and equality among nations.
- It encourages cooperation and understanding by breaking down economic barriers.
- The moral implications of free trade suggest that it leads to social progress and human welfare.
