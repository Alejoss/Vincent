> The most important thing is to understand that security is a process, not a product."

— [[Person – Whitfield Diffie]]

## Interpretation
- Security requires ongoing effort and adaptation rather than relying on a single solution.
- Understanding security as a continuous journey emphasizes vigilance and proactive measures.
- It highlights the importance of processes and practices over tangible products in maintaining safety.
