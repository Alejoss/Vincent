> Those who have the ability to control information will have the power."

— [[Person – Timothy C. May]]

## Interpretation
- Control over information equates to influence and authority.
- Power dynamics in society are increasingly dictated by information management.
- Access to and manipulation of information can determine social and political outcomes.
