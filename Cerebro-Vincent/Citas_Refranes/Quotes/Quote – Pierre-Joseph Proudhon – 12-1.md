> The state is the great fiction by which everyone seeks to live at the expense of everyone else.

— [[Person – Pierre-Joseph Proudhon]]

## Interpretation
- Proudhon critiques the state as a construct that enables exploitation among individuals.
- He suggests that the state creates a dependency where people benefit at the cost of others.
- This statement reflects a fundamental skepticism towards centralized authority and its impact on social relations.
