> Every genuine test of a theory is an attempt to falsify it, or to refute it.

— [[Person – Karl Popper]]

## Interpretation
- The essence of scientific inquiry lies in its ability to challenge and disprove theories.
- Falsifiability is a key criterion for determining the scientific validity of a theory.
- Testing a theory involves actively seeking evidence that contradicts it, rather than merely supporting it.
