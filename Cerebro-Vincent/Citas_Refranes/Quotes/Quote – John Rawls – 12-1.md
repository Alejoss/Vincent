> Political liberalism is a political conception of justice that aims to be acceptable to people with diverse and conflicting comprehensive doctrines.

— [[Person – John Rawls]]

## Interpretation
- Political liberalism seeks a framework of justice that includes various perspectives.
- It acknowledges the existence of diverse moral and philosophical beliefs in society.
- The goal is to create a fair political structure that all citizens can accept despite their differences.
