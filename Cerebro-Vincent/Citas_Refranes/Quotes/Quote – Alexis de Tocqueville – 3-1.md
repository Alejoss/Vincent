> The American Republic will endure until the day Congress discovers that it can bribe the public with the public's money.

— [[Person – Alexis de Tocqueville]]

## Interpretation
- The quote suggests that the longevity of the American Republic is contingent upon the integrity of its political system.
- It warns against the potential for corruption when elected officials exploit public resources for personal or political gain.
- The phrase highlights the dangers of populism and the risk of undermining democratic principles through financial manipulation.
