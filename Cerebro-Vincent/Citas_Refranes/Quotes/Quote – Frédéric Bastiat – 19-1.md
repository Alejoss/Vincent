> The true libertarian is one who believes that the only legitimate function of government is to protect the rights of individuals.

— [[Person – Frédéric Bastiat]]

## Interpretation
- A true libertarian advocates for minimal government intervention.
- The primary role of government should be to safeguard individual rights.
- This perspective emphasizes personal freedom and autonomy over collective control.
