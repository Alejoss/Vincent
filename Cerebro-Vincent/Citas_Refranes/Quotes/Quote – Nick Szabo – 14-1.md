> In the digital age, the concept of ownership is evolving."

— [[Person – Nick Szabo]]

## Interpretation
- Ownership is becoming more fluid and less tied to physical objects.
- Digital assets challenge traditional notions of property rights.
- The evolving landscape of technology influences how we perceive and enforce ownership.
