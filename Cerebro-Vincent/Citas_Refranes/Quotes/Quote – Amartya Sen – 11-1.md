> A person’s capability to function is a good measure of their well-being."

— [[Person – Amartya Sen]]

## Interpretation
- Well-being is intrinsically linked to an individual's ability to perform various functions in life.
- Capability encompasses both physical and mental aspects, reflecting a holistic view of health and happiness.
- Measuring well-being through capability shifts the focus from mere economic indicators to personal fulfillment and agency.
