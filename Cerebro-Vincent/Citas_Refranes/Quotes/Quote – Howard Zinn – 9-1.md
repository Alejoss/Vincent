> The idea is not to be a historian, but to be a human being.

— [[Person – Howard Zinn]]

## Interpretation
- Being a historian should not overshadow one's humanity.
- Understanding historical events requires empathy and a connection to human experiences.
- The focus should be on the impact of history on people's lives rather than just facts and dates.
