> The true test of a civilization is the way it treats its weakest members.

— [[Person – Albert Jay Nock]]

## Interpretation
- A civilization's moral character is revealed by its compassion towards the vulnerable.
- The treatment of the weakest members reflects the overall values and priorities of society.
- True progress is measured not just by wealth or power, but by the support offered to those in need.
