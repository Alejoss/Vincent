> You can’t be neutral on a moving train.

— [[Person – Howard Zinn]]

## Interpretation
- Indecision in the face of urgent social change equates to complicity.
- One must take a stance on issues of justice and progress, as neutrality is not an option.
- Active participation is necessary to create meaningful change in society.
