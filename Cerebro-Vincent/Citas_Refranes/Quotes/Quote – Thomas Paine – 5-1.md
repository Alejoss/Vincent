> The mind once enlightened cannot again become dark.

— [[Person – Thomas Paine]]

## Interpretation
- Once a person gains knowledge or understanding, they cannot unlearn or forget that insight.
- Enlightenment represents a permanent shift in perspective that influences future thoughts and actions.
- The quote suggests the transformative power of knowledge in shaping one’s worldview.
