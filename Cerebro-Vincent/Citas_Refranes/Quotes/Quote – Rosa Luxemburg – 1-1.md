> Freedom is always and exclusively freedom for the one who thinks differently.

— [[Person – Rosa Luxemburg]]

## Interpretation
- True freedom cannot exist without the protection of diverse opinions.
- Liberation is not just for the majority or those in power, but must extend to dissenters.
- The essence of a democratic society is the ability to accept and protect differing viewpoints.
