> There is no such thing as a view from nowhere.

— [[Person – Richard Rorty]]

## Interpretation
- All perspectives are shaped by individual experiences and contexts.
- Objective viewpoints are a myth; everyone perceives reality through their unique lens.
- Understanding is inherently subjective and influenced by cultural, historical, and personal factors.
