> The only way to safeguard freedom is to take it for yourself and to take it for others.

— [[Person – Julian Paul Assange]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom is an active pursuit that requires personal initiative.
- Safeguarding freedom involves collective responsibility and support for others.
- True liberty cannot be passively accepted; it must be claimed and defended.
