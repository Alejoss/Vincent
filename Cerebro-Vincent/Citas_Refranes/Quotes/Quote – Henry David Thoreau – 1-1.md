> The mass of men lead lives of quiet desperation.

— [[Person – Henry David Thoreau]]

## Interpretation
- Many people conform to societal expectations, sacrificing their true desires and aspirations.
- This state of existence reflects a profound sense of dissatisfaction and unfulfillment.
- The phrase suggests that individuals often suffer silently, trapped in routines that stifle their potential.
