> Always act in such a way that you treat humanity, whether in your own person or in the person of any other, never merely as a means to an end, but always at the same time as an end.

— [[Person – Immanuel Kant]]

## Interpretation
- Human beings should be respected for their intrinsic value.
- Using others solely for personal gain undermines their dignity.
- Moral actions must consider the humanity of all individuals involved.
