> No man's knowledge here can go beyond his experience.

— [[Person – John Locke]]

## Interpretation
- Knowledge is fundamentally derived from personal experiences.
- One's understanding of the world is limited to what they have encountered.
- The acquisition of knowledge is a process that relies on lived experiences.
