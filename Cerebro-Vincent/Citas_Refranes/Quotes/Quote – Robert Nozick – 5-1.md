> The minimal state is the most extensive state that can be justified.

— [[Person – Robert Nozick]]

## Interpretation
- The minimal state represents the least amount of government intervention necessary to maintain order.
- It implies that any expansion beyond this point lacks sufficient justification.
- This perspective prioritizes individual autonomy and limits state power.
