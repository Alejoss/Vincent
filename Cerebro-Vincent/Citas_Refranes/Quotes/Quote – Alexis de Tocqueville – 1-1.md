> The health of a democratic society may be measured by the quality of functions performed by private citizens.

— [[Person – Alexis de Tocqueville]]

## Interpretation
- A strong democratic society relies on the active participation of its citizens.
- The effectiveness of individuals in fulfilling civic duties reflects the overall health of democracy.
- Citizen engagement is crucial for the functioning and vitality of democratic institutions.
