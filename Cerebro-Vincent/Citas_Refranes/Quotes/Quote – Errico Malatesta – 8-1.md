> True freedom can only be achieved through the abolition of all forms of authority.

— [[Person – Errico Malatesta]]

## Interpretation
- True freedom is contingent upon the removal of oppressive power structures.
- The quote emphasizes a radical approach to achieving autonomy and self-governance.
- Malatesta argues that any form of authority inherently limits individual liberty.
