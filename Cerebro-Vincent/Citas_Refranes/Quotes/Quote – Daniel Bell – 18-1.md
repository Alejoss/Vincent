> Knowledge and information are the new capital of the 21st century."

— [[Person – Daniel Bell]]

## Interpretation
- In the modern economy, knowledge and information drive innovation and productivity.
- Unlike traditional capital, knowledge is intangible and can be shared and multiplied more easily.
- The value of human capital has increased, emphasizing the importance of education and expertise.
