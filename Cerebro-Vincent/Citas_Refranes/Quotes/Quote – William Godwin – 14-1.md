> There is no greater tyranny than that which is perpetrated under the shield of the law.

— [[Person – William Godwin]]

## Interpretation
- Legal systems can be misused to justify oppressive actions.
- Tyranny can be disguised as lawful governance, leading to a false sense of security.
- The quote warns against blind faith in authority when it is backed by legal frameworks.
