> The fundamental problem is that you can’t have an unbreakable encryption system and still have a way to get to the information in the system."

— [[Person – Whitfield Diffie]]

## Interpretation
- Unbreakable encryption ensures data security but eliminates access for external parties.
- The paradox highlights the conflict between privacy and accessibility in encryption technology.
- It suggests a fundamental trade-off between security and the ability to retrieve information.
