> I am the only one who can decide what I am.

— [[Person – Max Stirner]]

## Interpretation
- The individual possesses the ultimate authority over their identity.
- Personal autonomy is paramount in defining oneself.
- Societal expectations and norms should not dictate one's self-conception.
