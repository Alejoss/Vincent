> The ability to communicate freely is the foundation of a free society."

— [[Person – Timothy C. May]]

## Interpretation
- Free communication is essential for the exchange of ideas and opinions.
- A society that restricts communication undermines its own freedom and democracy.
- Open dialogue fosters trust and understanding among individuals in a community.
