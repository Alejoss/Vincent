> Justice is the first virtue of social institutions, as truth is of systems of thought.

— [[Person – John Rawls]]

## Interpretation
- Justice serves as the foundational principle for evaluating social institutions.
- Just as truth is essential for coherent and valid systems of thought, justice is crucial for the legitimacy of societal structures.
- The quote emphasizes the primacy of ethical considerations in organizing society.
