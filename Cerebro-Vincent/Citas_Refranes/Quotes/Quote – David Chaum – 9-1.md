> Anonymity can be a tool for social change and justice."

— [[Person – David Chaum]]

## Interpretation
- Anonymity allows individuals to speak freely without fear of retribution.
- It can empower marginalized voices to advocate for their rights.
- Anonymity can facilitate collective action in the pursuit of social justice.
