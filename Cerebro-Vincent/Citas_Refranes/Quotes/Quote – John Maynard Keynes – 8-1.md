> The social object of skilled investment should be to defeat the dark forces of time and ignorance which envelope our future.

— [[Person – John Maynard Keynes]]

## Interpretation
- Skilled investment aims to navigate uncertainties that may hinder future progress.
- The 'dark forces' represent the challenges posed by time and lack of knowledge.
- Investing wisely can illuminate paths to a better future despite inherent risks.
