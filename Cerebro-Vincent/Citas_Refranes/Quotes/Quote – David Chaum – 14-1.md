> Cryptography is the foundation for a new era of trust."

— [[Person – David Chaum]]

## Interpretation
- Cryptography provides secure communication, enhancing confidence among individuals and organizations.
- Trust, traditionally built through intermediaries, can now be established through technological means.
- This shift signifies a potential transformation in how society interacts and transacts.
