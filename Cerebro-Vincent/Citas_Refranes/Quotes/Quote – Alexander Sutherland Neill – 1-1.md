> The child is not a vessel to be filled, but a fire to be kindled.

— [[Person – Alexander Sutherland Neill]]

## Interpretation
- Children should be encouraged to explore their passions and interests.
- Education should focus on igniting curiosity rather than rote memorization.
- The potential within each child should be nurtured to flourish freely.
