> The state is justified only if it can be shown to be necessary to the achievement of some end that individuals cannot achieve on their own.

— [[Person – Robert Nozick]]

## Interpretation
- The legitimacy of the state hinges on its necessity for collective goals.
- Individual capabilities must be insufficient for the state to be justified.
- The state's role is conditional upon its ability to facilitate outcomes unattainable by individuals alone.
