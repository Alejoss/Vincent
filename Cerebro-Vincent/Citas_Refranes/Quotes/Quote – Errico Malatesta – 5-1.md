> Freedom is not a gift to be bestowed, but a right to be claimed.

— [[Person – Errico Malatesta]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom is inherently possessed by individuals rather than something granted by authorities.
- It emphasizes the active role of individuals in asserting their rights.
- The quote suggests that true freedom requires effort and struggle to obtain.
