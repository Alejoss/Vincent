> The most important thing in technology is the people who use it.

— [[Person – Cory Doctorow]]

## Interpretation
- Technology is ultimately shaped by human needs and behaviors.
- The effectiveness of technology is measured by how well it serves its users.
- People's choices and interactions define the impact of technological advancements.
