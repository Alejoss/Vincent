> A feminist perspective can enrich our understanding of justice.

— [[Person – Susan Moller Okin]]

## Interpretation
- A feminist perspective challenges traditional notions of justice by highlighting gender inequalities.
- It brings attention to the ways in which justice can be experienced differently based on gender.
- Incorporating feminist views can lead to a more inclusive and equitable understanding of justice.
