> Mutual aid is as much a law of life as mutual struggle.

— [[Person – Peter Kropotkin]]

## Interpretation
- Kropotkin emphasizes that cooperation is as fundamental to existence as competition.
- The quote suggests that social bonds and mutual support are essential for survival and success.
- It challenges the notion that struggle is the only driving force in nature, highlighting the importance of collaboration.
