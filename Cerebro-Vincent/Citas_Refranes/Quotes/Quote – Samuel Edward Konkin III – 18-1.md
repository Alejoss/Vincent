> Self-ownership is the basis of all rights."

— [[Person – Samuel Edward Konkin III]]

## Interpretation
- Self-ownership asserts that individuals have control over their own bodies and lives.
- All rights, including personal and property rights, stem from the fundamental principle of self-ownership.
- Denial of self-ownership undermines the foundation of personal freedom and autonomy.
