> It is the duty of the patriot to protect his country from its government.

— [[Person – Thomas Paine]]

## Interpretation
- Patriotism involves vigilance against government overreach.
- Citizens have a responsibility to hold their government accountable.
- True loyalty may sometimes require opposition to government actions.
