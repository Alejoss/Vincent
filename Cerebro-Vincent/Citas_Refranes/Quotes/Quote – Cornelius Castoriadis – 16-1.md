> A true democracy is one in which the people are actively engaged in decision-making."

— [[Person – Cornelius Castoriadis]]

## Interpretation
- True democracy requires active participation from citizens.
- Decision-making should not be left solely to representatives but involve the populace.
- Engagement in governance empowers individuals and fosters a sense of community.
