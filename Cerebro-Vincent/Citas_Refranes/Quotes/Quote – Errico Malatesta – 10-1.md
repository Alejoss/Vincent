> We must create the conditions for a new society that reflects our values of equality and justice.

— [[Person – Errico Malatesta]]

## Interpretation
- The quote emphasizes the proactive role individuals must take in shaping society.
- It stresses the importance of aligning societal structures with the values of equality and justice.
- Creating new conditions suggests a transformative approach rather than passive acceptance of the status quo.
