> Real change requires not just shifting policies but transforming the very frameworks through which we understand justice."

— [[Person – Nancy Fraser]]

## Interpretation
- Real change goes beyond surface-level policy adjustments.
- It requires a fundamental shift in our conceptual understanding of justice.
- Transforming frameworks is essential for achieving lasting and meaningful change.
