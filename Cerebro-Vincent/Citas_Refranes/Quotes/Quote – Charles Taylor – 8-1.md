> We are all human beings, deserving of dignity and respect, regardless of our differences."

— [[Person – Charles Taylor]]

## Interpretation
- All individuals possess inherent worth and value.
- Diversity should not diminish one's entitlement to respect.
- Recognizing our shared humanity fosters a more inclusive society.
