> The family should be a site of equality, not oppression.

— [[Person – Susan Moller Okin]]

## Interpretation
- The family environment should promote equal rights and opportunities for all members.
- Oppression within the family undermines the potential for healthy relationships and personal growth.
- Equality in familial structures can lead to a more just and equitable society.
