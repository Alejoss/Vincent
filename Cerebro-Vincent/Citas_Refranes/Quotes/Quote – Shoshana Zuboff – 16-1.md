> The challenge is to find a way to protect both privacy and innovation."

— [[Person – Shoshana Zuboff]]

## Interpretation
- Balancing privacy concerns with the need for technological advancement is crucial.
- Innovation should not come at the expense of individual rights and privacy.
- Finding solutions that safeguard privacy can foster a more ethical approach to innovation.
