> The revolution is not an event; it is a process.

— [[Person – Rosa Luxemburg]]

## Interpretation
- Revolution is an ongoing journey rather than a singular moment of change.
- It involves continuous struggle and transformation within society.
- Understanding revolution as a process emphasizes the importance of sustained activism and engagement.
