> The most important aspect of a man is his character.

— [[Person – Albert Jay Nock]]

## Interpretation
- A person's true worth is determined by their moral and ethical qualities.
- Character shapes an individual's decisions and actions in life.
- External achievements are secondary to the integrity and values one holds.
