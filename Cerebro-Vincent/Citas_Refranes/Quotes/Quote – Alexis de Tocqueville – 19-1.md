> The chief characteristic of democracy is the equality of conditions.

— [[Person – Alexis de Tocqueville]]

## Interpretation
- Democracy promotes a society where individuals have equal rights and opportunities.
- The concept of equality in democracy challenges traditional hierarchies and privileges.
- This equality fosters a sense of community and shared purpose among citizens.
