> Over himself, over his own body and mind, the individual is sovereign.

— [[Person – John Stuart Mill]]

## Interpretation
- The individual has ultimate control over their personal choices and actions.
- Self-sovereignty emphasizes the importance of personal autonomy and freedom.
- This idea supports the notion that individual rights should be respected and protected from external interference.
