> Individuals must be free to pursue their own conception of the good life, as long as they do not infringe upon the rights of others.

— [[Person – Robert Nozick]]

## Interpretation
- Individuals have the autonomy to define their own values and life goals.
- Personal freedom is contingent upon respecting the rights of others.
- This quote emphasizes a balance between individual liberty and social responsibility.
