> The great source of both the revenue and the employment of the country is the labor of its people.

— [[Person – Adam Smith]]

## Interpretation
- The productivity of a nation's workforce is fundamental to its economic prosperity.
- Employment opportunities are directly linked to the effective utilization of labor.
- The labor of individuals is a critical driver of both government revenue and overall economic health.
