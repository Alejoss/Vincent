> To be governed is to be at every operation of our lives directed by the will of another."

— [[Person – Voltairine de Cleyre]]

## Interpretation
- Governance implies a loss of autonomy in our daily decisions.
- Every action we take can be influenced or dictated by external authority.
- True freedom is hindered when we are subject to the will of others.
