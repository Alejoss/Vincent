> Man is born free, and everywhere he is in chains.

— [[Person – Jean-Jacques Rousseau]]

## Interpretation
- Human beings are inherently free at birth, possessing natural rights and autonomy.
- Societal structures, laws, and conventions impose restrictions that limit individual freedom.
- The contrast highlights the conflict between natural liberty and the constraints of civilization.
