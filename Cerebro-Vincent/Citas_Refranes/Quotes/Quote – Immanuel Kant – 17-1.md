> If you want to be a philosopher, you must learn to think for yourself.

— [[Person – Immanuel Kant]]

## Interpretation
- Philosophy requires independent thought and critical analysis.
- Relying on others' ideas without scrutiny hinders true understanding.
- Genuine philosophical inquiry is rooted in personal reasoning and reflection.
