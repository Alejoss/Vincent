> We need to be vigilant about the rights we have and the rights we are willing to give up.

— [[Person – Cory Doctorow]]

## Interpretation
- It emphasizes the importance of awareness regarding our existing rights.
- It warns against complacency in surrendering rights for convenience or perceived security.
- It calls for active engagement and defense of personal freedoms.
