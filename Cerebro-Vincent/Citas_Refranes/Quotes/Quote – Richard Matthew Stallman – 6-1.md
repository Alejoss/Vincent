> The free software movement is a grassroots movement.

— [[Person – Richard Matthew Stallman]]

## Interpretation
- The free software movement originates from individual users advocating for their rights and freedoms.
- It emphasizes community involvement and collaboration rather than top-down control.
- Grassroots movements rely on collective action and shared values among participants.
