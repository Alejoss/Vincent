> Freedom and responsibility are two sides of the same coin."

— [[Person – Charles Murray]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom inherently comes with the obligation to act responsibly.
- One cannot fully enjoy freedom without acknowledging the consequences of their choices.
- The balance between freedom and responsibility is essential for a functioning society.
