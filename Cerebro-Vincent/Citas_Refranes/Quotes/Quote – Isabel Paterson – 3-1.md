> The only way to deal with the future is to make it the present."

— [[Person – Isabel Paterson]]

## Interpretation
- Taking proactive steps today shapes the future we desire.
- Living in the present allows us to influence potential outcomes.
- The future is not a distant concept but something we can actively create now.
