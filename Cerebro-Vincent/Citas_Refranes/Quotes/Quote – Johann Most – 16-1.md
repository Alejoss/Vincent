> The individual is greater than the state."

— [[Person – Johann Most]]

## Interpretation
- The quote emphasizes the primacy of personal autonomy over governmental authority.
- It suggests that individual rights and freedoms should not be subordinated to the needs of the state.
- This perspective advocates for the value of personal identity and self-determination in society.
