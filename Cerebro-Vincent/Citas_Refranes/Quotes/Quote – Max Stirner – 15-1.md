> My existence is my own.

— [[Person – Max Stirner]]

## Interpretation
- The quote emphasizes individual sovereignty and personal autonomy.
- It asserts that one's life and choices belong solely to oneself, free from external control.
- This reflects Stirner's philosophy of egoism, prioritizing self-interest and self-ownership.
