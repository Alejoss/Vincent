> Freedom of thought is the most important freedom of all.

— [[Person – William Godwin]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom of thought enables individuals to form their own beliefs and ideas.
- It is foundational for personal autonomy and the pursuit of knowledge.
- Without freedom of thought, other freedoms become meaningless or suppressed.
