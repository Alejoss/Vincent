> The true measure of development is the improvement in people's lives."

— [[Person – Peter Bauer]]

## Interpretation
- Development should focus on the well-being and quality of life of individuals.
- Economic growth and infrastructure improvements are secondary to actual improvements in people's daily experiences.
- True progress is measured by tangible changes in health, education, and happiness of the population.
