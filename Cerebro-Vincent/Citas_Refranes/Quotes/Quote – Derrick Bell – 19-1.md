> Activism is not a choice; it is a necessity for those who care about justice."

— [[Person – Derrick Bell]]

## Interpretation
- Activism is essential for achieving justice and cannot be avoided by those who are concerned.
- It emphasizes a moral obligation to act against injustice rather than remaining passive.
- The quote suggests that caring about justice inherently involves taking action.
