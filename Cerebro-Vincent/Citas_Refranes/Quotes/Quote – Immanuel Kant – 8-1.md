> Two things fill the mind with ever-increasing wonder and awe, the starry heavens above and the moral law within.

— [[Person – Immanuel Kant]]

## Interpretation
- The vastness of the universe inspires a sense of wonder about existence and our place in it.
- The moral law within refers to the innate sense of right and wrong that guides human behavior.
- Together, these elements highlight the dual nature of human experience: the external and the internal.
