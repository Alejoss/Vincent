> The general tendency of things throughout the world is to render mediocrity the ascendant power among mankind.

— [[Person – John Stuart Mill]]

## Interpretation
- Mediocrity tends to dominate society, overshadowing exceptional talent.
- The forces of conformity and social norms often suppress individuality and innovation.
- This tendency reflects a broader commentary on the challenges faced by those striving for excellence.
