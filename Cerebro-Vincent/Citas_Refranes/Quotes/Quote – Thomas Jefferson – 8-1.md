> In matters of style, swim with the current; in matters of principle, stand like a rock.

— [[Person – Thomas Jefferson]]

## Interpretation
- Flexibility in personal expression and style is important to adapt to changing circumstances.
- Core beliefs and principles should remain unwavering and steadfast, regardless of external pressures.
- Finding a balance between adaptability and integrity is crucial for personal and ethical growth.
