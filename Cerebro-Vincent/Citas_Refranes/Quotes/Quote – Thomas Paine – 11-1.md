> To argue with a person who has renounced the use of reason is like administering medicine to the dead.

— [[Person – Thomas Paine]]

## Interpretation
- Engaging with someone who rejects rational thought is futile.
- Reason is essential for meaningful dialogue and understanding.
- Attempts to persuade the unreasonable are as ineffective as treating the dead.
