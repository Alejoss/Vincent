> The only way to achieve true equality is to abolish all forms of oppression."

— [[Person – Voltairine de Cleyre]]

## Interpretation
- True equality cannot exist where any form of oppression persists.
- Abolishing oppression is a necessary step toward achieving fairness and justice for all.
- The quote emphasizes the interconnectedness of various forms of oppression in the struggle for equality.
