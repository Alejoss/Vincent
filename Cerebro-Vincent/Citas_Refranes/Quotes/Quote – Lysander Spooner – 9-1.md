> Vices are not crimes."

— [[Person – Lysander Spooner]]

## Interpretation
- Spooner distinguishes between immoral behaviors (vices) and actions that violate legal statutes (crimes).
- He argues that personal choices or habits should not be subjected to legal punishment unless they harm others.
- This perspective promotes individual liberty and challenges the role of the state in regulating personal morality.
