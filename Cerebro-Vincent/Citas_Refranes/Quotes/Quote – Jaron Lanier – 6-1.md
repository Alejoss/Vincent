> The world is a much more complex place than the simple models that we create for it.

— [[Person – Jaron Lanier]]

## Interpretation
- Our understanding of reality is often oversimplified.
- Complex systems cannot be fully captured by straightforward models.
- This highlights the limitations of human knowledge and prediction.
