> The structure of a language influences the way people think.

— [[Person – Noam Chomsky]]

## Interpretation
- Language shapes cognitive processes and perceptions.
- The way we express ideas can limit or expand our understanding.
- Cultural and linguistic diversity leads to varied ways of thinking.
