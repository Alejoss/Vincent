> The political is not a sphere separate from the social, but rather a dimension of social relations."

— [[Person – Chantal Mouffe]]

## Interpretation
- The political is intertwined with everyday social interactions and cannot be viewed in isolation.
- Power dynamics and conflicts are inherent in all social relations, making the political a pervasive aspect of life.
- Understanding politics requires recognizing its roots in social contexts and relationships.
