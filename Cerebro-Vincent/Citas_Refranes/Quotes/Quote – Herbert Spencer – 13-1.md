> The true test of civilization is not the census, nor the size of the cities, nor the crops, but the kind of man the country turns out.

— [[Person – Herbert Spencer]]

## Interpretation
- Civilization is measured by the quality of individuals rather than mere statistics.
- The development of a society is reflected in the character and values of its people.
- Material achievements do not equate to true progress or enlightenment.
