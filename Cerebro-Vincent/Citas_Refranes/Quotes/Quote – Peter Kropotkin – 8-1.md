> The only way to get rid of the state is to abolish it.

— [[Person – Peter Kropotkin]]

## Interpretation
- Kropotkin advocates for a direct approach to dismantling state structures.
- He implies that reform or modification is insufficient for true liberation from state control.
- The quote suggests a radical stance on governance, emphasizing the necessity of complete abolition.
