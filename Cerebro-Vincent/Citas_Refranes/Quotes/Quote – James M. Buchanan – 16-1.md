> Public choice theory helps us understand the limitations of government intervention."

— [[Person – James M. Buchanan]]

## Interpretation
- Public choice theory examines how individual self-interest influences political decision-making.
- It suggests that government intervention may not always lead to optimal outcomes due to the motivations of politicians and bureaucrats.
- Understanding these limitations can inform more effective policy-making and governance.
