> The state is that great fiction by which everyone seeks to live at the expense of everyone else.

— [[Person – Frédéric Bastiat]]

## Interpretation
- The state creates a system where individuals attempt to benefit at the cost of others.
- This reflects a critique of the reliance on government for economic support.
- Bastiat emphasizes the illusion of collective benefit while highlighting individual self-interest.
