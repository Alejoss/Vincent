> It is one thing to be a subject of a government and another to be a servant of the government.

— [[Person – John Locke]]

## Interpretation
- Being a subject of a government implies having rights and responsibilities as a citizen.
- Being a servant of the government suggests a subordinate role with less autonomy.
- The distinction highlights the balance between individual rights and governmental authority.
