> The idea of a free society is based on the idea of the individual as a sovereign being.

— [[Person – Albert Jay Nock]]

## Interpretation
- A free society values the autonomy and rights of the individual.
- The notion of individual sovereignty implies personal responsibility and self-governance.
- A society that respects individual sovereignty facilitates freedom and creativity for all.
