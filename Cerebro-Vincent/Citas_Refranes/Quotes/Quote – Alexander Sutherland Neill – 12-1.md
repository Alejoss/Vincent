> The aim of education is to help the child to become the person they want to be.

— [[Person – Alexander Sutherland Neill]]

## Interpretation
- Education should prioritize the individual aspirations of the child.
- It emphasizes personal growth over standardized achievement.
- The role of educators is to facilitate self-discovery and autonomy.
