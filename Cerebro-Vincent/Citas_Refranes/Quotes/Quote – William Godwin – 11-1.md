> The power of the people is greater than the people in power.

— [[Person – William Godwin]]

## Interpretation
- The collective strength and will of the populace surpasses that of their leaders.
- It suggests that true authority lies with the masses, rather than those in positions of power.
- This quote emphasizes the potential for social change driven by public action.
