> The beginning is always today.

— [[Person – Mary Wollstonecraft]]

## Interpretation
- Every moment presents an opportunity to start anew.
- Procrastination should not hinder action, as change can begin at any time.
- Emphasizing the importance of the present encourages proactive living.
