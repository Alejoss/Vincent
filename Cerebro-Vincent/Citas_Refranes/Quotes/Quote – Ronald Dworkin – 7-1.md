> The law must be understood in the context of the political community it serves."

— [[Person – Ronald Dworkin]]

## Interpretation
- The law is not an isolated set of rules, but is deeply intertwined with the values and beliefs of the society it governs.
- Understanding law requires awareness of the social, cultural, and political dynamics that shape a community.
- Legal interpretation should consider the needs and perspectives of the citizens to be truly effective and just.
