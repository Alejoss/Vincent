> Legal interpretation requires more than mere application of rules; it needs an understanding of the underlying values."

— [[Person – Ronald Dworkin]]

## Interpretation
- Legal interpretation goes beyond simply following established rules.
- It requires a comprehension of the ethical and moral principles that inform those rules.
- Understanding values is essential to apply the law meaningfully and justly.
