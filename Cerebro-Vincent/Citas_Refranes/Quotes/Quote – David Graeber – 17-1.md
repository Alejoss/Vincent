> In a world where everything is commodified, it’s hard to find meaning."

— [[Person – David Graeber]]

## Interpretation
- Commodification leads to the reduction of intrinsic value in experiences and relationships.
- When everything is assigned a monetary value, it becomes challenging to appreciate non-material aspects of life.
- A lack of meaning may result from prioritizing profit over personal fulfillment and connection.
