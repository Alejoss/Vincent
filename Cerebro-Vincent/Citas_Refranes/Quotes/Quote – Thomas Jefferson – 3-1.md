> Honesty is the first chapter in the book of wisdom.

— [[Person – Thomas Jefferson]]

## Interpretation
- Honesty is foundational to gaining true wisdom.
- Without honesty, one cannot fully understand or learn from life experiences.
- Emphasizing integrity shows that moral character is essential for intellectual growth.
