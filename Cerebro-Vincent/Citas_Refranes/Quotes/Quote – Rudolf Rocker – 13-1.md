> Revolution is not a matter of gaining power, but of creating a new way of life."

— [[Person – Rudolf Rocker]]

## Interpretation
- Revolution should focus on transforming societal values rather than merely seizing control.
- The essence of revolution lies in fostering new social practices and relationships.
- True change is about building a sustainable and equitable future, not just altering leadership.
