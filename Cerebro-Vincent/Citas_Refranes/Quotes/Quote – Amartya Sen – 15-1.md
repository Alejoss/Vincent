> Democracy is not just about elections; it is about participation and freedom."

— [[Person – Amartya Sen]]

## Interpretation
- Democracy encompasses more than the act of voting; it includes active engagement from citizens.
- True democracy requires the protection of individual freedoms and rights.
- Participation in democratic processes is essential for a healthy and functioning society.
