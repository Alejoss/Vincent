> The time you enjoy wasting is not wasted time.

— [[Person – Bertrand Russell]]

## Interpretation
- Enjoyment is a valid use of time, regardless of productivity.
- Leisure and relaxation can contribute to personal well-being.
- The value of time should be measured by personal satisfaction rather than societal expectations.
