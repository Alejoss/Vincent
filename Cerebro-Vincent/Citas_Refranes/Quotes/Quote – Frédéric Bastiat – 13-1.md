> All the mischief in the world comes from the fact that men do not realize that they are not the owners of their own lives.

— [[Person – Frédéric Bastiat]]

## Interpretation
- Individuals often fail to recognize the extent of their autonomy and freedom.
- This lack of awareness can lead to negative consequences and societal issues.
- True ownership of one's life involves understanding personal responsibility and freedom.
