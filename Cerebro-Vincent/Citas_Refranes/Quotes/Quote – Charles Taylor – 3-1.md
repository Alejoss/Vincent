> The past is not simply a set of facts; it is an interpretation of those facts."

— [[Person – Charles Taylor]]

## Interpretation
- Historical facts are subject to subjective interpretation.
- Different perspectives can shape our understanding of the past.
- The way we interpret history influences our current identity and decisions.
