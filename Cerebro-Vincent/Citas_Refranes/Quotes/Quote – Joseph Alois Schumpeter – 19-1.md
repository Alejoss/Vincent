> The process of economic development is inherently linked to the process of entrepreneurship.

— [[Person – Joseph Alois Schumpeter]]

## Interpretation
- Economic growth cannot occur without innovative entrepreneurs who drive change.
- Entrepreneurship is a catalyst for new ideas, products, and services that stimulate development.
- The interplay between entrepreneurship and economic development shapes the dynamics of markets and societies.
