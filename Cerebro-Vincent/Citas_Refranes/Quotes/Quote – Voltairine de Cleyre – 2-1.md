> I am not a man, I am not a woman; I am a human being."

— [[Person – Voltairine de Cleyre]]

## Interpretation
- The quote emphasizes the idea of transcending traditional gender identities.
- It advocates for the recognition of individual humanity over societal labels.
- De Cleyre highlights the importance of seeing people as whole beings rather than categorizing them based on gender.
