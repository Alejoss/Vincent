> To move forward, we cannot afford to ignore the intersections of race, class, and gender in our analyses."

— [[Person – Nancy Fraser]]

## Interpretation
- Acknowledging the complexity of social issues requires an intersectional approach.
- Race, class, and gender are interconnected factors that shape individual experiences and societal dynamics.
- Ignoring these intersections can lead to incomplete or ineffective analyses and solutions.
