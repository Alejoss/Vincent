> The most important thing is to have a dream and pursue it, regardless of the obstacles.

— [[Person – Emma Goldman]]

## Interpretation
- Having a dream provides direction and purpose in life.
- Pursuing one's dream requires resilience in the face of challenges.
- The emphasis is on the importance of commitment to one's aspirations.
