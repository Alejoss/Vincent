> The more I see of people, the more I like my dog.

— [[Person – Alexis de Tocqueville]]

## Interpretation
- The quote reflects a growing disillusionment with human nature and society.
- It suggests that animals provide companionship and loyalty that people often do not.
- It highlights a preference for simplicity and authenticity over the complexities of human interactions.
