> I am the creator of my own values.

— [[Person – Max Stirner]]

## Interpretation
- The individual has the power to define what is valuable in their life.
- Personal autonomy and self-creation are emphasized over societal norms.
- Values are not inherent but constructed through personal choices and experiences.
