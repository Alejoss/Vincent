> The progress of society is ultimately linked to the progress of industry and commerce.

— [[Person – David Ricardo]]

## Interpretation
- Societal advancement is directly influenced by advancements in industrial practices.
- Commerce serves as a catalyst for economic and social development.
- The interdependence between industry and society shapes overall progress.
