> What worries you, masters you.

— [[Person – John Locke]]

## Interpretation
- Fears and anxieties can control our thoughts and actions.
- Being preoccupied with worries can lead to a loss of personal autonomy.
- Acknowledging and addressing worries can empower individuals to regain control.
