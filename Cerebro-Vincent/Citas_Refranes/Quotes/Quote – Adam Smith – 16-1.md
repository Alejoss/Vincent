> The principle of self-interest is the foundation of a productive economy.

— [[Person – Adam Smith]]

## Interpretation
- Self-interest drives individuals to work harder and innovate, leading to economic growth.
- In a market economy, the pursuit of personal gain can result in benefits for society as a whole.
- Self-interest is a natural motivator that facilitates trade and resource allocation.
