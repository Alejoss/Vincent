> The ego is the individual, the individual is the ego.

— [[Person – Max Stirner]]

## Interpretation
- The ego represents the essence of individuality, emphasizing personal identity.
- This statement suggests that one's identity is fundamentally tied to their ego, reinforcing self-importance.
- Stirner highlights the inseparability of the individual from their own sense of self.
