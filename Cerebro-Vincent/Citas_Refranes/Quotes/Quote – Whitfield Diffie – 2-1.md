> Public key cryptography is a great advance because it allows two parties to communicate securely without having to share a secret in advance."

— [[Person – Whitfield Diffie]]

## Interpretation
- Public key cryptography enables secure communication between parties without prior arrangements.
- It eliminates the need for a pre-shared secret, simplifying secure exchanges.
- The innovation enhances privacy and security in digital communications.
