> The future is not a given; it is something we must create."

— [[Person – Cornelius Castoriadis]]

## Interpretation
- The future is not predetermined or inevitable, emphasizing the role of human agency.
- It suggests that individuals and societies have the power to shape their destinies.
- Creating the future requires active participation and responsibility from all of us.
