> In order to create a democratic politics, we need to establish a space for the expression of the political."

— [[Person – Chantal Mouffe]]

## Interpretation
- Democratic politics requires an environment where diverse political views can be openly shared.
- The expression of political ideas is essential for fostering engagement and participation in democracy.
- Creating such a space encourages dialogue and can lead to the development of a more inclusive political landscape.
