> Freedom is central to the process of development."

— [[Person – Amartya Sen]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom enables individuals to pursue their own goals and aspirations.
- Development is not solely about economic growth but also about enhancing people's capabilities and choices.
- A society that prioritizes freedom fosters innovation, creativity, and overall well-being.
