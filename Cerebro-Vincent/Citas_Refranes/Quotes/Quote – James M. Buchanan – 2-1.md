> The political process is the mechanism through which individuals express their preferences."

— [[Person – James M. Buchanan]]

## Interpretation
- The political process serves as a channel for individual voices and choices.
- It highlights the importance of individual preferences in shaping collective decisions.
- The quote suggests that democracy relies on the active participation of individuals in expressing their needs and desires.
