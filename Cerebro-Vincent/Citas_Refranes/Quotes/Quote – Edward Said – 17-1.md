> Culture is a battleground; it is where the struggle for identity takes place."

— [[Person – Edward Said]]

## Interpretation
- Culture is not static; it is shaped by conflicts and negotiations.
- Identity is formed through cultural interactions and struggles.
- The concept of culture encompasses both personal and collective experiences in society.
