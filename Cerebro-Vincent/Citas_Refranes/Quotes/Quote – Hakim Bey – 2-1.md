> Anarchy is not a state of chaos, but a state of freedom."

— [[Person – Hakim Bey]]

## Interpretation
- Anarchy is often misunderstood as disorder, but it fundamentally represents autonomy.
- It emphasizes the liberation of individuals from oppressive structures.
- True anarchy advocates for self-governance and personal responsibility rather than lawlessness.
