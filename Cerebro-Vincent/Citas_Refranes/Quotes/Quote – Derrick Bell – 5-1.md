> The purpose of education is to teach students to think critically, not to accept the status quo."

— [[Person – Derrick Bell]]

## Interpretation
- Education should empower students to analyze and question existing beliefs and systems.
- Critical thinking is essential for fostering social change and progress.
- Acceptance of the status quo limits intellectual growth and societal improvement.
