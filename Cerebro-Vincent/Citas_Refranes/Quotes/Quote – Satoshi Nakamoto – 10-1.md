> The ability to send money anywhere in the world instantly and without fees is a revolutionary idea.

— [[Person – Satoshi Nakamoto]]

## Interpretation
- This quote emphasizes the transformative potential of instant global money transfers.
- It suggests that eliminating transaction fees can democratize access to financial services.
- The phrase 'revolutionary idea' indicates a significant shift in how we perceive and use currency.
