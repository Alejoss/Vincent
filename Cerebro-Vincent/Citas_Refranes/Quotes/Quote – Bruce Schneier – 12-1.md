> We need to think about security as a system, not just as a checklist.

— [[Person – Bruce Schneier]]

## Interpretation
- Security should be approached holistically, considering interconnections and dependencies.
- Relying solely on checklists can lead to oversights and a false sense of security.
- A systemic view encourages proactive thinking and adaptability in addressing security challenges.
