> The most important thing to know about the welfare state is that it is not a solution to poverty."

— [[Person – Charles Murray]]

## Interpretation
- The welfare state may provide temporary assistance, but it does not address the root causes of poverty.
- Murray suggests that reliance on government support can create dependency rather than empowering individuals.
- The quote implies a need for alternative solutions to effectively combat poverty.
