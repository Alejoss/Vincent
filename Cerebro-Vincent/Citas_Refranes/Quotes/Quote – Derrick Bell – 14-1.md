> Silence in the face of injustice is complicity."

— [[Person – Derrick Bell]]

## Interpretation
- Remaining silent during acts of injustice implies an acceptance or endorsement of those actions.
- Inaction in the face of wrongdoing can be seen as supporting the status quo.
- Voicing opposition is crucial to challenge and combat injustice.
