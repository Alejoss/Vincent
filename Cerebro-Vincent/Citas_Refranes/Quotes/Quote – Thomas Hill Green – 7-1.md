> It is not only what we do, but also what we do not do, for which we are accountable.

— [[Person – Thomas Hill Green]]

## Interpretation
- Accountability extends beyond actions to include inactions.
- Individuals bear responsibility for opportunities they fail to seize.
- Moral and ethical considerations encompass both deeds and omissions.
