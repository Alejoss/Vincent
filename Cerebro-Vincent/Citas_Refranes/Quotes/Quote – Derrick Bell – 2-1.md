> The law is not a set of rules. It is a system that reflects the values of a society."

— [[Person – Derrick Bell]]

## Interpretation
- The law is shaped by the cultural and moral beliefs of the people it governs.
- Legal systems can change as societal values evolve over time.
- Understanding law requires examining the societal context from which it arises.
