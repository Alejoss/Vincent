> Privacy is a luxury good.

— [[Person – Cory Doctorow]]

## Interpretation
- Privacy is increasingly available only to those who can afford it.
- Access to privacy is often linked to socioeconomic status.
- The commodification of privacy highlights disparities in power and control.
