> The aim of anarchism is the liberation of the individual from all forms of oppression."

— [[Person – Rudolf Rocker]]

## Interpretation
- Anarchism seeks to empower individuals by removing constraints imposed by authority.
- The focus is on personal freedom and self-determination.
- Oppression can take many forms, including political, social, and economic.
