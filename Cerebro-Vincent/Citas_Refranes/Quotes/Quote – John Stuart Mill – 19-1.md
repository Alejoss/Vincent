> Genius can only breathe freely in an atmosphere of freedom.

— [[Person – John Stuart Mill]]

## Interpretation
- Creativity and innovation flourish in environments that promote liberty.
- Restriction stifles potential and hinders intellectual growth.
- True genius requires the freedom to explore and express ideas without constraint.
