> The idea of universal human rights is a radical one.

— [[Person – Noam Chomsky]]

## Interpretation
- Universal human rights challenge existing power structures and societal norms.
- The concept advocates for the inherent dignity and worth of every individual, regardless of their background.
- Recognizing universal human rights often requires confronting radical injustices and inequalities.
