> The categorical imperative is the only one that can be derived from the concept of a rational will.

— [[Person – Immanuel Kant]]

## Interpretation
- The categorical imperative is a fundamental principle that arises from the nature of rationality.
- It emphasizes that moral duties are universal and not contingent on personal desires.
- Kant asserts that true moral law is grounded in the rational will, guiding ethical behavior objectively.
