> The only thing that can be said to be true is that there is no truth.

— [[Person – Richard Rorty]]

## Interpretation
- Rorty suggests that the concept of absolute truth is inherently contradictory.
- This statement implies a skepticism towards objective knowledge and traditional epistemology.
- It reflects a philosophical stance that values perspectives and interpretations over definitive truths.
