> Gender inequality is not an inevitable feature of human existence.

— [[Person – Susan Moller Okin]]

## Interpretation
- Gender inequality is a social construct rather than a natural given.
- It suggests that change is possible through societal transformation.
- The quote challenges the notion that gender disparities are permanent or unchangeable.
