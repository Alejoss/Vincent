> Knowledge is the ultimate source of power.

— [[Person – Wilhelm von Humboldt]]

## Interpretation
- Knowledge equips individuals with the tools to influence and enact change.
- It serves as a foundation for informed decision-making and effective leadership.
- Access to knowledge can empower people and communities, enhancing their ability to navigate challenges.
