> The role of government is to protect us from each other.

— [[Person – Milton Friedman]]

## Interpretation
- Government's primary function is to maintain order and prevent conflict among individuals.
- This perspective emphasizes the importance of social contracts in protecting individual rights.
- Friedman suggests that without government intervention, society could descend into chaos due to self-interest.
