> These are the times that try men's souls.

— [[Person – Thomas Paine]]

## Interpretation
- The quote signifies moments of extreme challenge and adversity that test individuals' character.
- It suggests that difficult times reveal true strengths and weaknesses in people.
- Paine emphasizes the importance of resilience and perseverance during crises.
