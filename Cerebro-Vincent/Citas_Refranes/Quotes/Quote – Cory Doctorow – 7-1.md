> The world is full of people who don’t need to be persuaded that the Internet is a great place to share ideas.

— [[Person – Cory Doctorow]]

## Interpretation
- People inherently recognize the value of the Internet for idea-sharing.
- Persuasion is unnecessary for those already aware of the Internet's benefits.
- The quote suggests a collective understanding of the Internet's role in communication.
