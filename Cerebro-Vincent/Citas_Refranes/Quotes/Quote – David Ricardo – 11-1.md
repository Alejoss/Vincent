> It is not the demand for a commodity that creates its value, but the labor necessary to produce it.

— [[Person – David Ricardo]]

## Interpretation
- Value is determined by the labor required to create a product, not by consumer desire.
- This perspective shifts the focus from market demand to production processes.
- It emphasizes the importance of labor in establishing economic value.
