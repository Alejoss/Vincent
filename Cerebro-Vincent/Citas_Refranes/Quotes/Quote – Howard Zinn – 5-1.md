> There is no such thing as a neutral education. Education is a political act.

— [[Person – Howard Zinn]]

## Interpretation
- Education inherently reflects societal values and power dynamics.
- There are always underlying political motives in what is taught and how.
- Neutrality in education is an illusion, as it can perpetuate existing inequalities.
