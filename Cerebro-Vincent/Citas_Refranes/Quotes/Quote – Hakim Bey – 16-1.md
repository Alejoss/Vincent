> Every act of rebellion is an act of creativity."

— [[Person – Hakim Bey]]

## Interpretation
- Rebellion challenges the status quo and encourages new ways of thinking.
- Creativity is often born from the desire to resist oppression or limitations.
- Acts of rebellion can lead to innovative solutions and expressions of individuality.
