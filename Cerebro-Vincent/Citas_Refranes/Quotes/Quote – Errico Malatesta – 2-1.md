> Anarchism is based on the idea that human beings can and should be able to organize themselves freely.

— [[Person – Errico Malatesta]]

## Interpretation
- Anarchism promotes the belief in self-organization among individuals without hierarchical structures.
- It emphasizes freedom and autonomy as essential components of human interaction.
- The quote suggests that collective organization should be a voluntary and self-directed process.
