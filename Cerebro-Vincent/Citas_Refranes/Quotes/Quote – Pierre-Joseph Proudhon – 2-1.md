> Anarchy is order without power.

— [[Person – Pierre-Joseph Proudhon]]

## Interpretation
- Anarchy can exist as a form of social organization that is free from centralized authority.
- True order arises from voluntary cooperation and mutual aid among individuals.
- Proudhon suggests that power structures often disrupt natural societal harmony.
