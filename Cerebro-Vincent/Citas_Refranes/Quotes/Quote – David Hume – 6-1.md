> It is not contrary to reason to prefer the destruction of the whole world to the scratching of my finger.

— [[Person – David Hume]]

## Interpretation
- Hume suggests that personal preference can outweigh rational considerations.
- The quote highlights the subjective nature of value and prioritization in human thought.
- It illustrates the potential for extreme individualism in ethical reasoning.
