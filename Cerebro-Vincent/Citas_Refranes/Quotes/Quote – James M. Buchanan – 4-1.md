> The idea of the public interest is a myth."

— [[Person – James M. Buchanan]]

## Interpretation
- The concept of a collective public interest is often manipulated or misrepresented.
- Individuals or groups may prioritize personal interests over the so-called public good.
- This challenges the assumption that policies can universally benefit society.
