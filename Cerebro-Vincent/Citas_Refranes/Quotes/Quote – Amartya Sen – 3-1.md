> The idea of a ‘good life’ has to be understood in terms of the freedoms that a person has."

— [[Person – Amartya Sen]]

## Interpretation
- A good life is not defined by material wealth but by the freedoms available to individuals.
- The quality of life is intrinsically linked to personal agency and choices.
- Understanding well-being requires considering the opportunities and capabilities a person possesses.
