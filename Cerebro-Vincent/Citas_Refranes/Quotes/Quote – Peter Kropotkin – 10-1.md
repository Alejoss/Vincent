> Anarchism is a philosophy of freedom, of cooperation, and of mutual aid.

— [[Person – Peter Kropotkin]]

## Interpretation
- Anarchism advocates for individual freedom without oppressive authority.
- It emphasizes the importance of cooperative relationships among individuals.
- Mutual aid is seen as a foundational principle for building a supportive community.
