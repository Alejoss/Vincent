> Inflation is always and everywhere a monetary phenomenon.

— [[Person – Milton Friedman]]

## Interpretation
- Inflation is fundamentally linked to the supply of money in an economy.
- Changes in the money supply can directly influence price levels.
- Friedman's assertion emphasizes the role of central banks in managing inflation.
