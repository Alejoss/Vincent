> This world is but a canvas to our imagination.

— [[Person – Henry David Thoreau]]

## Interpretation
- The world serves as a medium for our creative expression.
- Our imagination shapes our perception and experience of reality.
- We have the power to reinterpret and transform our surroundings through creativity.
