> Anarchism is not a dream; it is the possibility of a real and just society.

— [[Person – Errico Malatesta]]

## Interpretation
- Anarchism is grounded in practical possibilities rather than mere idealism.
- It suggests that a just society can be achieved through non-hierarchical and collaborative means.
- The quote emphasizes the importance of striving for social justice and equity in our societal structures.
