> The ethics of technology are as important as the technology itself.

— [[Person – Jaron Lanier]]

## Interpretation
- Technological advancements should not be pursued without considering their ethical implications.
- The impact of technology on society necessitates a moral framework to guide its development and use.
- Ignoring ethics in technology can lead to harmful consequences, making it essential to integrate ethical thinking into tech innovation.
