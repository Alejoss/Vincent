> The past is not a dead thing; it’s alive and always in the making.

— [[Person – Howard Zinn]]

## Interpretation
- History is not static but continuously evolving with new interpretations.
- Our understanding of the past influences the present and shapes the future.
- The past can be revisited and redefined through ongoing discourse and reflection.
