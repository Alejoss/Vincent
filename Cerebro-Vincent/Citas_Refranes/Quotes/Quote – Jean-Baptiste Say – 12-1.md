> The entrepreneur is the one who sees opportunities where others see obstacles."

— [[Person – Jean-Baptiste Say]]

## Interpretation
- Entrepreneurs possess a unique perspective that allows them to identify potential in challenging situations.
- The ability to see beyond immediate difficulties is crucial for innovation and success.
- This mindset differentiates successful entrepreneurs from those who remain stagnant in the face of adversity.
