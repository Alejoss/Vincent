> The institutions of democracy must be designed to minimize the potential for abuse of power."

— [[Person – James M. Buchanan]]

## Interpretation
- Democratic institutions should be structured to limit the concentration and misuse of authority.
- Preventing power abuse is crucial for maintaining the integrity of a democratic system.
- The design of democratic frameworks is essential in safeguarding citizens' rights and freedoms.
