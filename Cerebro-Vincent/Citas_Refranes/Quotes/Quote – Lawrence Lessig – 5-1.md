> The future of our democracy depends on the freedom of our culture."

— [[Person – Lawrence Lessig]]

## Interpretation
- A healthy democracy relies on a vibrant and free cultural expression.
- Cultural freedom allows for diverse ideas and perspectives, essential for democratic discourse.
- Restricting cultural freedom can undermine democratic principles and civic engagement.
