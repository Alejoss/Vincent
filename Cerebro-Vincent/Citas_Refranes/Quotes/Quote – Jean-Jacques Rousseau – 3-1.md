> It is too difficult to think nobly when one thinks only of earning a living.

— [[Person – Jean-Jacques Rousseau]]

## Interpretation
- The pursuit of financial stability can distract from higher moral and ethical considerations.
- Focusing solely on survival limits one's capacity for noble thoughts and actions.
- A life centered on material gain can hinder personal growth and societal contributions.
