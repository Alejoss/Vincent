> The way we think about gender is inextricably linked to our understanding of justice.

— [[Person – Susan Moller Okin]]

## Interpretation
- Our perceptions of gender influence our definitions of fairness and equity.
- Understanding justice requires a critical examination of gender roles and norms.
- The interconnection suggests that achieving true justice necessitates addressing gender inequality.
