> The digital divide is not just about access; it’s about power.

— [[Person – Cory Doctorow]]

## Interpretation
- Access to technology is only one aspect of inequality in the digital age.
- Power dynamics shape who benefits from digital resources and who remains marginalized.
- The implications of the digital divide extend beyond connectivity to issues of control and influence.
