> There is a great deal of difference between a man who is a leader and a man who is a follower.

— [[Person – William Graham Sumner]]

## Interpretation
- Leaders are proactive and take initiative, while followers tend to be reactive and follow directions.
- The distinction highlights the different roles individuals play in society and the impact they have on others.
- Understanding this difference can provide insight into personal development and group dynamics.
