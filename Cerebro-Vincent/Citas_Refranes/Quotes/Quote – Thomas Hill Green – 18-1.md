> A society that overlooks the moral development of its members is doomed to fail.

— [[Person – Thomas Hill Green]]

## Interpretation
- Moral development is essential for individual growth within a society.
- Neglecting moral education leads to a breakdown of social cohesion.
- A society's success is fundamentally linked to the ethical standards of its members.
