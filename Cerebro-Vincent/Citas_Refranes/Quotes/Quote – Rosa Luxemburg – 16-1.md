> Revolutionaries must be able to understand the world in order to change it.

— [[Person – Rosa Luxemburg]]

## Interpretation
- Understanding the world is essential for effective revolutionary action.
- Knowledge empowers revolutionaries to identify and challenge systemic issues.
- A deep comprehension of societal structures is crucial for meaningful change.
