> To be free, a man must be able to choose."

— [[Person – Richard Cobden]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom is inherently linked to the ability to make choices.
- Without the power to choose, one cannot truly experience liberty.
- Choice is a fundamental aspect of individual autonomy and personal development.
