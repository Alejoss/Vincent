> Art is not a handicraft, it is the transmission of feeling the artist has experienced.

— [[Person – Leo Tolstoy]]

## Interpretation
- Art transcends mere technical skill, emphasizing emotional depth.
- The essence of art lies in its ability to convey personal experiences and emotions.
- True artistic expression connects the artist's feelings with the audience's understanding.
