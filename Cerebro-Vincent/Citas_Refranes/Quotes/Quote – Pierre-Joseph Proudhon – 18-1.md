> We must create a new society based on cooperation and mutual aid.

— [[Person – Pierre-Joseph Proudhon]]

## Interpretation
- Proudhon advocates for a societal framework that prioritizes collective support over individualism.
- The quote emphasizes the importance of community and social responsibility in building a better future.
- It suggests that cooperation and mutual aid are fundamental to achieving social progress and equity.
