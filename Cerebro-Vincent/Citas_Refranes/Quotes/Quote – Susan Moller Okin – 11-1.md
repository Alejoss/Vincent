> The ideal of gender equality must be pursued not only in law but also in practice.

— [[Person – Susan Moller Okin]]

## Interpretation
- Gender equality requires both legal frameworks and real-world application.
- Merely having laws in place is insufficient without societal adherence to those principles.
- Practical implementation is essential for achieving true gender equality.
