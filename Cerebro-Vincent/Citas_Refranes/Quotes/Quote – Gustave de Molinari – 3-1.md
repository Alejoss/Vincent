> The production of wealth is the result of two factors: the labor of man and the nature of the land."

— [[Person – Gustave de Molinari]]

## Interpretation
- Wealth creation is fundamentally tied to human effort and natural resources.
- The interplay between labor and land quality determines economic output.
- Both human ingenuity and environmental factors are essential for productivity.
