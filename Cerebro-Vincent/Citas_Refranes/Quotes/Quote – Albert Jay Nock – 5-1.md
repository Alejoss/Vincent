> The best way to make a fool of a man is to let him think he is wise.

— [[Person – Albert Jay Nock]]

## Interpretation
- Illusions of wisdom can lead to arrogance and folly.
- True wisdom often comes from humility and self-awareness.
- Believing oneself to be wise may prevent growth and learning.
