> I would rather die than be a slave."

— [[Person – Lucy Parsons]]

## Interpretation
- The quote emphasizes the value of freedom and autonomy over life itself.
- It reflects a deep resistance to oppression and injustice.
- Parsons' statement indicates that enduring subjugation is worse than death.
