> The richness of human experience cannot be captured by economic metrics alone."

— [[Person – Martha Nussbaum]]

## Interpretation
- Economic metrics fail to encompass the full spectrum of human emotions and experiences.
- Human experiences include qualitative aspects that are not quantifiable by numbers.
- A holistic understanding of well-being requires considering factors beyond economics.
