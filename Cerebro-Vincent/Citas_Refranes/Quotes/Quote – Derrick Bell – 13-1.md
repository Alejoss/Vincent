> When we talk about race, we must also talk about class and gender."

— [[Person – Derrick Bell]]

## Interpretation
- Discussions about race are incomplete without considering socioeconomic status.
- Class and gender intersect with racial identity, influencing individuals' experiences.
- A comprehensive understanding of social issues requires an intersectional approach.
