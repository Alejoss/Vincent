> If I can't dance, I don't want to be part of your revolution.

— [[Person – Emma Goldman]]

## Interpretation
- The quote emphasizes the importance of joy and personal expression in the context of social change.
- It suggests that a revolution lacking in creativity and freedom is not worth participating in.
- Goldman advocates for a holistic approach to activism that includes passion and self-fulfillment.
