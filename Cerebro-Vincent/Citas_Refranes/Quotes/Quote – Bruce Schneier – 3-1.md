> The Internet is a giant experiment in trust.

— [[Person – Bruce Schneier]]

## Interpretation
- The Internet relies on users trusting each other and the systems they interact with.
- This trust is fundamental for online interactions, commerce, and communication.
- However, it also exposes vulnerabilities when trust is broken or abused.
