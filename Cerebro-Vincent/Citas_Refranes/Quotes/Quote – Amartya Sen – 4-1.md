> Development requires the removal of major sources of unfreedom."

— [[Person – Amartya Sen]]

## Interpretation
- Development is fundamentally linked to the freedom and agency of individuals.
- Unfreedom can stem from poverty, social oppression, and lack of political rights.
- True progress necessitates addressing and eliminating barriers that restrict individual freedoms.
