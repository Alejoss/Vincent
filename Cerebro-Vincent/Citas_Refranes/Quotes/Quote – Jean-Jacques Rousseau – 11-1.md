> The social contract is an agreement between the governed and the government.

— [[Person – Jean-Jacques Rousseau]]

## Interpretation
- The social contract establishes mutual obligations between citizens and their governing bodies.
- It implies that legitimacy of government derives from the consent of the governed.
- This agreement is foundational to the principles of democracy and civil rights.
