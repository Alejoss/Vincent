> Justice is not merely about the end state of distribution but about the process by which a distribution comes about.

— [[Person – Robert Nozick]]

## Interpretation
- Justice involves both the outcome of wealth distribution and the fairness of the methods used to achieve that outcome.
- The process of distribution must adhere to ethical standards to be considered just.
- Focusing solely on the end result overlooks important moral considerations inherent in the distribution process.
