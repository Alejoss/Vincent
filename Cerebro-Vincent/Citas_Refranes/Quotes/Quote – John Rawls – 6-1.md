> A just society is one that is fair to all its members.

— [[Person – John Rawls]]

## Interpretation
- Justice is fundamentally about fairness and equality among all individuals.
- A just society prioritizes the rights and opportunities of every member.
- Fairness in society ensures that no group is marginalized or disadvantaged.
