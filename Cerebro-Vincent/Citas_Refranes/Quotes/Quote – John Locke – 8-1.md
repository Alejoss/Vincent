> It is the responsibility of the state to ensure the protection of the property of its citizens.

— [[Person – John Locke]]

## Interpretation
- The state has a duty to safeguard the assets and belongings of individuals.
- Property rights are fundamental to societal stability and individual freedom.
- A government’s legitimacy is partly based on its ability to protect citizens' property.
