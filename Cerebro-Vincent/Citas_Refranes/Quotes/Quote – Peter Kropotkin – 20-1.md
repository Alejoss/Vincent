> We must build a new society based on freedom, equality, and solidarity.

— [[Person – Peter Kropotkin]]

## Interpretation
- The quote advocates for the creation of a society that prioritizes individual liberties and collective well-being.
- It emphasizes the importance of equality in social relationships and opportunities.
- Solidarity suggests a sense of community and mutual support among individuals.
