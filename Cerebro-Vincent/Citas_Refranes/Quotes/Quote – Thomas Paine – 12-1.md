> Government, even in its best state, is but a necessary evil; in its worst state, an intolerable one.

— [[Person – Thomas Paine]]

## Interpretation
- Government is inherently flawed and can only serve as a necessary measure against chaos.
- In its best form, government is an imperfect solution to societal issues.
- In its worst form, government becomes oppressive and unbearable for the people.
