> The importance of philosophy lies in its ability to change the way we think.

— [[Person – Richard Rorty]]

## Interpretation
- Philosophy serves as a tool for transforming our thought processes.
- It highlights the dynamic nature of human understanding and intellectual growth.
- The emphasis is on the practical impact of philosophical inquiry rather than abstract theorizing.
