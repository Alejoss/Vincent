> We are all a little bit crazy. The trick is to find the right kind of crazy."

— [[Person – Mitchell Kapor]]

## Interpretation
- Everyone has their quirks and eccentricities.
- Embracing one's uniqueness can lead to positive outcomes.
- Finding a constructive or beneficial form of 'crazy' is essential for personal growth.
