> The concept of fairness is central to the idea of justice.

— [[Person – John Rawls]]

## Interpretation
- Fairness is a fundamental principle that underpins the notion of justice.
- Justice involves treating individuals equitably and ensuring impartiality in social arrangements.
- A just society is one where fairness is prioritized in decision-making and policy.
