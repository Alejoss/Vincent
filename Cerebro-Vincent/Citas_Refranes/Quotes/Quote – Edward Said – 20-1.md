> Literature has the power to challenge the status quo."

— [[Person – Edward Said]]

## Interpretation
- Literature serves as a vehicle for questioning societal norms and conventions.
- Through storytelling and critical perspectives, literature can inspire change and provoke thought.
- The power of literature lies in its ability to give voice to the marginalized and challenge prevailing ideologies.
