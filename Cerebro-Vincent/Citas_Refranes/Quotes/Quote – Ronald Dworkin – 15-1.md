> Moral principles are not just personal opinions; they are the foundation of law."

— [[Person – Ronald Dworkin]]

## Interpretation
- Moral principles provide a universal framework that transcends individual beliefs.
- The law is grounded in ethical standards rather than subjective views.
- Recognizing moral principles as foundational reinforces the integrity and legitimacy of legal systems.
