> The moral law within us is not a mere feeling, but an imperative.

— [[Person – Immanuel Kant]]

## Interpretation
- Kant emphasizes that moral law is a rational command rather than just an emotional response.
- This imperative nature suggests that moral duties are universal and binding on all rational beings.
- Kant distinguishes between subjective feelings and objective moral obligations.
