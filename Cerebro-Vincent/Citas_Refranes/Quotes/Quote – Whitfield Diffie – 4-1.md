> Cryptography is a tool that can be used for both good and evil."

— [[Person – Whitfield Diffie]]

## Interpretation
- Cryptography holds the potential for both positive and negative applications.
- The ethical implications of cryptographic tools depend on the intent of the user.
- Understanding the dual nature of cryptography is crucial for its responsible use.
