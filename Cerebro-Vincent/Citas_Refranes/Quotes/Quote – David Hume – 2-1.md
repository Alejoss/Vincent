> Reason is, and ought only to be the slave of the passions.

— [[Person – David Hume]]

## Interpretation
- Reason serves to support and fulfill our emotional desires rather than govern them.
- Human actions are primarily motivated by passions, with reason acting as a tool to achieve those ends.
- Hume emphasizes the importance of emotions in guiding human behavior over purely rational thought.
