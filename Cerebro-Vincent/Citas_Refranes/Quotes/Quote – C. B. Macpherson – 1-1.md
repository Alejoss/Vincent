> The concept of the 'possessive individual' is central to understanding modern capitalism."

— [[Person – C. B. Macpherson]]

## Interpretation
- The 'possessive individual' highlights the emphasis on personal ownership and self-interest in capitalist societies.
- This concept suggests that individual identity and value are closely tied to material possessions and economic success.
- Understanding this idea is crucial for analyzing the dynamics and implications of modern capitalism.
