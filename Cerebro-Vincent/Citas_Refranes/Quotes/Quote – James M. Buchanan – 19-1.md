> Self-interest is a powerful motivator in both the market and the political arena."

— [[Person – James M. Buchanan]]

## Interpretation
- Self-interest drives individual behavior in economic transactions.
- Political decisions are often influenced by personal gain rather than collective good.
- Understanding self-interest is crucial for analyzing both market dynamics and political actions.
