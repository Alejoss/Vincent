> The idea of justice is not a single idea but a family of ideas.

— [[Person – John Rawls]]

## Interpretation
- Justice encompasses a variety of concepts and principles rather than a singular definition.
- Different contexts and perspectives can lead to diverse interpretations of justice.
- Understanding justice requires an exploration of its multiple dimensions and their relationships.
