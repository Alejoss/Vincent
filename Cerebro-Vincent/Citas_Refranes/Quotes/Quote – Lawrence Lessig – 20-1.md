> The challenge of the 21st century is to preserve the commons."

— [[Person – Lawrence Lessig]]

## Interpretation
- The 'commons' refers to shared resources that require protection from overexploitation.
- Preserving the commons is essential for ensuring equitable access and sustainability.
- The challenge highlights the need for collective action and responsible stewardship in a rapidly changing world.
