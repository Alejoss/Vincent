> The rise of surveillance capitalism is a profound challenge to our democracy."

— [[Person – Shoshana Zuboff]]

## Interpretation
- Surveillance capitalism undermines individual privacy and autonomy.
- It concentrates power in the hands of a few tech companies, eroding democratic values.
- The pervasive data collection threatens informed citizen participation in governance.
