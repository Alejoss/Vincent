> The first step in a person's evolution is to be able to hold two contradictory beliefs at the same time.

— [[Person – Herbert Spencer]]

## Interpretation
- Cognitive flexibility allows individuals to navigate complex ideas and perspectives.
- Holding contradictory beliefs can foster critical thinking and deeper understanding.
- This evolution reflects a maturity in thought and the ability to embrace ambiguity.
