> Anarchism is the idea that we can live in a society without rulers.

— [[Person – Gustav Landauer]]

## Interpretation
- Anarchism proposes a society organized without hierarchical authority.
- It emphasizes the possibility of self-governance and mutual aid among individuals.
- The idea challenges traditional power structures and advocates for personal freedom.
