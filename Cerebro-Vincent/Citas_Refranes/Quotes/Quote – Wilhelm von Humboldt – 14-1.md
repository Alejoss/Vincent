> Education should be the training of the whole human being.

— [[Person – Wilhelm von Humboldt]]

## Interpretation
- Education should encompass emotional, intellectual, and physical development.
- The goal of education is to foster well-rounded individuals.
- Holistic education enables individuals to realize their full potential.
