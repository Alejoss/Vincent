> The rights of woman should be the same as the rights of man.

— [[Person – Mary Wollstonecraft]]

## Interpretation
- Women are entitled to the same legal and social rights as men.
- Equality between genders is a fundamental principle of justice.
- This statement advocates for the dismantling of systemic gender discrimination.
