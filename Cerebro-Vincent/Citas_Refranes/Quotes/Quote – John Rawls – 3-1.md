> Each person is to have an equal right to the most extensive basic liberty compatible with a similar liberty for others.

— [[Person – John Rawls]]

## Interpretation
- Everyone is entitled to equal freedoms and rights.
- Liberty must be balanced with the rights of others.
- The principle promotes a fair and just society.
