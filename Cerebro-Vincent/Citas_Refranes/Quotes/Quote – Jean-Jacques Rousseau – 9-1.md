> The strongest is never strong enough to be always the master, unless he transforms strength into right, and obedience into duty.

— [[Person – Jean-Jacques Rousseau]]

## Interpretation
- Power must be legitimized through moral or legal frameworks to maintain control.
- Physical strength alone is insufficient for sustained dominance.
- True authority is derived from the consent and duty of the governed.
