> The essence of individualism is that it is the only true basis for a free society."

— [[Person – Isabel Paterson]]

## Interpretation
- Individualism promotes personal autonomy and self-determination.
- A free society relies on the recognition and respect of individual rights.
- Collective conformity undermines true freedom and personal expression.
