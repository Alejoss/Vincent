> A good scientific theory is one that survives attempts to disprove it.

— [[Person – Karl Popper]]

## Interpretation
- A scientific theory should be testable and falsifiable.
- The resilience of a theory to counter-evidence indicates its robustness.
- Survival of challenges strengthens the credibility of scientific claims.
