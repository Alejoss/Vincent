> Charity is not a substitute for justice.

— [[Person – William Graham Sumner]]

## Interpretation
- Charity should complement justice rather than replace it.
- Relying solely on charity can lead to a neglect of systemic issues.
- True justice requires structural change, not just acts of goodwill.
