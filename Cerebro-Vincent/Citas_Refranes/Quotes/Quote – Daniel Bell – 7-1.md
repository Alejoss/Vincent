> Cultural pluralism is not just a matter of tolerance; it is a matter of respect."

— [[Person – Daniel Bell]]

## Interpretation
- Cultural pluralism requires an acknowledgment of diverse perspectives beyond mere acceptance.
- True respect involves valuing and understanding the differences that exist within cultures.
- Tolerance can be superficial, whereas respect fosters genuine dialogue and connection.
