> The interest of the landlord is opposed to the interest of the consumer.

— [[Person – David Ricardo]]

## Interpretation
- Landlords benefit from higher rents, which can harm consumers by increasing their living costs.
- There is a fundamental conflict between property owners seeking profit and consumers seeking affordability.
- This opposition highlights the socioeconomic dynamics between different classes in an economy.
