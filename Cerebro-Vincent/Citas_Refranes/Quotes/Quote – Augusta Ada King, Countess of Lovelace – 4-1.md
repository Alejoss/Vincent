> There is no reason why the machine should not be able to produce a result of the same degree of certainty as the human mind.

— [[Person – Augusta Ada King, Countess of Lovelace]]

## Interpretation
- The quote suggests that machines can achieve levels of certainty comparable to human reasoning.
- It implies a belief in the potential for artificial intelligence to replicate human cognitive functions.
- This reflects an early understanding of computational power and its implications for decision-making.
