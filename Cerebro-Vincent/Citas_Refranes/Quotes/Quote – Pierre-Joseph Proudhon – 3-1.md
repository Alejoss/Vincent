> The law is the will of the strongest.

— [[Person – Pierre-Joseph Proudhon]]

## Interpretation
- The law is a reflection of power dynamics rather than a neutral set of rules.
- Those with strength or authority shape laws to serve their interests.
- This perspective critiques the idea of justice being impartial or universal.
