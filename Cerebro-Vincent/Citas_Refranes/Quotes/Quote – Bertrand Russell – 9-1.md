> The secret of happiness is to face the fact that the world is horrible.

— [[Person – Bertrand Russell]]

## Interpretation
- Acknowledging the harsh realities of life can lead to a deeper understanding of happiness.
- Happiness does not come from ignorance but from acceptance of difficult truths.
- Facing the world's horrors can liberate individuals from unrealistic expectations and foster resilience.
