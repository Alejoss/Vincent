> The role of the judiciary is to uphold the integrity of the Constitution."

— [[Person – Dahlia Lithwick]]

## Interpretation
- The judiciary serves as a guardian of constitutional principles.
- It ensures that laws and actions align with constitutional values.
- The integrity of the Constitution is paramount for maintaining justice and democracy.
