> The duty of the historian is to tell the truth."

— [[Person – François Guizot]]

## Interpretation
- Historians are responsible for presenting an accurate account of events.
- The pursuit of truth is fundamental to the integrity of historical scholarship.
- This duty emphasizes the ethical obligations of historians to their audience.
