> When I reflect on the condition of women, I am sometimes inclined to think that they are a little too much like the canary bird in the cage.

— [[Person – Mary Wollstonecraft]]

## Interpretation
- Wollstonecraft compares women to caged canary birds to highlight their lack of freedom and autonomy.
- The metaphor suggests that women's potential is stifled by societal constraints, much like a bird trapped in a cage.
- This reflection calls for awareness and change regarding the oppressive conditions faced by women.
