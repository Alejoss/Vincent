> The struggle for justice is a struggle for everyone."

— [[Person – Lucy Parsons]]

## Interpretation
- Justice is a collective pursuit, affecting all members of society.
- The fight for justice transcends individual interests and encompasses communal well-being.
- Everyone's involvement is necessary to achieve true justice.
