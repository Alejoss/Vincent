> An idea is not a thing; it is a process of thinking.

— [[Person – Hannah Arendt]]

## Interpretation
- Ideas are dynamic and evolve through contemplation rather than being static objects.
- The process of thinking is essential to the formation and understanding of ideas.
- Recognizing the difference between ideas as processes and physical things highlights the importance of intellectual engagement.
