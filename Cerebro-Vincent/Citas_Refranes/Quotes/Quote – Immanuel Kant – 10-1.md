> All our knowledge begins with the senses, proceeds then to the understanding, and ends with reason.

— [[Person – Immanuel Kant]]

## Interpretation
- Knowledge is rooted in sensory experience.
- Understanding builds upon sensory data to form concepts.
- Reason synthesizes these concepts into higher-order thinking.
