> The United States is not a democracy; it is a business.

— [[Person – Noam Chomsky]]

## Interpretation
- Chomsky suggests that economic interests drive political decisions in the U.S.
- He implies that the influence of corporations undermines true democratic principles.
- The quote critiques the prioritization of profit over public welfare in governance.
