> We are not rich by what we possess but by what we can do without.

— [[Person – Immanuel Kant]]

## Interpretation
- True wealth is measured by our ability to live without material possessions.
- Independence from material goods can indicate a deeper sense of freedom.
- The focus is on personal capacity and resilience rather than accumulation of wealth.
