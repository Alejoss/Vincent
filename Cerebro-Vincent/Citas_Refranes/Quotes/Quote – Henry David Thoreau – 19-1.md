> I believe that water is the only drink for a wise man; wine is not so noble a liquor.

— [[Person – Henry David Thoreau]]

## Interpretation
- Thoreau elevates water as a pure and essential choice, symbolizing wisdom and clarity.
- He suggests that wine, often associated with indulgence, lacks the nobility of water.
- The quote reflects a broader commentary on simplicity and the value of nature over excess.
