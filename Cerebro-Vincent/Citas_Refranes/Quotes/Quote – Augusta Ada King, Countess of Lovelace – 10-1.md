> The Analytical Engine is capable of doing anything that can be expressed in symbols.

— [[Person – Augusta Ada King, Countess of Lovelace]]

## Interpretation
- The Analytical Engine represents a significant advancement in computing technology.
- It underscores the idea that computation can be abstracted through symbolic representation.
- This statement implies that any process or idea can be translated into a formalized method of calculation.
