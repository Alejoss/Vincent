> War does not determine who is right, only who is left.

— [[Person – Bertrand Russell]]

## Interpretation
- War is not a moral arbiter; it doesn't establish truth or justice.
- The outcome of war often leaves survivors rather than determining righteousness.
- The phrase highlights the futility of conflict in resolving ideological differences.
