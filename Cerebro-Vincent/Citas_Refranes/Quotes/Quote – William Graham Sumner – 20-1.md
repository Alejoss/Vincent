> A society that does not recognize the value of the individual is doomed to failure.

— [[Person – William Graham Sumner]]

## Interpretation
- The individual is the fundamental unit of society, and their worth must be acknowledged.
- Disregarding individual value can lead to societal dysfunction and collapse.
- A thriving society depends on the recognition and empowerment of its members.
