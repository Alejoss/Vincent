> The struggle for freedom is the struggle for the dignity of the individual."

— [[Person – Rudolf Rocker]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom is inherently linked to individual dignity and self-worth.
- The struggle for freedom involves advocating for and respecting each person's rights.
- True freedom cannot exist without recognizing and upholding the dignity of all individuals.
