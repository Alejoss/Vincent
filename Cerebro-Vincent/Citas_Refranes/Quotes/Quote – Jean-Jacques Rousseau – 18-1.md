> The only way to avoid being miserable is not to have enough leisure to be miserable.

— [[Person – Jean-Jacques Rousseau]]

## Interpretation
- Being overly idle or having too much free time can lead to feelings of misery.
- Engagement in activities can serve as a distraction from negative emotions.
- The quote suggests that a busy life might mitigate feelings of unhappiness.
