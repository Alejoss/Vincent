> The development of public key cryptography was a revolutionary step forward."

— [[Person – Martin Hellman]]

## Interpretation
- Public key cryptography transformed the way information is secured.
- It enabled secure communications without the need for shared secrets.
- This innovation has had lasting implications for privacy and security in the digital age.
