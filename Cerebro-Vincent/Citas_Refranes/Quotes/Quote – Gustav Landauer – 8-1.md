> The essence of socialism is the liberation of the individual through the collective.

— [[Person – Gustav Landauer]]

## Interpretation
- Socialism emphasizes the importance of community in achieving personal freedom.
- The collective effort and support can lead to individual growth and empowerment.
- True liberation for individuals is intertwined with the well-being of the group as a whole.
