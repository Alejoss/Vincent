> I am not a means to an end, I am an end in myself.

— [[Person – Max Stirner]]

## Interpretation
- The individual asserts their intrinsic value and significance beyond utility.
- This statement emphasizes self-ownership and personal autonomy.
- It challenges the notion of being merely a tool for others' purposes.
