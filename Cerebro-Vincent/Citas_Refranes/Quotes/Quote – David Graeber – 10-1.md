> We have to think of ourselves as part of a larger whole, not just individuals."

— [[Person – David Graeber]]

## Interpretation
- Our identity is interconnected with the community and society at large.
- Individual actions and choices have broader implications for the collective.
- Recognizing our interdependence can foster empathy and collaboration.
