> The ladder of success is best climbed by stepping on the rungs of opportunity.

— [[Person – Ayn Rand]]

## Interpretation
- Success requires taking advantage of available opportunities.
- Each opportunity serves as a necessary step toward achieving one's goals.
- Proactive engagement with opportunities is essential for upward progress.
