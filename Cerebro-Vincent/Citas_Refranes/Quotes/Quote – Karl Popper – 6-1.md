> We cannot know the future; we can only know that there are possibilities.

— [[Person – Karl Popper]]

## Interpretation
- The future is inherently uncertain and cannot be predicted with absolute certainty.
- What we can grasp are the various potential outcomes that may arise.
- Our understanding is limited to possibilities rather than certainties about what will happen.
