> Economic justice cannot be achieved without addressing gender disparities.

— [[Person – Susan Moller Okin]]

## Interpretation
- Economic justice is interconnected with gender equality.
- Ignoring gender disparities undermines the pursuit of fair economic systems.
- Achieving true economic equity requires a holistic approach that includes gender issues.
