> Public choice theory recognizes that political agents, like economic agents, act in their own self-interest."

— [[Person – James M. Buchanan]]

## Interpretation
- Political agents are motivated by personal gain, similar to how economic agents operate.
- This perspective challenges the notion that public officials always act for the public good.
- Public choice theory applies economic principles to political behavior, emphasizing self-interest.
