> We’re not just here to make a living. We’re here to make a life.

— [[Person – Ronald Wilson Reagan]]

## Interpretation
- Life should be about more than just financial survival.
- True fulfillment comes from pursuing meaningful experiences and relationships.
- We should strive to create a life rich in purpose and joy.
