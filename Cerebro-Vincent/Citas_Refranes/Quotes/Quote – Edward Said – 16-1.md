> The relationship between the West and the East is one of power and resistance."

— [[Person – Edward Said]]

## Interpretation
- The West often exerts dominance over the East, reflecting a colonial mindset.
- The East, in turn, resists this power dynamic, striving for autonomy and self-definition.
- This relationship highlights the complexities of cultural and political interactions between different regions.
