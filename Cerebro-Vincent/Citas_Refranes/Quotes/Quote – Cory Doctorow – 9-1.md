> It’s not enough to just not be evil; you have to be good.

— [[Person – Cory Doctorow]]

## Interpretation
- Merely avoiding wrongdoing is insufficient; active goodness is required.
- Ethical behavior goes beyond the absence of harm to include positive actions.
- A moral stance demands proactive engagement in promoting well-being.
