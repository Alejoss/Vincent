> Everyone thinks of changing the world, but no one thinks of changing himself.

— [[Person – Leo Tolstoy]]

## Interpretation
- People often desire to make a significant impact on the world around them.
- However, true change begins with self-reflection and personal growth.
- Many overlook the importance of internal transformation as a precursor to external change.
