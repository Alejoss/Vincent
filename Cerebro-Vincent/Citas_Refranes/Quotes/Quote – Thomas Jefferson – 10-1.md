> I like the dreams of the future better than the history of the past.

— [[Person – Thomas Jefferson]]

## Interpretation
- Jefferson prioritizes future aspirations over past experiences.
- He implies that the potential for progress is more valuable than historical achievements.
- This perspective encourages optimism and innovation in shaping the future.
