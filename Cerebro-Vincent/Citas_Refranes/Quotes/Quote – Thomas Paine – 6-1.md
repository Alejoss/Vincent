> A long habit of not thinking a thing wrong gives it a superficial appearance of being right.

— [[Person – Thomas Paine]]

## Interpretation
- Repeated acceptance of an idea can lead to a false sense of its correctness.
- People often conform to norms without questioning their morality or validity.
- Critical thinking is essential to challenge established beliefs and practices.
