> The only purpose for which power can be rightly exercised over any member of a civilized community, against his will, is to prevent harm to others.

— [[Person – John Locke]]

## Interpretation
- Power should be used only to protect individuals from harm caused by others.
- Authority must be justified by the intention to promote the common good.
- Exercising power against someone's will is only acceptable when it safeguards others' rights.
