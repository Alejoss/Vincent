> We cannot simply wait for a revolution; we must actively participate in creating it."

— [[Person – Cornelius Castoriadis]]

## Interpretation
- Revolution requires proactive involvement rather than passive anticipation.
- Active participation is essential for meaningful change.
- Individuals hold the power to shape their own societal transformations.
