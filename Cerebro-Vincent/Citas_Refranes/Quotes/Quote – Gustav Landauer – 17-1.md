> The spirit of the people is stronger than any state.

— [[Person – Gustav Landauer]]

## Interpretation
- The collective will and determination of the populace can surpass governmental power.
- True strength lies in the unity and spirit of individuals rather than institutional authority.
- This statement emphasizes the resilience and agency of people in the face of oppressive systems.
