> Every man is, or should be, the architect of his own fortune.

— [[Person – William Godwin]]

## Interpretation
- Individuals have the power and responsibility to shape their own lives.
- Personal agency and self-determination are crucial for achieving success.
- Fortune is not solely a result of luck; it can be constructed through effort and choices.
