> We need to reclaim our right to privacy and autonomy in the digital age."

— [[Person – Shoshana Zuboff]]

## Interpretation
- The quote emphasizes the importance of individual privacy in a world increasingly dominated by digital surveillance.
- It highlights the need for individuals to assert control over their personal information and autonomy.
- Zuboff suggests that reclaiming privacy is essential for maintaining democratic values in the digital era.
