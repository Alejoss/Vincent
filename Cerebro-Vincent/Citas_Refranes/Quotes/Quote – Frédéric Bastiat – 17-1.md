> The moral law is that which is based on the nature of man and the nature of his relations with others.

— [[Person – Frédéric Bastiat]]

## Interpretation
- The moral law is inherently connected to human nature.
- It emphasizes the importance of interpersonal relationships in determining morality.
- Understanding morality requires an awareness of both individual and societal dynamics.
