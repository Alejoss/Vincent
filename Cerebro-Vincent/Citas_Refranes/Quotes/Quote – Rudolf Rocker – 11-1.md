> Anarchism is not a vision of a future society, but a way of living in the present."

— [[Person – Rudolf Rocker]]

## Interpretation
- Anarchism emphasizes the importance of current actions and choices over future ideals.
- It advocates for living in accordance with its principles in everyday life rather than waiting for a perfect society.
- This perspective highlights the significance of personal and collective freedom in the present moment.
