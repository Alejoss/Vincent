> The most important thing about the future is that it is not a gift; it is an achievement."

— [[Person – Daniel Bell]]

## Interpretation
- The future requires effort and intention to shape rather than being passively received.
- Success in the future is the result of actions taken in the present.
- It emphasizes personal responsibility and proactive engagement in creating outcomes.
