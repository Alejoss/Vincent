> The importance of the arts in education cannot be overstated; they shape our understanding of ourselves and others."

— [[Person – Martha Nussbaum]]

## Interpretation
- The arts play a crucial role in personal identity formation.
- Engagement with the arts fosters empathy and understanding of diverse perspectives.
- Education in the arts enriches both individual and societal awareness.
