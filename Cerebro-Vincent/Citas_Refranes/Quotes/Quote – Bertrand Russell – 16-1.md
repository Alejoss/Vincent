> To fear love is to fear life, and those who fear life are already three parts dead.

— [[Person – Bertrand Russell]]

## Interpretation
- Fear of love signifies a deeper fear of living fully and authentically.
- Those who shy away from love are missing a vital aspect of human experience.
- Living in fear diminishes one's vitality and connection to life.
