> The history of mankind is a history of mutual aid and cooperation.

— [[Person – Peter Kropotkin]]

## Interpretation
- Human history is predominantly characterized by collaboration among individuals and groups.
- Mutual aid has been a crucial factor in the survival and progress of societies.
- This perspective challenges the notion that competition is the primary driver of human development.
