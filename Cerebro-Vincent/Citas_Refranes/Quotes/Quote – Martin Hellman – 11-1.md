> Privacy is not a luxury; it is a necessity."

— [[Person – Martin Hellman]]

## Interpretation
- Privacy is essential for personal autonomy and freedom.
- Without privacy, individuals are vulnerable to manipulation and control.
- Recognizing privacy as a necessity underscores its importance in a free society.
