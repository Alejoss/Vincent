> The Constitution is not a document to be revered, but a living guide to be interpreted."

— [[Person – Dahlia Lithwick]]

## Interpretation
- The Constitution should be seen as adaptable to contemporary issues rather than a static text.
- Interpretation allows for evolving understandings of rights and governance.
- Reverence can hinder practical application and necessary changes in law.
