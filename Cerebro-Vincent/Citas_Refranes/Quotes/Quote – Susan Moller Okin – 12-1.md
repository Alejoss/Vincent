> The notion of justice cannot be complete without addressing the injustices faced by women.

— [[Person – Susan Moller Okin]]

## Interpretation
- Justice is inherently linked to the experiences and rights of women.
- Ignoring women's injustices undermines the overall concept of justice.
- A comprehensive understanding of justice must include gender equity.
