> The struggle for freedom is the struggle against the state.

— [[Person – Peter Kropotkin]]

## Interpretation
- The pursuit of individual liberty often necessitates resistance to governmental authority.
- Kropotkin suggests that the state inherently restricts personal freedoms and autonomy.
- True freedom can only be achieved by dismantling oppressive state structures.
