> We need to cultivate empathy and compassion in order to build a better society."

— [[Person – Martha Nussbaum]]

## Interpretation
- Empathy and compassion are essential for understanding others' experiences.
- Cultivating these qualities can lead to positive social change.
- A better society is built on interpersonal connections and care for one another.
