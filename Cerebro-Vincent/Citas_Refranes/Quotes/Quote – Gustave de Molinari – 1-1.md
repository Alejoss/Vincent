> The state is the great fiction by which everybody seeks to live at the expense of everybody else."

— [[Person – Gustave de Molinari]]

## Interpretation
- The state is viewed as an artificial construct that facilitates exploitation among individuals.
- People rely on state mechanisms to benefit from the resources and labor of others.
- This perspective critiques the ethical implications of how societal structures can lead to parasitism rather than mutual support.
