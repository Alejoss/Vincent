> The state is a social institution that is a product of the collective effort of human beings.

— [[Person – Emma Goldman]]

## Interpretation
- The state is not a natural entity but rather created through human collaboration.
- It emphasizes the role of collective human agency in shaping societal structures.
- This perspective suggests that the state can be transformed by the collective will of the people.
