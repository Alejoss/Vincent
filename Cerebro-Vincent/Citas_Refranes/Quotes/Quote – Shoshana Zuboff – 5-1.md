> Data is the new oil, but it’s also the new pollution."

— [[Person – Shoshana Zuboff]]

## Interpretation
- Data has immense value and can drive innovation and economic growth similar to oil.
- However, the misuse and over-collection of data can lead to negative consequences akin to environmental pollution.
- This duality highlights the need for careful management and ethical considerations in data practices.
