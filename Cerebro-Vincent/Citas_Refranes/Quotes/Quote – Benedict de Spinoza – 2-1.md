> Intelligence is not the ability to store information, but to know where to find it.

— [[Person – Benedict de Spinoza]]

## Interpretation
- Intelligence is defined by the ability to access and utilize knowledge effectively.
- Merely storing information does not equate to true understanding or wisdom.
- The emphasis is on resourcefulness and the strategic use of knowledge.
