> Science is organized knowledge. Wisdom is organized life.

— [[Person – Immanuel Kant]]

## Interpretation
- Science represents a systematic approach to understanding the natural world through empirical evidence.
- Wisdom embodies the application of knowledge to navigate and enrich human experiences.
- Kant highlights the distinction between theoretical understanding (science) and practical application (wisdom) in life.
