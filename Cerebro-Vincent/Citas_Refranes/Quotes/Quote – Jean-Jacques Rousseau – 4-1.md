> The general will is always right, but the judgment of the people is often wrong.

— [[Person – Jean-Jacques Rousseau]]

## Interpretation
- The general will represents the collective interest of the community, which Rousseau believes is inherently correct.
- The judgment of the people, often influenced by emotions or misinformation, can lead to misguided conclusions.
- This quote emphasizes the tension between collective wisdom and individual opinion in democratic governance.
