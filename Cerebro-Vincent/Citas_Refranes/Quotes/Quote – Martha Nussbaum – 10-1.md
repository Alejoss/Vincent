> To live well is to be able to choose the life that one has reason to value."

— [[Person – Martha Nussbaum]]

## Interpretation
- Living well involves having the autonomy to make informed choices.
- Value-based reasoning is essential in determining what constitutes a good life.
- The quote emphasizes the importance of personal agency in achieving fulfillment.
