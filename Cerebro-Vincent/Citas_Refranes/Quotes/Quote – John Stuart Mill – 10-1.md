> The peculiar evil of silencing the expression of an opinion is, that it is robbing the human race.

— [[Person – John Stuart Mill]]

## Interpretation
- Silencing opinions deprives society of diverse perspectives and ideas.
- Each opinion has intrinsic value that contributes to the collective knowledge of humanity.
- Censorship limits progress and understanding by preventing discourse and debate.
