> The law is not a set of rules, but a set of practices."

— [[Person – Lawrence Lessig]]

## Interpretation
- Law is shaped by the actions and behaviors of individuals within society.
- It emphasizes the dynamic and evolving nature of legal frameworks rather than a static set of rules.
- Practices reflect societal values and norms, indicating that law is a living entity influenced by culture.
