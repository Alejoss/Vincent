> I am the sole judge of my own worth.

— [[Person – Max Stirner]]

## Interpretation
- The individual holds the ultimate authority over their self-assessment.
- Personal worth is subjective and defined by one's own standards.
- This perspective emphasizes self-empowerment and autonomy in value judgment.
