> We have a responsibility to speak out against injustices, even when they are committed by our own government.

— [[Person – Noam Chomsky]]

## Interpretation
- Citizens have a moral obligation to advocate for justice.
- Criticism of one's own government is essential for a healthy democracy.
- Silence in the face of wrongdoing perpetuates injustice.
