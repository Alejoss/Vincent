> An injury to one is an injury to all."

— [[Person – Lucy Parsons]]

## Interpretation
- The well-being of one individual is interconnected with the well-being of the community.
- Solidarity and collective responsibility are essential for social justice.
- A harm to any member of a group affects the entire group, emphasizing unity in struggle.
