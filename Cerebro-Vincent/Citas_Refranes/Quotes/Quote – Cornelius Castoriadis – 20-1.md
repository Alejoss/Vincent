> A society that is not open to change is a society that is doomed to stagnation."

— [[Person – Cornelius Castoriadis]]

## Interpretation
- A society resistant to change fails to evolve and adapt to new challenges.
- Stagnation can lead to decline, as innovation and progress are essential for a thriving society.
- Openness to change is crucial for growth and the overall vitality of a community.
