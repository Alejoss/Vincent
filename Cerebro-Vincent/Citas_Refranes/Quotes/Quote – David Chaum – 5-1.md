> The future of digital identity relies on privacy-preserving technologies."

— [[Person – David Chaum]]

## Interpretation
- Digital identity is increasingly important in our interconnected world.
- Privacy-preserving technologies are essential to protect individuals' personal information.
- The future of identity management must prioritize user privacy to gain trust and ensure security.
