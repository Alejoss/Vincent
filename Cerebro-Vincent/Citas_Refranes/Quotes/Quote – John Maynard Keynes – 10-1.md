> The ideas of economists and political philosophers, both when they are right and when they are wrong, are more powerful than is commonly understood

— [[Person – John Maynard Keynes]]

## Interpretation
- Economic and political ideas shape society in profound ways, regardless of their accuracy.
- The influence of these ideas often extends beyond what is typically recognized.
- Both correct and incorrect theories can drive significant societal change.
