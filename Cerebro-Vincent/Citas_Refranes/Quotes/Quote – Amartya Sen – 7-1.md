> The capability approach is a way of thinking about human well-being."

— [[Person – Amartya Sen]]

## Interpretation
- The capability approach emphasizes the importance of individual freedoms and opportunities in assessing well-being.
- It shifts the focus from mere economic metrics to what people can actually achieve in their lives.
- This framework encourages a comprehensive understanding of welfare that includes various dimensions of human life.
