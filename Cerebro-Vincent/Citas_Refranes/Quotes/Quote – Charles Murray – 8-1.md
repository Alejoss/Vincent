> Intelligence is not the only factor that determines success in life."

— [[Person – Charles Murray]]

## Interpretation
- Success is influenced by various factors beyond intelligence.
- Personal attributes such as perseverance, social skills, and opportunity also play significant roles.
- Understanding the multifaceted nature of success can lead to a more comprehensive view of achievement.
