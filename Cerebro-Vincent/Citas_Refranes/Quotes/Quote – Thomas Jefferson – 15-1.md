> I’m a greater believer in luck, and I find the harder I work the more I have of it.

— [[Person – Thomas Jefferson]]

## Interpretation
- Luck is not merely a chance occurrence but can be influenced by one's efforts.
- Hard work increases the likelihood of favorable outcomes and opportunities.
- Belief in luck is intertwined with personal initiative and perseverance.
