> I am not afraid of storms, for I am learning how to sail my ship.

— [[Person – Mary Wollstonecraft]]

## Interpretation
- The speaker embraces challenges as opportunities for personal growth.
- Learning and resilience are essential in navigating life's difficulties.
- Facing fears is a part of the journey towards self-improvement and empowerment.
