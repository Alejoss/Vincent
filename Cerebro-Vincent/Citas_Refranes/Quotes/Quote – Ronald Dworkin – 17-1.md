> A legal system that fails to promote justice is a legal system that has lost its legitimacy."

— [[Person – Ronald Dworkin]]

## Interpretation
- A legal system must uphold justice to maintain its authority and respect.
- The legitimacy of law is contingent upon its ability to deliver fair outcomes.
- Without justice, laws lose their moral foundation and societal acceptance.
