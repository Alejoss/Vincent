> A free society is one in which individuals are free to pursue their own happiness."

— [[Person – Samuel Edward Konkin III]]

## Interpretation
- A free society prioritizes individual autonomy and self-determination.
- Happiness is subjective, emphasizing the importance of personal choice.
- The pursuit of happiness is a fundamental right in a truly free society.
