> Entrepreneurship is the act of bringing together resources in a way that creates value."

— [[Person – Jean-Baptiste Say]]

## Interpretation
- Entrepreneurship involves the strategic combination of various resources.
- The primary goal of entrepreneurship is to generate value for society or the economy.
- Effective resource management is essential for successful entrepreneurial ventures.
