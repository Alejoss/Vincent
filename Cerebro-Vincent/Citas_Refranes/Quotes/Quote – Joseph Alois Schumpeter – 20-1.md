> The entrepreneur is not just a risk-taker; he is also a creative thinker.

— [[Person – Joseph Alois Schumpeter]]

## Interpretation
- Entrepreneurs embrace uncertainty but also innovate to create new opportunities.
- Risk-taking is essential, but it is the creative thinking that drives sustainable success.
- Creativity allows entrepreneurs to differentiate themselves and adapt in dynamic markets.
