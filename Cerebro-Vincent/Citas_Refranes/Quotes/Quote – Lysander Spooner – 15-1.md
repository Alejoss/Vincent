> Freedom is the right to do what we ought to do."

— [[Person – Lysander Spooner]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom is not merely the absence of restraint, but the ability to act in accordance with moral duties.
- True freedom is tied to ethical responsibilities rather than mere personal desires.
- The essence of freedom lies in our capacity to choose actions that align with our obligations to ourselves and others.
