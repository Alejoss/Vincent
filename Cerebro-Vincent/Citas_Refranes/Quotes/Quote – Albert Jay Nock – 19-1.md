> Liberty is not the absence of restraint, but the presence of opportunity.

— [[Person – Albert Jay Nock]]

## Interpretation
- Liberty involves more than just freedom from constraints; it requires enabling conditions for individual potential.
- True freedom is about having the resources and opportunities to pursue one's goals.
- Restraint can exist alongside liberty if it fosters a greater capacity for personal growth and achievement.
