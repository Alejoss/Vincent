> A person is not a means to an end, but an end in themselves.

— [[Person – Immanuel Kant]]

## Interpretation
- Individuals possess intrinsic value and should be respected as such.
- Using someone merely as a tool undermines their dignity and autonomy.
- This principle emphasizes moral responsibility in our interactions with others.
