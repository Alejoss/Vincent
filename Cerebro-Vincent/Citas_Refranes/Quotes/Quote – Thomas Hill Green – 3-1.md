> Freedom is not merely the absence of restraint but the presence of opportunities for self-realization.

— [[Person – Thomas Hill Green]]

## Interpretation
- True freedom involves having the means and opportunities to pursue one's potential.
- Absence of restraint alone does not equate to being free; one must also have the capacity to act on their desires.
- Self-realization is a fundamental aspect of a fulfilling and meaningful life.
