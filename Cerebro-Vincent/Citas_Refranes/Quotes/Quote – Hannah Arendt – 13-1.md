> The human condition is characterized by the fact that we are all capable of both great good and great evil.

— [[Person – Hannah Arendt]]

## Interpretation
- Human beings possess a dual capacity for moral action, enabling both altruism and malevolence.
- This inherent duality reflects the complex nature of human choices and actions.
- Our potential for both good and evil emphasizes the importance of ethical responsibility in society.
