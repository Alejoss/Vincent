> In a world of diversity, we need to learn to respect differences and to find common ground."

— [[Person – Martha Nussbaum]]

## Interpretation
- Respecting differences is essential in a diverse society.
- Finding common ground fosters unity and cooperation.
- Diversity should be viewed as a strength rather than a division.
