> The revolution is not a one-time event; it is a continuous process of change.

— [[Person – Peter Kropotkin]]

## Interpretation
- Revolution requires ongoing effort and engagement rather than a singular moment.
- It emphasizes the importance of sustained activism and social change.
- Change is a dynamic and evolving process that reflects the ongoing struggles of society.
