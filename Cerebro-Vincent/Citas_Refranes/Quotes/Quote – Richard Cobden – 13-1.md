> The best way to promote the welfare of mankind is to promote the welfare of the individual."

— [[Person – Richard Cobden]]

## Interpretation
- Promoting individual welfare leads to collective progress.
- Each person's well-being contributes to the overall health of society.
- Empowerment of individuals is key to achieving broader humanitarian goals.
