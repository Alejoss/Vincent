> Knowledge is a tool for social change."

— [[Person – Patricia Hill Collins]]

## Interpretation
- Knowledge empowers individuals and communities to challenge injustices.
- Access to information can lead to informed activism and societal transformation.
- Education and awareness are essential for fostering critical consciousness and driving progress.
