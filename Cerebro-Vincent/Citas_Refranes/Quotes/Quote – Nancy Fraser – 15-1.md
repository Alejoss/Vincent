> The voices of those who bear the brunt of injustice must be front and center in any discussions of change."

— [[Person – Nancy Fraser]]

## Interpretation
- Injustice affects specific groups more severely, and their perspectives are crucial for meaningful change.
- Ignoring the experiences of those most impacted by injustice leads to incomplete or ineffective solutions.
- Change initiatives must prioritize the input and needs of marginalized voices to be truly transformative.
