> I have no special talent. I am only passionately curious.

— [[Person – Karl Popper]]

## Interpretation
- Popper emphasizes the value of curiosity over innate talent.
- He suggests that passion and curiosity can drive significant intellectual achievements.
- This implies that anyone can pursue knowledge regardless of their initial capabilities.
