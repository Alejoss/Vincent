> The best way to protect your privacy is to be open about it.

— [[Person – Cory Doctorow]]

## Interpretation
- Being transparent about privacy concerns can foster trust and understanding.
- Openness can serve as a form of empowerment against surveillance.
- Discussing privacy openly encourages collective action and awareness.
