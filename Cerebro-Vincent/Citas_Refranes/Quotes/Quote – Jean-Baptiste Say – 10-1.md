> The entrepreneur is the innovator who brings new ideas to market."

— [[Person – Jean-Baptiste Say]]

## Interpretation
- The entrepreneur plays a critical role in the economy by translating innovative ideas into products or services.
- Innovation is essential for progress and competitive advantage in the market.
- The act of bringing new ideas to market signifies risk-taking and creativity inherent in entrepreneurship.
