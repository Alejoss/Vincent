> Our current economic system fails to recognize the value of care work."

— [[Person – David Graeber]]

## Interpretation
- The economic system prioritizes profit over essential caregiving roles.
- Care work, often undervalued, is crucial for societal well-being and stability.
- Recognizing care work's value could lead to a more equitable and sustainable economy.
