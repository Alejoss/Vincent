> Justice as fairness seeks to provide a framework for addressing the injustices of society.

— [[Person – John Rawls]]

## Interpretation
- Justice as fairness aims to create a just society by addressing inequalities.
- It emphasizes the need for a structured approach to rectify societal wrongs.
- The framework is designed to ensure that all individuals are treated equitably.
