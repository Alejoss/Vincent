> What you see and what you hear depends a great deal on where you are standing. It also depends on what sort of person you are.

— [[Person – Clive Staples Lewis]]

## Interpretation
- Perspective shapes our understanding of the world around us.
- Personal experiences and identity influence our perceptions.
- Context is crucial in interpreting information and experiences.
