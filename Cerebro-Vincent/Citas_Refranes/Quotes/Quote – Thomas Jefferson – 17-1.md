> The way to gain a good reputation is to endeavor to be what you desire to appear.

— [[Person – Thomas Jefferson]]

## Interpretation
- To build a solid reputation, one must align actions with desired perceptions.
- Authenticity and integrity are essential in shaping how others view us.
- Focusing on personal growth and character is key to achieving a positive image.
