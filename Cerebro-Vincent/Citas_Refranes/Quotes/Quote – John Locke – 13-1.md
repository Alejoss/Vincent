> Every man who claims a right to himself, can only exercise that right in the realm of his own self.

— [[Person – John Locke]]

## Interpretation
- Individual autonomy is fundamental to personal identity.
- Self-ownership implies personal responsibility and self-governance.
- The exercise of rights is confined to one's own sphere of existence.
