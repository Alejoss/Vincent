> The act of judgment is the act of understanding the world.

— [[Person – Hannah Arendt]]

## Interpretation
- Judgment requires an engagement with the complexities of the world.
- Understanding is an active process that shapes our perceptions and decisions.
- The ability to judge reflects our capacity to navigate moral and ethical landscapes.
