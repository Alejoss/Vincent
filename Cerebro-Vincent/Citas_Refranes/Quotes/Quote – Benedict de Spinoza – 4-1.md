> A free man thinks of nothing less than of death, and his wisdom is a meditation not on death but on life.

— [[Person – Benedict de Spinoza]]

## Interpretation
- A truly free individual prioritizes living fully over fearing death.
- Wisdom comes from engaging with the present and the vitality of life rather than fixating on mortality.
- Emphasizing life leads to a deeper understanding of existence rather than an obsession with its end.
