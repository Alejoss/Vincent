> Security is a continuous process, not a one-time thing.

— [[Person – Bruce Schneier]]

## Interpretation
- Security requires ongoing effort and vigilance rather than a single solution.
- Threats and vulnerabilities evolve, necessitating continuous adaptation.
- Individuals and organizations must regularly assess and update their security measures.
