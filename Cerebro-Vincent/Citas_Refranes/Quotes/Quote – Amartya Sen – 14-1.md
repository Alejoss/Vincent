> The diversity of human experience is a source of strength for societies."

— [[Person – Amartya Sen]]

## Interpretation
- Diverse experiences enrich societal perspectives and enhance problem-solving capabilities.
- Cultural and experiential variety fosters resilience and adaptability in communities.
- Embracing diversity can lead to more inclusive and equitable social structures.
