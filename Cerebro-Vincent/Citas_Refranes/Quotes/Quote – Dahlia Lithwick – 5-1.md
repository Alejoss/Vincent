> The power of the law lies in its ability to adapt to the changing needs of society."

— [[Person – Dahlia Lithwick]]

## Interpretation
- The law is dynamic and must evolve alongside societal changes.
- Adaptability ensures that the law remains relevant and effective.
- A responsive legal system reflects the values and needs of its community.
