> The priority of justice means that justice takes precedence over other social goods.

— [[Person – John Rawls]]

## Interpretation
- Justice is the fundamental principle that should guide societal structure.
- Other social goods, such as wealth or power, should be secondary to ensuring fairness and equity.
- Prioritizing justice fosters a more just and equitable society for all individuals.
