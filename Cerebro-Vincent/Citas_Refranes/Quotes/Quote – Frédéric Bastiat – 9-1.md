> Government is the great fiction through which everybody endeavors to live at the expense of everybody else.

— [[Person – Frédéric Bastiat]]

## Interpretation
- Bastiat critiques the notion of government as a means for individuals to benefit at others' expense.
- The quote suggests that government can create a dependency on the redistribution of resources.
- It highlights the idea of self-interest driving people's interactions with government.
