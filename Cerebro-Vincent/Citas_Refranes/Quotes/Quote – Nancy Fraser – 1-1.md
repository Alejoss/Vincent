> The challenge is to find ways to articulate the interests of marginalized groups without simply replicating the same hierarchies of power that have long characterized social movements."

— [[Person – Nancy Fraser]]

## Interpretation
- It is essential to advocate for marginalized groups while avoiding the reinforcement of existing power structures.
- Social movements must evolve to ensure inclusivity and equity rather than replicating past hierarchies.
- Finding effective ways to amplify marginalized voices is crucial for genuine social change.
