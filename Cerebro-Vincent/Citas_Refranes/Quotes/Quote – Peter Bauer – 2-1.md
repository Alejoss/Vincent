> Poverty is not a lack of resources; it is a lack of ideas."

— [[Person – Peter Bauer]]

## Interpretation
- Poverty can be addressed through innovative thinking rather than merely increasing resource allocation.
- The quote suggests that a lack of creativity and solutions contributes to the persistence of poverty.
- It emphasizes the importance of ideas and strategies in overcoming economic challenges.
