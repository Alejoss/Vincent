> Feminism must challenge not only the public sphere but also the private sphere.

— [[Person – Susan Moller Okin]]

## Interpretation
- Feminism should address inequalities in both public and private life.
- The private sphere, often seen as personal, is also a site of power dynamics.
- Challenging traditional roles in the home is essential for achieving gender equality.
