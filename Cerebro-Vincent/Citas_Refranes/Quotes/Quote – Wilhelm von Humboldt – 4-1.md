> The aim of all education is to develop the individual.

— [[Person – Wilhelm von Humboldt]]

## Interpretation
- Education should focus on nurturing personal growth and self-discovery.
- The development of the individual is central to the purpose of learning.
- True education empowers individuals to reach their fullest potential.
