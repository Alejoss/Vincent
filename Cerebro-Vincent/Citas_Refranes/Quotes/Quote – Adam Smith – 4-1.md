> The invisible hand of the market guides individuals to unintentionally promote the public good.

— [[Person – Adam Smith]]

## Interpretation
- Individuals pursuing their own self-interest can lead to positive outcomes for society as a whole.
- Market forces operate in a way that individuals' actions contribute to the collective welfare without intentional effort.
- The concept emphasizes the importance of free markets in achieving societal benefits.
