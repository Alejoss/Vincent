> In a world of conformity, the true individual is a rebel."

— [[Person – Hakim Bey]]

## Interpretation
- The quote suggests that individuality is often met with resistance in a conformist society.
- It implies that true self-expression and authenticity can only be achieved through rebellion against societal norms.
- Being a rebel is portrayed as a necessary stance for those who wish to assert their uniqueness.
