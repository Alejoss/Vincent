> There is no such thing as a peaceful revolution."

— [[Person – Lucy Parsons]]

## Interpretation
- Revolutions inherently involve conflict and struggle, challenging the status quo.
- The idea of a 'peaceful revolution' may be an illusion, as significant change often comes with resistance.
- True change often necessitates disruption and upheaval, making peace unlikely during such transitions.
