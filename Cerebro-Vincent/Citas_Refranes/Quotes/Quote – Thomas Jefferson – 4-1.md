> I have sworn upon the altar of God, eternal hostility against every form of tyranny over the mind of man.

— [[Person – Thomas Jefferson]]

## Interpretation
- Jefferson expresses a deep commitment to opposing any oppression that restricts intellectual freedom.
- The phrase 'altar of God' suggests that this fight against tyranny is a moral and spiritual duty.
- The use of 'eternal hostility' emphasizes the intensity and permanence of his resolve.
