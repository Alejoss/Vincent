> We must not only dream of a better world but actively work to create it."

— [[Person – Derrick Bell]]

## Interpretation
- Dreaming of a better world is essential for inspiration and vision.
- Active engagement and effort are required to turn dreams into reality.
- Change is a collective responsibility that necessitates action rather than passive hope.
