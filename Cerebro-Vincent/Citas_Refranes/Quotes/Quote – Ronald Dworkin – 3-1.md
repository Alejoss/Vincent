> Law is not just a set of rules; it is a form of political morality."

— [[Person – Ronald Dworkin]]

## Interpretation
- Law encompasses more than mere regulations; it reflects deeper ethical values.
- The essence of law is intertwined with moral principles that guide societal behavior.
- Understanding law requires recognizing its role in shaping and reflecting political ideals.
