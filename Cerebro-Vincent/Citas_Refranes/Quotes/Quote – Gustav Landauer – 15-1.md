> Anarchism is the highest form of humanism.

— [[Person – Gustav Landauer]]

## Interpretation
- Anarchism advocates for a society based on voluntary cooperation and mutual aid, emphasizing human dignity.
- It challenges hierarchical structures that diminish individual freedom and potential.
- Landauer suggests that true humanism flourishes in a context where individuals are free from oppressive systems.
