> The world of reality has its limits; the world of imagination is boundless.

— [[Person – Jean-Jacques Rousseau]]

## Interpretation
- Reality is constrained by tangible limits and physical laws.
- Imagination, in contrast, allows for limitless possibilities and creativity.
- This highlights the value of imagination in transcending the boundaries of reality.
