> The history of the world is the history of the powerful.

— [[Person – Howard Zinn]]

## Interpretation
- The quote emphasizes that historical narratives are often shaped by those in power.
- It suggests that the experiences and struggles of the marginalized are frequently overlooked.
- Zinn implies that understanding history requires a critical examination of who gets to tell the story.
