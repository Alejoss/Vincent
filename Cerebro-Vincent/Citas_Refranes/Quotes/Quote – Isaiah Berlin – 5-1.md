> The idea of freedom is a great one, but it has to be understood in its complexity.

— [[Person – Isaiah Berlin]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom is a multifaceted concept that cannot be reduced to a single definition.
- Understanding freedom requires consideration of its various dimensions and implications.
- Recognizing the complexity of freedom helps in navigating its challenges and responsibilities.
