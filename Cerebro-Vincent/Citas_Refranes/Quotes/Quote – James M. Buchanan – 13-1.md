> Voters are not always well-informed, and this can lead to suboptimal outcomes in public policy."

— [[Person – James M. Buchanan]]

## Interpretation
- Voter knowledge is often limited, impacting decision-making.
- Lack of information can result in ineffective or harmful public policies.
- The quality of democracy is affected by the informational deficits of the electorate.
