> The best way to fight against the state is to create a social environment that is free from it.

— [[Person – Gustav Landauer]]

## Interpretation
- Challenging the state requires proactive community-building rather than direct confrontation.
- Creating alternative social structures can diminish the influence of the state.
- A focus on grassroots initiatives fosters autonomy and self-governance.
