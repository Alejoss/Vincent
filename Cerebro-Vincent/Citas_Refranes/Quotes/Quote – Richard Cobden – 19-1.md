> The true source of wealth is in the labor of the people."

— [[Person – Richard Cobden]]

## Interpretation
- Wealth is generated through the efforts and productivity of individuals.
- The value of labor is central to economic prosperity and societal advancement.
- Recognizing the importance of the workforce is crucial for understanding economic dynamics.
