> The individual is the unit of analysis in the economic theory of public choice."

— [[Person – James M. Buchanan]]

## Interpretation
- The quote emphasizes that individuals are the foundational elements in understanding economic decisions and behaviors.
- It highlights the importance of individual choices and preferences in the realm of public policy.
- The focus on the individual suggests that collective outcomes in economics arise from the aggregation of individual actions.
