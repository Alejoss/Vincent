> Bitcoin is an experiment in monetary and social systems, not just technology.

— [[Person – Satoshi Nakamoto]]

## Interpretation
- Bitcoin challenges traditional monetary systems by introducing a decentralized approach to currency.
- The experiment encompasses social dynamics, exploring how individuals interact within a new economic framework.
- It highlights the interplay between technology and societal structures, suggesting that innovation can reshape both.
