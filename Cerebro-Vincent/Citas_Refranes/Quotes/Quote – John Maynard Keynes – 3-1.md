> In the long run, we are all dead.

— [[Person – John Maynard Keynes]]

## Interpretation
- Keynes emphasizes the futility of long-term economic predictions, as they may not address immediate human needs.
- The quote suggests that focusing solely on the long-term can overlook pressing issues in the present.
- It reflects a pragmatic approach to economics, highlighting the importance of addressing current challenges rather than deferring them.
