> Our personal data has become a tool for manipulation and control."

— [[Person – Shoshana Zuboff]]

## Interpretation
- Personal data is exploited by companies to influence individual behavior.
- The commodification of personal information raises ethical concerns about privacy.
- This manipulation can undermine autonomy and informed decision-making.
