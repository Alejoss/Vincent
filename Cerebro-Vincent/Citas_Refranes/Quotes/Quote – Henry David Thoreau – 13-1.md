> There is no remedy for love but to love more.

— [[Person – Henry David Thoreau]]

## Interpretation
- Love is a powerful force that cannot be easily resolved or cured.
- The only solution to the complexities of love is to embrace it more deeply.
- In times of emotional struggle, increasing our capacity for love can be a transformative response.
