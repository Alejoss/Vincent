> The growth of knowledge is a process of elimination of error.

— [[Person – Karl Popper]]

## Interpretation
- Knowledge advances by identifying and discarding falsehoods.
- Error plays a critical role in learning and understanding.
- The scientific method relies on testing and refining ideas to improve accuracy.
