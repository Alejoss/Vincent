> We are all entitled to equality of concern and respect."

— [[Person – Ronald Dworkin]]

## Interpretation
- Everyone deserves to be treated with equal consideration regardless of their background.
- Equality of concern implies a moral obligation to acknowledge the needs and rights of all individuals.
- Respect signifies recognizing the inherent dignity of every person, fostering a more just society.
