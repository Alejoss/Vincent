> Rather than love, than money, than fame, give me truth.

— [[Person – Henry David Thoreau]]

## Interpretation
- Thoreau prioritizes authenticity and honesty over societal values like love, wealth, and recognition.
- The quote reflects a yearning for deeper truths in life rather than superficial pleasures.
- It suggests that truth is a fundamental need for personal fulfillment and integrity.
