> The individual is the measure of all things.

— [[Person – Max Stirner]]

## Interpretation
- The individual holds the ultimate authority in determining value and meaning.
- Personal experience and perception shape one's understanding of reality.
- This perspective emphasizes individual autonomy and subjectivity over collective or societal norms.
