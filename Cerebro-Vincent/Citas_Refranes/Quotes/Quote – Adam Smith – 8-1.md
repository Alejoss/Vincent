> In a free economy, individuals pursuing their own interests can benefit society as a whole.

— [[Person – Adam Smith]]

## Interpretation
- Self-interest drives innovation and efficiency in a free market.
- Individual actions, when combined, can lead to collective benefits for society.
- The pursuit of personal gain can align with the greater good in an economic context.
