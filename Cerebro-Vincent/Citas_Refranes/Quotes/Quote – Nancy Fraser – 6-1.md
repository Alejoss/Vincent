> To achieve genuine equality, we must confront both the economic and the cultural dimensions of oppression."

— [[Person – Nancy Fraser]]

## Interpretation
- Genuine equality requires addressing both financial disparities and cultural biases.
- Economic oppression and cultural oppression are interconnected issues that must be tackled simultaneously.
- A holistic approach is necessary to dismantle systems of inequality effectively.
