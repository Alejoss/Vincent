> The only thing that can save mankind is the recognition of the rights of the individual.

— [[Person – Frédéric Bastiat]]

## Interpretation
- Individual rights are fundamental to the well-being and progress of humanity.
- A society that respects personal freedoms fosters true prosperity and harmony.
- Acknowledging individual rights is essential for addressing broader social issues.
