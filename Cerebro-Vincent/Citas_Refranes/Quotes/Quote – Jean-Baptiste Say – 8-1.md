> In a free market, the price of goods is determined by the relationship between supply and demand."

— [[Person – Jean-Baptiste Say]]

## Interpretation
- In a free market, prices fluctuate based on how much of a good is available and how much consumers want it.
- This relationship reflects the fundamental economic principle of equilibrium between supply and demand.
- The quote underscores the importance of market forces in determining value and price.
