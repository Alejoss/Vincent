> The moral life is not a matter of knowing what is right, but of acting upon it.

— [[Person – Thomas Hill Green]]

## Interpretation
- Moral understanding alone is insufficient without corresponding action.
- True morality is demonstrated through behavior rather than mere knowledge.
- Acting on one's moral beliefs is essential for a fulfilling moral life.
