> A man is rich in proportion to the number of things he can afford to let alone.

— [[Person – Henry David Thoreau]]

## Interpretation
- True wealth is measured not by possessions, but by the ability to resist the urge to acquire more.
- A person's richness is reflected in their self-discipline and contentment with what they have.
- The quote emphasizes the value of simplicity and minimalism in achieving a fulfilling life.
