> Freedom is not a gift to be given, but a right to be taken.

— [[Person – Peter Kropotkin]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom must be actively claimed rather than passively received.
- It emphasizes individual agency and self-determination.
- The quote critiques systems that bestow freedom as a privilege rather than recognizing it as an inherent right.
