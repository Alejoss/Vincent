> I earnestly wish to see the distinction of sex confounded in society, general knowledge diffused, and the social state of both sexes ameliorated.

— [[Person – Mary Wollstonecraft]]

## Interpretation
- Wollstonecraft advocates for the elimination of gender distinctions in society.
- She emphasizes the importance of widespread education for all individuals.
- Her ultimate goal is to improve the social conditions for both men and women.
