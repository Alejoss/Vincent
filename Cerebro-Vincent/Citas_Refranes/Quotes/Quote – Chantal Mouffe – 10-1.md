> The challenge is to create a political framework that allows for the expression of multiple perspectives."

— [[Person – Chantal Mouffe]]

## Interpretation
- Political frameworks should be inclusive of diverse viewpoints.
- Encouraging multiple perspectives can lead to a healthier democracy.
- The expression of various opinions is essential for robust political discourse.
