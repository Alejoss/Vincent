> The principle of mutualism is the principle of freedom.

— [[Person – Pierre-Joseph Proudhon]]

## Interpretation
- Mutualism advocates for cooperative relationships among individuals, promoting autonomy.
- Freedom in this context implies the absence of oppression and the ability for individuals to make choices.
- The principle suggests that true freedom is achieved through mutual aid and solidarity rather than competition.
