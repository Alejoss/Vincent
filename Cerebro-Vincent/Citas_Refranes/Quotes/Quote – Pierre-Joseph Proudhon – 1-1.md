> Property is theft.

— [[Person – Pierre-Joseph Proudhon]]

## Interpretation
- Proudhon argues that the institution of property inherently exploits others by depriving them of access to resources.
- The phrase suggests that ownership creates inequality and injustice in society.
- It challenges the conventional view of property as a natural right, framing it instead as a form of societal theft.
