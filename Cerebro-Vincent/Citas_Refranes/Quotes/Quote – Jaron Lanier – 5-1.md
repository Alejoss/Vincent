> If you’re not paying for the product, you are the product.

— [[Person – Jaron Lanier]]

## Interpretation
- Users of free services often trade their personal data for access, becoming commodities themselves.
- The quote highlights the hidden costs of free products, emphasizing the value of personal information.
- It serves as a warning about the implications of data privacy in a digital economy.
