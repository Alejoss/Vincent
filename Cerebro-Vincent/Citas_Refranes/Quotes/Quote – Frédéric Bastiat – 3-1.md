> Life, liberty, and property do not exist because men have made laws. On the contrary, it was the fact that life, liberty, and property existed beforehand that caused men to make laws in the first place.

— [[Person – Frédéric Bastiat]]

## Interpretation
- Laws are a response to pre-existing natural rights rather than their source.
- The existence of life, liberty, and property is fundamental and inherent to humanity.
- Legal systems should protect these rights rather than create them.
