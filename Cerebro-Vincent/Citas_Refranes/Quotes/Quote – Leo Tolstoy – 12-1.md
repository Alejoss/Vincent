> The stronger a man is, the more gentle he can afford to be.

— [[Person – Leo Tolstoy]]

## Interpretation
- True strength is characterized by the ability to show gentleness and compassion.
- Gentleness is not a sign of weakness but rather a reflection of inner strength.
- A strong person can navigate challenges without resorting to aggression or harshness.
