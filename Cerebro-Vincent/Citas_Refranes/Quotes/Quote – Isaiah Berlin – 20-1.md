> The richness of human experience is found in its contradictions and complexities.

— [[Person – Isaiah Berlin]]

## Interpretation
- Human experience is multifaceted and cannot be easily categorized.
- Contradictions and complexities enrich our understanding of life.
- Embracing ambiguity is essential to grasp the full spectrum of human existence.
