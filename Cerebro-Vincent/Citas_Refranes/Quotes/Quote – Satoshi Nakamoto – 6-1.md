> The system works without a trusted third party.

— [[Person – Satoshi Nakamoto]]

## Interpretation
- The quote emphasizes the decentralization of the system, allowing transactions to occur directly between parties.
- It highlights the elimination of intermediaries, reducing the need for trust in third parties.
- This innovation promotes transparency and security in transactions.
