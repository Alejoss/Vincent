> A great deal of the so-called 'progress' consists in the animalization of man."

— [[Person – Isabel Paterson]]

## Interpretation
- Progress is often misinterpreted as a move towards greater civilization when it may actually reflect a decline in human dignity.
- The term 'animalization' suggests a regression to base instincts, undermining higher rational and moral capacities.
- This perspective critiques modern society's values, implying that advancements may lead to a loss of humanity rather than its enhancement.
