> The most lively thought is still inferior to the dullest sensation.

— [[Person – David Hume]]

## Interpretation
- Hume suggests that direct sensory experiences are more impactful than abstract thoughts.
- Even the simplest sensations hold more significance than the most vivid ideas.
- This highlights the primacy of empirical experience in shaping human understanding.
