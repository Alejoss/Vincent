> In the end, the law is an instrument of property.

— [[Person – Pierre-Joseph Proudhon]]

## Interpretation
- The law serves to protect and maintain existing property rights.
- Property ownership influences legal structures and governance.
- Proudhon critiques the alignment of legal systems with capitalist interests.
