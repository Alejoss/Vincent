> All scientific knowledge is provisional.

— [[Person – Karl Popper]]

## Interpretation
- Scientific knowledge is not absolute and can be revised with new evidence.
- The provisional nature of science emphasizes the importance of skepticism and critical thinking.
- Popper advocates for a dynamic understanding of knowledge, contrasting it with dogmatic beliefs.
