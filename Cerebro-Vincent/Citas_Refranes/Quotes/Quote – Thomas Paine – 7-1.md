> The duty of a patriot is to protect his country from its government.

— [[Person – Thomas Paine]]

## Interpretation
- Patriotism involves holding the government accountable to the people.
- A true patriot prioritizes the welfare of the nation over blind loyalty to its rulers.
- Protecting the country may require opposing or challenging governmental actions that threaten its integrity.
