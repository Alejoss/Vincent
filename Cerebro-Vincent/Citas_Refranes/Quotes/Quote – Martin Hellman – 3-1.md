> Cryptography is a tool to help us protect our privacy, but it can also be used to oppress."

— [[Person – Martin Hellman]]

## Interpretation
- Cryptography serves as a safeguard for individual privacy.
- The same technology can be weaponized by those in power to control or suppress others.
- The duality of cryptography highlights the ethical implications of its use.
