> Do you want to know who you are? Don’t ask. Act! Action will delineate and define you.

— [[Person – Thomas Jefferson]]

## Interpretation
- Your identity is shaped more by your actions than by introspection.
- Taking initiative and engaging in activities is essential for self-discovery.
- Understanding oneself comes from doing rather than merely contemplating.
