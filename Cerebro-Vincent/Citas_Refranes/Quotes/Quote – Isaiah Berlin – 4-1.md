> To be an individual is to be a part of the larger whole.

— [[Person – Isaiah Berlin]]

## Interpretation
- Individual identity is inherently connected to the broader society or community.
- A person's uniqueness is defined in relation to their social environment.
- True individuality is recognized within the context of collective existence.
