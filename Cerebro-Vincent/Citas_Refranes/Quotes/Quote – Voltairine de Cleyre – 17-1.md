> I would rather be a free spirit than a puppet on a string."

— [[Person – Voltairine de Cleyre]]

## Interpretation
- The desire for autonomy and self-determination is paramount.
- Being a 'free spirit' symbolizes individuality and personal freedom.
- The 'puppet on a string' metaphor highlights the dangers of external control and manipulation.
