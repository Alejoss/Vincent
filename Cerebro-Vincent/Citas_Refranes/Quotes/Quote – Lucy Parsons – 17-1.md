> It is the duty of the oppressed to rise up against their oppressors."

— [[Person – Lucy Parsons]]

## Interpretation
- The quote emphasizes the moral responsibility of those who are oppressed to resist and challenge their oppressors.
- It underscores the idea that passive acceptance of oppression is not an option for those suffering injustice.
- The statement calls for collective action and solidarity among the oppressed to enact change.
