> The future is not a predetermined path; it is shaped by the decisions we make today."

— [[Person – Albert O. Hirschman]]

## Interpretation
- The future is flexible and influenced by our current choices.
- Individual and collective decisions play a crucial role in shaping outcomes.
- There is an inherent agency in shaping one’s destiny rather than being a passive recipient of fate.
