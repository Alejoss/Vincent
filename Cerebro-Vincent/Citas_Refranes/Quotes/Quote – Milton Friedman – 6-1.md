> We have a system that increasingly taxes work and subsidizes nonwork.

— [[Person – Milton Friedman]]

## Interpretation
- The current taxation system penalizes individuals who engage in productive work.
- By providing subsidies for nonwork, it may discourage employment and self-sufficiency.
- This creates a disincentive structure that can lead to a decrease in overall economic productivity.
