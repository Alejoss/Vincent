> I do not want women to be the slaves of men; I want them to be the companions of men.

— [[Person – Mary Wollstonecraft]]

## Interpretation
- Wollstonecraft advocates for gender equality, emphasizing mutual respect instead of subjugation.
- The quote highlights the importance of companionship and partnership in relationships between men and women.
- It critiques the societal norms that position women as subservient to men.
