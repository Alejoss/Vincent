> It is not enough to be aware of the problems; we must also be willing to act on them."

— [[Person – Charles Murray]]

## Interpretation
- Awareness of issues is the first step towards addressing them.
- Action is necessary to create meaningful change.
- Merely recognizing problems is insufficient without the commitment to solve them.
