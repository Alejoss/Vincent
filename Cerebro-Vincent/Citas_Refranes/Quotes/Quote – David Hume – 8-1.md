> Truth springs from argument amongst friends.

— [[Person – David Hume]]

## Interpretation
- Truth is often discovered through dialogue and debate among those who trust each other.
- Friends can challenge each other's ideas, leading to deeper insights.
- The process of argument can refine thoughts and reveal underlying truths.
