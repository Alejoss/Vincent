> One person with a belief is equal to a force of ninety-nine who have only interests.

— [[Person – John Stuart Mill]]

## Interpretation
- A strong belief can drive an individual to achieve great things, surpassing the collective power of those who are merely motivated by self-interest.
- Beliefs can inspire action and commitment, while interests may lead to passivity or inaction.
- The quote emphasizes the transformative potential of conviction over mere transactional motivations.
