> A human being is not a creature of circumstances, but a creator of circumstances.

— [[Person – Wilhelm von Humboldt]]

## Interpretation
- Individuals have the power to shape their own realities.
- People are proactive agents in their lives rather than passive victims of external forces.
- Human creativity and decision-making are fundamental in determining life circumstances.
