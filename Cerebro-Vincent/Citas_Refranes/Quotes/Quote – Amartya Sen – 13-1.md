> We must not lose sight of the importance of education in the pursuit of development."

— [[Person – Amartya Sen]]

## Interpretation
- Education is a foundational element for achieving sustainable development.
- Investing in education empowers individuals and communities, leading to broader societal progress.
- Neglecting education can hinder economic growth and social advancement.
