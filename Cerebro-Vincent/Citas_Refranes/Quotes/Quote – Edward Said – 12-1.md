> Colonialism is not a thing of the past; it is a continuing process."

— [[Person – Edward Said]]

## Interpretation
- Colonialism has evolved but persists in modern forms.
- The impact of colonialism continues to shape global power dynamics.
- Understanding colonialism as ongoing challenges our perceptions of history.
