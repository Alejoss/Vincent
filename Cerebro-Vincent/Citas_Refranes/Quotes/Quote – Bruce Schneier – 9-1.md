> Security is about making trade-offs.

— [[Person – Bruce Schneier]]

## Interpretation
- Security involves balancing various factors, such as risk and convenience.
- Making decisions in security often requires sacrificing one benefit for another.
- Understanding trade-offs is essential for effective security management.
