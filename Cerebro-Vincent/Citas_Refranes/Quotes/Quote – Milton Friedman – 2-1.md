> There’s no such thing as a free lunch.

— [[Person – Milton Friedman]]

## Interpretation
- All resources have a cost, even if it's not immediately apparent.
- Free offers often come with hidden expenses or consequences.
- Economic decisions always involve trade-offs, implying that nothing is truly free.
