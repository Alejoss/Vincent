> There is no greater tyranny than that which is perpetrated under the shield of the law and in the name of justice.

— [[Person – Milton Friedman]]

## Interpretation
- Law can be misused to justify oppressive actions.
- The pursuit of justice can sometimes mask underlying tyranny.
- True justice should protect individual freedoms, not infringe upon them.
