> The two most powerful warriors are patience and time.

— [[Person – Leo Tolstoy]]

## Interpretation
- Patience allows individuals to endure challenges and wait for favorable outcomes.
- Time serves as a transformative force that can heal wounds and bring change.
- Together, patience and time can overcome obstacles that seem insurmountable in the short term.
