> Freedom is not merely the absence of oppression; it is the presence of the conditions necessary for self-fulfillment.

— [[Person – Emma Goldman]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom involves more than just escaping oppression; it requires positive conditions for growth.
- Self-fulfillment is an essential aspect of true freedom, not just a lack of constraints.
- True freedom empowers individuals to achieve their potential and pursue their desires.
