> If the laws are to be maintained, they should be maintained in a way that does not infringe upon the rights of individuals.

— [[Person – Frédéric Bastiat]]

## Interpretation
- Laws must be enforced consistently to uphold justice.
- Individual rights are paramount and should not be compromised by legal enforcement.
- The balance between law and personal freedom is essential for a fair society.
