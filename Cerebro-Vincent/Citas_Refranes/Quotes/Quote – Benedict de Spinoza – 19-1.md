> Peace is not the absence of war, but the presence of justice.

— [[Person – Benedict de Spinoza]]

## Interpretation
- True peace requires active engagement in justice rather than merely avoiding conflict.
- Justice serves as the foundation for a harmonious society.
- The quote emphasizes that peace is a proactive state maintained through fairness and equity.
