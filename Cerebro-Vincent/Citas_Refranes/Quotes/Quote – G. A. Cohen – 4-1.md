> To think of justice as fairness is to think of social justice as a matter of distributing goods and opportunities according to fair principles."

— [[Person – G. A. Cohen]]

## Interpretation
- Justice is fundamentally tied to the equitable distribution of resources.
- Fair principles should guide how goods and opportunities are allocated in society.
- Social justice requires an evaluation of fairness in the context of societal structures.
