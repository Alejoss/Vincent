> The power of the Black women's collective experience is in its ability to challenge the dominant narratives of race, gender, and class."

— [[Person – Patricia Hill Collins]]

## Interpretation
- The collective experiences of Black women provide a unique perspective that can disrupt mainstream understandings of identity.
- This collective experience serves as a counter-narrative to societal norms that often marginalize their voices.
- By highlighting their lived realities, Black women can redefine and reshape conversations around race, gender, and class.
