> The Orient was almost a European invention, and had been since antiquity a place of romance, exotic beings, and otherworldly landscapes."

— [[Person – Edward Said]]

## Interpretation
- The concept of the Orient is largely shaped by European imagination rather than its true essence.
- It has been romanticized and idealized through literature and art, creating a fantasy of the East.
- This perspective reflects a colonial mindset that distorts the realities of Eastern cultures.
