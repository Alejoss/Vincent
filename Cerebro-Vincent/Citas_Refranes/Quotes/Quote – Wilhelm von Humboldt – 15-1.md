> The best way to find yourself is to lose yourself in the service of others.

— [[Person – Wilhelm von Humboldt]]

## Interpretation
- Self-discovery often comes from engaging with and helping others.
- Focusing on the needs of others can lead to personal growth and fulfillment.
- True identity may be revealed through acts of kindness and service.
