> No man has a right to expect his freedom to be guaranteed by another."

— [[Person – Lysander Spooner]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom is an individual responsibility rather than something bestowed by others.
- Expecting others to guarantee one's freedom undermines the essence of autonomy.
- True freedom comes from self-reliance and personal accountability.
