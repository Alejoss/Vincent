> It is justice, not charity, that is wanting in the world.

— [[Person – Mary Wollstonecraft]]

## Interpretation
- Wollstonecraft emphasizes the importance of justice over mere acts of charity.
- She suggests that societal issues stem from a lack of fairness and equality rather than insufficient goodwill.
- The quote calls for systemic change rather than temporary relief.
