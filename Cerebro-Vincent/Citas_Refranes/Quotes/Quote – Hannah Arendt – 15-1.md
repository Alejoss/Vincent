> The world is not only made up of what we see but also of what we think.

— [[Person – Hannah Arendt]]

## Interpretation
- Our perception is shaped not only by sensory experiences but also by our mental constructs.
- Thought processes influence our understanding of reality and the world around us.
- What we conceive intellectually holds as much significance as the tangible aspects of existence.
