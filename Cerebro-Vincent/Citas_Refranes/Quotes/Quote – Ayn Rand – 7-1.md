> Freedom is the public recognition of a private virtue.

— [[Person – Ayn Rand]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom is rooted in individual moral integrity and personal values.
- Public recognition suggests that society must acknowledge and respect individual virtues for true freedom to exist.
- The quote implies that personal ethics and societal acceptance are intertwined in the pursuit of liberty.
