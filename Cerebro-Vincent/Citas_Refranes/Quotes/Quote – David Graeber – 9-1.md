> The economy is not a machine; it’s a complex social system."

— [[Person – David Graeber]]

## Interpretation
- The economy functions through human interactions and relationships rather than mechanical processes.
- Viewing the economy as a complex social system highlights the influence of cultural, political, and social factors.
- This perspective encourages a more holistic understanding of economic issues beyond mere numbers and statistics.
