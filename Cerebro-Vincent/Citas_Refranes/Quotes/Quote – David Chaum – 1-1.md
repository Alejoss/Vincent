> The ability to communicate anonymously is a fundamental human right."

— [[Person – David Chaum]]

## Interpretation
- Anonymity in communication protects individuals from surveillance and oppression.
- It empowers free expression and the exchange of diverse ideas without fear of repercussion.
- Recognizing anonymity as a right underscores the importance of privacy in democratic societies.
