> The struggle for gender equality must include a critique of traditional family structures.

— [[Person – Susan Moller Okin]]

## Interpretation
- Gender equality cannot be achieved without reconsidering the roles and dynamics within traditional families.
- Traditional family structures often perpetuate gender roles that hinder equal opportunities for all genders.
- Critiquing these structures is essential for creating a more equitable society.
