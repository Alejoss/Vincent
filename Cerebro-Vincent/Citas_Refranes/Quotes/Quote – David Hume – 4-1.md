> Beauty in things exists in the mind which contemplates them.

— [[Person – David Hume]]

## Interpretation
- Beauty is subjective and depends on individual perception.
- The mind plays a crucial role in how beauty is experienced.
- External objects do not possess inherent beauty without an observer's contemplation.
