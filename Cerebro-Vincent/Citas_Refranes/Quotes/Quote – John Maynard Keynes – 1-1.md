> The difficulty lies, not in the new ideas, but in escaping from the old ones.

— [[Person – John Maynard Keynes]]

## Interpretation
- Old ideas can be deeply ingrained and resistant to change.
- Innovation is often stifled by traditional thinking and established norms.
- The challenge is not generating new concepts, but rather letting go of outdated beliefs.
