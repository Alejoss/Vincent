> A just society must ensure that all individuals have access to the means of their own development."

— [[Person – C. B. Macpherson]]

## Interpretation
- A just society prioritizes equitable access to personal growth opportunities.
- Individual development is essential for the overall health and fairness of society.
- Ensuring access to development means addressing systemic barriers that hinder individuals.
