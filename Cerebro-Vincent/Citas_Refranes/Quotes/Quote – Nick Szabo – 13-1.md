> The power of blockchain technology is that it enables new forms of collaboration and trust."

— [[Person – Nick Szabo]]

## Interpretation
- Blockchain technology facilitates decentralized collaboration among participants.
- It fosters trust by providing transparent and verifiable transactions.
- New forms of collaboration arise from the elimination of intermediaries.
