> The individual is the one who must choose the values of life, not the State or society.

— [[Person – Emma Goldman]]

## Interpretation
- Individuals have the autonomy to determine their own beliefs and values.
- The quote emphasizes the importance of personal freedom over societal or governmental control.
- It challenges the notion that the state should dictate moral or ethical standards.
