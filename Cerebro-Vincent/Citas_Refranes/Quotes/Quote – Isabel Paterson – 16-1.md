> The road to freedom is a long and arduous one, but it is the only road worth taking."

— [[Person – Isabel Paterson]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom requires significant effort and perseverance.
- The journey towards freedom is challenging but ultimately rewarding.
- Choosing the path of freedom is a fundamental and valuable endeavor.
