> The police are not your friends; they are the enemies of the working class."

— [[Person – Lucy Parsons]]

## Interpretation
- The police serve the interests of the ruling class rather than the general populace.
- Their actions often suppress the rights and freedoms of workers.
- Parsons emphasizes a divide between law enforcement and the working class's struggles.
