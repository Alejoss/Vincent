> The only freedom which deserves the name is that of pursuing our own good in our own way.

— [[Person – John Stuart Mill]]

## Interpretation
- True freedom is the ability to seek personal fulfillment.
- Individual autonomy is essential for personal development.
- Pursuing one's own good implies a rejection of external constraints on one's choices.
