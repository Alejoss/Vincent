> Each person must find their own way to contribute to the common good.

— [[Person – Thomas Hill Green]]

## Interpretation
- Individual responsibility is essential for societal progress.
- Personal contributions to the common good vary based on each person's unique abilities and perspectives.
- Empowerment comes from recognizing one's role in the community's welfare.
