> Poverty is not just a lack of money; it is not having the capability to realize one’s full potential as a human being."

— [[Person – Amartya Sen]]

## Interpretation
- Poverty encompasses more than just financial deficiency; it includes a lack of opportunities.
- True poverty restricts individuals from achieving their full potential and capabilities.
- Addressing poverty requires a holistic understanding of human development beyond economic measures.
