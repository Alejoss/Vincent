> Debt is structured around the idea that you can take something and then you owe it back."

— [[Person – David Graeber]]

## Interpretation
- Debt involves a reciprocal obligation where something acquired must be returned.
- This concept highlights the moral and social dimensions of financial transactions.
- It underscores the expectation of accountability in economic relationships.
