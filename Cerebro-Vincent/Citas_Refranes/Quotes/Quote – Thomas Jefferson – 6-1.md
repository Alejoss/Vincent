> A government big enough to give you everything you want is big enough to take everything you have.

— [[Person – Thomas Jefferson]]

## Interpretation
- A large government can provide extensive benefits and services to its citizens.
- However, such a powerful government also possesses the capacity to seize personal property and freedoms.
- The quote warns of the dangers of excessive governmental control and the balance between security and liberty.
