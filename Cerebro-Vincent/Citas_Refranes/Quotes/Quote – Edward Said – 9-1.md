> One of the major functions of the intellectual is to reveal the hidden structures of power."

— [[Person – Edward Said]]

## Interpretation
- Intellectuals play a crucial role in exposing underlying power dynamics in society.
- Revealing hidden power structures can lead to greater awareness and potential social change.
- The function of the intellectual is to challenge the status quo and provoke critical thinking.
