> I think that the most important thing we can do is to educate ourselves.

— [[Person – Howard Zinn]]

## Interpretation
- Self-education is fundamental for personal and societal growth.
- Knowledge empowers individuals to critically engage with the world.
- Understanding historical and social contexts is essential for informed action.
