> The goal of security is to enable trust, not eliminate it.

— [[Person – Bruce Schneier]]

## Interpretation
- Security measures should foster an environment where trust can flourish.
- Eliminating trust completely is impractical and counterproductive.
- The focus of security is to create reliable systems that people can depend on.
