> The law is not a mere collection of rules; it is a living force that must evolve with the human spirit."

— [[Person – Lysander Spooner]]

## Interpretation
- The law should adapt to reflect the changing values and beliefs of society.
- It emphasizes the dynamic nature of legal systems rather than viewing them as static.
- The quote suggests that law is inherently connected to human progress and development.
