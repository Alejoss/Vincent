> To be a judge is to be a moral philosopher."

— [[Person – Ronald Dworkin]]

## Interpretation
- Judges must engage with ethical principles when making legal decisions.
- The role of a judge transcends mere application of law, requiring deep moral reasoning.
- Judicial decisions often involve interpreting values and justice, akin to philosophical inquiry.
