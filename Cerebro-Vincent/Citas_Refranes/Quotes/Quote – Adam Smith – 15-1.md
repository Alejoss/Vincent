> Wealth, as it were, is the effect of the labor of the people.

— [[Person – Adam Smith]]

## Interpretation
- Wealth is created through the collective efforts of laborers.
- The value of wealth is tied to the productivity and contributions of the workforce.
- Labor is the fundamental driver behind economic prosperity.
