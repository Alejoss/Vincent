> The relationship between individual and state is one of mutual dependence."

— [[Person – C. B. Macpherson]]

## Interpretation
- Individuals rely on the state for protection and order.
- The state depends on individuals for legitimacy and support.
- This relationship highlights the interconnectedness of personal and political realms.
