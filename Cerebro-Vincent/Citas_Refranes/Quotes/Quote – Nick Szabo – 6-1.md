> The beauty of smart contracts is that they can operate independently of human intervention."

— [[Person – Nick Szabo]]

## Interpretation
- Smart contracts automate processes without needing people to execute them.
- This independence reduces the risk of human error or bias.
- The self-sufficiency of smart contracts enhances efficiency and reliability.
