> The man who is truly good is he who does good in the name of goodness itself.

— [[Person – Jean-Jacques Rousseau]]

## Interpretation
- True goodness is motivated by the intrinsic value of doing good, rather than by external rewards or recognition.
- This perspective emphasizes the importance of intention behind actions in defining moral character.
- Rousseau suggests that authentic virtue stems from a selfless commitment to the well-being of others.
