> The greatness of America lies in its ability to repair its faults.

— [[Person – Alexis de Tocqueville]]

## Interpretation
- America's strength is found in its capacity for self-improvement.
- The willingness to acknowledge and correct mistakes is key to national resilience.
- This ability reflects a dynamic and evolving society.
