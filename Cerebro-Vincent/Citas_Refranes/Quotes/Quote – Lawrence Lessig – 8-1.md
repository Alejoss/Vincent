> We have to be willing to give up some power to gain more."

— [[Person – Lawrence Lessig]]

## Interpretation
- Empowering others can lead to greater collective strength.
- Sometimes, relinquishing control is necessary for broader gains.
- True progress often requires sacrifices in individual power or autonomy.
