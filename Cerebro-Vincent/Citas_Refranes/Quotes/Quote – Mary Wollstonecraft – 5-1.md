> It is time to effect a revolution in female manners, time to restore to them their lost dignity.

— [[Person – Mary Wollstonecraft]]

## Interpretation
- Wollstonecraft calls for a fundamental change in how society perceives and treats women.
- She emphasizes the importance of restoring women's dignity, which has been undermined by societal norms.
- The quote suggests a need for empowerment and recognition of women's rights and worth.
