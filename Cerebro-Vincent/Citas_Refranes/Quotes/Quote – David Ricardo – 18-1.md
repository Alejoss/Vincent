> The principle of comparative advantage explains why trade is beneficial for all parties involved.

— [[Person – David Ricardo]]

## Interpretation
- Comparative advantage allows countries to specialize in producing goods where they have a relative efficiency.
- This specialization leads to increased overall production and mutual benefits from trade.
- Both parties can gain from trade even if one is more efficient in producing all goods.
