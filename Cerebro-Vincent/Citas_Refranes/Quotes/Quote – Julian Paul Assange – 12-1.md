> If we cannot find a way to keep people informed, we’re going to lose the ability to govern ourselves.

— [[Person – Julian Paul Assange]]

## Interpretation
- Access to information is crucial for informed decision-making in governance.
- Without transparency, the public's ability to participate in democracy diminishes.
- The flow of information is essential for accountability and self-governance.
