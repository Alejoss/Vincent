> We are not afraid of the future; we are afraid of the past.

— [[Person – Peter Kropotkin]]

## Interpretation
- The quote suggests that fears are rooted in past experiences rather than uncertainties about what lies ahead.
- It highlights the psychological burden of memories and historical events that shape our perceptions.
- Embracing the future requires overcoming the anxieties tied to previous disappointments or traumas.
