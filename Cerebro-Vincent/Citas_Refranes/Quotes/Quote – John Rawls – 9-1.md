> The moral powers of individuals are fully realized only in a just society.

— [[Person – John Rawls]]

## Interpretation
- Individuals can only achieve their full moral potential when they are part of a fair and equitable society.
- Justice provides the necessary conditions for personal and communal flourishing.
- Injustices hinder the development of moral capacities in individuals.
