> The pursuit of happiness is a right of every human being, but it must be pursued with respect for the rights of others.

— [[Person – Immanuel Kant]]

## Interpretation
- Happiness is an inherent right for all individuals.
- The pursuit of one's own happiness should not infringe upon the rights of others.
- Respect for others is essential in the quest for personal fulfillment.
