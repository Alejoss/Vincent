> The entrepreneur is the one who innovates, who introduces new goods, new methods of production, new markets, and new forms of organization.

— [[Person – Joseph Alois Schumpeter]]

## Interpretation
- The entrepreneur is a key driver of economic change through innovation.
- They play a crucial role in introducing new products and improving production methods.
- Entrepreneurs also contribute to the evolution of markets and organizational structures.
