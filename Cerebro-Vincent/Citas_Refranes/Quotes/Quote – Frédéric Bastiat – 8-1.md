> The law should be the shield of the oppressed, not the sword of the oppressor.

— [[Person – Frédéric Bastiat]]

## Interpretation
- The law is intended to protect individuals from harm and injustice.
- When misused, the law can become a tool for oppression rather than protection.
- A just society must ensure that the law serves to empower the vulnerable.
