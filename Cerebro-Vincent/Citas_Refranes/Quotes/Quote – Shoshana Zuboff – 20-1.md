> The future depends on our ability to create a new social contract for the digital age."

— [[Person – Shoshana Zuboff]]

## Interpretation
- The digital age requires a revised framework for societal norms and agreements.
- The future of society will be shaped by how we address issues related to technology and privacy.
- A new social contract is essential for balancing individual rights with collective responsibilities in a digital context.
