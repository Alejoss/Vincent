> The law can be a powerful tool for social change."

— [[Person – Dahlia Lithwick]]

## Interpretation
- The law serves as a mechanism to challenge injustices and promote equality.
- Legal frameworks can support movements aimed at reforming societal norms and values.
- By leveraging the law, individuals and groups can advocate for change in a structured manner.
