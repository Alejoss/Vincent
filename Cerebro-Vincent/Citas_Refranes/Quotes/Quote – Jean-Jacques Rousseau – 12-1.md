> The more we love our friends, the less we flatter them; it is by excusing nothing that true friendship is preserved.

— [[Person – Jean-Jacques Rousseau]]

## Interpretation
- True friendship thrives on honesty rather than superficial praise.
- Genuine affection involves recognizing and addressing flaws in our friends.
- Flattery can undermine the strength and authenticity of a friendship.
