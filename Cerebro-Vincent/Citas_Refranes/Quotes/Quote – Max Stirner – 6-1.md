> Man is the only being who can say "I."

— [[Person – Max Stirner]]

## Interpretation
- The use of 'I' signifies individual self-awareness and consciousness.
- This statement emphasizes the uniqueness of human identity and subjectivity.
- It suggests a distinction between humans and other beings in their capacity for self-reference and personal identity.
