> We can't just assume that because we have a computer, we have security.

— [[Person – Bruce Schneier]]

## Interpretation
- Having technology does not inherently provide protection against threats.
- Security must be actively implemented and cannot be taken for granted.
- Complacency in assuming safety can lead to vulnerabilities and risks.
