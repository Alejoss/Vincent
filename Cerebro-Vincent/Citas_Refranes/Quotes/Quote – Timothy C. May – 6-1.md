> The power of information is the power of freedom."

— [[Person – Timothy C. May]]

## Interpretation
- Access to information empowers individuals to make informed choices.
- Knowledge and information are essential for personal and societal autonomy.
- The control and dissemination of information directly influence the degree of freedom one experiences.
