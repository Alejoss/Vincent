> If we want to be free, we must be prepared to fight for our freedom.

— [[Person – Errico Malatesta]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom is not given; it must be actively pursued.
- The struggle for freedom often requires sacrifice and effort.
- A passive approach to freedom is insufficient for true liberation.
