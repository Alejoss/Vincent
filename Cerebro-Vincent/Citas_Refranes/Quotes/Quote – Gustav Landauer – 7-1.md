> To be free means to be a part of a community, not to be isolated.

— [[Person – Gustav Landauer]]

## Interpretation
- True freedom is found in connection with others rather than in solitude.
- Isolation limits personal growth and understanding, while community fosters support and development.
- Being part of a community enriches individual experience and promotes collective well-being.
