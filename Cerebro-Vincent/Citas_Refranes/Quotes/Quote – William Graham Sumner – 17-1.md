> The individual is the unit of society, and the state is the servant of the individual.

— [[Person – William Graham Sumner]]

## Interpretation
- The quote emphasizes the importance of the individual as the foundational element of society.
- It suggests that the state's primary role is to serve and protect the rights and interests of individuals.
- This perspective highlights a view of governance that prioritizes personal liberty over collective control.
