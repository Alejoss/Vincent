> The most radical revolutionary will become a conservative the day after the revolution.

— [[Person – Hannah Arendt]]

## Interpretation
- Revolutionary ideals may fade quickly when faced with the realities of governance.
- Those who seek radical change often become entrenched in maintaining the new order.
- The struggle for progress can lead to a desire for stability and preservation.
