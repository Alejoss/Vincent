> Anarchism is the only philosophy which brings to man the consciousness of himself.

— [[Person – Emma Goldman]]

## Interpretation
- Anarchism empowers individuals to recognize their own autonomy and identity.
- It suggests that self-awareness and personal freedom are interconnected.
- Goldman posits that traditional systems of authority obscure personal consciousness.
