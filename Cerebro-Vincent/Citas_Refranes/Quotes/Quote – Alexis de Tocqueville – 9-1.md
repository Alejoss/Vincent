> The taste for luxury is a part of the human heart.

— [[Person – Alexis de Tocqueville]]

## Interpretation
- Humans possess an innate desire for luxury and comfort.
- This longing reflects deeper aspects of human nature and societal values.
- The pursuit of luxury can influence behavior and social structures.
