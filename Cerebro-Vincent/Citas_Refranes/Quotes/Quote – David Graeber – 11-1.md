> The current economic system is not sustainable; it’s built on a foundation of inequality."

— [[Person – David Graeber]]

## Interpretation
- The existing economic framework prioritizes the interests of a few over the many.
- Sustainability is compromised by systemic inequalities that hinder equitable growth.
- A shift towards more equitable systems is necessary for long-term viability.
