> The rich should be ashamed of their wealth, when they see how many poor and distressed there are among them.

— [[Person – Adam Smith]]

## Interpretation
- Wealth can create a moral obligation to address societal inequalities.
- The presence of poverty among the wealthy should provoke introspection and guilt.
- True prosperity is intertwined with the well-being of the community as a whole.
