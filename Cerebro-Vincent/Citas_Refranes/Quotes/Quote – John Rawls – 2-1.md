> The original position is a hypothetical situation characterized by a fair choice of principles of justice.

— [[Person – John Rawls]]

## Interpretation
- The original position is a conceptual framework for determining justice principles.
- It emphasizes fairness in the selection of these principles.
- This hypothetical scenario helps to ensure impartiality and equality in justice deliberations.
