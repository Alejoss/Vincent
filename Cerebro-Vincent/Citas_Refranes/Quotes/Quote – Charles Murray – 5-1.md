> The American dream is not just about wealth; it’s about the opportunity to achieve your potential."

— [[Person – Charles Murray]]

## Interpretation
- The American dream encompasses more than financial success; it includes personal growth and self-fulfillment.
- Achieving one's potential is a fundamental aspect of the American identity.
- Opportunity is a crucial component that allows individuals to pursue their dreams and aspirations.
