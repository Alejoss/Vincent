> The essence of the Liberal outlook lies not in what opinions are held, but in how they are held.

— [[Person – Bertrand Russell]]

## Interpretation
- The focus of Liberalism is on the approach to beliefs rather than the beliefs themselves.
- It emphasizes open-mindedness and critical thinking in discourse.
- The manner in which opinions are expressed reflects the core values of Liberalism.
