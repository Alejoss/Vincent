> The role of government in the economy should be limited."

— [[Person – Jean-Baptiste Say]]

## Interpretation
- Government interference in the economy can hinder individual initiative and innovation.
- A limited role allows for greater market efficiency and self-regulation.
- It emphasizes the importance of personal freedom and entrepreneurship in economic growth.
