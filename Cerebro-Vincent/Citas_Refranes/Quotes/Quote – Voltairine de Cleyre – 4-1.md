> The only way to make a great deal of money is to give a great deal of service."

— [[Person – Voltairine de Cleyre]]

## Interpretation
- Financial success is directly linked to the value one provides to others.
- Offering substantial service can lead to greater rewards, both financially and socially.
- This perspective emphasizes the importance of contribution over mere profit-seeking.
