> When goods do not cross borders, soldiers will.

— [[Person – Frédéric Bastiat]]

## Interpretation
- Trade and open markets promote peace between nations.
- Protectionism and barriers to trade can lead to conflict and military intervention.
- A lack of economic cooperation can result in political and military tensions.
