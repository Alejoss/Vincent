> Access to knowledge is a fundamental right."

— [[Person – Lawrence Lessig]]

## Interpretation
- Access to knowledge is essential for individual empowerment and societal progress.
- Limiting access to knowledge can perpetuate inequality and hinder innovation.
- Recognizing knowledge as a right emphasizes the importance of open information in a democratic society.
