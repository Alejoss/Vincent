> It is the people who make history, not the leaders.

— [[Person – Howard Zinn]]

## Interpretation
- Power and agency lie with the masses rather than a select few leaders.
- Collective action and grassroots movements are essential for societal change.
- History is shaped by the experiences and struggles of ordinary people, not just political figures.
