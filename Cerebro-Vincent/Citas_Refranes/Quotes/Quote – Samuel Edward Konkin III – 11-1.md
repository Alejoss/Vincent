> The market is the best mechanism for organizing human cooperation."

— [[Person – Samuel Edward Konkin III]]

## Interpretation
- The market facilitates voluntary exchanges that enhance collaboration among individuals.
- It promotes efficiency by aligning individual interests with collective goals.
- Market mechanisms allow for decentralized decision-making, which can lead to innovative solutions.
