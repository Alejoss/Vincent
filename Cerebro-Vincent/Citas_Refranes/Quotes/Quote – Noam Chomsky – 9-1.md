> If you are not part of the solution, you are part of the problem.

— [[Person – Noam Chomsky]]

## Interpretation
- Individuals have a responsibility to actively engage in solving societal issues.
- Inaction or indifference contributes to the continuation of problems.
- One's stance on issues is defined by their level of involvement.
