> If man in the state of nature be absolute lord of his own person and possessions, why will he part with his freedom?

— [[Person – John Locke]]

## Interpretation
- Locke questions the rationale behind giving up natural freedom.
- He suggests that individuals must perceive benefits in societal agreements to justify the loss of absolute autonomy.
- The quote highlights the tension between personal liberty and the need for organized society.
