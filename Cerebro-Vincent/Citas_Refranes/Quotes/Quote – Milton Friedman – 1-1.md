> The government solution to a problem is usually as bad as the problem.

— [[Person – Milton Friedman]]

## Interpretation
- Government interventions often fail to resolve issues effectively.
- The solutions provided by the government may exacerbate the original problems.
- A critical view of government efficiency and competence in problem-solving.
