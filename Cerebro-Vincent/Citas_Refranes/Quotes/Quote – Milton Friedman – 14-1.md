> One of the great mistakes is to judge policies and programs by their intentions rather than their results.

— [[Person – Milton Friedman]]

## Interpretation
- Policies should be evaluated based on their actual outcomes, not merely their intended goals.
- Good intentions do not guarantee effective results in public policy.
- Critical analysis of policies requires focusing on empirical evidence rather than assumptions about their benefits.
