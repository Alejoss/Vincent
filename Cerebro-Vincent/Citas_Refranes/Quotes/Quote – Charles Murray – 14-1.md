> The welfare system should empower individuals rather than create dependency."

— [[Person – Charles Murray]]

## Interpretation
- Welfare programs should provide tools and resources for self-sufficiency.
- Creating systems that encourage independence is essential for individual empowerment.
- Dependency on welfare can hinder personal growth and societal contribution.
