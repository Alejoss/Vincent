> Every increase in the quantity of wealth will tend to lower the value of commodities.

— [[Person – David Ricardo]]

## Interpretation
- An increase in wealth leads to a higher supply of goods.
- As supply increases, the value or price of those goods tends to decrease.
- This reflects the basic economic principle of supply and demand.
