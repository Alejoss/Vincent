> The worst thing that can happen to a good cause is, not to be skillfully attacked, but to be ineptly defended.

— [[Person – Frédéric Bastiat]]

## Interpretation
- A good cause can suffer more from poor defense than from strong opposition.
- Inept defense can undermine the credibility and effectiveness of a cause.
- The skill and strategy in advocating for a cause are crucial for its survival and success.
