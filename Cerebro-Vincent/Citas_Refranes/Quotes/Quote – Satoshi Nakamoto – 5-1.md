> You can’t just take the existing system and make it better. You have to rethink it from the ground up.

— [[Person – Satoshi Nakamoto]]

## Interpretation
- Improving systems requires foundational change rather than superficial enhancements.
- Innovation necessitates a complete reevaluation of established norms.
- True progress comes from reimagining and redesigning systems rather than merely optimizing them.
