> Man is an animal that makes bargains: no other animal does this – no dog exchanges bones with another.

— [[Person – Adam Smith]]

## Interpretation
- Humans uniquely engage in trade and bargaining, distinguishing them from other animals.
- The quote emphasizes the social and economic aspects of human behavior.
- Bargaining reflects the complexity of human relationships and societal structures.
