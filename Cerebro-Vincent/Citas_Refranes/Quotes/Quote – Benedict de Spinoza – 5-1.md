> The highest activity a human being can attain is learning for understanding, because to understand is to be free.

— [[Person – Benedict de Spinoza]]

## Interpretation
- Learning for understanding empowers individuals to navigate the complexities of life.
- True freedom is achieved through knowledge and comprehension of the world.
- Understanding fosters autonomy and personal growth, allowing individuals to act in alignment with their true nature.
