> Nozick's entitlement theory articulates a clear framework for understanding property rights and justice.

— [[Person – Robert Nozick]]

## Interpretation
- Nozick's theory provides a systematic approach to the concept of property ownership.
- It emphasizes the importance of just acquisition and transfer of property.
- The framework aims to address questions of justice in relation to individual rights.
