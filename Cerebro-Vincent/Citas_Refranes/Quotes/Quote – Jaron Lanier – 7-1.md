> It's important that we have a conversation about what technology is doing to us and what we want it to do.

— [[Person – Jaron Lanier]]

## Interpretation
- We need to reflect on the impact of technology on our lives.
- It's crucial to define our expectations and desires for technology's role.
- Engaging in dialogue can help shape a future that aligns with our values.
