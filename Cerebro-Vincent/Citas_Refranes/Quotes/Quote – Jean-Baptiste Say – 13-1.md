> The individual is the best judge of his own interests."

— [[Person – Jean-Baptiste Say]]

## Interpretation
- Individuals possess unique insights into their own needs and desires.
- Personal judgment is essential for making decisions that align with one's own well-being.
- Trusting individual agency promotes self-determination and personal responsibility.
