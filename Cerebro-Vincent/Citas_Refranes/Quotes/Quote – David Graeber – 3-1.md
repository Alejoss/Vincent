> People often talk about the difference between 'real' and 'imagined' economies, but in fact, all economies are imagined."

— [[Person – David Graeber]]

## Interpretation
- All economic systems are constructed through shared beliefs and social agreements.
- The distinction between 'real' and 'imagined' economies is a false dichotomy.
- Understanding economies as imagined highlights their malleability and the role of human perception in shaping them.
