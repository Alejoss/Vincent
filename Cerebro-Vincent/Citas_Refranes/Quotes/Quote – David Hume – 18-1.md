> The life of man is a perpetual motion; he is always in motion, and cannot stand still.

— [[Person – David Hume]]

## Interpretation
- Life is characterized by constant change and activity.
- Human existence is a dynamic process, reflecting the nature of reality.
- Stagnation is impossible as we are perpetually influenced by time and experiences.
