> We need to create a new narrative about what work is and what it can be."

— [[Person – David Graeber]]

## Interpretation
- The current understanding of work is inadequate and needs redefinition.
- A new narrative can inspire innovative approaches to labor and its value.
- This shift in perspective can empower individuals and transform societal attitudes towards work.
