> Understanding the behavior of individuals in the political process is crucial for effective governance."

— [[Person – James M. Buchanan]]

## Interpretation
- Individuals' actions and motivations significantly influence political outcomes.
- Effective governance requires an understanding of these individual behaviors.
- Recognizing these dynamics can lead to better decision-making in political systems.
