> The most valuable of all talents is that of never using two words when one will do.

— [[Person – Thomas Jefferson]]

## Interpretation
- Conciseness in communication is highly valued.
- Effective expression often requires simplicity and clarity.
- Overcomplicating language can weaken the intended message.
