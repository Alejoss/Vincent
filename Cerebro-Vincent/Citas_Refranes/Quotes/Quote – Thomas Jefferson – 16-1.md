> Our liberty depends on the freedom of the press, and that cannot be limited without being lost.

— [[Person – Thomas Jefferson]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom of the press is essential for the preservation of liberty.
- Any restrictions on press freedom threaten the very existence of that liberty.
- A free press serves as a guardian against tyranny and oppression.
