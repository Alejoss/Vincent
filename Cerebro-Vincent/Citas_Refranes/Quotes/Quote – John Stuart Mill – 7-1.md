> The worth of a State, in the long run, is the worth of the individuals composing it.

— [[Person – John Stuart Mill]]

## Interpretation
- The value of a society is directly linked to the quality and character of its citizens.
- Individual development and well-being contribute to the overall strength of the state.
- A strong state is a reflection of the moral and intellectual capacities of its people.
