> What we need is a new way of thinking about the economy, one that recognizes the importance of human relationships."

— [[Person – David Graeber]]

## Interpretation
- The economy should prioritize interpersonal connections over mere financial transactions.
- Recognizing human relationships can lead to a more holistic and sustainable economic model.
- A shift in perspective is required to understand the economy's impact on social bonds.
