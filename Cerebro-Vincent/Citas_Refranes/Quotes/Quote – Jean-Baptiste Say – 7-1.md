> It is not enough to be a good producer; one must also be a good distributor."

— [[Person – Jean-Baptiste Say]]

## Interpretation
- Production alone does not guarantee success; effective distribution is equally crucial.
- A balance between creating goods and ensuring they reach consumers is essential for economic viability.
- The quote emphasizes the interconnectedness of production and distribution in a functioning economy.
