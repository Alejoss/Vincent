> The primary function of the media is to serve the interests of the powerful.

— [[Person – Noam Chomsky]]

## Interpretation
- Media often prioritize the narratives that benefit those in positions of power.
- This creates a skewed representation of reality that favors elites over marginalized voices.
- The media's role can be seen as a tool for maintaining existing power structures.
