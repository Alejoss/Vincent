> The best way to understand law is to see it as a part of the moral and political life of the community."

— [[Person – Ronald Dworkin]]

## Interpretation
- Law is not just a set of rules but is deeply intertwined with the community's moral values.
- Understanding law requires considering its impact on political and social dynamics.
- The relationship between law and community reflects broader ethical principles and collective beliefs.
