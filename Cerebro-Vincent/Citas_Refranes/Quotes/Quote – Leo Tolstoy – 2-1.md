> The sole meaning of life is to serve humanity.

— [[Person – Leo Tolstoy]]

## Interpretation
- Life's purpose is derived from selflessness and altruism.
- Serving others fosters a sense of connection and fulfillment.
- True meaning is found in the impact we have on the lives of others.
