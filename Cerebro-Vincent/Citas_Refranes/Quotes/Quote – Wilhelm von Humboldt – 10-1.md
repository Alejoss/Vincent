> The spirit of a people is the foundation of its culture.

— [[Person – Wilhelm von Humboldt]]

## Interpretation
- The essence of a society shapes its artistic and intellectual expressions.
- Cultural identity is rooted in the collective mindset and values of its people.
- Understanding a culture requires insight into the spirit and character of its society.
