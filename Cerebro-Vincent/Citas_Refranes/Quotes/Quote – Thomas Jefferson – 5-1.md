> When the people fear their government, there is tyranny; when the government fears the people, there is liberty.

— [[Person – Thomas Jefferson]]

## Interpretation
- Fear of government indicates oppression and a lack of freedom.
- When the government is accountable to the people, true liberty exists.
- The balance of power is essential for a just society.
