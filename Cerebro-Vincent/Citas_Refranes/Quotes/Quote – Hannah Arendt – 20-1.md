> Political judgment is the art of making sense of the world.

— [[Person – Hannah Arendt]]

## Interpretation
- Political judgment involves the ability to understand and interpret complex social realities.
- It emphasizes the importance of context and discernment in navigating political issues.
- This skill reflects a deep engagement with the world and the nuances of human experiences.
