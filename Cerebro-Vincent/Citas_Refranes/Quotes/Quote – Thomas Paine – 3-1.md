> What we obtain too cheap, we esteem too lightly; it is dearness only that gives everything its value.

— [[Person – Thomas Paine]]

## Interpretation
- Value is often linked to the effort or cost involved in obtaining something.
- When something is easily acquired, it may be taken for granted and not appreciated.
- True worth is recognized when there is sacrifice or struggle associated with it.
