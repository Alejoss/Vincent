> What we are witnessing is the commodification of personal data on an unprecedented scale."

— [[Person – Shoshana Zuboff]]

## Interpretation
- Personal data is being transformed into a marketable asset.
- This commodification is occurring at a scale never seen before.
- The implications of this trend raise concerns about privacy and individual autonomy.
