> Justice cannot be a passive aspiration; it must be an active pursuit in all spheres of life."

— [[Person – Nancy Fraser]]

## Interpretation
- Justice requires proactive engagement rather than mere hope.
- It must be pursued actively across all areas, including social, economic, and political spheres.
- A passive approach to justice undermines its effectiveness and relevance.
