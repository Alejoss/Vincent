> The idea of freedom is the most precious gift we can give to ourselves.

— [[Person – Rosa Luxemburg]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom is a fundamental and intrinsic value that enhances human dignity.
- The act of embracing freedom is an empowering choice that benefits one's self.
- Gifting ourselves freedom signifies personal growth and liberation from constraints.
