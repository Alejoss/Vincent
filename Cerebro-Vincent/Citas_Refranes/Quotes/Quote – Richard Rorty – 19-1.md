> To be human is to be part of a story that is always changing.

— [[Person – Richard Rorty]]

## Interpretation
- Human existence is inherently tied to narratives that evolve over time.
- Our identities and experiences are shaped by the fluid nature of these stories.
- Change is a fundamental aspect of the human condition, reflecting growth and adaptation.
