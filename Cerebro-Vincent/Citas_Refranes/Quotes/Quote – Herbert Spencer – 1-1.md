> The great aim of education is not knowledge but action.

— [[Person – Herbert Spencer]]

## Interpretation
- Education should prioritize practical application over mere accumulation of facts.
- The true purpose of learning is to inspire individuals to take action and effect change.
- Knowledge is valuable, but its ultimate goal should be to empower individuals to act effectively in the world.
