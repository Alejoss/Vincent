> Never put off till tomorrow what you can do today.

— [[Person – Thomas Jefferson]]

## Interpretation
- Procrastination can lead to missed opportunities.
- Taking action today fosters responsibility and productivity.
- Delaying tasks often increases stress and reduces efficiency.
