> The internet is a tool for empowerment."

— [[Person – Lawrence Lessig]]

## Interpretation
- The internet provides individuals with access to information and resources that enhance their capabilities.
- It enables marginalized voices to be heard and fosters democratic participation.
- As a platform for creativity and collaboration, the internet supports personal and collective empowerment.
