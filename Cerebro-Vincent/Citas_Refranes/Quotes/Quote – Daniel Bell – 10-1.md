> The great challenge of our time is to find a way to live together in a world that is increasingly diverse."

— [[Person – Daniel Bell]]

## Interpretation
- Society is becoming more diverse due to globalization and migration.
- The ability to coexist harmoniously amidst diversity is a pressing societal challenge.
- Finding common ground and fostering understanding is essential for social cohesion.
