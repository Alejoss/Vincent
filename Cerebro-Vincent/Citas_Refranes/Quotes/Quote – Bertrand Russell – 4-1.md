> Science may set limits to knowledge, but should not set limits to imagination.

— [[Person – Bertrand Russell]]

## Interpretation
- Science defines the boundaries of what we know based on empirical evidence.
- Imagination allows us to explore possibilities beyond current scientific understanding.
- Limitations in knowledge should not constrain creative thinking and innovation.
