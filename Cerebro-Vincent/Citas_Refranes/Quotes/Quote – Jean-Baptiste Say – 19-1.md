> Competition is the driving force behind innovation."

— [[Person – Jean-Baptiste Say]]

## Interpretation
- Competition motivates businesses to improve their products and services.
- The desire to outperform rivals leads to creative solutions and technological advancements.
- Without competition, the impetus for innovation diminishes, stifling progress.
