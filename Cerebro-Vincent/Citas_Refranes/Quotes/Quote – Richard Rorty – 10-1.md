> The goal of a liberal education is not to help us find the truth, but to help us become better selves.

— [[Person – Richard Rorty]]

## Interpretation
- Liberal education focuses on personal development rather than the pursuit of absolute truth.
- It encourages self-reflection and growth, shaping individuals into more thoughtful and empathetic beings.
- The emphasis is on fostering critical thinking and adaptability in a complex world.
