> A truly democratic society requires a commitment to the capabilities of all its members."

— [[Person – Martha Nussbaum]]

## Interpretation
- A democratic society thrives when it values and nurtures the potential of every individual.
- Recognizing and enhancing capabilities fosters equality and participation.
- Commitment to inclusivity is essential for true democracy to flourish.
