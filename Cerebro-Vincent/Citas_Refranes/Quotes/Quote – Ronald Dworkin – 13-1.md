> The principle of equality is fundamental to the concept of justice."

— [[Person – Ronald Dworkin]]

## Interpretation
- Equality serves as the cornerstone for any fair justice system.
- Without equality, notions of justice become compromised and subjective.
- Dworkin emphasizes that true justice cannot exist without recognizing and upholding equal rights for all individuals.
