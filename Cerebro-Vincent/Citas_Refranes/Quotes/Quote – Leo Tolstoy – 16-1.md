> The truth is not for all men, but only for those who seek it.

— [[Person – Leo Tolstoy]]

## Interpretation
- Truth is subjective and may not be universally accessible.
- Only those who actively pursue understanding can grasp deeper truths.
- The quest for truth requires effort and intention from the seeker.
