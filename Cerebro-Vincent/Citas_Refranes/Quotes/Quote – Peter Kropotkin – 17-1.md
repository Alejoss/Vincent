> Progress is not a straight line; it is a series of struggles and victories.

— [[Person – Peter Kropotkin]]

## Interpretation
- Progress involves overcoming obstacles rather than following a smooth path.
- Each struggle contributes to eventual success, highlighting the importance of perseverance.
- Victory is not guaranteed but is part of a dynamic and ongoing process.
