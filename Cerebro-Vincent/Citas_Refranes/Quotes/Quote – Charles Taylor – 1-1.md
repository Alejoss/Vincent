> It is important to understand that the world is not all about us, and that we have to work with others to achieve our goals."

— [[Person – Charles Taylor]]

## Interpretation
- The quote emphasizes the importance of collaboration and interdependence in achieving success.
- It suggests that individualistic perspectives can be limiting and that understanding others' roles is crucial.
- The statement encourages a broader view of our goals in relation to the community and society.
