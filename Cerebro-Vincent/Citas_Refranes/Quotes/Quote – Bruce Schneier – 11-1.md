> The Internet is a place where we can be both anonymous and identifiable.

— [[Person – Bruce Schneier]]

## Interpretation
- The Internet allows users to mask their identities while still leaving digital traces.
- Individuals can choose to remain hidden or reveal personal information depending on their needs.
- This duality presents both opportunities and challenges for privacy and security.
