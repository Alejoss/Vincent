> The mind has a great deal of power over the body, and the body has a great deal of power over the mind.

— [[Person – Mary Wollstonecraft]]

## Interpretation
- The relationship between the mind and body is reciprocal, indicating that mental states can influence physical health and vice versa.
- This quote highlights the interconnectedness of mental and physical well-being, suggesting that neither can be neglected without consequences.
- Wollstonecraft emphasizes the importance of holistic understanding in self-care and personal development.
