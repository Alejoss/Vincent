> The most revolutionary thing one can do is always to proclaim loudly what is happening.

— [[Person – Rosa Luxemburg]]

## Interpretation
- Transparency is crucial for social change, as it empowers individuals to recognize and challenge injustices.
- Speaking out against oppression is a form of resistance that can inspire collective action.
- Awareness and vocalization of current realities can mobilize people towards revolutionary goals.
