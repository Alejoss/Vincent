> It is not enough to possess a right; one must also know how to defend it.

— [[Person – Frédéric Bastiat]]

## Interpretation
- Possessing a right does not guarantee its protection or recognition.
- Knowledge and skill in advocacy are essential for defending one's rights.
- Indifference or ignorance can lead to the erosion of rights despite their existence.
