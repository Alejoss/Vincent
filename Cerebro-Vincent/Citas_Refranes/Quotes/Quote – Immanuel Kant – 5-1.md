> It is beyond a doubt that all our knowledge begins with experience.

— [[Person – Immanuel Kant]]

## Interpretation
- Knowledge is grounded in sensory experiences.
- Experience serves as the foundation for understanding the world.
- Cognitive processes build upon the data provided by our interactions with reality.
