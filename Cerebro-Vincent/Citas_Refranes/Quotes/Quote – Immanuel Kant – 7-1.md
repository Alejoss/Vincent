> We can judge the heart of a man by his treatment of animals.

— [[Person – Immanuel Kant]]

## Interpretation
- A person's moral character can be assessed by how they treat those who are vulnerable and defenseless.
- Compassion towards animals reflects deeper ethical values in human behavior.
- The treatment of animals serves as a litmus test for one's humanity and empathy.
