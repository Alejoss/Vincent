> The revolution is not an act but a process, a transformation of society.

— [[Person – Gustav Landauer]]

## Interpretation
- Revolution should be viewed as an ongoing journey rather than a single event.
- True societal change requires deep and lasting transformation at multiple levels.
- The focus is on the gradual evolution of societal values and structures.
