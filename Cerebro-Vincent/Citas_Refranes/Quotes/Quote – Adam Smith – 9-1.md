> The propensity to truck, barter, and exchange one thing for another is common to all men.

— [[Person – Adam Smith]]

## Interpretation
- Humans have an innate tendency to engage in trade and commerce.
- The act of exchanging goods and services is a fundamental aspect of human society.
- This propensity is universal, transcending cultures and societies.
