> The most important time is now. The most important person is the one you are with.

— [[Person – Leo Tolstoy]]

## Interpretation
- The present moment is crucial for living fully and meaningfully.
- Valuing the company of others emphasizes the significance of relationships.
- Focusing on the now fosters mindfulness and deeper connections.
