> The growth of knowledge depends entirely on disagreement.

— [[Person – Karl Popper]]

## Interpretation
- Disagreement fosters critical thinking and the questioning of established beliefs.
- Engaging with differing perspectives leads to the refinement and expansion of knowledge.
- Knowledge is not static but evolves through debate and discussion among individuals.
