> Intersectionality is not just about identity; it is about the interconnectedness of social categorizations."

— [[Person – Patricia Hill Collins]]

## Interpretation
- Intersectionality emphasizes the complexity of social identities beyond singular categories.
- It highlights how various social categorizations such as race, class, and gender interact and shape experiences.
- Understanding these interconnections is crucial for addressing systemic inequalities.
