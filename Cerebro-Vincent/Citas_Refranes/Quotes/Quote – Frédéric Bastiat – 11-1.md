> There are no greater tyrannies than those which are perpetrated under the shield of the law and in the name of justice.

— [[Person – Frédéric Bastiat]]

## Interpretation
- Laws can be used as tools for oppression rather than protection.
- Justice can be manipulated to serve the interests of the powerful.
- True tyranny often disguises itself as lawful authority.
