> To be sane in a world of madmen is in itself madness.

— [[Person – Jean-Jacques Rousseau]]

## Interpretation
- Being rational in an irrational society can lead to feelings of isolation and alienation.
- The quote suggests that societal norms can dictate what is considered sane or insane.
- True sanity may require conformity to madness, implying a critique of societal values.
