> The man who has a good idea, is not the man who has a good idea, but the man who can sell it.

— [[Person – William Graham Sumner]]

## Interpretation
- The value of an idea is often determined by the ability to communicate and promote it effectively.
- Creativity alone is insufficient; persuasion and marketing are crucial for success.
- Practical implementation and advocacy of an idea can be as important as the idea itself.
