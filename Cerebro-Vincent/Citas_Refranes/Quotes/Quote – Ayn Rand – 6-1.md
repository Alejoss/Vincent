> Money is only a tool. It will take you wherever you wish, but it will not replace you as the driver.

— [[Person – Ayn Rand]]

## Interpretation
- Money serves as a means to achieve goals rather than an end in itself.
- Individuals are responsible for directing their lives and making choices.
- Wealth should facilitate personal ambition, not substitute for personal effort or identity.
