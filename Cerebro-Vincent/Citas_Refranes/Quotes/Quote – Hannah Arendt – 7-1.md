> The law is not a static thing but a dynamic process.

— [[Person – Hannah Arendt]]

## Interpretation
- The law evolves and changes over time in response to societal needs and values.
- Understanding law requires recognizing its adaptability rather than viewing it as fixed.
- This perspective challenges traditional notions of law as an unchanging set of rules.
