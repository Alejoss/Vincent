> Freedom is the space for action, but it requires responsibility.

— [[Person – Hannah Arendt]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom allows individuals to take meaningful actions in their lives.
- With the ability to act freely comes the obligation to be accountable for those actions.
- True freedom is intertwined with the moral responsibility of one's choices.
