> The profits of capital are determined by the productivity of labor.

— [[Person – David Ricardo]]

## Interpretation
- Capital profits are intrinsically linked to how effectively labor is utilized.
- Higher productivity in labor leads to increased returns on capital investments.
- This highlights the importance of labor efficiency in determining economic outcomes.
