> Social and economic inequalities are to be arranged so that they are both to the greatest benefit of the least advantaged.

— [[Person – John Rawls]]

## Interpretation
- Inequalities should be structured to improve the situation of those who are worst off in society.
- The principle emphasizes fairness and justice in the distribution of resources.
- Rawls advocates for a system that prioritizes the needs of the least advantaged over the privileges of the more fortunate.
