> The increase of population is the principal cause of the increase of wealth.

— [[Person – David Ricardo]]

## Interpretation
- A growing population drives demand for goods and services, stimulating economic activity.
- Increased labor supply can lead to greater production and innovation.
- Population growth can enhance wealth distribution among a larger number of individuals.
