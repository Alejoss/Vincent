> To be a revolutionary, you must be a human being.

— [[Person – Emma Goldman]]

## Interpretation
- Revolutionaries must embody the values of humanity and compassion.
- True change comes from understanding and addressing human needs.
- A genuine revolutionary approach requires empathy and connection with others.
