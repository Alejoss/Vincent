> If you want to be happy, be.

— [[Person – Leo Tolstoy]]

## Interpretation
- Happiness is a state of being that is accessible to everyone.
- One's mindset and choice to embrace happiness are pivotal.
- External circumstances do not dictate one's happiness; it is an internal decision.
