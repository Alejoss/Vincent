> Happiness is the result of doing, not the result of getting.

— [[Person – Herbert Spencer]]

## Interpretation
- Happiness is derived from engaging in meaningful actions rather than merely acquiring material possessions.
- The process of doing fosters a sense of fulfillment and purpose.
- True contentment comes from active participation in life rather than passive consumption.
