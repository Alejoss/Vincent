> The only way to improve security is to make it easier for users to do the right thing.

— [[Person – Bruce Schneier]]

## Interpretation
- User-friendly security measures are essential for effective cybersecurity.
- Simplifying processes increases the likelihood that users will follow security protocols.
- Empowering users through easier options fosters a culture of security awareness.
