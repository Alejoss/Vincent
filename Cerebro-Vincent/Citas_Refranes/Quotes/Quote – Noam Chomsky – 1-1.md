> The responsibility of intellectuals is to speak the truth and expose lies.

— [[Person – Noam Chomsky]]

## Interpretation
- Intellectuals have a moral duty to seek and present factual information.
- They must challenge misinformation and promote transparency.
- Their role is crucial in fostering an informed society.
