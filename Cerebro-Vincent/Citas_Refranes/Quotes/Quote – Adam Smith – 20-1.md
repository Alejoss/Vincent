> The desire to better one’s condition is the basis of economic progress.

— [[Person – Adam Smith]]

## Interpretation
- Human motivation to improve personal circumstances drives economic activity.
- Individual aspirations lead to innovation and productivity enhancements.
- The pursuit of betterment fosters competition, which is essential for economic growth.
