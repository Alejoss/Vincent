> The welfare state must be reimagined as a tool for social equity, not just as a safety net."

— [[Person – Nancy Fraser]]

## Interpretation
- The welfare state should actively promote fairness and justice in society.
- It should be seen as a proactive measure to achieve social equity rather than merely a fallback option.
- Reimagining the welfare state emphasizes its role in addressing systemic inequalities.
