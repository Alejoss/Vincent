> The first man who, having enclosed a piece of ground, bethought himself of saying "This is mine," and found people simple enough to believe him, was the real founder of civil society.

— [[Person – Jean-Jacques Rousseau]]

## Interpretation
- The quote critiques the concept of private property and its role in the formation of society.
- Rousseau suggests that the claim of ownership marks the beginning of social inequality.
- It highlights the gullibility of people who accepted the idea of private land ownership as legitimate.
