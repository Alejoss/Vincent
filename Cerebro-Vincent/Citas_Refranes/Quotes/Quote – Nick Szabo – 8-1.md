> Value stored in a digital format is subject to the same laws of supply and demand as any other form of value."

— [[Person – Nick Szabo]]

## Interpretation
- Digital value, like physical value, is influenced by market forces.
- The principles of economics apply universally, regardless of the medium.
- Understanding supply and demand is crucial for valuing digital assets.
