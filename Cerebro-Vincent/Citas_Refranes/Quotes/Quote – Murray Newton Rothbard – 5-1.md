> The market is a process of discovery.

— [[Person – Murray Newton Rothbard]]

## Interpretation
- The market continuously reveals consumer preferences and needs.
- It emphasizes the dynamic nature of economic interactions and innovations.
- Market participants learn and adapt through the process of buying and selling.
