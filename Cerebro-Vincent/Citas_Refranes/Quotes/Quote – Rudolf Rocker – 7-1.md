> Society is not a mere aggregate of individuals; it is a living organism."

— [[Person – Rudolf Rocker]]

## Interpretation
- Society functions as a cohesive unit rather than just a collection of separate entities.
- The interconnectedness of individuals within society creates a dynamic and evolving system.
- Understanding society as a living organism emphasizes the importance of relationships and collaboration.
