> Anarchism is not a doctrine but a movement.

— [[Person – Peter Kropotkin]]

## Interpretation
- Anarchism emphasizes action and social change over rigid theoretical frameworks.
- It suggests that the essence of anarchism lies in collective efforts rather than individual beliefs.
- The quote highlights the dynamic and evolving nature of anarchist movements.
