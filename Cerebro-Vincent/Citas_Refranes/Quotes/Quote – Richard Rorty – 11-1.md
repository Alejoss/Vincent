> There is no such thing as a neutral standpoint in philosophical inquiry.

— [[Person – Richard Rorty]]

## Interpretation
- Philosophical inquiry is inherently biased by the perspectives of the inquirer.
- There are always underlying assumptions and values that influence our understanding.
- The quest for absolute objectivity in philosophy is ultimately unattainable.
