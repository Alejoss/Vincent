> A radical democracy acknowledges the importance of passion and commitment in political life."

— [[Person – Chantal Mouffe]]

## Interpretation
- Radical democracy values emotional engagement in politics.
- Passion and commitment are essential for active participation in democratic processes.
- Acknowledging these elements can lead to a more vibrant and responsive political landscape.
