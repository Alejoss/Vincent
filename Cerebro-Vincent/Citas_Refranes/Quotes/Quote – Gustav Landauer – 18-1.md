> Power is not something that we can hold; it is a relationship that we can change.

— [[Person – Gustav Landauer]]

## Interpretation
- Power is dynamic and exists within relationships rather than being a fixed possession.
- We have the ability to influence and alter the power dynamics around us.
- Understanding power as relational encourages active participation in societal change.
