> Digital gold is a new paradigm, where trust is established through cryptography rather than through central authorities."

— [[Person – Nick Szabo]]

## Interpretation
- Digital gold represents a shift in how value and trust are perceived in financial systems.
- Cryptography enables secure transactions without reliance on traditional financial institutions.
- This paradigm emphasizes decentralization and individual empowerment over centralized control.
