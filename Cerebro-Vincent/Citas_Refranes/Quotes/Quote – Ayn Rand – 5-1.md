> The evil of the world is made possible by nothing but the sanction you give it.

— [[Person – Ayn Rand]]

## Interpretation
- Individuals have the power to enable or disable evil through their choices.
- The quote emphasizes personal responsibility in the face of moral dilemmas.
- It suggests that complicity, even passive, can perpetuate wrongdoing.
