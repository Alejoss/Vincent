> The man who does not value himself, cannot value anything or anyone.

— [[Person – Ayn Rand]]

## Interpretation
- Self-worth is foundational to valuing others and the world around us.
- Without valuing oneself, a person lacks the capacity to appreciate relationships and experiences.
- Self-esteem influences one's ability to form meaningful connections and judgments.
