> The aim of radical democracy is to acknowledge and respect the diversity of identities."

— [[Person – Chantal Mouffe]]

## Interpretation
- Radical democracy seeks to create an inclusive space for various identities.
- Acknowledging diversity is essential for a genuinely democratic society.
- Respecting different identities fosters dialogue and understanding among groups.
