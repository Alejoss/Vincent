> The right to own property is the foundation of liberty."

— [[Person – Gustave de Molinari]]

## Interpretation
- Property ownership is essential for individual autonomy and freedom.
- Without the right to own property, individuals may struggle to exercise their rights and liberties.
- The protection of property rights is crucial for a just and free society.
