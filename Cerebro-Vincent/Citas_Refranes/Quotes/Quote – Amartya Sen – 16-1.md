> Inequality is not just an economic issue; it has profound social implications."

— [[Person – Amartya Sen]]

## Interpretation
- Inequality affects not only wealth distribution but also social cohesion.
- Social implications of inequality can lead to unrest and division within communities.
- Addressing economic inequality requires a broader understanding of its social effects.
