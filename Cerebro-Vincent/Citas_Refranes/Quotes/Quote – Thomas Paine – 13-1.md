> The only way to secure the rights of the people is to make them aware of them.

— [[Person – Thomas Paine]]

## Interpretation
- Awareness is crucial for the protection of individual rights.
- People must be informed about their rights to actively defend them.
- Ignorance of rights can lead to their erosion and abuse.
