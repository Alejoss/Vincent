> Education is the cultivation of the intellect, the heart, and the spirit.

— [[Person – Wilhelm von Humboldt]]

## Interpretation
- Education encompasses not only intellectual growth but also emotional and spiritual development.
- It emphasizes a holistic approach to learning, integrating various aspects of human experience.
- The quote suggests that true education shapes well-rounded individuals, fostering deeper understanding and empathy.
