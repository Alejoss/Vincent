> Education is the foundation of all progress.

— [[Person – William Godwin]]

## Interpretation
- Education equips individuals with knowledge and skills necessary for advancement.
- It serves as a catalyst for societal development and innovation.
- Without a strong educational foundation, progress in various fields is hindered.
