> When government regulates, it creates a set of incentives that can lead to unintended consequences.

— [[Person – Milton Friedman]]

## Interpretation
- Government regulation alters the behavior of individuals and businesses.
- These altered behaviors can result in outcomes that were not anticipated by policymakers.
- Unintended consequences highlight the complexity of economic systems and human motivation.
