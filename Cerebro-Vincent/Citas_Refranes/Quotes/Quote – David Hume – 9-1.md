> Custom is the great guide of human life.

— [[Person – David Hume]]

## Interpretation
- Custom influences our behaviors and choices, shaping our daily lives.
- It suggests that societal norms and traditions guide human actions more than rational thought.
- Hume emphasizes the importance of habitual practices in forming human understanding and morality.
