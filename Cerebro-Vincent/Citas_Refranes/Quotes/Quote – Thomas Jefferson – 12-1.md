> It is neither wealth nor splendor; but tranquility and occupation which give you happiness.

— [[Person – Thomas Jefferson]]

## Interpretation
- Happiness is derived more from inner peace than from external possessions.
- True contentment comes from engaging in meaningful activities rather than accumulating wealth.
- Finding tranquility in life is essential for achieving genuine happiness.
