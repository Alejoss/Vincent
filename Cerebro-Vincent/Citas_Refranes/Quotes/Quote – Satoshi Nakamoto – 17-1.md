> It’s important to remember that Bitcoin is a tool for freedom and innovation.

— [[Person – Satoshi Nakamoto]]

## Interpretation
- Bitcoin empowers individuals by providing them with financial autonomy.
- It fosters innovation by enabling new technologies and systems outside traditional financial frameworks.
- The emphasis on freedom highlights the potential to disrupt centralized control in the financial sector.
