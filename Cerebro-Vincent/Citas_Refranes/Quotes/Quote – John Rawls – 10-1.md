> The principles of justice are those that free and rational persons would accept in an initial position of equality.

— [[Person – John Rawls]]

## Interpretation
- Justice is based on a hypothetical social contract among rational individuals.
- The 'initial position of equality' emphasizes fairness and impartiality in establishing principles of justice.
- Free and rational persons seek agreements that do not favor any particular individual or group.
