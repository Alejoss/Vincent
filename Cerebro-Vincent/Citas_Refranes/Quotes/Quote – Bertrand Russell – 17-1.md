> Nothing is so exhausting as indecision, and nothing is so futile.

— [[Person – Bertrand Russell]]

## Interpretation
- Indecision drains mental and emotional energy.
- Prolonged uncertainty leads to feelings of futility and lack of progress.
- Taking action is more effective than remaining in a state of hesitation.
