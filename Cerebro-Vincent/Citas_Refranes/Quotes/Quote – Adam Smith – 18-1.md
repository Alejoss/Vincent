> No great improvements in the productive powers of labor can be made without the assistance of machinery.

— [[Person – Adam Smith]]

## Interpretation
- Machinery plays a crucial role in enhancing labor productivity.
- Without technological advancements, labor improvements are limited.
- The integration of machinery is essential for economic progress.
