> The only thing that is absolute is the ego.

— [[Person – Max Stirner]]

## Interpretation
- The ego is the central aspect of individual identity and existence.
- Stirner asserts that all other values and principles are relative compared to the individual's self.
- This perspective emphasizes personal autonomy and the rejection of absolute moral or societal norms.
