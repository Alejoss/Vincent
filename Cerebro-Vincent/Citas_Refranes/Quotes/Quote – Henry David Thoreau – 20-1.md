> There is more to life than merely increasing its speed.

— [[Person – Henry David Thoreau]]

## Interpretation
- Life should be appreciated for its quality rather than just its pace.
- The pursuit of speed can lead to a neglect of deeper experiences and fulfillment.
- True richness in life comes from mindfulness and intentional living.
