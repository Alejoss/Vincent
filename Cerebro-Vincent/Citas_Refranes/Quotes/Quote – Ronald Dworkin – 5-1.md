> A right is not just a claim; it is a claim that is backed by a moral principle."

— [[Person – Ronald Dworkin]]

## Interpretation
- A right involves more than just a mere assertion; it requires moral justification.
- The validity of a right is contingent upon its alignment with ethical principles.
- Rights are fundamentally linked to morality, emphasizing their importance in justice and law.
