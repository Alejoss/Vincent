> A just society is one that provides a fair distribution of resources and opportunities to all its members.

— [[Person – John Rawls]]

## Interpretation
- A just society prioritizes fairness in resource allocation.
- Opportunities should be accessible to every individual, regardless of their background.
- Equity is essential for social cohesion and overall well-being.
