> The power of the state is the most significant obstacle to human liberation.

— [[Person – Errico Malatesta]]

## Interpretation
- The state exerts control over individuals, limiting their freedom and potential.
- Human liberation requires the dismantling of oppressive state structures.
- True autonomy can only be achieved when individuals are free from governmental authority.
