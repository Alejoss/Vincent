> Reason is the foundation of all morality.

— [[Person – William Godwin]]

## Interpretation
- Reason serves as the basis for discerning right from wrong.
- Moral principles are grounded in rational thought rather than tradition or emotion.
- Humanity's ethical behavior relies on the capacity for logical reasoning.
