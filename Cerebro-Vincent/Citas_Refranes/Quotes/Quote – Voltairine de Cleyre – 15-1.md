> There is no such thing as a permanent solution to the problem of freedom."

— [[Person – Voltairine de Cleyre]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom is a dynamic state that requires constant vigilance and effort.
- Solutions to freedom are temporary and must be revisited as circumstances change.
- The pursuit of freedom is an ongoing struggle rather than a final destination.
