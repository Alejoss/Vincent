> The man who is not a slave to the whims of others cannot be a slave to his own whims.

— [[Person – Ayn Rand]]

## Interpretation
- A person who is not controlled by external influences is empowered to make rational choices.
- Freedom from external whims leads to greater self-discipline and self-governance.
- Mastery over oneself is essential for true personal autonomy.
