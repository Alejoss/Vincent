> A well-ordered society is one in which all members accept and know that others accept the same principles of justice.

— [[Person – John Rawls]]

## Interpretation
- A well-ordered society relies on a shared understanding of justice among its members.
- The acceptance of common principles fosters social cooperation and trust.
- Justice is not only about laws but also about mutual recognition of fairness among individuals.
