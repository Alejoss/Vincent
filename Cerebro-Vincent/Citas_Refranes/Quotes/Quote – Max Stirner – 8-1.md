> The person who knows how to govern himself is a true sovereign.

— [[Person – Max Stirner]]

## Interpretation
- Self-governance reflects true freedom and autonomy.
- A sovereign individual is one who can master their own desires and actions.
- Personal responsibility is key to authentic leadership and authority over oneself.
