> There is no such thing as a moral guarantee; we are simply called to try to be moral.

— [[Person – Hannah Arendt]]

## Interpretation
- Moral actions cannot be assured or guaranteed, as they are inherently uncertain.
- The responsibility of morality lies in the effort to strive for ethical behavior, not in achieving perfection.
- Individuals are called to engage in moral decision-making, acknowledging the complexities involved.
