> The concept of digital scarcity is revolutionizing our understanding of value."

— [[Person – Nick Szabo]]

## Interpretation
- Digital scarcity refers to the limited availability of digital goods, challenging traditional notions of abundance.
- This concept shifts how we perceive and assign value in a digital economy.
- Understanding digital scarcity is essential for grasping the implications of blockchain and cryptocurrency.
