> If there must be trouble, let it be in my day, that my child may have peace.

— [[Person – Thomas Paine]]

## Interpretation
- The speaker is willing to face challenges and hardships to secure a better future for the next generation.
- This reflects a sense of personal sacrifice for the greater good and the pursuit of peace.
- It highlights the idea that current struggles can lead to future benefits for one's offspring.
