> The principles of justice are chosen behind a veil of ignorance.

— [[Person – John Rawls]]

## Interpretation
- Rawls suggests that fair principles of justice should be determined without knowledge of one's own social status or personal circumstances.
- The 'veil of ignorance' encourages impartiality and fairness in the creation of societal rules.
- This concept promotes a just society by ensuring that no one has an unfair advantage based on their position.
