> The only way to keep your secrets safe is to not tell anyone.

— [[Person – Bruce Schneier]]

## Interpretation
- Secrets are inherently vulnerable when shared with others.
- Trusting others with sensitive information increases the risk of exposure.
- The most effective way to maintain confidentiality is through complete silence.
