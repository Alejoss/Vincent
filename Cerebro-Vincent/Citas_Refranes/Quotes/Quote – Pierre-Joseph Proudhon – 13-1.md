> The right to property is a right of the individual, not of the state.

— [[Person – Pierre-Joseph Proudhon]]

## Interpretation
- Proudhon emphasizes individual ownership over state control.
- He advocates for personal autonomy in economic matters.
- The quote reflects a critique of state intervention in property rights.
