> The idea of a just society requires that we think about the rights of individuals and the role of the state.

— [[Person – Robert Nozick]]

## Interpretation
- A just society must prioritize individual rights.
- The relationship between individual rights and state authority is crucial to justice.
- Understanding justice requires a balance between personal freedoms and governmental roles.
