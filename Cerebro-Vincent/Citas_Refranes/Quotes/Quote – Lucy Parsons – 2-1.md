> The man who has a job is the man who has a chance."

— [[Person – Lucy Parsons]]

## Interpretation
- Having employment provides individuals with opportunities for growth and advancement.
- A job can be seen as a gateway to financial stability and social mobility.
- This quote emphasizes the importance of work as a means to improve one's circumstances.
