> The purpose of education is not to fill the minds of students with facts, but to teach them to think.

— [[Person – Wilhelm von Humboldt]]

## Interpretation
- Education should focus on developing critical thinking skills rather than rote memorization.
- The goal is to empower students to analyze and understand rather than simply recall information.
- True education fosters creativity and independent thought in learners.
