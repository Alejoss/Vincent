> Progress is not an illusion; it happens, but it is slow and invariably disappointing.

— [[Person – Herbert Spencer]]

## Interpretation
- Progress is a real phenomenon, but it unfolds gradually.
- The pace of progress often leads to unmet expectations.
- Disappointment often accompanies the realization that change takes time.
