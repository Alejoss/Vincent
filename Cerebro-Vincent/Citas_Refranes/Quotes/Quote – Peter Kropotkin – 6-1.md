> The anarchist is simply one who advocates the abolition of the state in all its forms.

— [[Person – Peter Kropotkin]]

## Interpretation
- The quote defines an anarchist primarily by their opposition to state authority.
- It emphasizes a comprehensive rejection of all forms of government, not just select policies or structures.
- This perspective suggests a belief in the possibility of a society organized without hierarchical control.
