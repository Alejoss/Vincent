> In a free society, individuals should not be judged by the state."

— [[Person – Samuel Edward Konkin III]]

## Interpretation
- Judgment should be a personal matter, not dictated by government authority.
- A truly free society values individual autonomy over state intervention.
- The quote advocates for personal responsibility and freedom from state overreach.
