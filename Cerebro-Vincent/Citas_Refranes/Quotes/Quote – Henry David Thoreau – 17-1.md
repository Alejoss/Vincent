> An early-morning walk is a blessing for the whole day.

— [[Person – Henry David Thoreau]]

## Interpretation
- An early-morning walk fosters a sense of peace and mindfulness.
- It sets a positive tone for the rest of the day.
- Engaging with nature in the morning can enhance overall well-being.
