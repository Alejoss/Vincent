> There is no such thing as a good investment. There is only a good price.

— [[Person – John Maynard Keynes]]

## Interpretation
- The value of an investment is determined by its price rather than its intrinsic qualities.
- Investors should focus on the cost versus potential return rather than labeling an investment as inherently good or bad.
- Market conditions and perceptions heavily influence pricing, making timing crucial in investment decisions.
