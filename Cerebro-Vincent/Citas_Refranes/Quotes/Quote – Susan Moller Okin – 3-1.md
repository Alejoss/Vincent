> The family is a central institution in the socialization of both men and women.

— [[Person – Susan Moller Okin]]

## Interpretation
- The family plays a crucial role in shaping the identities and roles of individuals in society.
- Both men and women are influenced by familial structures and expectations during their upbringing.
- The socialization process within the family can reinforce traditional gender roles and values.
