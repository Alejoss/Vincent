> The war on terror is a war on dissent.

— [[Person – Noam Chomsky]]

## Interpretation
- The phrase suggests that actions taken under the guise of combating terrorism often suppress opposing viewpoints.
- It implies a critique of government policies that limit free speech and dissent in the name of national security.
- Chomsky indicates that the war on terror can be used as a tool to control and manipulate public opinion.
