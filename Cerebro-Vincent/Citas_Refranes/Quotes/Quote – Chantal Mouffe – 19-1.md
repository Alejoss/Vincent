> The political sphere must be understood as a space of contestation and struggle."

— [[Person – Chantal Mouffe]]

## Interpretation
- The political sphere is inherently dynamic and characterized by conflicting interests.
- Contestation signifies that political power is not fixed but rather constantly negotiated.
- Struggle emphasizes the active engagement of diverse groups in shaping political outcomes.
