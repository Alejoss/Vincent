> We must learn to live with each other, or we will perish together.

— [[Person – Gustav Landauer]]

## Interpretation
- The importance of coexistence suggests that our survival depends on mutual understanding and cooperation.
- Conflict and division can lead to collective downfall, highlighting the need for unity.
- Living harmoniously is essential for the sustainability of society and the planet.
