> A society that does not allow for the expression of dissent is not a democracy."

— [[Person – Cornelius Castoriadis]]

## Interpretation
- Dissent is essential for genuine democratic engagement.
- A lack of dissent suggests authoritarianism rather than freedom.
- True democracy thrives on diverse opinions and the ability to challenge the status quo.
