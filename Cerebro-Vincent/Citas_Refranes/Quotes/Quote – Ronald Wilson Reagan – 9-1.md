> The trouble with our liberal friends is not that they are ignorant; it’s just that they know so much that isn’t so.

— [[Person – Ronald Wilson Reagan]]

## Interpretation
- Reagan suggests that liberal individuals possess a wealth of information, but much of it is incorrect or misleading.
- The quote implies a critique of the confidence with which liberals assert their beliefs despite their inaccuracies.
- It reflects a broader political tension between differing ideologies and perceptions of knowledge.
