> It is the law that must be kept in check, not the individual."

— [[Person – Lysander Spooner]]

## Interpretation
- The law should serve the people rather than oppress them.
- Individuals possess inherent rights that should not be infringed upon by legal systems.
- Maintaining a balance between law and individual freedom is essential for justice.
