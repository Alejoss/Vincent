> Virtue can only flourish among equals.

— [[Person – Mary Wollstonecraft]]

## Interpretation
- True virtue requires a foundation of equality and mutual respect.
- In unequal relationships, power dynamics can corrupt moral values.
- For virtue to thrive, individuals must be empowered and treated as equals.
