> A desire presupposes the possibility of action to achieve it; action presupposes a goal that is worth achieving.

— [[Person – Ayn Rand]]

## Interpretation
- Desire inherently implies that one believes they can take action to fulfill it.
- Action is driven by the pursuit of meaningful goals that provide value.
- The quote emphasizes the interconnectedness of desire, action, and purposeful living.
