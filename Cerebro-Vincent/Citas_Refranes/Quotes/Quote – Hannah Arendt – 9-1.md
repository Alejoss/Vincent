> Forgiveness is the key to action and freedom.

— [[Person – Hannah Arendt]]

## Interpretation
- Forgiveness allows individuals to move past grievances and heal.
- It empowers people to take action without being burdened by resentment.
- Freedom is achieved when one can release the hold of past wrongs on their present choices.
