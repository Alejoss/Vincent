> To be hopeful is to be in a state of grace.

— [[Person – Howard Zinn]]

## Interpretation
- Hopefulness reflects a positive and elevated state of being.
- Being in a state of grace suggests a sense of inner peace and acceptance.
- Hope can empower individuals to overcome challenges and find meaning in difficult circumstances.
