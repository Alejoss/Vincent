> We need to rethink how we approach security in a digital age."

— [[Person – David Chaum]]

## Interpretation
- The traditional methods of security may no longer be effective in the context of rapid technological advancements.
- Rethinking security suggests a need for innovative solutions that address emerging threats.
- This statement emphasizes the urgency of adapting security strategies to protect digital information and privacy.
