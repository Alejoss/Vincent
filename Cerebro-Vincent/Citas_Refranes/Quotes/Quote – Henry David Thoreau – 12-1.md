> The only way to deal with the future is to function efficiently in the now.

— [[Person – Henry David Thoreau]]

## Interpretation
- Focusing on the present is essential for effectively shaping the future.
- Efficiency in current actions lays the groundwork for future success.
- Living mindfully in the moment allows for better preparedness for what lies ahead.
