> Revolution does not come from the barrel of a gun, but from the hearts of the people."

— [[Person – Johann Most]]

## Interpretation
- True revolution is driven by the passion and will of the people rather than by violence.
- The quote emphasizes the importance of collective emotional and ideological commitment in enacting change.
- It suggests that genuine transformation arises from shared beliefs and aspirations, not just force or weaponry.
