> If we want to make a better world, we have to start by imagining it."

— [[Person – David Graeber]]

## Interpretation
- Imagining a better world is the first step toward creating it.
- Vision and creativity are essential in social change.
- Without a clear vision of what improvement looks like, efforts may be misguided.
