> The Analytical Engine does not occupy space in the same sense that a material object does.

— [[Person – Augusta Ada King, Countess of Lovelace]]

## Interpretation
- The Analytical Engine represents a conceptual framework rather than a physical entity.
- It highlights the distinction between abstract computation and tangible objects.
- This suggests that computational processes exist in a different realm than physical space.
