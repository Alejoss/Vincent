> The essence of the universe is substance, which is self-existent and necessarily exists.

— [[Person – Benedict de Spinoza]]

## Interpretation
- Substance is the fundamental reality that underlies everything in the universe.
- This substance is self-existent, meaning it does not rely on anything else for its existence.
- The necessity of its existence implies that it cannot be conceived as non-existent, affirming its absolute nature.
