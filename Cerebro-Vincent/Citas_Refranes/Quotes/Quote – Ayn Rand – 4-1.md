> A creative man is motivated by the desire to achieve, not by the desire to beat others.

— [[Person – Ayn Rand]]

## Interpretation
- Creative individuals seek personal excellence and fulfillment rather than competing with others.
- Motivation rooted in achievement fosters innovation and personal growth.
- The focus on self-improvement over rivalry promotes a healthier and more constructive creative environment.
