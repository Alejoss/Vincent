> We should think of the internet as a tool for empowerment rather than a source of alienation.

— [[Person – Jaron Lanier]]

## Interpretation
- The internet has the potential to enhance individual capabilities and self-expression.
- Viewing the internet as empowering encourages positive engagement and collaboration.
- Recognizing the internet's role in alienation highlights the need for mindful usage and reform.
