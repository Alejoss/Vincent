> We must challenge the idea that there is a single way to be a woman of color."

— [[Person – Patricia Hill Collins]]

## Interpretation
- The quote emphasizes the diversity of experiences among women of color.
- It encourages the recognition of multiple identities and expressions within this group.
- Challenging monolithic representations promotes a more inclusive understanding of womanhood.
