> The function of the intellectual is to resist the received knowledge of power."

— [[Person – Edward Said]]

## Interpretation
- Intellectuals play a crucial role in questioning and challenging established narratives.
- They serve as a counterbalance to the dominant ideologies imposed by those in power.
- Resisting received knowledge fosters critical thinking and social change.
