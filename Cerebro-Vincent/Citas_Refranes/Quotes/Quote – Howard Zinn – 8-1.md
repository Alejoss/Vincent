> The American people are not going to be tricked into going to war.

— [[Person – Howard Zinn]]

## Interpretation
- The quote reflects a sense of skepticism towards government narratives that promote war.
- It suggests that the American public is increasingly aware and critical of the motivations behind military actions.
- Zinn emphasizes the idea of public agency and the power of collective dissent against war.
