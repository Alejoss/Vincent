> He who knows only his own side of the case knows little of that.

— [[Person – John Stuart Mill]]

## Interpretation
- Understanding a debate requires knowledge of opposing viewpoints.
- Narrow perspectives limit one's comprehension of complex issues.
- Engagement with diverse opinions fosters deeper insight.
