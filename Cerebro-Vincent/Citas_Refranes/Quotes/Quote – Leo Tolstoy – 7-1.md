> All happy families are alike; each unhappy family is unhappy in its own way.

— [[Person – Leo Tolstoy]]

## Interpretation
- Happy families share common traits and dynamics that contribute to their well-being.
- Unhappiness in families arises from unique circumstances and individual struggles.
- This quote suggests that the complexity of human relationships leads to diverse experiences of unhappiness.
