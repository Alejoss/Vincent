> The most perfect education is that which develops the faculties of the mind and heart.

— [[Person – Mary Wollstonecraft]]

## Interpretation
- A well-rounded education nurtures both intellectual and emotional growth.
- True education transcends mere knowledge, fostering empathy and moral understanding.
- The development of both mind and heart is essential for a balanced and fulfilling life.
