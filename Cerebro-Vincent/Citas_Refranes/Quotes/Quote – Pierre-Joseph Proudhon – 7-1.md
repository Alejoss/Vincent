> To be governed is to be kept in sight, inspected, spied upon, directed, legislated, regulated, indoctrinated, controlled, evaluated, censured, and commanded.

— [[Person – Pierre-Joseph Proudhon]]

## Interpretation
- Governance is a process that involves constant observation and intervention in individuals' lives.
- The quote highlights the oppressive nature of authority and the loss of personal autonomy.
- Proudhon critiques the various mechanisms through which power maintains control over society.
