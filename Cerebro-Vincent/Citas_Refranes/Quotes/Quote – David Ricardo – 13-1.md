> It is the labor of the workman which creates the value of the commodities he produces.

— [[Person – David Ricardo]]

## Interpretation
- The value of a product is directly linked to the effort and skill of the laborer.
- Labor is the primary source of economic value in the production process.
- This perspective emphasizes the importance of workers in the economy.
