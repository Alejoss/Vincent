> We are not just what we do; we are what we are capable of doing.

— [[Person – Hannah Arendt]]

## Interpretation
- Our identity extends beyond our current actions and achievements.
- Potential and capability are essential aspects of who we are.
- Self-understanding involves recognizing our possibilities for growth and change.
