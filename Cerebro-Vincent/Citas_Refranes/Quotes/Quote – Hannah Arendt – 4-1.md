> Totalitarianism is a form of government in which the state seeks to regulate every aspect of public and private life.

— [[Person – Hannah Arendt]]

## Interpretation
- Totalitarianism seeks total control over individuals, limiting personal freedoms.
- The state intervenes in both public and private spheres, blurring boundaries.
- Such a regime aims to create a uniform society by regulating all aspects of life.
