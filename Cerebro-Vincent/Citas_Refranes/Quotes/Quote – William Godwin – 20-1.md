> Every human being has the right to think independently and express themselves.

— [[Person – William Godwin]]

## Interpretation
- Individuals possess an inherent freedom to form their own thoughts.
- Self-expression is essential for personal autonomy and societal progress.
- Respecting independent thought fosters diversity and innovation in ideas.
