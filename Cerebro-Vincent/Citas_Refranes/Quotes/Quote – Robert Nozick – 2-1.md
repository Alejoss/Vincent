> A person is entitled to his holdings as long as they were acquired fairly and without violating the rights of others.

— [[Person – Robert Nozick]]

## Interpretation
- Individuals have a right to possess what they have justly obtained.
- Fair acquisition is essential for rightful ownership.
- Respecting the rights of others is a prerequisite for legitimate holdings.
