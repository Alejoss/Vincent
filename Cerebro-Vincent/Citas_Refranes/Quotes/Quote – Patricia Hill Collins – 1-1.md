> Black feminist thought is an intersectional approach to understanding the experiences of Black women."

— [[Person – Patricia Hill Collins]]

## Interpretation
- Black feminist thought examines the unique challenges faced by Black women in society.
- It emphasizes the importance of considering multiple identities and oppressions simultaneously.
- This approach seeks to highlight and validate the lived experiences of Black women.
