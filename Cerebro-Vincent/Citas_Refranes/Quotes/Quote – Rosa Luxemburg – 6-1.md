> The masses are the only ones who can bring about real change.

— [[Person – Rosa Luxemburg]]

## Interpretation
- Real change is not achievable through elite actions alone.
- The collective power of the masses is essential for true transformation.
- Grassroots movements and popular will drive significant societal progress.
