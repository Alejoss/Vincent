> In a world without objective truth, the best we can do is to be more tolerant of different views.

— [[Person – Richard Rorty]]

## Interpretation
- Objective truth is an unattainable concept, leading to subjective perspectives.
- Embracing tolerance allows for a more harmonious coexistence of diverse viewpoints.
- Acceptance of differing beliefs fosters dialogue and understanding in society.
