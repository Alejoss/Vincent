> Freedom is the right to be wrong, not the right to do wrong."

— [[Person – Isabel Paterson]]

## Interpretation
- Freedom allows individuals to make mistakes and learn from them.
- The quote distinguishes between personal liberty and moral responsibility.
- True freedom encompasses the ability to choose, even if those choices may lead to errors.
