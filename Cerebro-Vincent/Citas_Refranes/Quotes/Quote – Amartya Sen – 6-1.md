> It is important to recognize that the quality of life cannot be reduced to mere wealth."

— [[Person – Amartya Sen]]

## Interpretation
- Quality of life encompasses various factors beyond financial wealth.
- Emphasizing wealth alone overlooks critical aspects such as health, education, and happiness.
- A holistic view of well-being is essential for understanding human welfare.
