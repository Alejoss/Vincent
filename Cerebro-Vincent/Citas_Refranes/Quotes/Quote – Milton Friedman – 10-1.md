> Nothing is so permanent as a temporary government program.

— [[Person – Milton Friedman]]

## Interpretation
- Temporary government programs often become long-lasting due to bureaucratic inertia.
- Once established, such programs tend to persist even when their initial purpose has faded.
- This highlights the challenge of government intervention and the difficulty in reversing policies once they are enacted.
