> The actions of men are the best interpreters of their thoughts.

— [[Person – John Locke]]

## Interpretation
- People's behaviors provide clearer insights into their true beliefs than their words.
- Actions reveal intentions and convictions that may be hidden in speech.
- Observing behavior is essential for understanding the mindset of individuals.
