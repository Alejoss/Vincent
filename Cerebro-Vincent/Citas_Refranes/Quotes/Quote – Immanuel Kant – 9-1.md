> Experience without theory is blind, but theory without experience is mere intellectual play.

— [[Person – Immanuel Kant]]

## Interpretation
- Experience provides the empirical evidence needed to support theoretical concepts.
- Theory offers a framework for understanding and interpreting experiences.
- Both experience and theory are essential for a comprehensive understanding of the world.
