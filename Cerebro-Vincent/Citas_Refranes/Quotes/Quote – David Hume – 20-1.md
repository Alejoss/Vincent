> There is nothing more chimerical than a man who believes he is free.

— [[Person – David Hume]]

## Interpretation
- Hume suggests that the belief in personal freedom is an illusion, akin to a fantasy.
- He implies that human actions are often influenced by external factors beyond individual control.
- This challenges the notion of free will, inviting reflection on determinism in human behavior.
