> There are many truths of which the full meaning cannot be realized until personal experience has brought it home.

— [[Person – John Stuart Mill]]

## Interpretation
- True understanding often requires personal experience rather than just theoretical knowledge.
- Some lessons and truths are only fully appreciated when lived through.
- Personal experiences shape our comprehension of complex ideas.
