> If you think technology is going to fix everything, you are wrong.

— [[Person – Cory Doctorow]]

## Interpretation
- Technology alone cannot solve complex societal issues.
- Over-reliance on technology can lead to disillusionment.
- A multifaceted approach is necessary for genuine progress.
