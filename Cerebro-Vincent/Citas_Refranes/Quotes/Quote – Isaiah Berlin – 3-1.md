> The desire to be a good person is not enough to make one a good person.

— [[Person – Isaiah Berlin]]

## Interpretation
- Intentions alone do not determine moral character.
- Goodness requires action and the application of one's values.
- A genuine commitment to ethical behavior is essential for moral integrity.
