# Person – Jean-Jacques Rousseau

## About
Jean-Jacques Rousseau was a pivotal figure in the development of political philosophy, particularly known for his ideas on the social contract and the nature of human freedom. His works laid the groundwork for modern democratic theory and influenced the French Revolution.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1712-06-28
- Died: 1778-07-02

### Notable Works
- The Social Contract
- Discourse on the Origin and Basis of Inequality Among Men
- Emile, or On Education

### Categories
- Political Philosophy
- Social Contract Theory
- Romanticism
- political philosophy
