# Person – Albert Jay Nock

## About
Albert Jay Nock was an American libertarian author and social critic known for his advocacy of individualism and his critique of the state. He played a significant role in the development of libertarian thought in the early 20th century, particularly through his writings on education and government.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1870-10-13
- Died: 1945-08-19

### Notable Works
- Our Enemy, the State
- The Myth of a Guilty Nation
- The Jeffersonian Tradition
