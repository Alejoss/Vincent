# Person – Johann Most

## About
Johann Most was a German-American anarchist and political activist known for his advocacy of revolutionary socialism and his role in the development of anarchist thought in the United States. He was a prominent figure in the anarchist movement, particularly through his writings and speeches that emphasized direct action and the necessity of violent revolution.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1846-02-05
- Died: 1906-03-17

### Notable Works
- The Science of Revolutionary Warfare
- Anarchism and Anarcho-Syndicalism
- The Anarchist Cookbook
