# Person – Francesco Ferrer

## About
Francesco Ferrer was a Spanish educator and anarchist known for founding the Modern School movement, which advocated for secular and progressive education. His ideas challenged traditional educational methods and promoted rationalism and critical thinking, making significant contributions to libertarian education.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1859-04-10
- Died: 1909-10-13

### Notable Works
- The Origin and Ideals of the Modern School
- The Modern School
- The Education of the Future
