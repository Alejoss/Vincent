# Person – David Graeber

## About
David Graeber was an American anthropologist, activist, and prominent figure in the anarchist movement. He is known for his critiques of capitalism and his influential role in the Occupy Wall Street movement, as well as for his writings that challenge conventional economic theories.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1961-02-12
- Died: 2020-09-02

### Notable Works
- Debt: The First 5,000 Years
- Bullshit Jobs: A Theory
- Fragments of an Anarchist Anthropology
