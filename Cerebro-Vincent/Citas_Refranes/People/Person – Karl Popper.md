# Person – Karl Popper

## About
Karl Popper was an influential philosopher of science and political thinker known for his advocacy of liberal democracy and criticism of totalitarianism. His ideas on falsifiability and the open society have significantly impacted libertarian thought and the philosophy of science.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1902-07-28
- Died: 1994-09-17

### Notable Works
- The Logic of Scientific Discovery
- The Open Society and Its Enemies
- Conjectures and Refutations
- The Poverty of Historicism

### Categories
- Philosophy of Science
- Political Philosophy
- Libertarianism
