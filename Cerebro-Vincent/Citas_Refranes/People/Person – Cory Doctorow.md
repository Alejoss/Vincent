# Person – Cory Doctorow

## About
Cory Doctorow is a Canadian-British author, activist, and journalist known for his work in the fields of technology, copyright, and digital rights. As a prominent figure in the cypherpunk movement, he advocates for privacy and freedom of information in the digital age.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1971-07-17
- Status: Alive or unknown

- Homeland
- Down and Out in the Magic Kingdom
- For the Win
