# Person – Dahlia Lithwick

## About
Dahlia Lithwick is a prominent legal analyst and journalist known for her insightful commentary on the U.S. Supreme Court and legal issues. Her writings often reflect a liberal perspective, advocating for civil rights and social justice.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1970-01-01
- Status: Alive or unknown
