# Person – Noam Chomsky

## About
Noam Chomsky is a renowned linguist, philosopher, cognitive scientist, historian, and social critic known for his contributions to the field of linguistics and his critique of political systems. He is a prominent figure in anarchist thought, advocating for libertarian socialism and critiquing state power and capitalism.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1928-12-07
- Status: Alive or unknown
