# Person – Peter Kropotkin

## About
Peter Kropotkin was a Russian anarchist, geographer, and philosopher known for his advocacy of anarcho-communism and mutual aid. His works emphasized the importance of cooperation and social organization in human society, challenging the prevailing notions of survival of the fittest.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1842-12-09
- Died: 1921-02-08

### Notable Works
- Mutual Aid: A Factor of Evolution
- The Conquest of Bread
- Fields, Factories and Workshops
