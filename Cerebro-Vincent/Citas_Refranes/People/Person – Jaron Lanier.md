# Person – Jaron Lanier

## About
Jaron Lanier is a computer scientist, author, and virtual reality pioneer known for his critiques of social media and the digital economy. He advocates for a human-centered approach to technology and has contributed significantly to discussions on the ethical implications of the internet.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1960-10-03
- Status: Alive or unknown

### Notable Works
- You Are Not a Gadget
- Who Owns the Future?
- Ten Arguments for Deleting Your Social Media Accounts Right Now
