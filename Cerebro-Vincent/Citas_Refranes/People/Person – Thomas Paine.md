# Person – Thomas Paine

## About
Thomas Paine was an English-American political activist, philosopher, and revolutionary. He is best known for his influential writings that inspired the American Revolution and laid the groundwork for modern libertarian thought, advocating for individual rights and government accountability.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1737-01-29
- Died: 1809-06-08

### Notable Works
- Common Sense
- The Rights of Man
- The Age of Reason

### Categories
- libertarianism
- political philosophy
- revolutionary thought
