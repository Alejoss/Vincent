# Person – Lysander Spooner

## About
Lysander Spooner was an American individualist anarchist, political theorist, and abolitionist known for his critiques of government and advocacy for natural law. His writings challenged the legitimacy of state authority and emphasized individual sovereignty and voluntary cooperation.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1808-01-19
- Died: 1887-05-14

### Notable Works
- The Unconstitutionality of Slavery
- No Treason: The Constitution of No Authority
- Letter to Thomas F. Bayard
