# Person – Patricia Hill Collins

## About
Patricia Hill Collins is an influential American sociologist known for her work on intersectionality, race, gender, and social class. She has significantly contributed to feminist theory and is recognized for her advocacy of Black feminist thought.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1948-05-01
- Status: Alive or unknown

### Notable Works
- Black Feminist Thought: Knowledge, Consciousness, and the Politics of Empowerment
- Sister Outsider
- From Black Power to Hip Hop: Racism, Nationalism, and Feminism
