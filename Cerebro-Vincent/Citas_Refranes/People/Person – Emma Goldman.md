# Person – Emma Goldman

## About
Emma Goldman was a prominent anarchist political activist and writer known for her advocacy of free speech, women's rights, and labor rights. She played a significant role in the development of anarchist theory and practice in the early 20th century, influencing generations of activists.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1869-06-27
- Died: 1940-05-14

### Notable Works
- Anarchism and Other Essays
- Living My Life
- My Disillusionment in Russia

- Political Activism
- Feminism
