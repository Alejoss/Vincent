# Person – Derrick Bell

## About
Derrick Bell was an American lawyer, academic, and civil rights activist, known as one of the founding figures of critical race theory. His work focused on the intersection of race, law, and social justice, challenging the traditional narratives of racial progress in the United States.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1930-11-06
- Died: 2011-10-05

### Notable Works
- Faces at the Bottom of the Well
- And We Are Not Saved
- Race, Racism and American Law
