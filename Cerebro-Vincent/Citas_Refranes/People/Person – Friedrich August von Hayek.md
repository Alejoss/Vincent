# Person – Friedrich August von Hayek

## About
F. A. Hayek was an Austrian-British economist and political philosopher known for his defense of classical liberalism and free-market capitalism. He significantly contributed to the development of libertarian thought, particularly through his critiques of socialism and central planning.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1899-05-08
- Died: 1992-03-23

### Notable Works
- The Road to Serfdom
- The Constitution of Liberty
- Law, Legislation and Liberty
- The Sensory Order
