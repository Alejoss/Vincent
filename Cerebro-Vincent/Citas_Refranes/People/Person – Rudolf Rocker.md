# Person – Rudolf Rocker

## About
Rudolf Rocker was a prominent anarchist theorist and activist known for his advocacy of anarcho-syndicalism and his critiques of both capitalism and state socialism. He played a significant role in the development of anarchist thought in the early 20th century and was influential in labor movements across Europe and the United States.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1873-03-25
- Died: 1958-09-19
