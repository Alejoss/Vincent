# Person – Lucy Parsons

## About
Lucy Parsons was a prominent American labor organizer, radical socialist, and anarchist known for her advocacy for workers' rights and her powerful oratory. She played a significant role in the Haymarket affair and was a co-founder of the Industrial Workers of the World (IWW).

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1853-01-01
- Died: 1942-03-07

### Notable Works
- Freedom, Equality, and the Anarchist Movement
- The Life of an American Anarchist
