# Person – Richard Cobden

## About
Richard Cobden was a British manufacturer and politician known for his advocacy of free trade and his role in the Anti-Corn Law League. He is often associated with classical liberalism and libertarian principles, emphasizing individual liberty and limited government intervention in the economy.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1804-06-03
- Died: 1865-04-02

### Notable Works
- The Political Economy of the Corn Laws
- Free Trade and the Working Classes
- The Cobden Club Essays

### Categories
- classical liberalism
- libertarianism
- free trade
