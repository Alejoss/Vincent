# Person – Voltairine de Cleyre

## About
Voltairine de Cleyre was an influential American anarchist and feminist known for her advocacy of individualism and free thought. She contributed significantly to anarchist literature and philosophy, emphasizing the importance of personal liberty and social justice.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1866-11-17
- Died: 1912-06-20

### Notable Works
- The Dominant Idea
- Anarchism and American Traditions
- The Philosophy of Anarchism

### Categories
- Anarchism
- Feminism
- Individualism
- anarchism
