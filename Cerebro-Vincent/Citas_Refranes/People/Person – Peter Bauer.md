# Person – Peter Bauer

## About
Peter Bauer was a prominent economist and a leading advocate of free-market principles and libertarianism. He is known for his critiques of foreign aid and his emphasis on the importance of individual entrepreneurship in economic development.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1915-09-16
- Died: 2002-01-26

### Notable Works
- Equality, the Third World, and Economic Delusion
- The Development Frontier
- The Economics of Underdeveloped Countries

### Categories
- libertarianism
- economics
- development economics
