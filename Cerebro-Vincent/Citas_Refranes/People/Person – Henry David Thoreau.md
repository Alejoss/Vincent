# Person – Henry David Thoreau

## About
Henry David Thoreau was an American essayist, poet, and philosopher, best known for his role in the transcendentalist movement. His works advocate for simple living, nature, and civil disobedience, influencing both environmentalism and social justice.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1817-07-12
- Died: 1862-05-06

### Notable Works
- Walden
- Civil Disobedience
- A Week on the Concord and Merrimack Rivers
- The Maine Woods

### Categories
- Transcendentalism
- Philosophy
- Environmentalism
- Civil Disobedience
- transcendentalism
