# Person – David Chaum

## About
David Chaum is a computer scientist and cryptographer known for his pioneering work in digital cash and privacy-preserving technologies. He is a key figure in the cypherpunk movement, advocating for the use of cryptography to enhance privacy and individual freedom in the digital age.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1955
- Status: Alive or unknown

### Notable Works
- Blind Signatures for Untraceable Payments
- The Dining Cryptographers Protocol
- Digital Cash: A Conceptual Framework
