# Person – Chantal Mouffe

## About
Chantal Mouffe is a prominent political theorist known for her work on agonistic democracy and the critique of liberalism. Her contributions have significantly influenced contemporary debates on democracy, identity, and political conflict.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1943-06-17
- Status: Alive or unknown

### Notable Works
- The Democratic Paradox
- On the Political
- Agonistics: Thinking the World Politically
