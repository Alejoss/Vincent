# Person – Susan Moller Okin

## About
Susan Moller Okin was a prominent feminist political philosopher known for her critiques of traditional political theory and her advocacy for gender equality. Her work emphasized the importance of considering women's experiences and perspectives in political discourse.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1936-04-19
- Died: 2004-03-03

### Notable Works
- Justice, Gender, and the Family
- Women in Western Political Thought
- Is Multiculturalism Bad for Women?
