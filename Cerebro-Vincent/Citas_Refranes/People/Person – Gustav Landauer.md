# Person – Gustav Landauer

## About
Gustav Landauer was a German anarchist, writer, and social activist known for his contributions to anarchist theory and his advocacy for a decentralized, communal society. He played a significant role in the Bavarian Soviet Republic and emphasized the importance of culture and community in achieving social change.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1870-04-09
- Died: 1919-05-02
