# Person – John Stuart Mill

## About
John Stuart Mill was a British philosopher and political economist, known for his contributions to liberal thought and utilitarianism. His writings on liberty, individual rights, and the role of government have significantly influenced modern libertarianism and liberal political theory.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1806-05-20
- Died: 1873-05-08

### Notable Works
- On Liberty
- The Subjection of Women
- Utilitarianism
- A System of Logic
