# Person – Martin Hellman

## About
Martin Hellman is an American cryptographer and a professor emeritus at Stanford University, best known for his pioneering work in public-key cryptography. He co-invented the Diffie-Hellman key exchange protocol, which laid the foundation for secure communications over the internet.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1945-10-02
- Status: Alive or unknown
