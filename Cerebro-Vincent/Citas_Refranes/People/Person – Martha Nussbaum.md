# Person – Martha Nussbaum

## About
Martha Nussbaum is an influential American philosopher known for her work in ethics, political philosophy, and the capabilities approach. Her contributions to liberalism emphasize the importance of human dignity and the role of emotions in moral reasoning.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1947-05-06
- Status: Alive or unknown

### Notable Works
- The Fragility of Goodness
- Women and Human Development
- Creating Capabilities
- The Monarchy of Fear
