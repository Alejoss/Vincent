# Person – François Guizot

## About
François Guizot was a French historian, politician, and a prominent figure in the development of liberalism in France during the 19th century. He served as the Minister of Education and later as Prime Minister, advocating for constitutional government and the importance of education in a democratic society.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1787-10-04
- Died: 1874-07-12
