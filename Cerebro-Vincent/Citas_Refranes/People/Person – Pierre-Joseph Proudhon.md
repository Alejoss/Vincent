# Person – Pierre-Joseph Proudhon

## About
Pierre-Joseph Proudhon was a French political philosopher and the first person to declare himself an anarchist. He is known for his critique of capitalism and advocacy for mutualism, emphasizing the importance of self-managed labor and cooperative societies.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1809-01-15
- Died: 1865-01-19

### Notable Works
- What is Property?
- System of Economic Contradictions
- The Philosophy of Misery

### Categories
- Anarchism
- Mutualism
- Socialism
- anarchism
