# Person – Bertrand Russell

## About
Bertrand Russell was a British philosopher, logician, and social critic known for his advocacy of liberalism and pacifism. He played a significant role in the development of modern philosophy and was a prominent figure in the anti-war movement during the 20th century.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1872-05-18
- Died: 1970-02-02

### Notable Works
- Principia Mathematica
- A History of Western Philosophy
- The Problems of Philosophy
- Why I Am Not a Christian
- Political Ideals
