# Person – Kevin David Mitnick

## About
Kevin David Mitnick is a renowned American computer security consultant and author, known for his high-profile hacking activities in the 1980s and 1990s. After serving time in prison, he became a leading figure in cybersecurity, advocating for ethical hacking and security awareness.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1963-08-06
- Status: Alive or unknown

### Notable Works
- The Art of Deception
- The Art of Intrusion
- Ghost in the Wires
