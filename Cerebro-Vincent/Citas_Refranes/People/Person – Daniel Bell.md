# Person – Daniel Bell

## About
Daniel Bell was an American sociologist and a prominent figure in the study of post-industrial society. He is best known for his contributions to liberal thought and his analysis of the changing nature of work and society in the modern era.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1919-05-10
- Died: 2011-01-25

### Notable Works
- The Coming of Post-Industrial Society
- The Cultural Contradictions of Capitalism
- The End of Ideology

### Categories
- liberalism
- sociology
- political theory
