# Person – James M. Buchanan

## About
James M. Buchanan was an American economist known for his foundational work in public choice theory, which applies economic principles to political decision-making. He was awarded the Nobel Prize in Economic Sciences in 1986 for his contributions to the understanding of political behavior and the role of government.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1919-10-03
- Died: 2013-01-09

### Notable Works
- The Calculus of Consent
- Public Choice: A Survey
- The Limits of Liberty
- Democracy in Deficit
