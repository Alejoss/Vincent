# Person – Thomas Hill Green

## About
Thomas Hill Green was a British philosopher and a key figure in the development of liberal thought in the late 19th century. He is best known for his work on the philosophy of idealism and his advocacy for a more active role of the state in promoting individual freedom and social justice.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1836-04-09
- Died: 1882-03-26

### Notable Works
- Prolegomena to Ethics
- Lectures on the Principles of Political Obligation
- The Works of Thomas Hill Green
