# Person – Benedict de Spinoza

## About
Benedict de Spinoza was a Dutch philosopher who is considered one of the great rationalists of the 17th century. His work laid the groundwork for the Enlightenment and modern biblical criticism, emphasizing the use of reason in understanding the universe and ethics.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1632-11-24
- Died: 1677-02-21
