# Person – Hannah Arendt

## About
Hannah Arendt was a German-American political theorist known for her works on the nature of power, totalitarianism, and the human condition. Her analysis of the banality of evil and her exploration of the relationship between freedom and authority have had a profound impact on political thought.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1906-10-14
- Died: 1975-12-04

### Notable Works
- The Human Condition
- The Origins of Totalitarianism
- Eichmann in Jerusalem
- On Revolution
- The Life of the Mind
