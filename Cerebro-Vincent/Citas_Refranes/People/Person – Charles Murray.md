# Person – Charles Murray

## About
Charles Murray is an American political scientist, author, and libertarian theorist known for his controversial views on social policy and intelligence. He gained prominence with his book 'The Bell Curve,' which discusses the role of intelligence in social outcomes and has sparked significant debate in academic and public spheres.
