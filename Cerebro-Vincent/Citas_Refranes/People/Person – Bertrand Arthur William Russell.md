# Person – Bertrand Arthur William Russell

## About
Bertrand Russell was a British philosopher, logician, and social critic known for his work in analytical philosophy and for his advocacy of pacifism and social reform. He made significant contributions to logic, the philosophy of mathematics, and political theory.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1872-05-18
- Died: 1970-02-02

### Notable Works
- Principia Mathematica
- A History of Western Philosophy
- The Problems of Philosophy
- On Liberty
- Why I Am Not a Christian
