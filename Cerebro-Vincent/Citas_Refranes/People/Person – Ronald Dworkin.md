# Person – Ronald Dworkin

## About
Ronald Dworkin was a prominent American philosopher and legal scholar known for his contributions to liberal theory and constitutional law. He advocated for the moral reading of the Constitution and emphasized the importance of individual rights and equality in a democratic society.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1931-12-11
- Died: 2013-02-14
