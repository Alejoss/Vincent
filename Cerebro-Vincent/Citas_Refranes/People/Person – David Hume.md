# Person – David Hume

## About
David Hume was a Scottish philosopher, historian, and economist known for his influential contributions to empiricism and skepticism. His work laid the foundation for modern philosophy, particularly in the areas of human understanding and the philosophy of science.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1711-04-26
- Died: 1776-08-25

### Notable Works
- A Treatise of Human Nature
- An Enquiry Concerning Human Understanding
- An Enquiry Concerning the Principles of Morals
- The History of England

### Categories
- Empiricism
- Skepticism
- Philosophy of Mind
- Political Philosophy
- empiricism
