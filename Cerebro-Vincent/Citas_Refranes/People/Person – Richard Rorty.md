# Person – Richard Rorty

## About
Richard Rorty was an American philosopher associated with pragmatism and a prominent figure in contemporary philosophy. He is known for his critique of traditional epistemology and his advocacy for a more flexible, conversational approach to truth and knowledge.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1931-10-04
- Died: 2017-06-08

### Notable Works
- Philosophy and the Mirror of Nature
- Contingency, Irony, and Solidarity
- Achieving Our Country
