# Person – Murray Newton Rothbard

## About
Murray Rothbard was an influential American economist, historian, and political theorist known for his role in developing libertarian thought and the Austrian School of economics. He advocated for a free-market economy, individual liberty, and minimal government intervention.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1926-02-02
- Died: 1995-01-07

### Notable Works
- Man, Economy, and State
- Power and Market
- For a New Liberty
- The Ethics of Liberty
- America's Great Depression
