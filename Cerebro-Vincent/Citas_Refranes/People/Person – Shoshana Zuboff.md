# Person – Shoshana Zuboff

## About
Shoshana Zuboff is an American author, scholar, and thought leader best known for her work on the social, economic, and psychological implications of digital technology. Her influential books, particularly 'The Support Economy' and 'The Age of Surveillance Capitalism', explore the intersection of technology and society, emphasizing the impact of data privacy and corporate surveillance.
