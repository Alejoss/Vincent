# Person – John Rawls

## About
John Rawls was an influential American political philosopher best known for his work in moral and political philosophy. His seminal book, 'A Theory of Justice,' introduced the concepts of the 'original position' and the 'veil of ignorance,' reshaping contemporary discussions on justice and fairness.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1921-02-21
- Died: 2002-11-24

### Notable Works

- Political Liberalism
- The Law of Peoples
