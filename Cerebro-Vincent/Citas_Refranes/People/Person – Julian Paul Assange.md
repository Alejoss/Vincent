# Person – Julian Paul Assange

## About
Julian Assange is an Australian journalist, computer programmer, and founder of WikiLeaks, known for his role in publishing classified documents and exposing government misconduct. He is a prominent figure in the cypherpunk movement, advocating for privacy, freedom of information, and transparency.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1971-07-03
- Status: Alive or unknown
