# Person – William Godwin

## About
William Godwin was an English philosopher and political writer, known for his foundational contributions to anarchist thought and his advocacy for individual liberty and rationalism. His seminal work, 'Political Justice,' laid the groundwork for later anarchist and socialist theories.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1756-03-03
- Died: 1836-04-07

### Notable Works
- Political Justice
- Enquiry Concerning Political Justice
- The St. Leon
- Memoirs of the Author of A Vindication of the Rights of Woman

### Categories
- anarchism
- philosophy
- political theory
