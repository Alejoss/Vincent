# Person – Samuel Edward Konkin III

## About
Samuel Edward Konkin III was a prominent libertarian theorist and the founder of agorism, a libertarian socialist philosophy that advocates for counter-economics as a means of achieving a free society. He was influential in the development of the modern libertarian movement and contributed significantly to libertarian literature and activism.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1947
- Status: Alive or unknown

### Notable Works

- An Agorist Primer
