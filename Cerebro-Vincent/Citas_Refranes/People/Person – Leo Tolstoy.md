# Person – Leo Tolstoy

## About
Leo Tolstoy was a Russian writer and philosopher known for his deep moral and spiritual inquiries, which led him to embrace anarchist principles in his later life. He advocated for nonviolent resistance and a form of Christian anarchism, influencing various social movements and thinkers.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1828-09-09
- Died: 1910-11-20

### Notable Works
- War and Peace
- Anna Karenina
- The Kingdom of God Is Within You
- The Death of Ivan Ilyich

### Categories
- anarchism
- Christian anarchism
- philosophy
- literature
