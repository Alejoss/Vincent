# Person – Immanuel Kant

## About
Immanuel Kant was a central figure in modern philosophy, known for his work in epistemology, ethics, and metaphysics. His ideas about the nature of human understanding and the moral imperative have had a profound influence on subsequent philosophical thought.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1724-04-22
- Died: 1804-02-12

### Notable Works
- Critique of Pure Reason
- Critique of Practical Reason
- Groundwork for the Metaphysics of Morals
- Critique of Judgment

### Categories
- Transcendental Idealism
- Deontological Ethics
- Metaphysics
