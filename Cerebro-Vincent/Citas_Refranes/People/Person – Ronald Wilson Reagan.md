# Person – Ronald Wilson Reagan

## About
Ronald Reagan was the 40th President of the United States, serving from 1981 to 1989. He is known for his conservative policies, promotion of free-market principles, and a strong anti-communist stance, which resonated with libertarian ideals of limited government and individual liberties.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1911-02-06
- Died: 2004-06-05
