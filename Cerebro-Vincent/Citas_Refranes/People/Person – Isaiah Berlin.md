# Person – Isaiah Berlin

## About
Isaiah Berlin was a British social and political theorist, philosopher, and historian of ideas, known for his defense of liberalism and pluralism. His influential essays and lectures emphasized the importance of individual freedom and the value of diverse perspectives in political thought.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1909-06-06
- Died: 1997-11-05

### Notable Works
- Two Concepts of Liberty
- The Hedgehog and the Fox
- Four Essays on Liberty
- The Roots of Romanticism
