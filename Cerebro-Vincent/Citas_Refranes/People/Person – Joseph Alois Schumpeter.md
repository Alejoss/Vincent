# Person – Joseph Alois Schumpeter

## About
Joseph Alois Schumpeter was an Austrian-American economist and political scientist known for his theories on entrepreneurship and innovation within capitalism. His work emphasized the role of the entrepreneur in economic development and contributed significantly to the understanding of business cycles and economic evolution.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1883-02-08
- Died: 1950-01-08

### Notable Works
- The Theory of Economic Development
- Capitalism, Socialism and Democracy
- Business Cycles
