# Person – Lawrence Lessig

## About
Lawrence Lessig is a prominent American legal scholar and political activist known for his work in cyberlaw and intellectual property. He is a co-founder of Creative Commons and has been a leading advocate for reforming copyright laws to better accommodate the digital age.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1961-06-03
- Status: Alive or unknown
