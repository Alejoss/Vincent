# Person – Albert O. Hirschman

## About
Albert O. Hirschman was a prominent economist and social scientist known for his contributions to development economics and his critiques of conventional economic thought. His works emphasized the importance of human agency and the unpredictability of social change, challenging deterministic views in liberalism.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1915-04-07
- Died: 2012-12-10

### Notable Works
- Exit, Voice, and Loyalty
- Development Projects Observed
- The Strategy of Economic Development
