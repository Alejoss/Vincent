# Person – Amartya Sen

## About
Amartya Sen is an Indian economist and philosopher known for his contributions to welfare economics, social choice theory, and development economics. His work has significantly influenced liberal thought, particularly in the areas of justice, poverty, and human capabilities.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1933-11-03
- Status: Alive or unknown

### Notable Works
- Development as Freedom
- The Idea of Justice
- Poverty and Famines
- The Argumentative Indian
