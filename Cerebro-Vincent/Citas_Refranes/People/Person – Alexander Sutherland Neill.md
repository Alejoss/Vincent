# Person – Alexander Sutherland Neill

## About
Alexander Sutherland Neill was a Scottish educator and writer, best known for founding Summerhill School, one of the first democratic schools in the world. His philosophy emphasized freedom and self-directed learning, aligning with anarchist principles of individual liberty and anti-authoritarianism.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1883-12-17
- Died: 1973-09-23

### Notable Works
- Summerhill: A Radical Approach to Child Rearing
- The Problem of Childhood
- Freedom, Not License

### Categories
- anarchism
- education
- libertarianism
