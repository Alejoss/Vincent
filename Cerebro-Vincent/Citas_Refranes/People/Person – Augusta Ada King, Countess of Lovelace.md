# Person – Augusta Ada King, Countess of Lovelace

## About
Augusta Ada King, Countess of Lovelace, is often regarded as the first computer programmer for her work on Charles Babbage's early mechanical general-purpose computer, the Analytical Engine. She recognized the machine's potential beyond mere calculation, envisioning its ability to manipulate symbols and create complex algorithms.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1815-12-10
- Died: 1852-11-27

### Notable Works
- Notes on the Analytical Engine
- Translation of Luigi Federico Federico's Memoir on the Analytical Engine

- computing
