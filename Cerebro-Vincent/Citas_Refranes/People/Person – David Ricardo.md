# Person – David Ricardo

## About
David Ricardo was a British political economist and one of the most influential figures in classical economics. He is best known for his theories on comparative advantage and rent, which laid the groundwork for modern economic thought.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1772-04-18
- Died: 1823-09-11

### Notable Works
- Principles of Political Economy and Taxation
- On the Principles of Political Economy
- The High Price of Bullion
- Essay on the Influence of a Low Price of Corn on the Profits of Stock
