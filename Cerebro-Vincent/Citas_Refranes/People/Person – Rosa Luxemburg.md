# Person – Rosa Luxemburg

## About
Rosa Luxemburg was a Polish-German Marxist theorist, philosopher, and revolutionary socialist. She played a significant role in the development of socialist thought and was a prominent figure in the German Social Democratic Party, advocating for revolutionary socialism and critiquing reformism.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1871-03-05
- Died: 1919-01-15

### Notable Works
- The Accumulation of Capital
- Reform or Revolution
- The Mass Strike, The Political Party and the Trade Union
- The Russian Revolution

### Categories
- Marxism
- Socialism
- Revolutionary Politics
- marxism
