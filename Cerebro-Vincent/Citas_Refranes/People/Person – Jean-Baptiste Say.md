# Person – Jean-Baptiste Say

## About
Jean-Baptiste Say was a French economist and businessman known for his contributions to classical economics, particularly the formulation of Say's Law, which posits that supply creates its own demand. His work helped shape economic thought in the early 19th century and influenced later economists.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1767-01-05
- Died: 1832-11-15

### Notable Works
- Traité d'économie politique
- Catéchisme d'économie politique
- Cours complet d'économie politique pratique

- Liberalism
