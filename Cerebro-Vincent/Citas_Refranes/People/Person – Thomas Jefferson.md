# Person – Thomas Jefferson

## About
Thomas Jefferson was an American statesman, diplomat, and Founding Father who served as the third President of the United States. He is best known for drafting the Declaration of Independence and advocating for individual liberties and limited government, principles that resonate with libertarian thought.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1743-04-13
- Died: 1826-07-04

### Notable Works
- Declaration of Independence
- Notes on the State of Virginia
- The Federalist Papers (co-author)

### Categories
- Libertarianism
- Classical Liberalism
- Enlightenment Thought
- libertarianism
