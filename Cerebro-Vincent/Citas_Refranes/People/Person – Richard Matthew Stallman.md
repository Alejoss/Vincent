# Person – Richard Matthew Stallman

## About
Richard Stallman is a prominent software freedom activist and the founder of the Free Software Foundation. He is known for initiating the free software movement and developing the GNU operating system, which laid the groundwork for many modern software applications.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1953-03-16
- Status: Alive or unknown

### Notable Works
- GNU Manifesto
- Emacs
- GCC (GNU Compiler Collection)
- GNU General Public License
