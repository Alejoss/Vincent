# Person – Wilhelm von Humboldt

## About
Wilhelm von Humboldt was a German philosopher, linguist, and government functionary who played a significant role in the development of liberal thought in the 19th century. He is best known for his ideas on individual freedom, education, and the importance of language in shaping human experience.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1767-06-22
- Died: 1835-04-08

### Notable Works
- The Limits of State Action
- On Language
- The Sphere of the State
