# Person – Nick Szabo

## About
Nick Szabo is a computer scientist, legal scholar, and cryptographer known for his pioneering work in digital contracts and cryptocurrency. He is credited with the concept of 'smart contracts' and has been influential in the development of blockchain technology.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1964
- Status: Alive or unknown

### Notable Works

- Bit Gold
