# Person – Satoshi Nakamoto

## About
Satoshi Nakamoto is the pseudonymous creator of Bitcoin, the first decentralized cryptocurrency, and the author of its original white paper published in 2008. Nakamoto's work has significantly influenced the fields of digital currency and cryptography, embodying the principles of the cypherpunk movement.

### Notable Works
- Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System
- Bitcoin White Paper

### Categories
- cypherpunk
- cryptography
- digital currency
