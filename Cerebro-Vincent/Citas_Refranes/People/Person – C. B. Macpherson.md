# Person – C. B. Macpherson

## About
C. B. Macpherson was a Canadian political philosopher known for his critiques of liberal democracy and his analysis of the relationship between capitalism and democracy. His work emphasized the importance of social justice and the need for a more equitable distribution of power and resources.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1911-06-15
- Died: 1987-11-15

### Notable Works
- The Political Theory of Possessive Individualism
- Democracy in Alberta
- The Life and Times of Liberalism

### Categories

- Liberalism
