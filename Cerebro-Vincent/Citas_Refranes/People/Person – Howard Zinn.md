# Person – Howard Zinn

## About
Howard Zinn was an American historian, playwright, and social activist known for his influential work in the field of history from a leftist perspective. His most notable contribution is 'A People's History of the United States,' which presents American history through the lens of marginalized groups and critiques traditional narratives.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1922-08-24
- Died: 2017-01-27

### Notable Works
