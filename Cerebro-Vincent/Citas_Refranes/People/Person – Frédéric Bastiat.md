# Person – Frédéric Bastiat

## About
Frédéric Bastiat was a French economist, political theorist, and author known for his defense of classical liberalism and free-market economics. His writings, particularly on the concept of opportunity cost and the critique of protectionism, have significantly influenced libertarian thought.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1801-06-30
- Died: 1850-12-24

### Notable Works
- The Law
- What Is Seen and What Is Not Seen
- Economic Harmonies
- The Broken Window Fallacy

### Categories
- libertarianism
- classical liberalism
- economics
- political philosophy
