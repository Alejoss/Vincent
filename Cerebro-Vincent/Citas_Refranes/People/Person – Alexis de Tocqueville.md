# Person – Alexis de Tocqueville

## About
Alexis de Tocqueville was a French political thinker and historian, best known for his works on democracy and society in America. His seminal book, 'Democracy in America', explores the effects of democracy on American society and has had a lasting impact on political philosophy.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1805-07-29
- Died: 1859-04-16

### Notable Works
- Democracy in America
- The Old Regime and the Revolution
- The Ancien Régime and the Revolution
