# Person – Clive Staples Lewis

## About
C.S. Lewis was a British writer and theologian known for his works on Christian apologetics and fantasy literature. His influential writings, including 'Mere Christianity' and 'The Chronicles of Narnia', have made significant contributions to both literature and Christian thought.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1898-11-29
- Died: 1963-11-22

### Notable Works
- Mere Christianity
- The Screwtape Letters
- The Chronicles of Narnia
- The Problem of Pain
- A Grief Observed

### Categories

- Fantasy literature
- Theology
