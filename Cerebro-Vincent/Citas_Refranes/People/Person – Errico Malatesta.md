# Person – Errico Malatesta

## About
Errico Malatesta was an influential Italian anarchist, known for his advocacy of revolutionary anarchism and his role in the international anarchist movement. He contributed significantly to anarchist theory and practice through his writings and activism, emphasizing the importance of direct action and the need for social revolution.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1853-12-14
- Died: 1932-07-22

### Notable Works
- Anarchy
- The Method of Freedom
- The Anarchist Revolution
