# Person – Bruce Schneier

## About
Bruce Schneier is a renowned cybersecurity expert, author, and speaker known for his insights into security technology and policy. He has significantly influenced the field of cybersecurity through his writings and advocacy for privacy and security in the digital age.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1963-04-15
- Status: Alive or unknown

### Notable Works
- Secrets and Lies
- Liars and Outliers
- Data and Goliath
- Click Here to Kill Everybody

### Categories
- cybersecurity
- privacy advocacy
- technology policy
