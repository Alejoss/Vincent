# Person – Gustave de Molinari

## About
Gustave de Molinari was a Belgian economist and a prominent advocate of classical liberalism and libertarianism. He is best known for his work on the role of the free market in providing public goods and his critique of state intervention.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1819-03-03
- Died: 1912-01-28

### Notable Works
- Les Soirées de la Liberté
- De la Production de la Sécurité
- Économie Politique

### Categories
- Libertarianism
- Classical Liberalism
