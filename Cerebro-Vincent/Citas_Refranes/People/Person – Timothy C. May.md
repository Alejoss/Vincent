# Person – Timothy C. May

## About
Timothy C. May was a prominent American cryptographer and one of the founding members of the cypherpunk movement, advocating for the use of cryptography to enhance privacy and personal freedom. He is best known for his influential writings on digital privacy and the implications of technology on society.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1951-04-11
- Status: Alive or unknown

### Notable Works
- The Crypto Anarchist Manifesto
- The Future of Privacy
- The Digital Cash Revolution
