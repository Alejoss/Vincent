# Person – Johann Caspar Schmidt

## About
Max Stirner was a German philosopher and one of the most influential figures in the development of individualist anarchism. His seminal work, 'The Ego and Its Own,' critiques traditional notions of authority and morality, advocating for the primacy of the individual and self-interest.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1806-10-25
- Died: 1856-06-26

### Notable Works
- The Ego and Its Own
- The False Principle of Our Education
- Art and Religion
