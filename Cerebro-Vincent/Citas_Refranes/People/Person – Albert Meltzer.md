# Person – Albert Meltzer

## About
Albert Meltzer was a British anarchist and writer known for his contributions to anarchist theory and practice. He was a prominent figure in the anarchist movement in the UK and was involved in various social and political causes throughout his life.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1920-01-07
- Died: 1996-05-07

### Notable Works

- The Anarchist Revolution
- The London Anarchist Group
