# Person – Herbert Spencer

## About
Herbert Spencer was an English philosopher and sociologist known for his contributions to the development of social Darwinism and the application of evolutionary theory to philosophy and society. He coined the phrase 'survival of the fittest' and advocated for individualism and limited government intervention.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1820-04-27
- Died: 1903-12-08

### Notable Works
- The Principles of Sociology
- The Study of Sociology
- Education: Intellectual, Moral, and Physical
