# Person – Max Stirner

## About
Max Stirner was a German philosopher and one of the most significant figures in the development of anarchist thought. His work, particularly 'The Ego and Its Own', critiques traditional notions of morality and authority, advocating for individual autonomy and self-interest.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1806-10-25
- Died: 1856-06-26

### Notable Works
- The Ego and Its Own
- The False Principle of Our Education
- Stirner's Critics
