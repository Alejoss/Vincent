# Person – Edward Said

## About
Edward Said was a Palestinian-American scholar, literary critic, and public intellectual, best known for his influential work in postcolonial studies. His seminal book 'Orientalism' challenged Western perceptions of the East and laid the groundwork for the field of postcolonial theory.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1935-11-01
- Died: 2003-09-25

### Notable Works

- Culture and Imperialism
- The Question of Palestine
- Covering Islam
