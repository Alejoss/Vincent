# Person – Mitchell Kapor

## About
Mitchell Kapor is a prominent computer scientist and entrepreneur known for his role in the development of software applications and his advocacy for privacy and digital rights. He is a founding member of the cypherpunk movement, which promotes the use of cryptography to protect privacy and civil liberties in the digital age.
