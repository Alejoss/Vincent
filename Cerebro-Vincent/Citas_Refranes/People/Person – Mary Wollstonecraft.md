# Person – Mary Wollstonecraft

## About
Mary Wollstonecraft was an English writer, philosopher, and advocate of women's rights, best known for her work 'A Vindication of the Rights of Woman'. She is considered one of the founding figures of modern feminism, arguing for women's education and equality in her writings.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1759-04-27
- Died: 1797-09-10

### Notable Works
- A Vindication of the Rights of Woman
- A Vindication of the Rights of Men
- Letters Written During a Short Residence in Sweden, Norway, and Denmark
