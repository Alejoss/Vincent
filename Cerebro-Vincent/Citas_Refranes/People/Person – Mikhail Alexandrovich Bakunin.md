# Person – Mikhail Alexandrovich Bakunin

## About
Mikhail Bakunin was a Russian revolutionary and one of the most influential figures in the development of anarchist theory. He is known for his critiques of state authority and capitalism, advocating for a society based on voluntary cooperation and mutual aid.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1814-05-30
- Died: 1876-07-13

### Notable Works
- God and the State
- The Political Philosophy of Bakunin
- Statism and Anarchy

### Categories

- Political Philosophy
- Socialism
