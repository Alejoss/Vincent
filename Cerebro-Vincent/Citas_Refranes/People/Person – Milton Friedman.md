# Person – Milton Friedman

## About
Milton Friedman was a prominent American economist and a leading figure in the Chicago School of Economics. He was known for his advocacy of free-market capitalism and his contributions to the fields of consumption analysis, monetary policy, and the role of government in the economy.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1912-07-31
- Died: 2006-11-16

### Notable Works
- Capitalism and Freedom
- A Monetary History of the United States
- Free to Choose
- The Role of Monetary Policy
