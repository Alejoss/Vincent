# Person – William Graham Sumner

## About
William Graham Sumner was an American social scientist and a leading figure in the development of classical liberalism and libertarian thought in the late 19th century. He is known for his critiques of social reform and his advocacy for individualism and free markets.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1840-10-30
- Died: 1910-04-12

- The Challenge of Facts
