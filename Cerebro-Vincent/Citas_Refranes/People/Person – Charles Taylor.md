# Person – Charles Taylor

## About
Charles Taylor is a Canadian philosopher known for his contributions to political philosophy, particularly regarding liberalism, multiculturalism, and the philosophy of language. His work emphasizes the importance of identity and the role of community in shaping individual values and beliefs.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1931-11-05
- Status: Alive or unknown
