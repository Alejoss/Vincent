# Person – G. A. Cohen

## About
G. A. Cohen was a prominent political philosopher known for his work on Marxism, egalitarianism, and political theory. His contributions, particularly in critiques of capitalism and discussions on distributive justice, have significantly influenced contemporary debates in political philosophy.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1941-04-14
- Died: 2009-08-05

### Notable Works
- Karl Marx's Theory of History: A Defence
- Self-Ownership, Freedom, and Equality
- If You're an Egalitarian, How Come You're So Rich?
