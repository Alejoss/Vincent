# Person – Cornelius Castoriadis

## About
Cornelius Castoriadis was a Greek-French philosopher, economist, and social critic known for his contributions to anarchist thought and his critique of bureaucratic socialism. He co-founded the Socialisme ou Barbarie group and emphasized the importance of autonomy and self-creation in society.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1922-03-11
- Died: 2017-12-26

### Notable Works
- The Imaginary Institution of Society
- Crossroads in the Labyrinth
- The Rising Tide of Insignificancy
