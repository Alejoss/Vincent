# Person – Isabel Paterson

## About
Isabel Paterson was a prominent American author and political commentator known for her advocacy of individualism and free-market principles. She was a significant figure in the development of libertarian thought in the 20th century, particularly through her influential writings and her role in the founding of the libertarian movement.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1886-01-22
- Died: 1961-01-10

### Notable Works
- The God of the Machine
- The Shadowy Third
- The Golden Vanity
