# Person – Nancy Fraser

## About
Nancy Fraser is a prominent American feminist theorist known for her work on social justice, feminism, and critical theory. She has significantly contributed to debates on the intersection of gender, class, and race, advocating for a more inclusive understanding of justice.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1947-05-20
- Status: Alive or unknown
