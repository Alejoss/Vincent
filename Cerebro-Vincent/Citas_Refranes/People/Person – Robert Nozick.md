# Person – Robert Nozick

## About
Robert Nozick was an American philosopher best known for his work in political philosophy and libertarianism. His book 'Anarchy, State, and Utopia' is a seminal text that defends a minimal state and critiques redistributive justice.

### Biographical Information
- Born: 1938-11-16
- Status: Alive or unknown
